Design notes

Numbered top-to-bottom, in the order the redesigned surface is read.

1

New · the plate absorbs the prescriptionThe identity plate, inscribed

No boxed cards. The page opens in the reveal plate's own grammar — The Blade, the pinyin line, then an inscription band between hairlines carrying the two verdicts: ↑ Fire is your catalyst — the energy you need / ↓ Earth is your friction — the energy you don't. Identity is the frame; the prescription is what's engraved on it. Each line taps into that element's full reading (app-energy); the name taps into the Day Master card.

2

Preserved + valence layerThe dominance wheel

Geometry untouched — size still encodes presence, seat still encodes rank (DOMINANCE_WHEEL_RULES intact). New: every node carries an ↑/↓ pip, catalysts wear a lit ring, friction sits a shade quieter. The crowned Metal node wearing a ↓ is the point: it visually breaks the "biggest = best" misreading.

3

The hardest case, made a featureThe hollow seat glows

Fire is 0% and the major catalyst. Its node renders dashed-hollow with the strongest halo and the major pip — the emptiest seat is the loudest ask. The hint line beneath states the lesson: size is how much you carry; the marks are what it does for you.

4

Absorbed, not removedOne identity surface

The below-wheel DayMasterCta is gone — identity moved above the wheel and became the prescription's frame, so "who you are" and "what you need" are read in the same breath. Its route survives: the plate's name and the wheel's center seal both open the Day Master card. Only one seal remains on the surface (the wheel's center), so the reveal ceremony's gliding seal keeps a single landing spot.

5

Heavy modification · spines → role rowsThe shelf, regrouped

Presence-ranked accordion spines become row cards under two labeled bands: Catalyst — feed these and Friction — ease off. Presence survives as the gauge + %, spine cap and pigment DNA carry over, hooks are rewritten from the catalyst/friction angle. Chips handle the collisions: Major, Missing, Your core.
