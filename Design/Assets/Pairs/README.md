# Pair paintings (core element × energy)

The 25 pair paintings from the N1 brief (`Design/Library/Design_Pass_2026-10/N1_Pair_Illustrations_Brief.md`), painted at F2 xieyi to match the locked Day Master set. One painting per core element × energy, shared by the yin and yang stems of an element.

- **Masters:** `pair_{core}_{energy}.jpg`, the size the model painted them (1536×1024), saved as JPG at quality 90.
- **App copies:** `Elementum_App/public/concept-arts/pairs/pair_{core}_{energy}.png`, 1100×733, palette-compressed under 300 KB.
- **Used on:** card 4 of the Day Master page ("Through your five energies"), as a shallow ink band under each energy title (`InkArt kind="pair"` in `MergedViews.jsx`, `.dmp-ink.pair` in `merged.css`). The energy sheet's slot (N1 step 2) is still to wire.
- **Painted so far:** the five Metal pairs (2026-10-27): metal × metal, wood, fire, earth and water. 20 to go.

## Fan leaves (card 4)

- **What changed:** owner 2026-10-27 picked layout B, the round fan, then asked for "less detailed, more like traditional shanshui, minimalism is the key". Card 4 now uses a second set: `fan_{core}_{energy}`, each a minimal fan-leaf painting (团扇) in the manner of Song album leaves and Ma Yuan. The subject is small and centred, with few strokes, pale ink and most of the paper blank.
- **Masters:** `fan_*.jpg` here, at 1024×1024.
- **App copies:** `public/concept-arts/pairs/fan_*.png`, cropped to the middle 62% so the subject fills the circle, at 480×480.
- **The 3:2 `pair_` files** stay for the energy sheet (N1).
- **Painted so far:** the five Metal fans.

## Scroll strips (card 4, current)

- **Why:** owner 2026-10-28 kept the fan's minimal level of detail but asked for two things: "the stem object must show itself clearly and convey the relation vividly", and the picture should "fill the left rectangle and bleed into the text".
- **What they are:** card 4 now uses `strip_{core}_{energy}`. Each is a tall, minimal hanging-scroll painting (9:16) in which the stem's own object acts with the energy. For the Metal core:
  - Body: two swords leaning edge to edge.
  - Mind: a sword rising out of the mountain.
  - Order: a sword in the forge fire.
  - Action: a sword lodged in a split young trunk.
  - Expression: a sword standing in a stream.
- **Masters:** `strip_*.jpg` here.
- **App copies:** `public/concept-arts/pairs/strip_*.png`, 432×768, under 300 KB.
- **The `fan_` files** are retired from the page but kept.
