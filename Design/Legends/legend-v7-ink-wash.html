▸§V7.0

Brief — what scenery-margin painting solves

Replacing flat silk with composed silk · ten plates, one protocol.

Through v6 the canonical screen background was a single flat colour — var(--silk) · #F1E9D6 — with all painterly content concentrated inside cards (the StemTile gradient, the PainterlyHero bleed image, the HalftoneTile photo). Cards earned the painting; the field around them was inert.

That works for hierarchy but produces a recurring failure mode in long flows: screens that are mostly form, list, or single-CTA read as plain. The brand register evaporates whenever there isn’t a reason to put a hero on the screen — onboarding step 3 of 7, settings, an empty state, a chapter divider. Users see Elementum’s vocabulary on landing screens and dashboards; they see a generic iOS form sheet on everything in between.

The reference paintings carry a different solution. In Fan Kuan’s Travelers Among Mountains and Streams, in Ma Yuan’s Spring Path / Dancing & Singing, in Bada Shanren’s Two Birds, in Shi Tao’s Master Hong-ji Planting Pines — the painting does not fill the silk. Most of the surface is empty. Scenery is painted against the edges, in a corner, along a band; a vast quiet centre is left for the eye and (in the original use) for poetry, seal, colophon. The empty space is not absence — it is the painting’s structural device.

v7 codifies that as a UI primitive. Each of the 10 plates is a 390×844 PNG that paints scenery into one specific region of the canvas and leaves the rest as fibered silk paper. The unpainted region is a quiet zone — that is where text, cards, and controls are allowed to live. The painted region is permanent visual register: mountains at the top of an onboarding screen, a chapter-mark cluster behind a reading divider, water and trees flanking a centred poem.

The four ideas v7 introduces

  • 1 · Composed silkThe plate is the silk — cream substrate, fiber grain, and any painted scenery are baked in as a single PNG. App code references plates by token (.plate--corner-stamp), never by file path. Never composite a plate over var(--silk); that double-paints the paper.
  • 2 · Painted region + quiet zoneEvery plate has a documented painted region (where scenery lives) and a quiet zone (where UI is allowed). The taxonomy in §V7.1 publishes both for all 10 plates so layout decisions can be made without opening Photoshop.
  • 3 · Plate as scene-setter, not subjectPlates carry register — the brand’s ink-wash mood — not content. They never carry the day’s element, the user’s pillar, or any data. Element-specific painting still belongs in v6 §S1 StemTile / §S3 PainterlyHero, which sit on top of the plate.
  • 4 · Plates compose with v6 components without conflictStemTile, BranchTile, PainterlyHero, SectionTabs, ModalFromCard, HalftoneTile all keep their own internal painting. The plate is what they sit on. §V7.2 publishes the layering rules so a screen never paints scenery on top of scenery.

What v7 does not do: change tokens; retire any v4 / v6 component; introduce new pigments; or alter the IA. The --silk token is preserved for the rare flat-paper case (modals, sheets, embedded cards). Every plate sits on top of --silk behaviorally — if the image fails to load, the screen still reads as Elementum.

Plate taxonomy · the 10 named backgrounds

Each plate documented by painted region, quiet zone, intended use.

The set is closed at ten. New screens compose from these; new plates are added only by amendment to this section. Each plate is a 390×844 PNG (1× resolution; 2× produced at handoff), painted in the same register: cool grey-green ink on warm silk, atmospheric perspective, broken-edge brushwork, fibered grain throughout. The painting is muted enough that body text at --ink reads cleanly across all plates without overlay.

▸§V7.2

Layering protocol · how plates compose with v6 components

The plate is z-0; everything else is the picture.

A plate alone is not a screen. It is the ground on which a screen is built. The protocol below is a four-rule contract that prevents the most common failure mode — double-painting, where a plate’s scenery and a v6 component’s internal painting fight for the same region.

1
The plate is the ground; UI sits in the quiet zone

Place the plate as .plate at z-index:0 of .phone-content. All headings, body text, cards, and controls live at z-index ≥ 1. Position content using the quiet zone published in §V7.1 as a soft layout grid — never paint UI over the plate’s painted region.

2
v6 cards keep their own painting; the plate gets quieter

When a screen carries a v6 §S1 StemTile grid, a §S3 PainterlyHero, or a §S6 HalftoneTile, choose a quieter plate — .plate--quiet-paper or .plate--corner-quartet. The card carries the painted weight; the plate is breath. Never put a PainterlyHero on a plate with painted scenery in the same vertical band as the hero.

3
Plates do not animate, and they do not respond to data

The plate is fixed for the screen’s lifetime. It does not crossfade between elements, does not tint by day-master, does not pulse on read. Element-driven motion belongs to v4 §V4 (γ inline expansion) and v6 PainterlyHero. The plate is the silk; the painting on top of it is what changes.

4
Tab-bar & modals sit above plates, not on them

The v4 §V5 tab-bar and any bottom-sheet uses --cardstock-bg + 12px backdrop-blur as before; it floats above the plate without interaction. ModalFromCard (v6 §S5) covers the plate entirely with its own gradient hero — the plate is hidden during modal, then reasserts on dismiss.

▸§V7.4

Drop-in checklist · what changes in the engine

10 plates · 1 layering rule · 0 token changes.

v7 is additive. No tokens are renamed; no v4 / v6 components are retired; no IA shifts. The engine work is image asset registration plus a single CSS rule for the plate slot.

What lands in the engine

  • Asset bundle10 plates at 390×844 (1×) and 780×1688 (2×), shipped as bg/bg-*.png. Total weight ~3.5MB across the set; cache-on-first-paint per plate.
  • CSS additionsOne .plate base rule (z-0, full-bleed, cover) + 10 .plate--{name} classes mapping to background-image. No new tokens. No new layout primitives.
  • Screen-template changeThe screen-template .phone-content gains a single <div class="plate plate--{name}" /> at the top, before the existing UI subtree. Existing screens default to .plate--quiet-paper — the no-op plate.
  • Mapping registryA new screen → plate map (~30 entries) lives in src/styles/plates.jsx, sealed against §V7.2’s compatibility matrix. Adding a screen without a plate decision falls back to --quiet-paper.
  • Sealed againstv4 §V1 ink-wash glyphs · v4 §V2 reading icons · v4 §V3 Reveal layout · v4 §V4 inline expansion · v4 §V5 tab-bar fade · v6 §S1–§S6 components — all consume plates without modification.

Provenance. The ten plates were studied against five reference paintings: Fan Kuan’s Travelers Among Mountains and Streams (the vertical scale of mountain mass), Ma Yuan’s Spring Path / Dancing & Singing (corner-and-margin composition), Bada Shanren’s Two Birds (radical empty space), and Shi Tao’s Master Hong-ji Planting Pines (cloud-and-foliage edge gestures). The plates do not reproduce these works; they translate the compositional grammar — painted region, quiet zone, atmospheric perspective — into a UI vocabulary.
