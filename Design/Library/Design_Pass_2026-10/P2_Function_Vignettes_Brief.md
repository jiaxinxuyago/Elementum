# P2 · The five function vignettes (commission brief)

> Ruled DES_15 EJ8 A (owner 2026-10-22). Proposal only until the art lands; the app renders the slot from `public/concept-arts/functions/{fn}.png` and shows nothing there until the file exists (`FN_ART` in `src/components/journey/EnergyJourney.jsx`).

## Where it sits
The function page's definition ("Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours…"), an 84 × 84 slot beside the first paragraph on every energy page's THE FUNCTION. One vignette per function, element-agnostic: the same Mind vignette serves Earth-as-Mind and Water-as-Mind.

## Format
- 480 × 480, 1:1, PNG on the house paper (`#F1E9D6`), no transparency.
- The spot-illustration register of the stem seals (`concept-arts/stems/proc`): one object on open paper, mist to paper at the edges, no text, no seal, no border.
- Ink and one pigment, the function's own: Body dmBorder `#584A3E`, Mind water `#3E5F85`, Expression metal `#6A849A`, Action wood `#587a4d`, Order fire `#9E5540`.
- No italic anywhere; nothing else in the design changes.
