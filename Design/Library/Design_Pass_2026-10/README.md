## The recommended combination ("V5")

Built out as `V5_The_Combination.html` (https://claude.ai/artifact/Tja5Ak53p4dW8Vq3GKQGtB).

Each part comes from the variant that does it best. These are rows R1 to R11 on the ruling sheet, option A each.

| Surface | Take from | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Your reading (the hub) | **V2** | Identity strip, the wheel at full width, then teaser tiles in a two-column grid sized by weight. |
| Tile faces | **V1 + V3** | A painted band from an existing plate, the claim from the reading, one visual (dial, ink dot, life strip, domain glyphs) and the reading time. |
| Long sections | **V2 + V1** | Read as leaves by default (one idea each; tap right to go on, tap left to go back). "Read it all as one page" opens V1's scrolling page with sticky anchors. |
| Energies | **All + V4** | The wheel disk opens the sheet. "Read" opens the energy page. Swipe sideways between the five energy pages (five dots). Each page ends on "{Energy} across your chart": its domains, its decades, its days. |
| How am I running? | **V4 + V2** | Opens on V4's balance graphic (what adds to you, what spends you, the seal and dial between them), then the reading in leaves. Codex tests stay folded; the Blueprint door comes last. |
| Progress and next step | **V2 + V3 + V1** | Unread ink dot on tiles, the stem seal stamps a finished tile (one red per screen), "2 of 7 read" above the grid, and a Next tile at the end of every section. |
| Where the app opens | **V3** | The first visit lands on Your reading. Later visits land on Today with a "Continue reading" card. |
| Your Almanac | **V2 + V3** | Today as one large painted card, the week ribbon under it, month and year as tiles, Continue reading. Day, Month and Year read as short leaves; time runs sideways. |
| Birth chart | **V3** | One page, reached from "Birth chart ›" on the hero. |
| Phase 2 | **V4** | The five-sign lens rail across Domains, Chapters, the birth chart and the Almanac. |

### Why this combination

- V2's leaves reuse the one pattern the app already does best: the eight-card first reading.
- V1's page mode keeps skimmers and long readers happy.
- V3's landing gives the daily return the reference apps show matters most.
- V4's pager and balance graphic make the five energies feel like one system.
- The lens rail adds a concept and does not fit Stars, so it waits.

**Fixed in every option:** the background arts, element signs, iconography, stem signs, the dominance wheel, the locked relation labels, chop-red once per screen, and no italic.
