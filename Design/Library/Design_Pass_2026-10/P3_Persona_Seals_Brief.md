## Format
- 240 × 240, round composition, PNG on the house paper (`#F1E9D6`), no transparency.
- In the manner of the stem seals (`concept-arts/stems/proc`) but ink and paper only: chop red stays one per screen and is not used here.
- One object per persona, no text, no border. No italic.
