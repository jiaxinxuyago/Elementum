How I would combine them

My recommended path takes V2’s album and leaves as the base, keeps V1’s full-page reading as the "read it all as one page" mode, borrows V3’s rule that returning visits land on Today with a "continue" card, and takes V4’s sideways pager between energies and its balance graphic at the top of "How am I running?". The lens rail waits for a second phase. The rows below let you accept or change each part of that, one at a time.

No variant swaps in new art. These are proposals only, for you to rule in rows N1 to N3.

N1 · The 25 pair illustrations for the energy sheet (backlog GO-2)

One painting per pair of your core element and one of the five energies, so every chart’s five sheets have a picture: 5 core elements × 5 energies = 25. Each shows the relation the sheet names in the locked words (feeds you, shapes you, you feed it, you shape it, more of you).

  • Format 1100 × 733 (3:2), the same as the concept-arts/library plates, so the sheet slot and any tile can use it.
  • House ink-wash: rice-paper ground, one focal point, the core’s stem motif from DES_14 §1, mist fading to paper on the bottom third so it melts into the sheet.
  • Colour only from the two elements’ pigments; no text, no seal (the screen’s one red stays elsewhere).
  • Yin and yang stems of the same element share a painting; the sheet’s story sentence carries the stem.

N2 · Portrait crops of the existing plates

Leaves and story cards (V2) want 3:4 crops. Each element already has two portrait plates (t_{element}_{1,2}_p); cropping three more per element from the existing landscape plates gives five per element without new painting.

N3 · Mirror the yin-stem plates from the vault

DES_14 §5 gap 2: the yin-stem paintings (丁 candle, 辛 jewel, 癸 dew, 乙 grass, 己 field) already exist in the art vault but are not mirrored into public/. Mirroring them lets energy cards and sheets match a yin chart’s stem. No generation needed.

N4 · Seven chapter marks (V3 only)

Small square ink marks, one per reading section, for a picture index. Only needed if V3’s contents page is adopted.
