## Who is the target reader (age, gender, market, language level, astrology literacy, motivations)?

### Takeaway
Elementum writes for the Western, English-language Co-Star / The Pattern / CHANI generation (18 to 35, female-skewing, therapy-literate, irony-fluent, "allergic to horoscope slop"), at a grade 6 to 7 reading level and a CEFR B1 to B2 vocabulary aimed at "the lowest end" of that band, so that an intermediate ESL reader understands it "without translating". Its stated mechanism of resonance is "Post-Barnum": "named cost beats named virtue". The three test readers are the Screenshot Curator, the Depth Migrant and the Heritage-Curious.

## What are the ruled tone rules for the friction pole?

### Takeaway
Friction text must name drive, tension and cost outright (D3), with the reader as the subject and the cause as the reader's own need (D10), closing on a sentence the reader keeps (D11), in the tone of "a reminder, never a criticism" (D12). Hedges, capacity openers/closes, boasts, biography and doom are banned; since 2026-10-16 the literal reminder phrase ("Remember that ...") may close at most three of nine ledger passages and at most one of the five shown.

The rule: "a catalyst passage reads a little like a compliment, recognition in the opener and an invitation in the close; a friction passage reads like a reminder, never a criticism, and its close keeps what the reader can keep ('keep in mind', 'remember', 'worth remembering', or an equivalent) ... Amended 2026-10-14 (v0.7.2): the reminder and the invitation are tones, not frames; the frame varies across a page and may be carried by the content (B9); the harness's marker check stays a note." — [02_RULES_REGISTER.md D12](Reading/Database/Prompts/02_RULES_REGISTER.md). ## How is a friction page structured in the app, and which parts carry the negative content?

### Takeaway
The energy's function page runs THE FUNCTION (definition) → THE CHARACTER (a "Friction" pill, the two-word verdict "Release it.", a release-to-grow dial, three metric bars, then three to five keyword chips, each tapping open one ledger passage) → THE REMEDY (a tinted box headed "Seek X, the Y energy." plus life advice). The definition's third beat, the chips and their passages carry the negative content; the remedy carries a short restatement of it.
