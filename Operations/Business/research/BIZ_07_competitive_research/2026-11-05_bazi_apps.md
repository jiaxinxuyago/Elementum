# Competitive research: English-facing BaZi apps (for Elementum)

Research date: 2026-11-05. Method: web search only (no direct page fetches). All facts come from search results and snippets, so even "primary" facts (App Store listings, the vendor's own site) were read second-hand through snippets. Confidence: **H** = primary source (store listing, vendor site or official account), **M** = several sources agree, **L** = one source, conflicting sources, or from memory (marked).

Apps covered (7): FateTell, Cantian AI, Meta BaZi (with its sibling BaZi Hero), free calculators (bazi-calculator.com and Master Sean Chan's calculator / Tian Qi), Joey Yap (iBaZi and BaZi Profiling), OpenFate BZTI (identity-first archetype card), BluePrint - Chinese BaZi. Brief notes on others are at the end.

---

## 1. FateTell (top priority)

**What it is / owner / platform**
- AI BaZi app and web platform. Developer: AURAAI LIMITED (Hong Kong), founded 2024. Founder: Levy Xi Yuan (西元), an HKUST graduate with an NLP background. (H for the App Store developer name; M for the founder: KrASIA and baijing.cn agree)
- iPhone only (iOS 17+), plus web at fatetell.com. No Google Play listing found. (H/M)
- Positioning: "Be Your Own Fate Teller". Calls itself "a wellness AI for self-discovery. We trained a vertical LLM on millions of BaZi (Chinese Astrology) data points to act as your counselor" (Product Hunt, launched Feb 13 2026). One directory says "it's not about prediction, but about learning and growth through ancient wisdom". (H)
- The App Store name has changed: it appears as both "FateTell - Chinese Astrology" and "FateTell - Eastern Wisdom". One listing calls it "Pocket Book of Fate · BaZi Readings". (M)
- The founder told KrASIA they use a mix of models ("DeepSeek, Gemini, GPT, and Claude") and admitted that "generation speed is a bit slow". (L: one interview)
- Also ships an MCP server (fatetell-mcp on GitHub) so Claude or ChatGPT can cast FateTell charts. (H)

**Traction (dates matter; the numbers conflict)**
- App Store ratings at different snapshots: 4.9 from 31 ratings; 5.0 from 7; and (latest) **4.6 from 251 ratings**. Other aggregators show 4.6 from 297 and 4.7 from 1.6K. (L/M: the counts vary by snapshot and storefront)
- AppGoblin (data updated Apr 10 2026): 46 to 52 ratings, about 2.6K installs, about 405 MAU, revenue under $10K a month, 100% in-app purchases. Appllama: about 1K downloads in the last month, version 1.7.3. (L: third-party estimates)
- Self-reported: "over 20,000 users". woshipm and baijing (Chinese trade press, company-sourced) report the web version launched overseas in late 2024, aimed first at overseas Chinese in North America and Australia; breakeven after about one year; annual revenue of "several million RMB"; paying users up 3x from 2025 to H1 2026; repurchase rate about 38.7%; about 70% of revenue from subscriptions; spend per paying user above $60. **Audience: 25 to 35, highly educated, about 60% women.** (L: company self-report, but it matters, because this is Elementum's exact demographic)
- Has a subreddit, r/FateTell_official (no organic Reddit discussion found). On X as @FateTell; released a public "Brandbook" in 2026. (H)

**Onboarding**
- Latest release notes: "a completely new onboarding journey: create your Profile, then discover your Zodiac, Day Master, Fortune Trend, Daily Reading reminders" and an "improved post-onboarding subscription screen", so the paywall comes right after onboarding. (H)
- Asks for birth date, time and place (the privacy policy lists date and time of birth; time zone and hemisphere are derived from the birthplace). (H)
- Unknown birth hour: **not documented.** The MCP chart tool treats birth time as optional; the app's handling was not found. (L)
- BaZi terms a new user meets in session 1: at least Zodiac, Day Master, Five Elements, Fortune Trend, and on the chart screen also "10-year luck pillar and annual pillar". **Estimate: 4 to 6 terms** before the paywall. (M for the terms; the count is my inference)

**Vocabulary**
- Uses standard English BaZi terms: "Day Master", "Five Elements", "luck pillar", "annual pillar". Brand copy: "At the heart of your chart is your Day Master, the element that represents your core self... reading your BaZi is simply decoding the complex energetic relationships between your Day Master and the other seven characters." (H)
- No evidence found of whether the in-app ten-god labels are literal ("Eating God") or plain. Not verified. (L)

**Identity**
- The identity is the **Day Master + Five Elements distribution + season + zodiac**. These are the share-card subjects: "Share cards can be made for the Day Master, Five Elements distribution, season, and zodiac, with portrait, standard, and square layouts." (H, release notes)
- No archetype name or type code found. The identity you can say out loud is the traditional one (for example "Yang Wood Day Master"), not a brand-made archetype. (M)

**Guidance**
- Daily: an "Energy Index" with tailored "Do's" and "Don'ts", a daily draw (a "fortune toss") preceded by a breathing meditation, widgets, and Daily Reading reminders. (H/M)
- Yearly / luck: an "interactive trend chart and detailed annual readings", luck pillars, and paid reports: "FateBook" (lifetime report) and "AnnualBook" (decade and year trends); Chinese press quotes 命之书 at $39.99 and 运之书 at $19.99, and the App Store also shows a one-time report at $29.99. (M)
- Tone: a mix. It is positioned as a counselor and wellness tool, but the product is "fortune telling" (fortune toss, Fortune Trend, Fortune Book). The founder conceded that "some readings can be scary or fatalistic". So it leans **predictive with a wellness wrapper**, not "how to use your energy". (M)

**Visual style**
- Strongly design-led. A user review: "hands down the most visually stunning astrology product I've used in ages." The Brandbook makes the Five Elements the visual foundation. Exact illustration style (Chinese motifs versus abstract) not confirmed. (M)

**Social**
- Share cards (above). Multiple profiles ("Profiles" tab to manage several people). **No compatibility or friend-compare feature found** (a compatibility hit in the results was a different app, FateAlert). (M)

**Price / paywall**
- Free download; subscriptions. Pro: $4.99/week, $59.99/year with a 7-day trial (an earlier price of $39.99/year was seen on Appllama). Master: $99.99/year. Monthly VIP: $19.99. Plus one-off reports. The paywall comes right after onboarding. (H, but prices change between snapshots)

**Complaints**
- "$60/year for basic access" not worth it without more features; the developer replied that the subscription "doesn't include enough yet" and pointed to a refund process. Cancellation and refund requests. Slow generation (the founder admits it). Fatalistic or scary readings (the founder admits it). (H/M)

## 2. Cantian AI

- **What:** An AI BaZi + I Ching + feng shui + naming platform; it began as the "Chinese Bazi Fortune Teller" GPT in the ChatGPT Store. Developer: Cantian AI Limited. iOS (15.1+), English, Entertainment category, 16+ rating; also web at cantian.ai. Publishes the open-source `bazi-mcp` calculator, used by many AI agents. (H)
- **Traction:** Claims "Trusted by over 1,000,000 users" (App Store copy; likely counts GPT users). The mwm.ai aggregator shows a 4.7/5 rating; the rating count was not found. G2: "not enough reviews". (L)
- **Onboarding:** Build a BaZi profile, then **choose an AI agent persona style ("gentle, direct, scholarly, or energetic")**. Unknown hour handling not found. (H for the persona choice, L for the rest)
- **Vocabulary:** Standard BaZi English plus feature names: "Daily Guide", "soulmate portrait", "life timeline". No ten-god term samples found. (L)
- **Identity:** No archetype or type code found. Identity = your chart + an AI companion with long-term memory. (L)
- **Guidance:** **Predictive.** A "2027 report", compatibility reading, "soulmate portrait", life timeline, a "customized Daily Guide", and "trend analysis with risk alerts"; aggregators say the focus is "love and wealth" and "career and wealth planning... financial investments". (M)
- **Visual:** Not determined from the snippets. (L)
- **Social:** Compatibility reading (paid in Stones). No share cards or community found. (M)
- **Price:** Free plus an in-app currency: "Cantian Stones" (100 Stones = $4.99), plus a 1-week membership. Reports and chats cost Stones; Stones can also be earned. (H)
- **Complaints:** None independent found; accuracy claims come only from testimonials. (L)

## 3. Meta BaZi (and its sibling BaZi Hero), by Longevita

- **What:** Meta BaZi is an AI BaZi analysis app (iOS and metabazi.com) that "presents itself as a strategy tool rather than a fortune-telling app". The same developer (Longevita, bundle `com.phenomena.bazihero`) makes **BaZi Hero**, "BaZi charts for professionals"; the developer says Meta BaZi is now the maintained, fuller app. (M)
- **Traction:** Meta BaZi: **4 ratings, 3.3 average** (App Store); the site claims "4.7 from 500+ reviews" (unverified). BaZi Hero: **4.6 from 145 ratings**. (H for the store numbers)
- **Onboarding:** Date of birth and "preferably the time, though it can work without it" (it accepts an unknown hour). Free tier: 20 credits. (M)
- **Vocabulary:** Its own branded functional layer on top of the classical one: "**Energy Signature**", "**Talent Bridges**", "**Supertalents**", "Qi Boost (useful elements)", plus Day Master strength, luck pillar diagnosis and True Solar Time. Example: "Select any Luck Pillar to see how elements, components, Energy Signature, interactions, Talent Bridges, and Supertalents evolve across time." This is the closest to "functional" naming among the apps found, but it is aimed at practitioners and is dense. (H)
- **Identity:** "Energy Signature" is a profile, not a sayable one-word identity; it supports a profile library and Advanced Search across many charts (a practitioner tool). (M)
- **Guidance:** Strategy and talent framing; tap a chart section to get AI analysis, a Diary, and alerts on "significant chart formations". Daily chart widgets (BaZi Hero). Tone: analytic and practical, not lucky-day. (M)
- **Visual:** Charts and data viz; not illustrated, as far as could be determined. (L)
- **Social:** A compatibility feature is mentioned in one listing summary. (L)
- **Price:** Site: Free / Premium $9.99 a month or $99 a year / Pro $19.99 a month or $199 a year. App Store IAP: $10.99 / $99.99 / $21.99 / $199.99. BaZi Hero (EU): €11.99 a month or €119 a year. (H)
- **Complaints:** Too few reviews to tell. (L)

## 4. Free BaZi calculators (the most-used English ones)

### 4a. bazi-calculator.com ("BaZi Calculator by Voytek")
- **What / traction:** A long-running free web calculator. Similarweb (April 2026) lists it as the **#1 competitor by monthly visits for fengshuied.com** and in the top 10 for fengshui-republic.com, which makes it the de facto English reference calculator. Its own exact visit count was not found. Has an AI version on Product Hunt (Jan 31 2026; not confirmed to be the same product). (M)
- **Onboarding:** Birth date, time and place; options to show pillar names in English, display ten-gods information, and toggle transformations. (H)
- **Vocabulary:** Practitioner grade: Na Yin, hidden stems, Qi phase, stem-branch interactions, Day Master strength, literal ten-god names. Self-described as aimed at consultants; testimonial: "better than 90% of the Chinese 4P software (forget the English ones)". (H)
- **Identity / guidance / social:** None in the consumer sense: a chart, not a story. Has a "famous people" chart database. (M)
- **Visual:** A table-based utility. (M)
- **Price:** Free. (H)

### 4b. Master Sean Chan: free calculator + Tian Qi 天啟 AI
- **What:** A free four-pillar calculator showing the "Day Master plus the Ten God relationships across pillars", and **Tian Qi**, an AI reader "in Master Sean Chan's voice" running the classical 子平 method (穷通宝鉴 climate adjustment, 用神 selection). Uses solar time. The site is in many languages. (H)
- **Vocabulary:** Chinese plus literal English: "Rob Wealth (劫財)", "Direct Resource", "壬日主見正印 — Yang Water Day Master with Direct Resource". Explicitly states that a ten god "is a relationship, not an identity", and argues "the trendy Ten Gods profiling system is flawed". (H)
- **Identity:** It rejects identity labelling by design. (H)
- **Price:** Free calculator and AI; paid consultations and a "BaZi Bootcamp". (H)

## 5. Joey Yap: iBaZi / iBaZi HD and BaZi Profiling

- **What:** Malaysian master; Mastery Academy. Apps: iBaZi (iPhone, 2012 to 2013), **iBaZi HD (v1.2.2, "last update: 9 years ago")**, free web "JY Apps" (BaZi Plotter and others). The apps are effectively abandoned; the business is courses, books and events (for example a "Feng Shui & Astrology 2027" event in Dallas, 24 Oct 2026). (H)
- **Traction:** The app ratings are negligible or stale (iProTongShu 2020: 8 ratings). Brand traction is in books, courses and YouTube or podcasts. (M)
- **Vocabulary:** Literal translations throughout ("Eating God", "Seven Killings", "Hurting Officer", "Rob Wealth", "Direct Officer"). (H)
- **Identity (important precedent):** The **"BaZi Profiling" system gives each ten god a sayable archetype**: The Leader (Rob Wealth), The Artist (Eating God), The Performer (Hurting Officer), The Pioneer (Indirect Wealth), The Diplomat (Direct Officer), The Warrior (Seven Killings), The Analyzer (Direct Resource), The Philosopher (Indirect Resource); the Director (Direct ...) appears in a book title. A chart yields "the full list of the ten Profiles, arranged from the highest percentage to the lowest", with "Healthy to Under Pressure" levels. Sold as a 10-book series (about 2010) and courses. The remaining two names (Friend and Direct Wealth; "The Manager"?) were not confirmed. (M for 8 names; L, from memory, for the rest)
- **Guidance:** Practical talent and career framing in the profiling material ("What does your 'style' of operation say about you"), but the main apps are predictive almanacs (Tong Shu, auspicious dates). (M)
- **Visual:** Corporate and traditional; book covers. Not app-native. (L)
- **Price:** iBaZi HD was $25.99, cut to $4.99 (2015), later $9.99; iProTongShu RM69.90. Profiling reports and courses are paid. (H/M)
- **Complaints:** Staleness, no updates. (M, inferred from the update dates)

## Answers

### (a) Does any English BaZi app already frame the ten-god families as plain functions (like Body, Mind, Expression, Action, Order) and teach how to use each one?
**No: none was found.** Evidence:
- The mainstream apps and calculators (FateTell, Cantian, bazi-calculator.com, Sean Chan, Joey Yap) use classical literal translations ("Eating God", "Seven Killings", "Direct Officer", "Rob Wealth") or just "Day Master + Five Elements". Sean Chan explicitly rejects turning ten gods into identities.
- The five families do have semi-plain English labels in teaching material: **Companion, Output, Wealth, Power/Influence, Resource** (Nova Masters, Fatefolio, Shastrology, daymaster.pro). These are still jargon ("Wealth" for what you control, "Resource" for what supports you) and come from glossary pages, not product UX.
- The nearest to functional language is **Meta BaZi / BaZi Hero** ("Energy Signature", "Talent Bridges", "Supertalents", "Qi Boost"). It is aimed at practitioners, has 4 ratings, and does not rename the five families.
- **BluePrint** ("career superpower... where your energy naturally thrives") and **Joey Yap's Profiling** (talent framing) teach use, but per ten god or per career, not as five plain functions.
- No app found frames them as a body/mind/expression/action/order set with "how to use this energy" guidance. (Confidence M: absence of evidence from snippet search; in-app screens were not seen.)

### (b) Is any of them a "Pattern-grade", illustrated, identity-first product?
**No: none was found at Pattern / Co-Star level.**
- **FateTell** is the most design-led ("most visually stunning astrology product I've used in ages", a Brandbook, share cards), but its identity is the traditional Day Master, its frame is fortune (Fortune Trend, fortune toss, Fate Book), and it has no compatibility or friends feature. Traction is modest (about 250 to 300 US ratings; about 400 MAU estimated in April 2026; company-claimed 20K+ users).
- **Joey Yap's ten Profiles** are a sayable, ranked identity system ("The Artist", "The Warrior"), but they live in books and courses, not a modern illustrated app.
- **OpenFate BZTI** has a shareable type code and card (32 types), but it is a free web widget with no visible traction.
- **MingGe and daymaster.pro** use archetype names, but are small and unverified.
(Confidence M)

### (c) Where is the open space?
1. **Plain-function vocabulary + "how to use it" guidance.** Nobody translates the five ten-god families into everyday functions and coaches use (practical) instead of prediction (lucky days, wealth, risk alerts). The incumbents are predictive (Cantian: love and wealth, a 2027 report; FateTell: fortune toss and Fate Book) or practitioner-technical (Meta BaZi, bazi-calculator.com).
2. **A sayable identity of your own, illustrated and made for sharing.** Share cards exist (FateTell, Bazi Fortune, BluePrint), but they share Day Master or element charts, not a memorable named identity. Joey Yap shows that archetype names work; BZTI shows the type-code idea; no one has combined them with real illustration and modern design.
3. **Social / compatibility done as "how we work together", not "soulmate".** FateTell has none; Cantian sells "soulmate portrait" and compatibility for Stones. Friend-compare with plain functions is open.
4. **Unknown-hour honesty.** Most apps don't document it; Meta BaZi says it "can work without it". A clear "your Day Master and three pillars are certain; your hour is open" flow is an easy trust win for therapy-literate users.
5. **Demographic fit is proven, but under-served.** FateTell's own data (25 to 35, highly educated, about 60% women, high repurchase) suggests demand in Elementum's exact segment, but its users complain about value ("$60/year for basic access"), fatalistic tone, and slow AI output. Room for a calmer, non-fatalistic, faster and more transparent product (with no fear-based readings), and a fair free tier.
6. **Android and web-first.** FateTell, BluePrint, Meta BaZi and BaZi Hero are iPhone-only, so web and Android are open.
