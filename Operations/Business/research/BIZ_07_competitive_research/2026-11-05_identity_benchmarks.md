# Identity benchmarks for Elementum: competitive research

Prepared 2026-11-05. Method: web search snippets only (no direct page fetches). Confidence: **H** = primary source (official site, App Store listing, founder quote in press); **M** = several secondary sources agree; **L** = one source, conflicting sources, or "from memory" (my prior knowledge, not re-verified in this session). Prices change by region and over time; re-check the stores before quoting them externally.

Products covered (6): Co-Star, The Pattern, CHANI, 16Personalities, myBodyGraph (Jovian Archive), and consumer Human Design apps (HumanDesign.ai, "Human Design App").

## 1. Co-Star (Co–Star Astrology Society, NYC; launched Oct 2017)

**Sayable unit**
- The unit is the Western "big three" (Sun / Moon / Rising, "I'm a Leo sun, Scorpio moon, Virgo rising"), drawn from a full natal chart. Co-Star didn't invent the unit; it made the *full chart* feel normal to a mass audience. The chart shows sun, moon and ascendant. (M: NCC; L from memory for the "big three" framing in the UI)
- Units: 12 signs x 3 placements, plus planets and houses. The naming is inherited, not branded.

**Virality mechanics**
- **Brutal push notifications as meme.** The daily "Day at a Glance" push is blunt, cryptic and funny. Screenshots of it became a meme format. Inverse says the notifications "have become a cultural meme". The CEO's profile (Yahoo/Bustle) says they are "brutal. Of course, they were designed that way." Guler has described them as "trolling" users. (M)
- **Friends and compare charts.** Free users can add friends and compare charts. Reviewers call this the best part of the app. The jobs page describes building "top friends for the day, asynchronized messaging, and groups". (M: reviews plus costarastrology.com/jobs)
- **Origin story is a gift.** Guler gave a friend's child a chart at a baby shower and it was a hit (Wikipedia). The product is relational from day one. (M)
- **Scale.** Launched late 2017. Its own estimate was about 20% of young Americans downloading it (The Astrology Podcast ep. 286, 2021). About 30M registered users reported July 2023 (Statista). 450K US active users in Feb 2024 (Statista/Data.ai). That is a big gap between registered users and active users, and the novelty seems to have decayed. (M; active-user figure L, one source)
- **Funding.** $5.2M seed (TechCrunch, Apr 2019); about $6M total reported by Marketplace 2020. A later $15M Series A is L, from memory.

**Onboarding**
- Birth date, then birth time (with a privacy note and a Skip button), then birth place, then account. You get a chart and that day's reading straight away. (H: screensdesign.com screenshot teardown; M: press)
- **Jargon:** heavy (houses, aspects, transits). Short, oracular copy offsets it. The "this is me" moment arrives within about a minute, but it comes from the tone of the daily copy, not from understanding.

**Retention**
- A daily push horoscope (H: App Store listing says "daily push notifications based on your horoscope"). The daily do/don't lists, friends' days, and "The Void", an AI Q&A launched July 2023 (Statista), all bring people back. Ask-Co-Star questions are a paid product (H: FAQ).

**Visual identity**
- Stark black-and-white, serif typography and grainy archival/NASA-style imagery. Co-Star says it uses NASA JPL ephemeris data (M: Hudson Valley Library and fastweb describe NASA data). People recognize and share the monochrome screenshot of a one-line notification. (Aesthetic details: L from memory)

**Price (2026)**
- Free core: chart, daily copy, notifications, friend compare. Third-party reviews disagree on the subscription: Pro-Star is listed at $8.99/mo on the US App Store per a Sept 2026 review; others say $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr. There are one-off purchases from about $2.99 to $12.99 (question packs, readings). (L-M: conflicting third-party sources; verify on the store)
- One 2026 review claims Midjourney acquired Co-Star. **Unverified (L).** Do not repeat this.

**Lessons for Elementum**
- Copy: a *daily one-liner with personality*, written to be screenshotted. Make friend compare free, because that is the growth loop. Use a monochrome, recognizable screenshot format.
- Avoid: cruelty as a brand voice. It doesn't fit a therapy-literate audience, and critics call it out (SCOUT/Inquirer; Valley Magazine "time to delete Co-Star"). Avoid heavy jargon with no explanation. Avoid relying on novelty: the gap between 30M registered and about 450K US active suggests weak long-term retention.

## 2. The Pattern (Lisa Donovan, founded ~2016; public beta 2018)

**Sayable unit**
- **Deliberately none.** Donovan: "We don't even do sun sign astrology." (H: Bustle founder quote.) The app "hides the astrology" (Whisper review) and gives long, psychological second-person descriptions of your "pattern" instead. You can't say "I'm a X". People share passages and say "it read me". (M)
- That is the key contrast for Elementum. The Pattern won on *emotional accuracy*. It lost the sayable label that makes MBTI and zodiac spread.

**Virality mechanics**
- **Word of mouth, no marketing.** The 2018 public beta launched "without any marketing or PR" (Global Player podcast summary). About 30K users came by word of mouth before launch (Wikipedia mirror, L). Over 15M profiles by 2021 (Wikipedia, M). Bustle calls its word-of-mouth success "sensational". (M)
- **Bonds.** A compatibility feature for friends and partners. A "refreshed Bonds experience" appears in recent release notes (H: App Store). Bonds deepen engagement and ask people to invite each other. In 2021 Donovan announced a dating product called "Connect" (M).
- **Founder as creator.** Donovan was an early YouTube star (LisaNova), so she understands creator culture (M).

**Onboarding**
- Birth date, time and place, then text-heavy personality readings. No onboarding teardown was found in search. **Jargon: almost none** in the readings (M). The "this is me" moment comes from reading a long passage. It's slower than Co-Star but lands harder: reviewers call it "creepily accurate and deeply therapeutic" (M).

**Retention**
- Timing/cycles ("what you're going through now"), daily insights and Bonds, plus paid audio reports. (M: store listings and reviews)

**Visual identity**
- Minimal, mostly text, with soft abstract imagery. Nothing iconic to share beyond text screenshots. (L from memory)

**Price (2026)**
- Free download. "Go Deeper+" subscriptions "starting at $14.99" (H: Google Play/App Store wording). Third parties report about $10/mo, $29.99 for 3 months, $19.99/quarter, or $59.99 to $99.99/yr, so the figures conflict (L). One-off audio reports and Bonds cost $4.99 to $29.99 (L). 20 custom profile slots cost $19.99 (H: App Store IAP list).
- **Paywall backlash:** recent reviews say the free tier was cut, "you can't even read the extensive breakdown of your own chart without paying", and "went downhill after... new paywall with audio readings". (M)

**Lessons for Elementum**
- Copy: therapeutic, second-person, non-jargon descriptions that make people feel *seen*. Keep the mechanics out of the way.
- Avoid: having no label at all (Elementum's Day Master and 50 types fix this). Avoid putting a person's *own core profile* behind a paywall: it kills the share loop and creates resentment.

## 3. CHANI (Chani Nicholas; app launched Dec 2020)

**Sayable unit**
- The Western big three, with emphasis on **rising sign**: the personalized podcast is tied "to your rising sign, not just your sun sign" (H: App Store). CHANI's own vocabulary sits on top of that: "your purpose", "Big Three" readings. No new unit is invented. Identity is carried by the *author* (Chani's voice), not by a label. (M)

**Virality mechanics**
- Growth comes from the creator's audience: Chani's long-running horoscope site, her bestseller *You Were Born for This* (2020) and her social following (L from memory). There is no notable friend-compare loop (no evidence found). Shareable items are quotes and affirmations. (L)
- Scale: about 1M downloads soon after launch, per Chani's bio (L, older). Aggregators estimate about 2.8M to 3M installs and about 200K MAU (L).

**Onboarding**
- Birth data, then a birth chart overview, with the "Me" tab previewing birth-chart readings (H: App Store). Language is warm, justice- and healing-oriented, and therapy-adjacent. "Therapy-informed" is how reviewers and the press frame it, not an official claim (L).

**Retention: the strongest ritual stack in the set**
- Daily horoscope and Moon phase/sign (free). Weekly readings paired with **rituals, journal prompts, altar suggestions and guided meditations**. Push notifications for planetary updates. A yearly forecast with monthly breakdowns. A personalized podcast. (H: App Store and CHANI Android launch post.) A user calls the weekly reading-plus-meditation-plus-prompt-plus-ritual bundle "their favorite part" (H: store review).

**Visual identity**
- Warm earth tones, soft illustration and photography. The brand is recognizable but not built for sharing. (L from memory)

**Price (2026)**
- Free tier, or **$11.99/mo or $107.99/yr USD** (H: App Store; regional variants include €13.49/€109.99). Price is the top complaint (M). Ratings are about 4.9 (H).

**Lessons for Elementum**
- Copy: rituals and practices (journal prompts, short meditations, "how to use today's energy"). This maps directly to Pillar 3. Use warm, healing language for a therapy-literate audience. Keep a generous free daily layer.
- Avoid: building growth on one celebrity voice, which doesn't scale without the author. Avoid having no social or compare loop.

## 4. 16Personalities (NERIS Analytics Ltd, UK)

**Sayable unit: the gold standard**
- A four-letter code plus a **branded, evocative name**: INFJ = "Advocate" (M: Journal du Net), INTJ = "Architect" (H: 16personalities.com/academy/architect URL). The full list (Mediator, Protagonist, Logician, Commander and so on) is L from memory.
- **16 types, grouped into 4 "Roles"** (Analysts, Diplomats, Sentinels, Explorers) (M), plus an **-A/-T suffix** (Assertive/Turbulent), so there are 32 variants (H: own framework page). Each Role has a colour (purple, green, blue, yellow): L from memory.
- This gives a three-level hierarchy: Role (4, a colour), Type (16, name plus code), Variant (A/T). It is a close analogue to Elementum's Element (5), Day Master (10) and 50 types.
- NERIS mixes MBTI-style letters with Big Five-style trait scales and does not use Jungian functions (H: "Our Framework").

**Virality mechanics**
- **Test-as-content loop.** It's free and gives an instant result people want to post. Weverse Magazine: people "take [it] so they can share their results on social media" (M). There are more than 1.5 billion test completions as of 11 June 2026 (H, self-reported on the 16personalities.com/for-ai page).
- **Meme culture.** About 53M posts tagged #mbtimemes; short video lets creators "act out type stereotypes" (L: meme.com aggregator).
- **Korea case.** MBTI, via the 16Personalities test, became a social identity in Korea: dating-app compatibility matching and filters, and K-pop idols disclosing their types (M: CNN syndication via ktvz, Asiae, Weverse TXT x MBTI). The sharp rise among 20-somethings came from about 2020 to 2022 (M).
- Gen Z adoption is covered in The Star (Malaysia), Oct 2025 (M). Experts warn against over-identification (M).

**Onboarding**
- About 60 to 100 Likert-scale statements, 10 to 15 minutes, with no sign-up needed to see the result (M; question count conflicts). There is **zero jargon** before the result. The "this is me" moment is the type reveal with an illustration and a long profile of strengths, weaknesses, relationships and careers (M).

**Retention**
- Weak by design: you take the test once. Return visits come from premium guides, the Academy, articles, a Substack ("Grow with 16Personalities") and retaking the test (M).

**Visual identity**
- **Illustrated characters for each type** (flat or low-poly 3D figures in role colours). These, with the colour of the type's Role, are what people recognize and screenshot. (L from memory; third-party pages describe "a unique illustrated character" per type)

**Price (2026)**
- The core test and profile are free. Premium is a one-time purchase: "Premium Career Suite" about US$29 or £24.99 (L: two third-party sources). "Pro Suite" EUR 99 (L). The Premium Profile can be downloaded as a PDF (H: help page).

**Lessons for Elementum**
- Copy: **give every type a name and an illustrated character** ("Yang Wood: The Pioneer"); four-letter codes alone aren't enough. Use a three-tier hierarchy with colour-coded families (5 Elements = 5 colours). Make the result free, instant and sign-up-free. Write long, flattering-but-honest profiles. Charge one time for deep guides.
- Avoid: weak retention. Elementum's daily-energy pillar should cover what 16P lacks. Watch for typecasting and over-identification; the therapy-literate audience will respond to "a lens, not a box".

## 5. myBodyGraph (Jovian Archive, official Human Design platform)

**Sayable unit**
- **Human Design "Type": 5 units:** Generator, Manifesting Generator, Projector, Manifestor, Reflector (M). Approximate shares are Generator 36%, MG 32%, Projector 22%, Manifestor 9%, Reflector 1% (L: Wikipedia-derived table; figures vary).
- Second-level units: **Strategy** ("wait for the invitation" for Projectors), **Authority** (Sacral, Emotional and so on) and **Profile** (12 profiles such as 3/5 or 6/2) (M). Typical sayable stacks: "I'm a 3/5 Projector", "I'm a manifesting generator".
- The names are **role and energy nouns**, which suit a self-help identity. Strategy works as an action rule, which makes the system *practical*. That is a direct analogue for Elementum's Pillar 3.

**Virality mechanics**
- TikTok: #humandesign has about 171.8K posts (H: TikTok tag page snapshot, date unclear). Content runs to type-vs-type memes ("Generator x Projector... guarantees success", "projector mother, generator children", Kris Jenner as a Projector), the Projector "burnout / undefined Sacral" narrative, and boundaries talk (M: TikTok discover pages).
- Press: Well+Good ("a spiritual personality test"), VICE ("the next big thing in wellness", reported second-hand), Primer AU: Google searches jumped around the start of Covid and kept rising, linked to young people leaving organized religion (M).
- Celebrity and podcast hooks: Huberman-related discover pages and wellness podcasts (L).

**Onboarding**
- Birth date, exact time and place, then a free **BodyGraph** chart: a nine-centre diagram with Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile and Incarnation Cross (M). **Jargon is very heavy** (centres, gates, channels, definition). The "this is me" moment depends on getting from the Type to its Strategy, and most people get there through a third-party creator, not the official app (L, inference).

**Retention**
- Transits, courses and paid reports, with teaching built on Ra Uru Hu's lineage (M).

**Visual identity**
- **The BodyGraph** (a human silhouette with nine coloured geometric centres) is instantly recognizable and screenshotted. It's the single most "ownable" chart visual among the benchmarks (M).

**Price (2026)**
- The basic chart is free (M). Paid plans conflict: $9.99/mo is cited, and one 2026 review lists annual tiers of $349, $499 and $699. Single-chart unlock $49, Transit Tools $59/yr (L: third-party blogs/gists).

**Lessons for Elementum**
- Copy: a **small set of top-level types plus an action strategy per type** (for example "Wood: grow before you push"). A signature diagram that is yours alone: a Five Elements cycle or wheel, or the Four Pillars as an illustrated object. A "what this means for your energy and burnout" narrative, which resonates with this audience.
- Avoid: jargon walls and expensive lineage-gated pricing. Avoid unbalanced or derogatory type-vs-type memes ("only combination that guarantees success"). Present compatibility as *dynamics*, not verdicts.

## Lessons by pillar

### Pillar 1: A sayable, viral identity system (10 Day Masters, 50 types, compatibility)
1. **Use a three-level hierarchy, as 16P does.** Element (5, a colour) > Day Master (10, polarity plus element) > Type (50). Give each level a short, evocative English noun as well as the technical name: "I'm a Yin Water: The Mist" or similar. Codes alone don't spread; 16P pairs "INFJ" with "Advocate".
2. **Keep the headline unit at 10 or fewer.** HD's 5 types and the 12 zodiac signs spread because people can remember all of them and name their friends'. The 50 types are the "profile" depth layer, like HD's 3/5. Market "the 10 Day Masters" first.
3. **Free compatibility is the growth engine** (Co-Star friends, Pattern Bonds, Korean MBTI dating filters). Frame pairings as Five Elements *dynamics* (generating and controlling cycles: "Wood feeds Fire"), never as verdicts. HD's "only combo that guarantees success" memes show how fast a system turns divisive.
4. **Treat the test as content.** Make the result free and instant, with no sign-up, then offer a share card, as 16P does (1.5B completions). With birth data the reveal is instant, which is an advantage over 16P's 10-minute test.
5. **Plant meme formats on purpose**: "the Day Masters as roommates" or "as texts". The 53M-post #mbtimemes culture, Co-Star's notifications and HD's "Projector burnout" content all started from a repeatable format.

### Pillar 2: The easiest BaZi for Western readers
1. Follow **The Pattern's** approach: show the plain-English meaning first and hide the stems, branches and pillars behind a "see the mechanics" toggle. Co-Star and HD both show jargon early, and HD apps now need AI explainers to compensate.
2. Write **one action rule per type**, like HD's Strategy. It turns abstract theory into something usable.
3. Handle an unknown birth time gracefully: the Day Master needs only the birth date. HD and Co-Star both offer a "Skip" for time, which lowers friction. Tell users that the Day Master works without a birth time; this is a real advantage over HD and Rising signs.

### Pillar 3: Practical guidance (energy use, personality not fortune)
1. Copy **CHANI's ritual stack**, translated to Five Elements: daily element-of-the-day plus a "how to use your energy today" line (free), a weekly reading with a journal prompt and a small practice (premium).
2. Copy the **daily push one-liner** (Co-Star) but in a kind, therapeutic voice. Make it screenshot-worthy without being cruel.
3. The non-fortune stance sets Elementum apart. The press frames BaZi as *timing/fortune* (TikTok tag pages; SCMP on 10-year luck pillars for career moves). Elementum can own "BaZi as personality and energy management", which is closer to what HD's burnout and boundaries content proves resonates.

### Pillar 4: The most visual app, rooted in Chinese aesthetics
1. Make the **visual the shareable unit**: 10 Day Master characters, as 16P has illustrated characters. Design one signature diagram (an illustrated Four Pillars or Five Elements wheel) that becomes as recognizable as the BodyGraph.
2. Use a **colour per element**, as 16P does per Role. Colour is the fastest recognition cue in a feed.
3. Make the share card portrait 9:16 and readable as one screenshot, as Co-Star's monochrome card is. Put the name and character on top and one line of copy beneath.

### Monetization and paywall
- A free core identity plus free compatibility. Charge for depth: full 50-type profiles, yearly and monthly guides, rituals, AI questions. **Never paywall the person's own core reading** (The Pattern backlash).
- Benchmarks: subscriptions at $8 to $15/mo or $38 to $108/yr. A one-time deep report at about $25 to $30 is also an option, as 16P does.
