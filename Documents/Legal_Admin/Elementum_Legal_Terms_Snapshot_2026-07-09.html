Privacy Policy

The short version: your birth data never leaves your device — with one opt-in exception, the AI Consultant (see below). Our server stores only your account email, what you’ve purchased, and (if you opt in) a push-notification address. We don’t sell data, run ads, or use third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

Your birth date, time, location, and the computed chart and readings live in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. This also means: if you use Elementum on a new device, you re-enter your birth details there.

What our server stores

  • Account: your email address (and, with Google sign-in, your Google account email) — via Supabase, our authentication and database provider.
  • Entitlements: which tier/purchases your account owns.
  • Billing linkage: a Stripe customer reference. Card details live with Stripe only.
  • Push subscription (optional): if you enable the daily reading, a push endpoint for your device and your preferred send hour. The daily message is derived from the calendar date — it contains no personal data.
  • Consultant usage counters (Advisor tier, if used): per-day message counts and token totals — numbers only, never conversation content — to enforce fair-use limits.

AI Consultant (Advisor tier)

The AI Consultant is the one opt-in exception to “your birth data never leaves your device.” When you send the Consultant a message, that message, your recent messages in the conversation, and a summary of your chart and readings (derived from your birth details, plus your Self-Report if you completed one) are transmitted over an encrypted connection, through our server, to Anthropic, our AI model provider, solely to generate the reply. This happens only when you actively use the Consultant, and the app asks for your consent before your first message. Our server stores no conversation content — your chat history lives only on your device, and clearing your device data removes it. Under our API terms, Anthropic does not use these messages to train its models. The Consultant offers reflective guidance, not professional advice (see the disclaimer above).

Service providers (sub-processors)

Supabase (accounts & database) · Stripe (payments) · Cloudflare (hosting & delivery) · Google (optional sign-in) · Anthropic (AI Consultant replies, only when you use it) · Apple/Google/Mozilla push services (notification delivery, if enabled).

Your choices

  • Use the free experience without any account at all.
  • Turn notifications off anytime (Profile → Daily reading) — the push address is deleted from our server.
  • Clear your device data anytime (Profile, or your browser settings) — that removes your birth data and chart.
  • Request account deletion or a copy of your account data: email jiaxinxuyago@gmail.com.
