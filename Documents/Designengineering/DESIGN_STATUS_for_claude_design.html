The aesthetic north star

一
Contemplative, editorial, calmReads like a quiet almanac or fine book — not a dashboard. Generous negative space; nothing shouts.
二
One raster focal point per screenA single painterly ink-wash image (a hero band or soft page scenery) — never two competing images.
三
Ink-wash + negative spaceImagery is loose Chinese-brush landscape (mist, ridges, water, strokes), low-opacity, bleeding into paper.
四
“The chart is the profile”We resist feature bloat. Depth lives in the readings, not in settings or chrome.
五
Restraint with accentsBronze is the metallic; gold = decade only; red seal at most once per screen. Pigment tints stay faint (6–25%).
六
Daily utility, zero frictionThe Today screen must deliver value in 0 taps.

Label your boxes with our slot vocabulary

So your wireframes map cleanly onto our asset + component system, tag every visual box with one of these (the mockups above use them). Pure-data boxes — calendars, timelines, bars, forms — need no tag.

PAGE-BGFull-screen subtle scenery behind a whole tab, low opacity.
SCENE-HEROThe painterly picture band at the top of a screen or section.
TILEA catalogue thumbnail: mark + label + title.
STEM-THUMBA per-identity signature portrait (e.g. the day-master).
MARKA small element seal / icon — line art, inline.
TEXTUREPaper grain, mist band, divider accent — element-agnostic.

What we want from you

The deliverable, per screen
Exactly 3 distinct structural wireframes of the screen we brief you on — grayscale, boxes + labels, hierarchy only. Each must fit 390px wide with no horizontal scroll, be readable in under 2 seconds, carry a slot tag on every visual box, and come with a one-line trade-off. We dispatch one screen per session; a separate brief gives you that screen's content list and which reference files to attach.
Please do not
  • Produce finished or colored visuals, or invent painterly art — that's applied later, in code.
  • Redesign the palette, type, scales, or the 5-tab IA — those are locked.
  • Give hedged “option A or maybe B” boxes — commit to 3 genuinely different skeletons.
  • Expand Profile into a settings hub, or add a second focal image to any screen.
  • Use spacing/radius values outside the scales above.

Send this page once at the start of the engagement (it's screen-agnostic context). Then dispatch the per-screen Wireframe Dispatch prompt one screen at a time, attaching that screen's legend/component reference files + a current screenshot.
