## The card

- Construct: the opener formula "{Element} is your {Function}, and as a catalyst / as a friction, it is …" followed by a concrete-life inventory of what the function does when it runs right (catalyst) or overgrows (friction). The field carries one word of the pair's chemistry, the energy's or the core's material, in the field; the self pair's Body definitions may carry the body's own material (sleep, food, recovery, stamina, muscle, breath, rest) (owner 2026-09-30, the list widened the same day to word stems and either element of the pair): the mechanism said in the material, an arena noun or the material's verb (Water flows, pours, refills or settles, Wood grows, builds or compounds, Fire is heat, fuel or light, Earth holds, shelters or carries, Metal cuts or holds the line), never a prop staged as imagery. Family-level, no god split, and family-level by construction (owner 2026-09-30): the inventory carries one item per persona of the family, from the six ruled domain words in the cell facts (for the Action door a Horizon noun and a Steward noun: an opening and a saving; for Expression an Artisan noun and a Virtuoso noun: a meal and a stage), so the one definition serves both siblings. The five Order friction definitions each carry the inner-judge sentence (the pressure ends up turned on yourself). **The five self pairs (金_金, 木_木, 火_火, 土_土, 水_水) open on the core's own volume instead of the pole word: "{Element} is your Body, and running thin / running over, it is …"** (the station's five self cells; REA_02 §5h, the core reads its own supply).
- Reasoning chain: the function noun → the energy's chemistry in one word (what the material does: flows, grows, burns, holds, cuts) → what that function IS in a life (books, mentors, long walks · study, comfort, preparation, shelter), one item for each of the family's two personas from their ruled domain words → the state's effect in one plain sentence, one word of the pair's chemistry somewhere in the field → an unsparing close.
- Style: understandable without knowing either persona's name; the templated opener is a lawful match across cells; everyday nouns; the repetition law with the same cell's turn, advice and carry.
- Checks: ≤55w, opener formula present, one word of the pair's chemistry, the energy's or the core's material, in the field; the self pair's Body definitions may carry the body's own material (the harness blocks, matching word stems for either element of the pair; the element's own name in the opener does not count, the opener names it as a label), zero dashes, rep-block, one inventory item per persona of the family (the read decides; the harness notes an inventory that hits only one persona's domain words).
- Exemplar (金_土 friction): "Earth is your Mind, and as a friction, it is intake with no off switch. Study, comfort, preparation, shelter: everything that goes in counts as progress, so nothing ever has to come out. Learning becomes the most respectable way to stand still." The exemplar carries "shelter", an Earth word, so under the widened list (2026-09-30) it passes E7 as it stands (under the morning's narrower list it failed and was named for the re-cut; three station definitions still fail the widened rule, 木_水 friction and both 木_火 definitions, and are re-cut in the next round). The exemplar's inventory ("Study, comfort, preparation, shelter") is the Alchemist's Learning and the Sage's Shelter, so it passes E8 as it stands.
