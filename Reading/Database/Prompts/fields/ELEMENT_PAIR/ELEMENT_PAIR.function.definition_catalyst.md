## The card

- Construct (v0.7.3, owner 2026-10-14, D14, function first): three beats in a fixed order, three or four sentences, 30 to 50 words, the B1 to B2 zone, the v0.7.2 sentence law throughout. (1) What the function is, for anyone: the first sentence is this function's fixed sentence, word for word, the function noun its subject, identical on every chart (Body "Your Body is the strength the whole of you stands on." · Mind "Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours." · Expression "Your Expression is the gate where inner work becomes outer fact." · Action "Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide." · Order "Your Order is the rules you answer to and the rank you hold by them."; from REA_02 §5f, ruled at Q2). (2) What this element makes of it: one sentence, the element as the agent with its plain verb or state (E7: Earth holds, is steady, stores; Wood grows, builds; Fire is heat, light; Water flows; Metal is hard, exact), ending on what that does to the function for this reader ("Earth makes it slow and stored, so what you learn is held and turned over before it counts."); no amount of the element, no pole. (3) The pole, as a state of that function: one or two sentences opening on the pole beat ("As a friction, …" / "As a catalyst, …"; the self pair "Running over, …" / "Running thin, …"), naming what the surplus or the shortfall does to the function, then the family's two drives as one either-or (E8, the tension sentence, D9, both personas inside it) and the cost as the close (D11, the reader its subject). The amount of the element is never in the words (no "you hold more Earth than you spend", no "little Water", no "Metal to spare": the gate blocks them; the metrics bars carry it from step 2 of the owner's order, the state turn and the verdict until then). The old opener formula "{Element} is your {Function}, and as a …" is retired: the app's title line above the definition already says it. Never a prop (E9); the chemistry word is beat 2's verb.
- Reasoning chain: the function noun → its fixed sentence (beat 1, verbatim) → the element's plain verb or state and what it makes of the function for this reader (beat 2) → the pole's state of the function, named without an amount (the opening of beat 3) → the family's two drives as one either-or in that state, anchored in their ruled domain words → the cost as the close.
- Style: understandable without knowing either persona's name; the templated opener is a lawful match across cells; everyday nouns; the repetition law with the same cell's turn, advice and carry.
- Checks: 30 to 50 words, three or four sentences, the first sentence equal to the function's fixed sentence (blocking), a pole-beat sentence present (blocking), no amount of the element (blocking: more/less/little/much/enough + the element, the element + to spare / runs thin / runs over), the tension sentence present (blocking), zero dashes, rep-block, one drive per persona of the family inside beat 3 (the read), the element's verb in beat 2 (E7, the read; the harness's chemistry-stem check stays), the v0.7.2 gate (no arena noun, no loose-logic turn, at most two commas and two clause joints a sentence with the fixed sentence stripped, no trailing which, the last sentence a closure with the reader as its subject).
- Exemplars (DRAFT 2026-10-14, the golden chart's five pages under the construct, ruled at Q5 as drafts until the batch 1 re-cut is read; the writers cut them into 30 to 50): 金_土 friction (Mind, Earth): "Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours. Earth makes it slow and stored, so what you learn is held and turned over before it counts. As a friction, the store runs ahead of use, so you keep learning ahead of deciding and the finished thing waits past its time." · 金_木 catalyst (Action, Wood): "Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Wood makes it growth, so you build by adding to what stands. As a catalyst, the work waits for you, so you chase an opening as much as you keep a steady love and finish best once you pick one." · 金_金 friction (Body, Metal, the self pair): "Your Body is the strength the whole of you stands on. Metal makes it hard and exact, so you hold a line once drawn. Running over, the will has nowhere to go, so you trust your own standard more than a peer's and win more arguments than you keep friends." · 金_水 catalyst (Expression, Water): "Your Expression is the gate where inner work becomes outer fact. Water makes it flow, so what you feel and make reaches people as it comes. As a catalyst, the gate opens too little, so you judge more than you let flow and keep your kindness and your best ideas waiting too long." · 金_火 catalyst (Order, Fire): "Your Order is the rules you answer to and the rank you hold by them. Fire makes it heat, so a standard shapes you the moment it is lit. As a catalyst, the heat is scarce, so you seek a crisis to meet as much as a standard to hold and finish more under rules you never wrote."
