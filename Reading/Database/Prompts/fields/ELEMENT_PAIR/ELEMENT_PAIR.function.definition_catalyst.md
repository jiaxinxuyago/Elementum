## The card

- Construct: the opener formula "{Element} is your {Function}, and as a catalyst / as a friction, it is …" followed by a concrete-life inventory of what the function does when it runs right (catalyst) or overgrows (friction). The field carries one word of the energy's chemistry anywhere in it (owner 2026-09-30): the mechanism said in the material, an arena noun or the material's verb (Water flows or has no banks, Wood grows or compounds, Fire is heat, Earth holds or is intake past use, Metal cuts), never a prop staged as imagery. Family-level, no god split. The five Order friction definitions each carry the inner-judge sentence (the pressure ends up turned on yourself). **The five self pairs (金_金, 木_木, 火_火, 土_土, 水_水) open on the core's own volume instead of the pole word: "{Element} is your Body, and running thin / running over, it is …"** (the station's five self cells; REA_02 §5h, the core reads its own supply).
- Reasoning chain: the function noun → the energy's chemistry in one word (what the material does: flows, grows, burns, holds, cuts) → what that function IS in a life (books, mentors, long walks · study, comfort, preparation, shelter) → the state's effect in one plain sentence, the chemistry word somewhere in the field → an unsparing close.
- Style: understandable without knowing either persona's name; the templated opener is a lawful match across cells; everyday nouns; the repetition law with the same cell's turn, advice and carry.
- Checks: ≤55w, opener formula present, one word of the energy's chemistry anywhere in the field (the harness blocks; the element's own name does not count, the opener names it as a label), zero dashes, rep-block.
- Exemplar (金_土 friction): "Earth is your Mind, and as a friction, it is intake with no off switch. Study, comfort, preparation, shelter: everything that goes in counts as progress, so nothing ever has to come out. Learning becomes the most respectable way to stand still." The exemplar carries no Earth word anywhere in the field, so the 金_土 friction definition is re-cut in the next round with the others E7 names (the chemistry is in "intake", which is not on the list; "intake that holds and never harvests" would pass).
- Sources: REA_16 §2c (function row, AXIS HOMING 2026-09-03, PAIR HARVEST 2026-09-04); REA_02 §5f, §5h (inner judge, 2026-09-17).
