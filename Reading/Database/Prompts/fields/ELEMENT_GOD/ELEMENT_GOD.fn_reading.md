## The card

- Construct: per pole, three rows index-aligned to `adj_chips`, each `{word, angles:{drive, cost_self, cost_others}}`, where the three keys name three angles of one tension (renamed 2026-10-01 from the 2026-09-03 door keys `trait`, `scene`, `outside`, path b of Q1): `drive` = the DRIVE, `cost_self` = the COST TO THE READER, `cost_others` = the COST TO OTHERS. The rule (owner 2026-09-30, the pivot to tension-naming; the construct from the study §1.4): one row, one tension, the one the chip points at, analysed from three angles. Every passage is 2 or 3 sentences at 30 to 45 words (owner 2026-10-14, iteration 3; 4 to 5 sentences until then) and runs the same chain: (1) the claim, a flat statement of the drive as a fact of how the reader is built, in the second person, whose action is the action the chip states, in the chip's own words or their plain inflections, kept as the main action of every sentence of the row's three passages, never a delegate verb that is one instance of it and never a metaphor in its place (owner 2026-10-14, D13), never a capacity ("You can"), never a hollow affirmation, never a biography; (2) the mechanism, why the drive runs, stated as the reader's own need (perfectionism, safety, being useful, not being judged; owner 2026-10-14, iteration 7) and, where the passage carries it, the energy's volume in ordinary words ("There is more Earth in your mind than you use"), no arena noun, no prop, no hedge, never an external pressure ("because someone is waiting"); the mechanism sentence may stay implicit where the passage carries the tension without it (owner 2026-10-14, Q32); (3) the tension, the drive's two branches as a comparison of two of the reader's own wants (rather than, as much as, ahead of, instead of, or), both branches the reader's own acts, in the plainest words on the page (this is the sentence the reader screenshots), never a concession on but or yet; (4) the cost, from this passage's angle, as the closing sentence, which is the line the reader keeps: a conclusion, a resonance or a lift, at least eight words, never a bare restatement and never a tag (iteration 4); on the friction pole a reminder ("Keep in mind that", "Remember that", or an equivalent), never a criticism; on the catalyst pole an invitation, the passage reading a little like a compliment (iteration 5). The sentences progress on causal joints (because, so, since, this is why, whenever, until, over time, each time, the more, once, or any connective that carries the logic: a sample to vary beyond, never a set) or on their order alone, with fewer turns, not none (iteration 2 and the v0.7.2 review: however, the difficulty is and and then never; but, yet or still at most once per passage and never as the tension's hinge); no sentence carries more than two commas (iteration 3); a sentence carries at most two clause joints and no trailing which-clause, a relation that can be a phrase is a phrase, and the causal joint may stay implicit when the order of the sentences carries it (owner 2026-10-14, iteration 8, B10); across the three angles of one row the openers differ in shape (any shapes: a noun-phrase subject, a verb-phrase subject, a where or whenever clause, a fronted phrase, the plain "You ...", varied rather than rationed) and no joint word repeats within a passage, and the close frame varies across a page ("Remember that" on every friction row is a formula: the reminder may live in the content; iteration 6 and the v0.7.2 review); in every sentence the subject is the reader and a third party is only the object (iteration 7). The drive passage (key `drive`) lands on the drive's own price, briefly. The cost-to-self passage (key `cost_self`) opens on the reader's interior too and closes on the chip's sharpest price to the reader, named outright in the chip's domain (the chance that closes, the rest not taken, the closeness not had, the money not kept) as the main point of the last sentence, never left implicit, never an event (owner 2026-10-14). The cost-to-others passage (key `cost_others`) opens on the drive as the reader's own act and lands on what that act takes from the people in the persona's domain, stated as a fact of the reader's drive, never as what those people say, think or decide about the reader, never a chorus, never a witness. Where a chip's cost falls mostly on one side, the other cost passage names the smaller, true cost at its real size and never inflates it to match (owner 2026-10-14). No passage opens on a picture, on a moment or on other people. A picture is permitted where it sharpens the tension and never required; an object is allowed only from the persona's ruled domain and only inside a claim. No other person appears except as the object of the reader's drive. No stamp (no clock time, no day, no named place: the gate's floor), no narration, no claimed memory, no aphorism close by default. The three passages say one tension three ways so any row works at any blend position; the app rotates which angle shows by position. (The domain words are in the cell facts.) Two candidates are asked for on the drive passage (key `drive`) in the handoff skeleton (`candidates: 2`, owner 2026-09-30, moved from the scene door 2026-10-01): two different namings of the same tension, each gated, the read picks one; the screenshot line is where the choice matters.
- Reasoning chain: the chip → the drive behind it, what the reader keeps doing and why it feels necessary, in one plain sentence → the mechanism from the chart's supply (the energy, its volume, what it feeds or starves), stated flat → the either-or: the two acts the drive splits into, both the reader's own, in the words the reader would use for them → the cost from each angle: to the drive itself (what it protects and what that protection shuts out), to the reader (the difficulty, the need, the price paid alone), to others (what the reader's act takes from the people in the persona's domain) → the sibling test on the tension (the other persona of the family names a different either-or on this element) → write the three passages, drive, cost to self, cost to others, each opening on the drive and landing on its own angle's cost; keep a picture only where it sharpens the tension, cut every object that does not belong to the persona's domain, and cut every sentence that performs.
- Style: B1–B2 zone, no fragment, no aphorism close, no tag close, no image close (owner 2026-10-14; the earlier "fragments ≤1 per passage, aphorisms ≤1–2 per assembled reading, burstiness kept" retired on this card), sentences of ten to twenty-four words and the short sentence never the close; no arena imagery anywhere in the passage (the gate blocks blade, edge, ore, forge, cut, mountain, steel, sword, sheath, knife, whetstone, mine, sharp, branch, orchard, prune, root, stream, river, flood, flame, heat, burn); the Artisan's examples are giving, care, kindness and making, never food or cooking by default (owner 2026-10-14, Q40). The plain psychological noun is lawful in the tension sentence (need, security, control, comfort, closeness, pressure, please, lose yourself, hold back, give in); no elemental or astrological term inside the tension sentence (the element stays in the mechanism sentence and the page). Close and level on every angle: the reader is the subject of every sentence, the reading stands beside them, no camera narrating them and no chorus judging them; no wit at the reader's expense, no sentence that exists for the writer's sake. The plainness test the read runs: the sentence could be said, without embarrassment, by the therapist winding down a session, in the engraving's dress (no hedge, no affirmation, no biography, no dash). The cost domain (owner 2026-09-29, re-cut 2026-10-01): the cost is named in what the persona rules (its three domain words) or in ordinary life (home, money, friends, family, the body, food, rest), work one domain among many and never the default; the Horizon's cost lands on an opening or a venture, the Steward's on savings or a steady love, the Alchemist's on learning or solitude, the General's on pressure or command. A concrete instance from the persona's domain is colour: it appears once at most and is never the subject of a sentence; the subject is the reader and the action is the chip's (owner 2026-10-14, D13). No stamp (no clock time, no day, no named place as the anchor of an act), no filler, never one acted-out event, never a claimed memory, never another person's private thoughts, never a confirmed prediction, never a safety-critical procedure as proof of character. The size test and the boast test stay for any object that survives: if the sentence would make most readers of this chart say "that is not my life", the object is too big; if an object can be cut and the tension is no less clear, cut it.
