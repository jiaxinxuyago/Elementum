## The card

- Construct: per pole, three rows index-aligned to `adj_chips`, each `{word, angles:{drive, cost_self, cost_others}}`, where the three keys name three angles of one tension (renamed 2026-10-01 from the 2026-09-03 door keys `trait`, `scene`, `outside`, path b of Q1): `drive` = the DRIVE, `cost_self` = the COST TO THE READER, `cost_others` = the COST TO OTHERS. The rule (owner 2026-09-30, the pivot to tension-naming; the construct from the study §1.4): one row, one tension, the one the chip points at, analysed from three angles. Every passage is 4 to 5 sentences at 35 to 55 words and runs the same chain: (1) the claim, a flat statement of the drive as a fact of how the reader is built, in the second person, never a capacity ("You can"), never a hollow affirmation, never a biography; (2) the mechanism, why the drive runs, stated flat from the chart's supply (the energy's volume in ordinary words: the shelter closing over the blade, Earth feeding the mind faster than it is spent), no hedge; (3) the tension, the drive's two branches as an either-or the reader picks from, or as pull-and-obstacle, both branches the reader's own acts, in the plainest words on the page (this is the sentence the reader screenshots); (4) the cost, from this passage's angle. The drive passage (key `drive`) lands on the drive's own price, briefly. The cost-to-self passage (key `cost_self`) opens on the reader's interior too and spends its last two sentences on what the drive costs the reader: a difficulty, a need or a price, comparative where it helps, never an event. The cost-to-others passage (key `cost_others`) opens on the drive as the reader's own act and lands on what that act takes from the people in the persona's domain, stated as a fact of the reader's drive, never as what those people say, think or decide about the reader, never a chorus, never a witness. No passage opens on a picture, on a moment or on other people. A picture is permitted where it sharpens the tension and never required; an object is allowed only from the persona's ruled domain and only inside a claim. No other person appears except as the object of the reader's drive. No stamp (no clock time, no day, no named place: the gate's floor), no narration, no claimed memory, no aphorism close by default. The three passages say one tension three ways so any row works at any blend position; the app rotates which angle shows by position. (The domain words are in the cell facts.)
- Reasoning chain: the chip → the drive behind it, what the reader keeps doing and why it feels necessary, in one plain sentence → the mechanism from the chart's supply (the energy, its volume, what it feeds or starves), stated flat → the either-or: the two acts the drive splits into, both the reader's own, in the words the reader would use for them → the cost from each angle: to the drive itself (what it protects and what that protection shuts out), to the reader (the difficulty, the need, the price paid alone), to others (what the reader's act takes from the people in the persona's domain) → the sibling test on the tension (the other persona of the family names a different either-or on this element) → write the three passages, drive, cost to self, cost to others, each opening on the drive and landing on its own angle's cost; keep a picture only where it sharpens the tension, cut every object that does not belong to the persona's domain, and cut every sentence that performs.
- Style: B1–B2 zone, fragments ≤1 per passage, aphorisms ≤1–2 per assembled reading and never the default close, endings vary, burstiness kept (a long winding sentence, then a short one; the benchmark's uniform length is its one template tell). The plain psychological noun is lawful in the tension sentence (need, security, control, comfort, closeness, pressure, please, lose yourself, hold back, give in); no elemental or astrological term inside the tension sentence (the element stays in the mechanism sentence and the page). Close and level on every angle: the reader is the subject of every sentence, the reading stands beside them, no camera narrating them and no chorus judging them; no wit at the reader's expense, no sentence that exists for the writer's sake. The plainness test the read runs: the sentence could be said, without embarrassment, by the therapist winding down a session, in the engraving's dress (no hedge, no affirmation, no biography, no dash). The cost domain (owner 2026-09-29, re-cut 2026-10-01): the cost is named in what the persona rules (its three domain words) or in ordinary life (home, money, friends, family, the body, food, rest), work one domain among many and never the default; the Horizon's cost lands on an opening or a venture, the Steward's on savings or a steady love, the Alchemist's on learning or solitude, the General's on pressure or command. No stamp (no clock time, no day, no named place as the anchor of an act), no filler, never one acted-out event, never a claimed memory, never another person's private thoughts, never a confirmed prediction, never a safety-critical procedure as proof of character. The size test and the boast test stay for any object that survives: if the sentence would make most readers of this chart say "that is not my life", the object is too big; if an object can be cut and the tension is no less clear, cut it.
- Checks: 35–55w per passage, zero dashes, the sibling test on the tension (the two personas of a family name different either-ors on this element; the harness checks the chips against the sibling cell, the read checks the tension sentence), no banned register, no capacity opener on any passage ("You can", "People can", "Listeners can", "Others can", "can keep you" as the opener only; "can" anywhere after the first clause is untouched: the harness blocks it, owner 2026-09-30, confirmed opener-only 2026-09-30), every passage opens on the drive as a flat claim about the reader (never a picture, never a moment, never other people), one tension sentence per passage (two clauses in the second person joined by or, but, yet or while, or whether … or not: the harness notes its absence, the read decides, since a tension can be named without the joiners), each passage lands on its own angle's cost (drive: the drive's price; cost to self: a difficulty, a need or a price the reader pays; cost to others: what the reader's act takes from the people in the persona's domain, stated as the reader's act, never a chorus: the read decides), the mechanism sentence stated flat from the chart's supply (no may, might, can, could, likely: the read), no stamp on any passage (a stated hour, "o'clock", am or pm, "at {hour} in the {morning, afternoon, evening}", "at midnight", "at noon", "it is {day} night", a named city, street, shop or season as the anchor of an act: the harness blocks on these forms, the floor kept from 2026-09-29), no object noun outside the persona's domain words in the first sentence (the harness notes it, the read decides), no narration, no aphorism close by default, the size test and the boast test on any object that survives (the harness keeps its non-blocking note on a number above two within four words of a venture, city, business, company, country, language, degree, startup or house).
