## The card

- Construct: `{talents: [3 × {label, line, metric}], places: [3 × {domain, line}], action}`, one per ELEMENT_GOD cell on the catalyst pole. THE EDGE says what the gift buys the reader, as 16 Personalities' Strengths and Career Paths do beside the percentages: fixed words per cell, the levels from the chart beside them.
  - A **talent** is one thing the reader is good at because of this persona on this element: `label` two to four everyday words, a noun phrase or a verb phrase ("Holds the room", "Spots the gap first", "Makes it last"); `line` one sentence under fourteen words, the reader its subject, that says what the talent does for them in plain terms; `metric` the key of the bar it draws on, one of the function's three (Body: independence, competitiveness, solo_team · Mind: intuition, stability, instinct_research · Expression: warmth, creativity, reserved_expressive · Action: ambition, discipline, focused_multitasking · Order: leadership, responsibility, flexible_structured), each talent a different bar where the persona allows it. The app renders the bar's level from the chart as five dots beside the label; the words never name a level.
  - A **place** is where the gift pays, one line under sixteen words tagged to one life domain, `domain` one of career, money, relationships, health (one line per domain, three of the four, the persona's ruled domain words deciding which three). The line is a setting or a field the reader can recognise as theirs: an everyday scene ("the emergency nobody planned for"), a field of work named as a field where the gift pays and never as a prescription ("work that hands you the deadline: nursing, dispatch, a kitchen on a Friday"), a money, relationship or health setting in the same register. These lines are the teaser for the Domains section (the Seeker tier): they imply the four domains without reading them.
  - The **action** is the smallest one, one sentence under twenty words, the band's remedy verb (Channel an outlet, Refill a source), imperatives allowed, an object and a cadence, from the persona's domains or ordinary life.
- Reasoning chain: (1) start from the persona's classical portrait on this element and the catalyst chips and ledger rows of the cell (the station's other fields are the facts; the talents name the gift the chips already carry, in its use); (2) the sibling test on every talent and place (the Twin's edge is not the Rival's on the same element); (3) each talent to its bar (the god's own trait bar first, the style bar where the talent is a way of working, the sibling god's trait bar never); (4) the places from the persona's three domain words mapped to the four life domains (Opportunity, Ventures, Father → money and career; Pressure, Command, Crisis → career and relationships; Children, Enjoyment → relationships and health; and so on), one line each; (5) the action last, cut from the current catalyst advice where one exists.
- Checks: the shape (three talents, three places with three distinct domains, one action); the label two to four words; the line and the place under their caps; the metric a key of this function's bars; the vocabulary zone; no report-card register, no virtue list ("reliable, gifted, natural"), no capacity opener; no prescription of an investment or a medical act and no field of work as an instruction ("become a nurse" fails, "work that hands you the deadline, nursing among it" passes); no number, band or state of a chart; no arena imagery; no four-word run shared with the cell's other fields; the sibling test by eye.
- Style: the talents read like a friend summing you up over dinner, not a reference letter; concrete nouns over abstract ones; the place lines a picture at a size the reader owns; the career line names two or three fields, never one, and never a title.

  - places: `{domain: "career", line: "Work that pays for reach: sales, trade, anything with a territory to open."}` · `{domain: "money", line: "A second income beside the first, started small and let run."}` · `{domain: "relationships", line: "The friend who brings the plan, and the people who show up for it."}`
