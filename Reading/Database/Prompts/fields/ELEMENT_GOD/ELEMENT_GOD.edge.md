## The card

- Construct: `{claim, lead, domains: [3], metric}`, one per ELEMENT_GOD cell on the catalyst pole. THE EDGE is the ceremonial entry from the gift to the Domains tier (owner 2026-10-16): it says in one sentence what the gift buys the reader, then opens three doors, one per ruling domain, each a large button with the domain's glyph and its name, nothing beneath (owner 2026-10-16: "Remove the text under each domain, unnecessary"). A tap opens the ceremonial sheet (the seat's teaser line, the seat's domain reading blurred behind the Seeker pill, the unlock with the price); unlocked, it lands in THE DOMAINS at that word. The energy page's THE DOMAINS tile stays the first entry. It sits in THE REMEDY's box at the remedy's size.
  - The **claim** is fixed: `How to use <Element>, the <Function> energy.` (the element's English name, the function's name from the five: Body, Mind, Expression, Action, Order). The app renders it as the title line with the energy's glyph.
  - The **lead** is a passage of thirty-five to fifty-five words at the quality of the shipped catalyst advice (owner 2026-10-16: "use this quality level of text ... I especially like the opening"). It opens on `You` and names, in one sentence, the one thing the gift does to the reader when it has nowhere to go ("You get hardest on yourself when your strength has nothing to build."); then a short imperative of two to four words that turns it ("Give it something."); then two or three concrete things to do, each with an object and a cadence, the last two may be joined by "and" ("Agree to one venture before you are sure. Put a date on the saving you keep putting off. Fix one thing at home with your own hands, and write the first line of the plan tonight."). Where it comes naturally, the examples land in the cell's three ruling domains, so the doors beneath read as where each example goes deeper. Never an opener on `It` ("It makes you", "It is why you", "It gives you" are retired), never a virtue list, never a capacity opener, never a level or state of the chart, never a four-word run shared with the pair's catalyst advice (the advice is the register, not the source).
  - The **domains** are the god's three ruling domains in the canonical taxonomy (Wealth, Health, Career, Love, Family, Social, Mind, Growth), fixed per god (`backlog/2026-10-15_god-ruling-domains.md`), in the table's order. They are not written; the harness fills them from the table and the fixtures hold every catalyst edge to it. The app renders each as a door.
  - The **metric** is the key of the bar the gift draws on most, one of the function's three (Body: independence, competitiveness, solo_team · Mind: intuition, stability, instinct_research · Expression: warmth, creativity, reserved_expressive · Action: ambition, discipline, focused_multitasking · Order: leadership, responsibility, flexible_structured). The app renders its chart level as five dots in the head; the words never name a level.
- Reasoning chain: (1) start from the persona's classical portrait on this element and the catalyst chips and ledger rows of the cell (the station's other fields are the facts; the lead names what that gift buys, in its use, never a rewrite of the rows); (2) the sibling test on the lead and every phrase (the Twin's edge is not the Rival's on the same element); (3) the three domains from the table, nothing written for them (Wealth what you earn, own and grow · Health the body's reserves · Career your work, rank and standing · Love partnership · Family the people you come from and raise · Social your circles and your name among peers · Mind how you think, learn and decide · Growth what you are becoming); (4) the metric last, the god's own trait bar first, the style bar where the gift is a way of working, the sibling god's bar never.
- Checks: the shape (the claim exact, one lead, the three domains equal to the table's row in order, one metric); the lead thirty-five to fifty-five words in three to six sentences, opening on You, with one imperative of two to four words; the vocabulary zone on every word; no report-card register, no virtue list ("reliable, gifted, natural"), no capacity opener; no prescription of an investment or a medical act and no field of work as an instruction; no number, band or state of a chart; no arena imagery; no four-word run shared with the cell's chips, ledger rows, pair definition or advice; no "will never" guarantee; no phrase repeated across the core's cells; the sibling test by eye.
- Style: the lead reads like the shipped catalyst advice: a friend naming the itch and handing you three things to do this week; the examples are objects and cadences, never principles.
