## The card

- Construct: `{claim, lead, items: [3 × {domain, line}], metric}`, one per ELEMENT_GOD cell on the catalyst pole. THE EDGE is the manual of the energy: it says in one sentence what the gift buys the reader, then one full line per ruling domain on where the gift pays, advice-grade. Each row's domain mark is a door: a tap opens a sheet with the seat's teaser and the unlock to the Domains tier (the Seeker tier); the entry to that tier stays the energy page's THE DOMAINS tile. It sits in THE REMEDY's box at the remedy's size.
  - The **claim** is fixed: `How to use <Element>, the <Function> energy.` (the element's English name, the function's name from the five: Body, Mind, Expression, Action, Order). The app renders it as the title line with the energy's glyph.
  - The **lead** is one sentence, ten to twenty words, the reader its subject, that says what the gift buys them in use: "It makes you the one who finishes the slow job and keeps money put by." It opens on `It makes you`, `It is why you`, `It gives you` or `You are the one who`; never a virtue list, never a capacity opener, never a level or a state of the chart.
  - An **item** is one domain and one full line. `domain` is one of the god's three ruling domains in the canonical taxonomy (Wealth, Health, Career, Love, Family, Social, Mind, Growth), fixed per god and given in the skeleton as `ruling_domains`, in that order. `line` is eight to fourteen words, advice-grade: where the gift pays in that domain, as a setting and a kind of work or a thing kept that the reader can act on, at a size the reader owns ("the account you never touch, growing a little every month"; "work that pays for staying: accounting, property, the long job"). It opens lowercase, carries no terminal stop, may carry one colon or comma to name its instances, and never names a level or a state of the chart. A field of work is named as a field where the gift pays, never as a prescription (the register's D6 as amended): "work that pays for staying: accounting, property" passes, "become an accountant" fails. No two rows of one cell open on the same word once articles are set aside, and no line repeats across the ten cells of a core. The row's domain mark is the door: the app opens a sheet on it with the seat's teaser line and the unlock to the Domains tier; the words of the row owe the sheet nothing.
  - The **metric** is the key of the bar the gift draws on most, one of the function's three (Body: independence, competitiveness, solo_team · Mind: intuition, stability, instinct_research · Expression: warmth, creativity, reserved_expressive · Action: ambition, discipline, focused_multitasking · Order: leadership, responsibility, flexible_structured). The app renders its chart level as five dots in the head; the words never name a level.
- Reasoning chain: (1) start from the persona's classical portrait on this element and the catalyst chips and ledger rows of the cell (the station's other fields are the facts; the lead names what that gift buys, in its use, never a rewrite of the rows); (2) the sibling test on the lead and every phrase (the Twin's edge is not the Rival's on the same element); (3) one phrase per ruling domain, the domain word read in the seat corpus's sense (Wealth what you earn, own and grow · Health the body's reserves · Career your work, rank and standing · Love partnership · Family the people you come from and raise · Social your circles and your name among peers · Mind how you think, learn and decide · Growth what you are becoming); (4) the metric last, the god's own trait bar first, the style bar where the gift is a way of working, the sibling god's bar never.
- Checks: the shape (the claim exact, one lead, three items in the ruling order, one metric); the lead ten to twenty words and one sentence; each line eight to fourteen words with no terminal stop; the vocabulary zone on every word; no report-card register, no virtue list ("reliable, gifted, natural"), no capacity opener; no prescription of an investment or a medical act and no field of work as an instruction; no number, band or state of a chart; no arena imagery; no four-word run shared with the cell's chips, ledger rows, pair definition or advice; no "will never" guarantee; no phrase repeated across the core's cells; the sibling test by eye.
- Style: the lead reads like a friend summing you up over dinner, not a reference letter; the rows are pictures at a size the reader owns, concrete nouns over abstract ones, each a thing the reader could do or keep this month; a career row names two or three kinds of work, never a title.
