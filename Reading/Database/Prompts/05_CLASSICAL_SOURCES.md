## 2. The two cycles and the ten equations (REA_02 §5d, locked)

The law verbs are **feeds** (生) and **tames** (克). Tames is relational, not domination: the tamed keeps its power and gains a shape.

| 汉字 | Law | Image line |
|---|---|---|
| 木生火 | Wood feeds Fire | dry branches make the flame |
| 火生土 | Fire feeds Earth | ash becomes soil |
| 土生金 | Earth feeds Metal | ore grows in the mountain |
| 金生水 | Metal feeds Water | dew beads on the cold blade |
| 水生木 | Water feeds Wood | rain raises the forest |
| 木克土 | Wood tames Earth | roots hold the hillside |
| 土克水 | Earth tames Water | banks give the river its path |
| 水克火 | Water tames Fire | rain ends the blaze |
| 火克金 | Fire tames Metal | the forge softens the blade |
| 金克木 | Metal tames Wood | the knife prunes the branch |

Derivation grammar: "[A] feeds/tames [B], so [A] is your [Function]" (Earth feeds Metal, so Earth is your Mind). Relative to the core: the feeder is Mind, the fed is Expression, the tamed is Action, the tamer is Order, the self is Body (REA_02 §5f).

## 3. Strength, band, valence, volume (REA_02 §5c, §5h)

- **Band** (身强 / 中和 / 身弱 → Overfueled / Balanced / Underfueled). Definition lines, mandatory on first surfacing: Overfueled "More fuel comes in than your core burns, the surplus wants somewhere to go. Built into your chart, not today's mood." · Underfueled "Your core burns more than it takes in, the right intake is what your chart asks for. Built in, not a mood." · Balanced "Intake and burn hold each other, the condition the other two work toward." Remedy verbs: **Channel** (克泄耗, for Overfueled) and **Refill** (生助, for Underfueled). The words strong and weak never reach a reader.
- **Valence** (用神 / 忌神): catalyst = wanted, friction = unwanted, from the band. Role is valence, never quantity.
- **Volume:** absent ≤0.5% · thin ≤10% · present · abundant ≥20% · dominant ≥40%. The reading picks its sentence from valence × volume, never from valence alone.
- **The excess override** (太过): a dominant non-core energy is a friction whatever the band, read through its 渊海子平 idiom. The core is exempt.
- **The door law:** gifts show through catalysts, shadows through frictions; a shadow is the function overgrown; underuse is spoken by the catalyst turns, never as a shadow.

## 6. The translation protocol (REA_04 PART 8, compressed)

Classical concepts are translated into mechanism claims in plain English, never quoted, never labelled. The lexicon layer rules: one concept, one name; the forbidden list (Chinese characters and romanisations, the structural labels, the astrological and mystical register, hollow affirmations, false precision and catastrophizing); the anchors (a scene with ordinary objects and a clock time, a paired checkable cost).
