## Why

THE EDGE's rows became the door into THE DOMAINS (the Seeker tier). The app carried three domain vocabularies: the seats' canonical taxonomy of eight (REA_02 §5e, every position reading declares its domains from it and the Seeker readings are keyed by it), the gods' three god-flavored nouns each (REA_02 §4, the free domain map: Peers, Independence, Self-reliance and so on), and the four life domains the edge's first draft used. A door that says one word and opens on another is no door. The owner's ruling: the edge rows speak the seats' words, and each god's three are fixed.

## What uses it

- THE EDGE (`ELEMENT_GOD.edge`, v4): each cell's three rows carry exactly the god's three, in this order; the writer cuts the phrase per cell.
