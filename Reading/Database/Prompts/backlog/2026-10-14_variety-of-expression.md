**Status: RULED 2026-10-14 by the owner on the batch 1 definitions as rendered in the app, APPLIED the same day as v0.7.4 (B11 new, B9 amended, the master prompt, both cards, both gates); batch 1 re-polished for variety by the sealed writers and read by the owner before adoption.**

## The owner's words

> I noticed the words 'So' are repetitively used in the description. I want you to reduce the usage on the same relational advs and seek for more diversity of expression on those, or skip them while the sentence itself reveals the logic effectively. Scrutinize repetitive usage on certain words and eliminate the double usage on them and keep better readability with more variety of word choices.

## The rule (B11)

No connective used twice in one field; the connective dropped where the order of the sentences already carries the logic (B7 allows the implicit joint since v0.7.2); no content word used three times in one field, and twice only where the repeat carries the logic (the chip's own words exempt, D13); across a page's fields the joints vary and "so" stays rare (a note above four). The gate blocks a repeated connective and a word used three times in both gates; the read judges the page.
