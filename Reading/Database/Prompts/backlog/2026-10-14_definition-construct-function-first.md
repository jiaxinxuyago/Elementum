## The takeaway

The fault is in the card's construct, not in the writers. The opener formula "{Element} is your {Function}, and as a friction, it is …" makes the element the subject and reaches the pole inside the first sentence, so the function is never defined and the reader meets the pole's symptom before the thing it is a state of. The app's own title line above the definition already says "Earth is your Mind.", so the definition repeats the title and spends its first twelve words on nothing. The construct below gives the definition three beats in a fixed order, the function noun as the subject of the first, the element as the agent of the second, the pole as a state of the function in the third, and leaves the volume (how much of the element) out of the words entirely, because step 2 puts it in a bar.

## The construct (the ruling of this item)

Three beats, three or four sentences, 30 to 50 words, the B1 to B2 zone, the v0.7.2 sentence law throughout.

1. **What the function is, for anyone.** One sentence, the function noun as its subject, in the second person, the same sentence on every chart (ruled once per function, five sentences, from REA_02 §5f's locked reads): "Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours." Never the element, never the pole, never the reader's particular case.
2. **What this element makes of it.** One sentence, the element as the agent, its plain verb or state (E7: Earth holds, is steady, stores; Wood grows, builds; Fire is heat, light; Water flows; Metal is hard, exact), ending on what that does to the function for this reader: "Earth makes it slow and stored, so what you learn is held and turned over before it counts." No volume, no pole.
3. **The pole, as a state of that function.** One or two sentences opening on the pole beat ("As a friction, …" / "As a catalyst, …"; the self pair "Running over, …" / "Running thin, …"), naming what the surplus or the shortfall does to the function, then the family's two drives as one either-or (E8, the tension sentence, D9) and the cost as the close (D11): "As a friction, the store runs ahead of use, so you keep learning ahead of deciding and the finished thing waits past its time."

What leaves the definition: the opener formula (the title carries it); the mechanism sentence with the element's volume ("You hold more Earth than you spend"), which the metrics bars will carry from step 2 and the state turn and the verdict carry until then. What stays: the chemistry verb (E7, now in beat 2), the two drives in one either-or (E8, D9, in beat 3), the reader as the subject of the last sentence (D10), the closure (D11), the arena ban (E9), the sentence law (B7, B8, B10).

## The golden chart's five pages, before and after (the after as DRAFT exemplars, Q5)

Before is the station text adopted 2026-10-14 (batch 1, cap 45). After is the draft under the construct; word counts in brackets.

**金_土 · Earth is your Mind · friction**
- Before (51w): Earth is your Mind, and as a friction, it is intake with nowhere to go. You hold more Earth than you spend, so you keep learning ahead of deciding and sheltering ahead of letting go. Since preparing is where you feel most at ease, you keep the finished thing waiting past its time.
- After (53w): Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours. Earth makes it slow and stored, so what you learn is held and turned over before it counts. As a friction, the store runs ahead of use, so you keep learning ahead of deciding and the finished thing waits past its time.

**金_木 · Wood is your Action · catalyst**
- Before (53w): Wood is your Action, and as a catalyst, it is the work your strength waits for. You carry less Wood than force to spend, so you chase an opening as much as you keep a steady love. You build best once you pick one and finish it, since the open one costs you the other.
- After (48w): Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Wood makes it growth, so you build by adding to what stands. As a catalyst, the work waits for you, so you chase an opening as much as you keep a steady love and finish best once you pick one.

**金_金 · Metal is your Body · friction, the self pair**
- Before (53w): Metal is your Body, and running over, it is will with nowhere to go. There is more Metal in you than you spend, so you trust your own standard more than you hear a peer's. You turn every shared stake into a contest, and over time you win more arguments than you keep friends.
- After (50w): Your Body is the strength the whole of you stands on. Metal makes it hard and exact, so you hold a line once drawn. Running over, the will has nowhere to go, so you trust your own standard more than a peer's and win more arguments than you keep friends.

**金_水 · Water is your Expression · catalyst**
- Before (49w): Water is your Expression, and as a catalyst, it is the care you hold back and the talent you keep quiet. Since you carry little Water, you judge more than you let flow. You want to be right more than heard, so you keep your kindness and your best ideas waiting too long.
- After (54w): Your Expression is the gate where inner work becomes outer fact. Water makes it flow, so what you feel and make reaches people as it comes. As a catalyst, the gate opens too little, so you judge more than you let flow and keep your kindness and your best ideas waiting too long.

**金_火 · Fire is your Order · catalyst**
- Before (54w): Fire is your Order, and as a catalyst, it is pressure that shapes your strength. You take in more fuel than you spend, so you look for a crisis to meet as much as a standard to hold your career to. You finish more under a rule you never wrote than under your own.
- After (55w): Your Order is the rules you answer to and the rank you hold by them. Fire makes it heat, so a standard shapes you the moment it is lit. As a catalyst, the heat is scarce, so you seek a crisis to meet as much as a standard to hold and finish more under rules you never wrote.
