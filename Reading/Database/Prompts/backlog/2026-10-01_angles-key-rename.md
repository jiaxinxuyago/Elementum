## Why the keys move

The v0.7 item retired the three doors as the organising idea: each ledger row is three angles of one tension, the drive, its cost to the reader, its cost to others (REA_16 §2c THE THREE ANGLES OF ONE TENSION, D3 as superseded 2026-10-01). Path (a) kept the 2026-09-03 door names as storage keys with a `$angle_map` header note; the owner chose path (b), so the keys say what the passages are and no map is needed. The app's rotation law is unchanged in substance: the assembled ledger picks the angle by position (row 0 the drive, row 1 the cost to the reader, row 2 the cost to others, then round again), so no two adjacent rows carry the same angle first.
