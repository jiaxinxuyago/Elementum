> The owner's note on iteration 1: all three blind versions read better than the polished set, and the next pass should dig deeper sentence by sentence (递进, not 转折), with tighter logic between sentences (no "The difficulty is that", no "You are ... and then ..."), more analytical and logical, modelled on how the 16 personalities readings expand a trait: the trait, the reason it exists, how it shows, where it leads, what it costs, each sentence following from the one before with a causal joint (because, so, which means, the more ... the more, each time, over time, until).
