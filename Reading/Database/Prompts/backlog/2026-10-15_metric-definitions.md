# Backlog · the fifteen metric definitions, for the glossary (owner 2026-10-15)

**Status: BACKLOGGED 2026-10-15. Not rendered in the app. The level glosses are retired; these fifteen fixed lines, one per metric, are the only words a bar may ever get, and only in the glossary sheet when the owner says so.**

## The owner's words

> I would argue if the gloss lines are necessary, we would need to come up different stages of level for each metrics and that would be expensive for generation but provide less effective reading. The metric keywords can already explain themselves like the MBTI metrics without further explaining the any specific level of distributions.

> no level glosses, static bars, and 15 metric definitions in the glossary (backlog them for now and do not reveal them from our app as for now from expandable panels)

## The rule

A bar's label explains itself (Independence, Intuition, Warmth, Ambition, Leadership; the style bars by their two ends). No line reads the level: a line per level is a reading of the person, which the ledger owns, and it multiplies with every scale change. The one line a metric may carry is its definition, fixed, the same at every level, as 16 Personalities defines each trait pair once. These live in the glossary sheet, reached from the measure panel, when the owner opens that door; nothing on the panel expands.
