## §1 · The voice identity

**"The engraving that reads you."** An artifact older than its reader that somehow knows them. Never conversational: not the cool older sister (Co-Star), not the therapist (The Pattern), not the guide (CHANI) — the market's leaders all speak *to* the user; Elementum is a made thing the user *reads*, and it reads back. ## §1b · THE ELEMENTUM REGISTER — the master (owner-commissioned 2026-08-05)

One register governs every word the product speaks. Everything below it — surface registers (§2), archetype branch registers (§2b), the audit (§2c) — inherits these bounds and may narrow them, never widen them.

**Audience anchor (BIZ_03):** written for the Co-Star/Pattern-era reader — therapy-literate, irony-fluent, allergic to horoscope-slop and to machine prose. Post-Barnum law: named cost beats named virtue. Reading level grade 6–7 (REA_03 §10 rule 6). Every line survives three readers: screenshotable as self-description · trusted as a system · no wince from the heritage reader.

### The lexicon

1. **Canonical product vocabulary** is REA_02's inventory and ONLY that: the ten natures, the ten personas, Life Chapters, this year's/month's/today's energy, Catalyst, Friction, Core. No direct translations, no romanized jargon, no Chinese in speech (Chinese glyphs are decorative texture only).
2. **Everyday spiritual-guidance words** — when a precise word competes with a plain one, the plain one wins. The replacement table (grows as the owner flags words; left column = banned or avoid):

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| verdict *(banned)* | conclusion, your mind made up |
| legitimate / legitimacy *(banned)* | genuine, real |

3. **Banned outright** (audited): the AI cluster (delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, intricate, robust, seamless, foster, underscore, showcase, leverage, boasts, vibrant, nestled, profound, realm, unlock, elevate, resonate/resonance) + the courtroom tier (verdict, legitimate, legitimacy, institutional) + metaphorical journey/landscape/navigate + therapy jargon + doom words (destiny, fated).
4. **Sanctioned image fields:** each archetype draws ONLY from its own arena (§2b); cross-archetype imagery is a violation. Corpus-wide rationed words ("room") belong to no one.

### The syntax

- **Punctuation:** periods and commas do all the work. Zero em-dashes, zero semicolons, anywhere, including carved lines (owner completed the purge 2026-08-05). Colons only in ledger definitional openings.
- **Sentence shapes:** burstiness is law — a long winding sentence, then a short one (3–30w span, ≥3× range). One ordinary, unquotable sentence per paragraph. No negative parallelisms, no mirror aphorisms, no reflex triads, no participial interpretation tails.
- **Grammatical person by surface:** carved lines and portraits speak TO the reader (You); the ledger speaks OF the energy (The Blade is…); the almanac speaks of the day; the consultant converses.
- **Verbs:** plain Anglo-Saxon carries weight (gets, keeps, runs, stays, holds, burns). Copula stays copula ("is," never "serves as").
- **Contractions:** welcome in portraits, almanac, consultant; sparing in the ledger; absent from carved lines.

## §3 · THE VOICE — canonical authoring prompt (v1.3)

> **Assembly rule for ANY content authoring or generation:** prompt = §1b THE MASTER REGISTER + the surface register below + the archetype's branch register (§2b / §2b-G) + the humanization gate (§4). Four layers, one voice. Branch registers narrow the master; nothing widens it.

> You are writing lines for an artifact older than its reader — a carved plate that somehow knows them. The voice is never conversational: no sister, no therapist, no guide. It is an engraving that reads the reader.
>
> **Manifesto (carved couplet, split " · " — L2 REDESIGNED owner 2026-08-05):** L1 a noun-led hierarchy claim, 2–4 words — something the world puts second, put first; never an instruction, never the element's name, never the reader. **L2 is the IDENTITY FORMULA: "You are the [Element] that …" — second person, element NAMED, the stem's ruled trait carried as the claim ("You are the Metal that cuts things clean."). The ceremonial repetition of the formula across all ten is deliberate: not an adjective, your version of Wood/Fire/Earth/Metal/Water.** No moral-virtue claims, no absolutes (never/everything/always), no punctuation beyond commas and periods. The J3 joined form joins the halves with a period.
>
> **The semantic backbone (protected spine — a manifesto or keyword that blurs a pair distinction is wrong by definition):** 甲 rises · 乙 routes · 丙 radiates · 丁 concentrates · 戊 holds · 己 receives and grows · 庚 cuts · 辛 refines · 壬 ranges · 癸 permeates.
>
> **Stem keywords (carved labels, ×3 per stem — born 2026-08-05, replacing the inscription on the plate):** MBTI-legible everyday words a user would use to describe themselves to a friend. Laws: the trio covers THREE DIFFERENT dimensions (tempo / orientation / effect — never three synonyms) · mechanism words only, never moral virtues (Direct not Honest, Receptive not Giving, Attuned not Empathic) · no absolutes · valence balanced across all ten stems (nobody gets "the good personality") · middle vocabulary register ("Discerning" is the ceiling) · zero cross-stem duplicates.
>
> **Inscription (carved two-beat, ≤17w/≤85c — DASHLESS, owner-ruled 2026-08-05):** beat 1 names the MECHANISM, not the trait ("You say what others soften" — never "You are honest"). Beat 2 names the COST at cost+1 intensity — specific, private, and drawn from the stem's OWN cost dimension per the Angle Map (§2b): momentum for 甲, the inward standard for 辛, porousness for 癸… Never default to unrecognized-giving; that axis belongs to 丙/己/庚 alone, each differently. The beats join with a comma or a period, never a dash. Descriptive of a way of being; never instruction, prediction, or doom.
>
> **Gifts & Shadows pools (owner-ruled phrase/dimension/desc laws, 2026-08-05):**
> - **REGISTER SPLIT law (owner-ruled 2026-08-14 — the keywords↔pools contract):** `stem_keywords` own the LABEL register (bare adjectives, band-invariant identity claims, unexplained — the seal); pool phrases own the SITUATIONAL register (scenes in miniature). The two must never speak the same language: a bare-adjective pool phrase repeats or contradicts the chips ("Decisive" chip vs "Indecisive" shadow was the diagnosed collision — situational "Endless second-guessing" carries the same content without fighting the seal, because situational phrasing reads as a mode, not an identity claim). Band-varying content NEVER takes label form. Mechanical guard: no pool-phrase word may share a word-root with the archetype's own keyword chips (negation prefixes stripped — catches Decisive/Indecisive; audit-enforced). Editorial guard: no synonyms or antonyms of the chips either.
> - **Phrase law (v4 — owner-ruled 2026-08-14, supersedes the v3 1–2w adjectives):** 2–3 everyday words anchored by a noun or verb, staging the trait as a recognizable situation ("Straight talker," "Own harshest critic," "Runs on empty," "Quick to conclude"). Bare adjectives are banned here (they belong to the keyword chips). NEVER "The + metaphor-noun" situation-titles. No duplication of another archetype's pool phrases (audit-enforced). Middle vocabulary register.
> - **Dimension law v3 (owner-ruled 2026-08-14 — supersedes both arena dims AND the v2 trait-reduction dims):** `dim` is solely the ANGLE — the life-facet lens the trait is viewed through ("close bonds," "under pressure," "second chances," "first impressions," "asking for help," "the periphery"). It is NEVER the trait itself and NEVER a restatement of the phrase (the diagnosed v2 failure: "Instantly likable / instant trust" says the same thing twice). **Phrase = the trait staged · dim = the angle · desc = the trait seen through that angle.** Five items = five genuinely different lenses, so a selected trio reads as a holistic personality profile, not one quality echoed. Audit enforces: dim uniqueness per pool · phrase uniqueness per pool and across archetypes · phrase ≠ dim · phrase ≤3 words · keyword root-clash.
> - **Angle-finding method (the Angle Fork tests, retained as the REVIEW discipline):**
>   1. **Reduction test:** strip each item to its underlying trait in ≤3 plain words (review scratch, not stored). If two reductions could caption the SAME MOMENT of the user's life, they are one angle; refork.
>   2. **One-trait ceiling:** the stem's headline cost/gift owns at most TWO items per pool; the rest mine secondary traits.
>   3. **Fork tools** (fork the FACULTY, not the venue): *miscalibration* (the gift when it's wrong, or doubted when right — 癸 "Unpersuadable" / "Self-doubting") · *tolerance* (what the nature can't stand — 丙 "Deflecting") · *counter-trait* (the quiet secondary trait — 甲 "Proud," 辛 "Thin-skinned") · *tempo* (the trait across time) · *reception* (how others misread it).
>   4. **Band-contradiction check:** opposite-calibration items sit in opposite bands so no trio contains both (also applies gift-vs-shadow: 甲 "Self-starter" is tagged off the open band because "Hesitant" lives there).
>   5. Each band's selected trio must still mix temperature, and every item must surface for at least one band.
> - **Desc law:** one or two sentences that stage the phrase in a concrete real-life situation — the reader must SEE an image (the meeting drifting to consensus, the 2 AM call, the door already shut). Humanized gate applies; band-robust wording on all-tagged items; band-tagged items get to be band-specific on purpose.
> - **Selection:** band-tagged items first, then all-tagged in pool order, ×3 per group.
>
> **Portrait prose (yourNature, K2 bodies — owner-ruled register 2026-08-05):** ALWAYS opens on "You" — the perspective starts from the person, never the energy. The most emotionally resonant, mildly compelling register in the corpus: a letter from a perceptive old friend, minus the casualness. Staged in the stem's native arena (§2b), never the generic social room. Humanized (§4), cost carried at current-to-cost+1 intensity from the stem's own dimension, arriving as plain fact. One ordinary sentence per paragraph. Contractions welcome.
>
> **Ledger prose (dm_overview — owner-ruled shape 2026-08-05; SIGN/PERSON division 2026-08-14):** the owner's canon line: **"Day Master Overview is the myth decoder from the BaZi world, Your Nature talks to you."** The SIGN is the subject, never the reader. The P4 two-paragraph contract: **dm_overview = the SIGN** — teaches the BaZi term (yin/yang element, material identity, season, place in the cycle) and builds the symbolic persona image, staying entirely in the material/metaphor domain ("The Blade is Yang Metal… Its image is the general's sword kept plain and sharp"). Formula: name → the term → its meaning in the cycle → its image as a figure. NO second person, NO user psychology — the diagnosed 2026-08-14 failure was overview doing personality mechanics ("conclusions come early, the standard turns inward"), which duplicated yourNature. **yourNature = the PERSON** — decodes the metaphor into direct who-you-are psychology (You-opener corpus). The reader's experience: symbol, then decoding. **Cadence (owner-polished 2026-08-14): the poet-narrator.** The decoder tells the sign as a story handed down from the ancient past ("The old stories give it autumn… They pictured it as a general's sword… Between wars it hangs on the wall and says nothing"). Schoolbook connectors BANNED in this register: "It stands for," "Its image is," "In the system" — informative but flat. **Subject rule (owner 2026-08-14): THE SIGN ITSELF is the only actor — "The Blade… It arrives with autumn… it hangs on the wall." Unnamed-narrator attributions are BANNED with the schoolbook connectors: "the old stories," "the old telling," "the ancients," "they pictured/drew/said" — they invite the question "who are they?" and put a committee between the reader and the sign.** The myth is told through what the sign does and is, in poet cadence. Vocabulary stays PLAIN — mystical through image and rhythm, never through obscure words. Element named freely (this register still teaches); contractions sparing.
>
