## Current conclusions

Elementum has a coherent reading architecture and a distinctive human voice worth preserving. The strongest next step is to repair selection consistency and source-to-copy reasoning while keeping recognizable personality language, elemental imagery and ordinary scenes.

The first evaluation was too restrictive about emotional language and insufficiently attentive to dated, field-specific owner rulings. It also treated some intentional design choices as defects. The revised assessment below corrects those errors.
