## 甲 · The Oak · Yang Wood

### The Sign (dm_overview, the myth decoder: the sign is the subject, never the reader)

The Oak is Yang Wood, the first sign in the count of days, the oldest picture of beginning itself. It is the seed that argues with the ground and wins, the trunk that spends a hundred years walking toward the sky. Spring belongs to it, and so does every road that is still mostly promise. Whatever refuses to stay small grows under this sign.

### Your nature (yourNature_desc, baseline; always opens on "You")

You see where things could go before most people see that they could go anywhere, and you're moving while they're still deciding. What you build tends to outlast you. You're just rarely there to see it, because halfway through every ending you've already started the next thing.

**Your nature · Overfueled (strong core) variant:** You are the seed that argued with the ground and never stopped arguing. Every day wants to be bigger than the last one, and the present rarely gets a full visit from you. What you build outgrows you, then waits for a finishing that has already moved on. The next summit was visible from halfway up this one. It always is.

**Self card · Overfueled (strong core):** Growth with no off switch. Reaching is your resting state. Every day wants to be a bigger one, and the part of you that could coast never gets the vote.

**Your nature · Balanced (moderate core) variant:** You grow the way the old tree grows, upward without hurry and without doubt. The reach comes when called and rests when it isn't needed, so what you start, you tend to finish. People join your climb because the pace leaves them able to. Rings get added either way. **Self card · Balanced (moderate core):** Growth on a steady trellis. The reach is there when you call it and quiet when you don't. Things get started, and things get finished, in that order more often than not.

**Your nature · Underfueled (weak core) variant:** You hold the tall tree's patience more than its hunger. The push comes in seasons now, and between them the vision just sits in you, waiting for weather. When the growing starts it runs slower than you'd like and truer than you expect. Spring has never once missed a year. Yours simply keeps its own calendar.

**Self card · Underfueled (weak core):** Growth in a quiet season. The push comes in seasons, not as a constant. On the days it arrives you move like spring. On the others, you're allowed to be winter.

**Self card · Overfueled (strong core):** The winding never pauses. Every wall is already being solved. Your mind routes around things by reflex now, even the ones that would have opened if you'd knocked.

**Self card · Balanced (moderate core):** The winding knows its walls. You adapt when the wall is real and hold your line when it isn't. The route bends. The destination has stopped negotiating.

**Self card · Underfueled (weak core):** The winding rests between walls. You still find the way around, just not every day. Some walls get climbed next month, and the vine loses nothing by waiting.
