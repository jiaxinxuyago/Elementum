## How a Day Master video is built (S01 to S10)

About 30 to 35 seconds, faceless, one Day Master per post.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 s | The hook (big text) over the element's house art | none, or the hook read once |
| 2 to 8 s | Name and label, e.g. "The Blade · Yang Metal · 庚" | The intro: "In BaZi, your Day Master is the element of the day you were born. There are ten. This one is The Blade, Yang Metal." |
| 8 to 16 s | The three keywords, one at a time | The overview line |
| 16 to 30 s | Your Nature, as captions | "If this is your Day Master:" then Your Nature |
| 30 to 35 s | The inscription, alone, then the CTA card | none |

**CTA card (until the freeze, ruling M2 A):** "Your Day Master is one of ten. Find yours when the founding round opens. Link in bio."
**CTA card (after the freeze):** "Find your Day Master free. Link in bio."
**Pinned comment:** "Your Day Master comes from your birth date. Elementum works it out on your phone, and your birth details never leave it. Please don't post your birthday in the comments."
**Visuals:** the element's house art (`Design/Documents/DES_14_Asset_Map_Concept_Arts.md`) or a screen recording of the app's reveal for that stem, a slow push-in, and one soft ambient track for the whole series so the series reads as one thing. Text-to-speech or a hired voice, the same voice for every post.
**Link:** `https://elementum.life/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=b01-sNN` (the waitlist page path replaces the root until the freeze, per M2).

## S01 · The Oak · Yang Wood · 甲

- **Hook:** The Day Master that starts before the path is clear.
- **Overview line (VO):** "It is the seed that argues with the ground and wins, the trunk that spends a hundred years walking toward the sky."
- **Keywords:** "Driven" · "Upright" · "Pioneering"
- **Your Nature (VO):** "You see where things could go before most people see that they could go anywhere, and you're moving while they're still deciding. What you build tends to outlast you. You're just rarely there to see it, because halfway through every ending you've already started the next thing."
- **Inscription (end card):** "You grow toward a light you can't prove yet, and never stop to rest under it."
- **Caption:** The Oak is the first of the ten Day Masters. Already started something new this week? #bazi #fiveelements #daymaster #yangwood #chineseastrology #personality
- **Campaign:** `b01-s01`

## S02 · The Vine · Yin Wood · 乙

- **Hook:** The Day Master people call lucky. It isn't luck.
- **Overview line (VO):** "It is the grass and the climbing plant, life that cannot force a door and so finds every window."
- **Keywords:** "Adaptable" · "Persistent" · "Resourceful"
- **Your Nature (VO):** "You get where you were going, one way or another, and you make it look easy enough that people miss how hard it was. The route changes constantly. The destination never has. Being flexible gets read as being soft, and you stopped correcting that a long time ago. It's useful."
- **Inscription (end card):** "You find a way around what stops other people, and they call it luck."
- **Caption:** The Vine bends. It still gets there. Tag the friend who always finds the side door. #bazi #fiveelements #daymaster #yinwood #chineseastrology #personality
- **Campaign:** `b01-s02`

## S03 · The Sun · Yang Fire · 丙

- **Inscription (end card):** "You warm whoever stands near you, and run on warmth nobody returns."
- **Caption:** The Sun gives first and rarely gets asked back. Who's your Sun? #bazi #fiveelements #daymaster #yangfire #chineseastrology #personality
- **Campaign:** `b01-s03`

## S05 · The Mountain · Yang Earth · 戊

- **Hook:** The Day Master nobody asks how it's doing.
- **Overview line (VO):** "Travelers set their course by it and never ask how it is doing."
- **Keywords:** "Steady" · "Centered" · "Dependable"
- **Your Nature (VO):** "You're the one everyone builds their plans on, the way people build on ground they trust without thinking about the ground. Things shake, and you don't. What that takes stays out of sight, because you keep it there, and because nobody ever asks a mountain how much it's carrying."
- **Inscription (end card):** "You hold what others set down, and nothing has ever offered to hold you."
- **Caption:** Check on your Mountain friend. They won't bring it up. #bazi #fiveelements #daymaster #yangearth #chineseastrology #personality
- **Campaign:** `b01-s05`
- **Note:** the hook and the overview line say the same thing on purpose (the hook sets it up, the line pays it off). If it feels repetitive in the edit, use the overview line "It carries snow on its shoulders and the sky's own weight, and it calls the arrangement fair." instead.
