Resolution log

Where the two source files disagreed — and how each was reconciled.

This library was assembled from Moodboards/mood-foundation.html (sections M1–M3) and Legends/legend-primitives.html (sections §1, §2, §6). Where they conflicted, Design/tokens.css was treated as the single source of truth; where tokens.css was silent, the newer of the two files won, with the rationale recorded below.

Color · alpha ladder
Mood-M1 showed 5 alphas (10 / 1A / 40 / CC / 100). Legend-§1 dropped the CC column. Resolution: tokens.css declares 5 alphas including CC ≈ 80% for eyebrow text — Mood wins. Ladder below restored to 5 columns.
Color · paper coverage
Mood-M1 omitted silkFold, inkMist, inkDeep, borderStd, borderLight. Legend-§1 covered them but missed --advisor. Resolution: all paper/ink/border tokens shown; advisor added to reserved accents.
Type · "The Blade" color
Mood-M2 set the locked headline in --ink; Legend-§2 set it in --walnut. Resolution: Legend wins — walnut is the documented archetype-name color.
Type · italic rule
Mood-M2 said italic appears in specimens, locked pair, voice chips. Legend-§2 carries the NEW v2 rule (2026-05-06): italic restricted to sub-headlines + microcopy only. Resolution: v2 rule is canonical; supersedes M2's looser phrasing.
Type · scale coverage
Mood-M2 was a weight-matrix view (38/32/24/18/16/14 × 400/500/600). Legend-§2 was a use-case view (40 hero, 38, 32, 24, 18; EB 16/15/14/13/11/10). Resolution: kept Legend's broader use-case scale, added Mood's weight matrix as a secondary view. 11 is the regular nav/micro-label; 11.5 is the italic microcopy chip — distinct uses, both kept.
Surface · count
Mood-M3 listed 5 surfaces (cardstock, tinted-metal, quiet, elevated, active-cardstock). Legend-§6 said 3 surfaces (cardstock, tinted ×5, quiet) and called any addition a doc-patch trigger. Resolution: Legend's 3-surface taxonomy is canonical. elevated and active-cardstock are composed patterns, not core surfaces — shown as such in §3.
Surface · tinted variants
Mood-M3 only previewed tinted-metal; Legend-§6 showed all 5 pigments. Resolution: Legend wins — all 5 tinted variants shown below.
Color · doc-patch trigger
Legend-§1 explicitly forbids intermediate alpha values like ${color}25 or ${color}55; Mood-M1 was silent. Resolution: rule retained — only 10 / 1A / 40 / CC / 100 are valid pigment alphas.
Scope · iconography + recipes
v1 covered color, type specimens, and surfaces — but said nothing about typography recipes (composition patterns) and had no iconography section at all. Resolution: v2 absorbs the components library's recipe set (10 atoms + 5 composed) at the end of §2, and adds a full §3 iconography reference — sprite inlined (40 <symbol> defs), catalogued across 6 sub-sections (5 element marks, 10 day-master stems, 4 force roles, 5 reading icons, 5 tab icons, 15 affordances). Primitives is now the canonical reference for color, type, AND icons; surface taxonomy renumbers from §3 → §4. components-library.html §2 + §3
v3 · legend absorption
v3 absorbs the remaining still-true sections of legend-primitives.html ahead of its retirement: §4 radius + §5 spacing (now §5 geometry & motion, with shadows and easing from tokens.css), §8 italic gallery (condensed into §2 under the v2 rule banner), §9 border palette (dashed-affordance rule folded into §1 borders), and §10 anti-patterns (now §6). Legend §11 page templates migrate to Library_Screens/; legend §12 drift log is retired — every item was resolved in the app tokens long ago.
v3 · scoped tokens
The live app evolved two scoped token sets after the May seal, both sealed by the JourneyCatalogue handoff: reading accents (--ghost / --up / --down / --bud / --ally / --frame, scope .reading) and the journey page theme (--pg-*, scope .jny, light + dark ramps — the only dark-theme surface in the app). Both are now shown in §1 and recorded in the tokens.css appendix. --shadow-tile (guidance/feature tile lift) existed only in app tokens — added to tokens.css 2026-07-28, drift closed.
v3.1 · live-app reality audit
Full primitives audit vs the running app (2026-07-28, owner hard rule: design HTMLs track what the app renders). Headline: Design/tokens.css is never loaded at runtime — main.jsx imports only global.css (no :root anywhere in src); every var(--*) resolves from the scoped re-declarations in reading.css / journey.css. tokens.css is spec, not delivery. Each section below now carries a REALITY panel where the app diverged from locked law — divergences are flagged, not blessed; relock-vs-remediate is an owner call. Iconography moved wholesale to Library_Iconography.html (owner directive — §3 is now a pointer).
v3 · icon canon
Sprite reconciled against Design/icons.svg (canon = 34 symbols). ico-back was in canon but missing here — added to §3f. Seven affordances (ico-arrow-up / -down, ico-info, ico-alert, ico-check, ico-pin, ico-bell) exist in this sprite but NOT in icons.svg — kept for reference, flagged "sprite only"; do not consume via /icons.svg#id until canonized.

§4

Surface taxonomy

Three canonical surfaces · five tinted variants · two composed patterns. DES_04 §3.5.D

The system has three surfaces. A new surface is a doc-patch trigger. "Elevated" and "Active-cardstock" are composed patterns — combinations of an existing surface with a documented shadow or rim — not new surfaces.

Reality · 2026-07-28
Cardstock holds (47 var uses + 14 literals) and the tab bar formula is intact — but quiet is nearly dead (2 consumers) and the app''s most-used surface is undocumented: warm-frost rgba(248,244,236,α) — 108 uses at 22 alphas (dominant rungs .5/.55/.6/.7/.9/.94/.95) across reading, journey, Today, Compat, Guidance. Distinct from cardstock (248,241,225). The journey surface adds --pg-card rgba(251,247,236,.75) plus a full dark ramp (prefers-color-scheme + [data-theme]) — the app''s only dark mode, needing first-class taxonomy status. There is also an undocumented blur ladder: 2 / 3 / 6 / 8 / 12(tokenized) / 20px. Owner call: admit warm-frost + pg-card + blur ladder into §3.5.D, or remediate.
