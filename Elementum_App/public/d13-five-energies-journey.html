The Five Energies Journey — Reveal is the Catalogue

The complete UX spec for the redesigned reading surface, prepared for the Claude Design polish pass (visual wireframe → rendered screens). Every text slot carries a word budget — the binding contract between UX and writing, and the direct input to schema v2. Governing principle (DOC5 §0 / D13): reading first, system second — every face carries a conclusion; mechanism lives one tap beneath. Demo chart throughout: 庚 The Blade (Metal 42 · Earth 28 · Water 16 · Wood 14 · Fire 0 · catalyst Earth ↑ · friction/absent Fire ↓). All copy DRAFT.

Architecture (D13 a–c)

  • One surface, two modes: first run = Beat 1 ceremony → Beat 2; every revisit = Beat 2 directly (it IS the Reading tab)
  • Reading tab = static birth-chart deep-dive; ALL temporal readings live on the Calendar (Today) tab
  • Day Master at the wheel's center — tap → Day Master card (free); nodes/tiles → energy cards ×5
  • "Pillar Chart →" opens the merged data page; standalone Energy Map screen retires

Ceremony & motion

  • Beat 1 = the Naming: a ceremonial identity plate, share-card-ready (one artifact, two jobs)
  • The seal is the continuity object: on scroll it shrinks + glides to become the wheel's center; painting dissolves to silk
  • Wheel ring peeks above the plate's bottom edge — the only scroll affordance needed
  • Reduced-motion: all sequences compose instantly / cross-fade

Voice & gating

  • Inscriptions: second person · full edge · bilingual (4–6 字 + EN line) · "compassionate precision" register
  • Ten Gods always render persona names + structural definition fragment (D12a); engine translations never surface
  • Free: plate, wheel, faces, first R-claim per card, Day Master card. Seeker: depth layers, inside opened cards only (§19)
  • Hour unknown: "~" on all %s · quiet explainer chip → Resonance · foundry mark 「時未定」
