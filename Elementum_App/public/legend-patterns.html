Onboarding system · 7 steps

One assembly — OnboardingShell — drives all seven steps. Five input archetypes between them. DES_04 §7 · flow/onboarding.jsx

Each step occupies the full screen. The shell is fixed: 3px progress strip at the very top, a tracked-caps step counter, EB-Garamond question, italic poetic subtitle, an input zone, then a back/continue pair. Only the input zone changes. The progression bar uses a --bronze → --bronzeLight gradient and animates 500ms on step change.

§14

Drift log v2 · open questions

Where v2 surfaced gaps in v1, or v1 + DES_04 disagree. The anchor remains authoritative.
v1 §4 has no "form input" radius slot
v1 §4 locks the radius scale at 1 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 / 22 / 999. Form inputs in flow/onboarding.jsx use radius: 12 consistently — covered by the existing scale. Resolution: no new primitive. Annotate v1 §4 to call out the form-input slot at 12 explicitly so engineers don't drift to 10 or 14. Doc-clarification patch only.
Toggle switch geometry · 44 × 26 vs 48 × 28
DES_04 specifies a 44 × 26 toggle. flow/onboarding.jsx ships 48 × 28 with a 24 × 24 knob. Both compose from radius 999 (v1 §4). The 48×28 reads more legibly at the form density used in onboarding; 44×26 is fine for in-page settings. Resolution: standardise on 48 × 28 / knob 24 for primary opt-in surfaces (onboarding, profile push notifications) and 44 × 26 / knob 22 for inline list toggles. Document both in tokens.jsx and reference here. DES_04 §15 · flow/onboarding.jsx Step7
Modal entry shadow — fourth elevation tier?
v1 §6 locks three card surfaces (cardstock, quiet, parchment), none of which carry a downward elevation shadow. Modals A and C (bottom sheets) need 0 -8 40 rgba(0,0,0,0.12) — an upward-pointing shadow that no v1 surface defines. Resolution: add a fourth surface variant to v1 §6 — "elevated" — for sheet/modal containers only. Same fill as cardstock; defining property is the upward shadow + larger top-corner radius (22 22 0 0 or 24 24 0 0). Doc-patch v1 §6.
Drag handle — new primitive or composed?
The 40 × 4 rounded pill on bottom sheets uses radius 999 (allowed) and --paperHair fill (allowed). It's a composition of v1 atoms, not a new primitive. Resolution: document as an assembly here in v2 §5; no v1 patch.
Tab bar translucent surface · rgba(253,253,252,0.85) + 12px blur
This is a fourth card surface and shares the same gap as the modal-elevation question above — neither cardstock nor quiet nor parchment cover translucent + blur. Resolution: same patch as the modal item — add the "elevated" variant to v1 §6 with two sub-modes: blurred chrome (tab bar, sticky header) at rgba(253,253,252,0.85) + 12px blur, and elevated sheet (modals A and C). v2 is the canonical home for these until v1 is patched.
Notification toggle in onboarding uses #cfc7b3 off-state
The off-state pill background in flow/onboarding.jsx Step 7 is #cfc7b3 — close to --paperHair (#CDBE9E) but not identical, and not in the v1 token set. Resolution: swap to --paperHair in tokens.jsx and onboarding.jsx Step 7 + Step 7A. Functional code patch.
Welcome sign-in pill backdrop alpha
flow/welcome.jsx uses rgba(245,239,224,0.72) for the sign-in pill — a slightly cooler tint than the locked rgba(248,241,225,0.92) cardstock surface, plus 6px backdrop-blur (also new in v1). Resolution: two paths. (a) If we keep this for legibility against the bottom mountains band, register both cardstock-blurred as a v1 §6 sub-mode of the new "elevated" surface (above), and the 72% alpha cream variant as the canonical "translucent over imagery" surface. (b) Or simplify: replace with the locked cardstock 0.92 alpha and drop the blur — the bottom mountain band already has a strong fade-to-light gradient, so legibility may be fine without the cooler tint. Recommend (b) for now — fewer primitives. Re-test on dark variants of the mountain art before locking.
Open: keyboard-on state · onboarding text inputs
DES_04 §7 doesn't spec the layout when the iOS soft keyboard pushes content up. The 130px top padding + flex-column shell will compress the input zone gracefully, but the back/continue buttons may overlap the keyboard. Resolution: deferred to v3 (full screen prototypes). Note here so engineering doesn't ship onboarding without testing.
Open: bottom-sheet drag-to-resize physics
Pattern A (compatibility result) is described as "drag-resizable" — drag-down dismisses, but does it also support snap-points (e.g. half / full)? Resolution: deferred. Spec in v3 alongside the Friends screen prototype.
Open: tab-bar first-appearance animation
DES_04 §11 line 1105 says the tab bar "fades in over 400ms after the dashboard content has settled (~200ms post-mount)." Confirm: does this also apply when navigating away from /dashboard/* back to /chart-reveal? Suggested behaviour: tab bar slides down + fades out 250ms, mirroring the entrance. Resolution: spec in v3.
