1 · The names

Each star gets a plain meaning word plus “Star”, chosen from what it does in the classics, so an English reader understands the chip on sight. The 汉字 stays beside it on every card (D5). Words already used by the app are avoided: the ten characters (Twin, Rival, Artisan, Virtuoso, Horizon, Steward, General, Magistrate, Alchemist, Sage) and the ten Day Masters (Blade, Candle, Field, Jewel, Mountain, Oak, Ocean, Rain, Sun, Vine).

2 · How a hard star is said

The classics read these stars as robbery, wounds, bereavement and widowhood, and several read them differently “in a woman’s chart”. None of that reaches the text (G1, G2). Each reading names a plain cost, the want under it, and what carries the reader (BIZ_04). The mixed stars follow the same pattern, with the price inside the gift.
