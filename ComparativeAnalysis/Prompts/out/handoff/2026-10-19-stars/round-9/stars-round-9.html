1 · The names

Every star is a person from the old stories, in two words: an image that carries the meaning, then the person. The ten characters stay one word. No name repeats a character or a Day Master (the Blade, the Oak…), none is gendered, and the 汉字 stays on every card (D5). Each row gives who the star is in Chinese myth, the version 4 name, the version 3 one-word name, and the version 2 name.

N1天乙贵人 the Noble Stranger Help · literally “Heaven’s Noble”

Who it is. 天乙 is the noblest spirit in the old sky, the one folk tales send down the road when you are stuck. It shows up as a stranger of standing who backs you before you have proved yourself.

N2文昌 the Bright Scholar Help · literally “Flourishing Letters”

Who it is. 文昌帝君, the god of writing and exams, the one students still pray to before a test.

N3天德贵人 the Heavenly Guardian Help · literally “Heaven’s Virtue”

Who it is. Heaven’s own goodness standing over you. Trouble reaches you and passes lightly, as if someone stepped in front of it.

N4月德贵人 the Moon Mender Help · literally “Month’s Virtue”

Who it is. The quieter twin of N3, the moon’s kindness. Trouble turns into something you can use, and kindness comes back to you.

N5禄神 the Rice Farmer Help · literally “Spirit of the Stipend”

Who it is. 禄星, the middle one of the three star gods 福禄寿 on every New Year shelf: the god of your earned reward.

N6金舆 the Golden Courtier Help · literally “Golden Carriage”

Who it is. The golden carriage that carries nobles in comfort. Ease, good taste, a gentle manner, people looking after you.

N7将星 the Banner Captain Help · literally “General’s Star”

Who it is. The star over the general’s tent, the banner people rally under. The General is already one of the ten, so the star is the one who leads the troop.

N8红鸾 the Red Matchmaker Help · literally “Red Phoenix”

Who it is. The red 鸾 bird of weddings, and 月老, the old man under the moon who ties a red thread between two people.

N9天喜 the Joyful Host Help · literally “Heaven’s Joy”

Who it is. 喜神, the spirit of happy occasions, who walks in with weddings, births and good news.

N10华盖 the Temple Poet Help, classically mixed · literally “Flowered Canopy”

Who it is. The canopy held over emperors and over the Buddha. The classics give it art, faith and a gifted mind that walks alone.

N11驿马 the Post Rider Help, classically mixed · literally “Post Horse”

Who it is. The relay horse of the old post roads, always on its way to the next town.

N12桃花 the Blossom Charmer Help, classically mixed · literally “Peach Blossom”

Who it is. The peach-blossom spirit of the old tales, whose charm draws people in. The classics also warn about where that leads.

N14劫煞 the Road Bandit Test · literally “Robbing Sha”

Who it is. The bandit on the mountain road who takes without warning, as in the old outlaw tales.

N15亡神 the Secret Keeper Test · literally “Fleeing Spirit”

Who it is. 亡 is “fled, gone missing”. The spirit that slips out of sight and keeps a part of you hidden.

N16孤辰 the Lone Watcher Test · literally “Orphan Hour”

Who it is. The lone figure who needs to be alone even among family and close company.

N17寡宿 the Quiet Hermit Test · literally “Widow’s Lodge”

Who it is. The quiet lodge where someone lives a long season alone. The literal is gendered, the old hermit of the mountain is not.

N18魁罡 the Thunder Champion Test · literally “Dipper’s Head”

Who it is. 魁星, the fierce top scorer who kicks the Big Dipper, and 罡, the dipper’s handle. A fierce, decisive, all-or-nothing mind.

N19空亡 the Empty Seat Test · literally “Empty and Gone”

Who it is. An empty seat at the table: what sits there arrives late, or lighter than promised.
