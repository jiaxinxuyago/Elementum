# The Blade

Geng · Yang Metal · Identity

Original text and proposed rewrite, section by section. All wording is preserved from the source JSON files. This is a review draft.

## Manifesto

<!-- source-field: manifesto -->

**Original**

> Precision before intention · You are the Metal that cuts things clean.

**Rewrite**

Truth before company · You are the Metal that cuts excuses short.

## The sign

<!-- source-field: dm_overview -->

**Original**

> The Blade is Yang Metal, the metal that has already been through fire. It arrives with autumn, the season that ends what summer started, and it does the harvest's honest arithmetic, deciding what was worth growing. Kept plain and kept sharp, it is drawn once, only for the true thing. Between wars it hangs on the wall and says nothing, which is its own kind of speech.

**Rewrite**

The Blade is a length of Yang Metal made for a clean cut. In autumn it takes its place beside the harvest, where growth meets an edge and what is ready comes away. Fire gives it shape. Its strength narrows to an edge that can part what force alone would crush. After the cutting, it lies flat against the stone, plain enough to leave the work in view.

## Your nature · Overfueled

<!-- source-field: band.yourNature_desc -->

**Original**

> You are what the sword is like when it wakes up as a person. The cut is always ready and mostly used. You put the truth ahead of comfort, yours and everyone's, every day, in every setting. People bring you the questions nobody else will answer straight, then flinch at exactly what they asked for. The edge never rests. That includes the nights, and it includes you.

**Rewrite**

You carry that cutting edge into conversations that only asked for company. A loose excuse catches your attention and you pull it apart, even when the evening could have continued without a correction. You have the energy to keep going. Being right doesn't tell you when to stop, and a clean answer can leave more distance between you and the person who asked.

## Core energy card · Presence

<!-- source-field: band.self_card.presence -->

**Original**

> The cut is always available and mostly used. Clarity this constant is a climate, and the people near you dress for it.

**Rewrite**

You bring the whole blade to small doubts. A passing remark gets as much force as a question that needs a serious answer.

## Gift · Order

<!-- source-field: gifts[6] -->

**Original**

> **Crisis performer**
>
> *under pressure*
>
> The day everything breaks is the day you are calmest. Others freeze. You are already cutting the problem into pieces that can be solved.

**Rewrite**

**Calm under pressure**

*when plans break*

You can turn pressure into a clear next step. When a plan falls apart, you can cut the problem down to the part that needs attention first.

## Gift · Action

<!-- source-field: gifts[5] -->

**Original**

> **Plays the long game**
>
> *trimming a plan*
>
> You cut so the thing can grow. What you plant and keep cutting back, money, skill, trust, comes back with interest because you never let it sprawl.

**Rewrite**

**Builds to last**

*effort over time*

You can keep shaping a project after the first excitement passes. Each careful cut leaves the useful work intact, so the next attempt can build on what is already there.

## Gift · Expression

<!-- source-field: gifts[4] -->

**Original**

> **No dressed-up answers**
>
> *communication*
>
> What is inside you moves outward without forcing. When you speak, you say the thing the table has been talking around, and the answer comes back plain.

**Rewrite**

**Says it simply**

*being understood*

You can give an idea a clean edge without making the telling feel like work. A few plain words let someone else pick it up and use it.

## Shadow · Body

<!-- source-field: shadows[1] -->

**Original**

> **Never reconsiders**
>
> *second chances*
>
> Once you have decided what something is, it stays that way. The person who grew, the plan that improved, the second chance that was earned, they all arrive at a door already shut.

**Rewrite**

**Rejects correction**

*changing a decision*

You can make a decision so final that the next piece of evidence has nowhere to land. The cut keeps its first shape, even when a different answer would fit what is there.

## Shadow · Mind

<!-- source-field: shadows[3] -->

**Original**

> **Overprepared**
>
> *showing the work*
>
> Fed past use, the edge stalls. Everything goes in and nothing comes out, and the brooding gets mistaken for depth.

**Rewrite**

**Prepares without starting**

*putting learning to use*

You keep sharpening an answer that needs to be tried. Another note makes the plan feel fuller, while the edge stays on the stone and the work waits for a first cut.
