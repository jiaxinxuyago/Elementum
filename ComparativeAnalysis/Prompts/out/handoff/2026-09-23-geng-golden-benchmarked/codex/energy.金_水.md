## The relationship

<!-- source-field: mechanism.base -->

**Original**

> In the old reckoning water is metal's child: cold iron beads the morning dew, and metal, melted, runs bright as any river. The hard thing does not stay hard. At its finest edge it turns liquid, and what was fixed begins to flow, the clear stream carrying the mountain's sharpness out into the world.

**Rewrite**

A cold blade gathers dew before the morning warms it. Drops join along the steel until their own weight pulls them down. They take its chill with them. What held a hard shape gives rise to a flow, and the water follows a slope the edge itself cannot travel.

## What this energy means for you

<!-- source-field: function.definition_catalyst -->

**Original**

> Water is your Expression, and as a catalyst, it is output that flows. Words, cooking, small kindnesses, the bright remark that turns an evening: whatever is inside you moves outward without forcing, and people are fed by it before they notice they were hungry.

**Rewrite**

Water is your Expression, and as a catalyst, it is the part of your effort that reaches another person. A thought becomes a sentence someone can follow, or care becomes a meal you share. What you make gives your attention somewhere beyond yourself to go.

## A characteristic

<!-- source-field: ledger[0] -->

**Original**

> **Fluent**
>
> Expression comes to you the way walking comes to other people, without assembly or rehearsal. What costs everyone else three drafts costs you a breath, and the ease is invisible from inside. You do not know where the words are before they arrive. They just do.

**Rewrite**

**Fluent**

Words come most easily when you are sharing something you enjoy. A small detail can carry the next sentence along without a speech prepared around it. You can explain how a thing works while making it, and the telling keeps pace with your hands.

## An everyday scene

<!-- source-field: ledger[1] -->

**Original**

> **Nourishing**
>
> They arrive wound tight, sit down at your kitchen table, and something loosens over the second hour. Soup helps, and so does the way you ask questions. By the door they are lighter and cannot name what happened. You can. It happens every time.

**Rewrite**

**Nourishing**

At eight, a bowl sits on the table while a friend searches for words. You offer another spoonful without asking for a neat ending to the story. Making something gives your care a plain form, and the conversation can slow down beside the food.

## How others may experience you

<!-- source-field: ledger[2] -->

**Original**

> **Silver-tongued**
>
> Friends bring you along to negotiations the way people bring jumper cables, just in case. Sellers quote you one price and agree to another, later, unsure how the distance was crossed. The recordings would show nothing but pleasant conversation. That is the whole trick.

**Rewrite**

**Silver-tongued**

Listeners can follow your wording past a point they first argued against. You find a different way through the objection, with a turn of phrase that keeps the exchange moving. That skill can help a difficult conversation, though agreement reached smoothly still needs something solid behind it.

## Practical guidance

<!-- source-field: function.advise_catalyst -->

**Original**

> Ease is a gift right up until it dodges every hard thing. Pick one piece of what you make and finish it to done, not to pleasant. Say the awkward true sentence once a week, to the person it belongs to. Comfort that never risks anything eventually feeds no one.

**Rewrite**

Being understood starts before the wording feels finished. Give yourself ten minutes to write without fixing each line. Read the page aloud, then mark the sentence you most want understood. Share that sentence with one person.
