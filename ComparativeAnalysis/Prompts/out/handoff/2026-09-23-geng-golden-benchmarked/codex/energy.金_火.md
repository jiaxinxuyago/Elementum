## The relationship

<!-- source-field: mechanism.base -->

**Original**

> The refined prevails over the stubborn: fire alone commands metal. In the forge the unbending learns to bend, ore gives up its rock, and the blade takes whatever shape the flame decides. Heat is the one argument metal cannot win, and what survives the fire comes out finer than what went in.

**Rewrite**

Heat makes hard Metal open to a different shape. Deep in the forge, steel gives under a blow that would glance off its cold surface. The change takes pressure as well as flame. Once the metal is drawn out, the heat can leave while the new form stays, ready for a task its first shape could not meet.

**Original**

## What this energy means for you

<!-- source-field: function.definition_catalyst -->

**Original**

> Fire is your Order, and as a catalyst, it is structure with heat in it. Pressure organizes you instead of scattering you, rules turn into momentum, and when things fall apart you are the one holding a straight line through the middle of it.

**Rewrite**

Fire is your Order, and as a catalyst, it is a demand that gives your strength a direction. Deadlines make the next choice concrete. Rules give effort a limit, and a difficult task can gather your attention around what actually needs doing.

## A characteristic

<!-- source-field: ledger[0] -->

**Original**

> **Decisive**
>
> Deciding fast is native wiring for you, and it reads as relief to everyone stuck deliberating. Options do not improve with age, in your experience, and your experience keeps proving it. The choice gets made while others are still framing the question. Then everyone breathes.

**Rewrite**

**Decisive**

You get clearer when a choice cannot wait much longer. Pressure cuts away the options that only looked important while there was time to discuss them. You can name a workable next move and take responsibility for it, even while part of the answer is still missing.

## An everyday scene

<!-- source-field: ledger[1] -->

**Original**

> **Daring**
>
> The call everyone dreaded sat on your list for exactly one day. You made it standing up, between two other tasks, and it went the way these things usually go with you, which is fine. Three weeks of collective dread died in four unremarkable minutes.

**Rewrite**

**Daring**

At nine, your notes hold a question that could upset the meeting's plan. You raise it while there is still time to change the work. The answer does not have to be pleasant for the question to be worth asking. You are willing to hear it.

## How others may experience you

<!-- source-field: ledger[2] -->

**Original**

> **Crisis-ready**
>
> There is a particular quiet that falls when things break and you are present, because everyone knows where the decisions will come from. It looks like glamour from a distance and it is really planning under pressure, done fast, and it has saved more days than anyone has counted.

**Rewrite**

**Crisis-ready**

People can use your instructions when the plan they were following breaks down. You pick out what needs attention and give the next task a clear shape. That focus is useful under pressure, though the same force can press too hard on others once the urgent part is over.

## Practical guidance

<!-- source-field: function.advise_catalyst -->

**Original**

> Fire that only knows battle starts inventing battles. Save your command for what deserves it: decide the small stuff instantly and cheaply. Build one boring routine that runs when nothing is burning. Peacetime is what the fighting was for, so practice standing in it.

**Rewrite**

You do not need a crisis to give your strength a purpose. Set a deadline for one task you keep moving. Ask someone to check the result against a simple requirement. Keep a small daily promise when nothing is urgent. Write the time down.

## Energy card · Closing line

<!-- source-field: cta_verdict -->

**Original**

> Pressure is your forge, and the heat you choose on purpose finishes an edge that comfort would have left dull.

**Rewrite**

Your strength needs something that can answer back, and a fair test can turn stubborn effort into work with a purpose.
