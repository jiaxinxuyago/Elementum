## First read against Elementum's open rules

- **This is the horoscope-slop register the audience research names.** Sun-sign address ("Today, Taurus"), abstract virtue nouns (comfort, stability, determination, satisfaction, passions, joy, values), three paragraphs that could be sent to anyone, and a closing string of imperatives ("Remember to balance", "be mindful", "ensure", "Stay true to yourself"). BIZ_03 §2 calls this the thing the target reader is allergic to, and the rating prompt under it ("help us enhance your predictions") says the copy is tuned by thumbs, not by a voice.
- **It does use pictures, and they are generic.** "cooking a delicious meal, enjoying nature, or spending time with loved ones": objects of a kind, not of a person. This is the one place in the owner's screenshots where a competitor illustrates, and the illustration is the weakest line on the page. Evidence for the door rule's real pain point: a picture is only worth having when it belongs to the persona (Elementum's ruled-domains rule, E6), not when it is a stock image of pleasantness.
- **Hedge and affirmation.** "You may feel", "making it a great day", "This day is ideal", "embrace the moments that make you feel grounded and fulfilled". One hedge, four affirmations, no cost anywhere except "while it's tempting to indulge".
- **Energy named once, as a cause.** "the energy of the Taurus moon enhances your natural inclination": the same energy-as-agent move as The Pattern's "supportive energy encouraging you", one degree vaguer.
- **Sentence shape.** 13 to 30 words, every sentence declarative or imperative, grade level about 9, 138 words on one screen with no pacing.
- **Repetition.** "indulge" twice, "feel" three times, "your" eleven times in 138 words.
