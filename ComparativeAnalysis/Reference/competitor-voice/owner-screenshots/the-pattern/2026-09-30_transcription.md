## First read against Elementum's open rules (the study integrates this)

- **Chips.** Card labels are two-word pairs, mostly virtues ("Sensitive & Adaptable", "Confident & Grounded") with one symptom ("Security Angst"). Consistent with the owner's 2026-09-30 ruling that gifts may be virtues and shadows are symptoms. The label sits under the configuration's name, which is the Nebula principle Elementum already uses on the seat pages.
- **Openers and hedging.** P2 opens on a biography claim ("Your environment growing up has deeply affected you"), which Elementum's D2 forbids, then a trait pair ("At heart, you're extremely sensitive and adaptable"), then three consecutive sentences on "may" ("you may have absorbed", "may have caused", "you may need"). P1 has "might be impacted" and "you can find ways". Hedge density: 6 hedges in 8 sentences. The capacity opener appears once, inside the summary's last sentence, not as the first words.
- **Imagery, scene, place.** None. Every noun is abstract and psychological (environment, beliefs, culture, family, values, inner self, relationship life, energy). No object, no picture, no time of day, no place. The Pattern's "how does it know" effect here comes from naming a tension (accept values without question, or lose yourself pleasing loved ones), not from a scene.
- **Sentence shape.** 12 to 26 words, all declarative, no fragments, no questions. Grade level about 8 to 9 (Elementum's target is 6 to 7 on the Day Master page, B1 to B2 on the energy pages).
- **Energy as a material.** P1 says "find ways to channel this energy", the same verb Elementum's manual uses for the Overfueled band, and treats the transit as a thing with a length and peak windows. This is the register the owner asked for in the prescription beat, said by the market leader in its own product.
- **Cost placement.** The cost lands after the virtue in P2 (sensitive and adaptable, then losing yourself). Elementum's inscriptions do the same; the door rows put the cost in the turn.

## Second batch (P8 to P10), the timing reading

- **Pacing by screen.** A timing reading is a swipe deck of seven screens, one paragraph of 25 to 45 words each, the sentence alone on the page. The unit the reader meets is the screen, not the paragraph, which is the same constraint Elementum's caps-by-page table encodes.
- **Energy as an agent.** "There's supportive energy encouraging you to take risks", "things are flowing", "your visionary qualities are expanding". The energy does things; the reader responds. Close to the register the owner ruled for the prescription beat, with one difference: The Pattern never names the energy's material, only its mood.
- **Conditional on the reader's state.** P9 opens "If you already feel secure about yourself and know what you want, these inherent traits will be even more magnified". A branch by the reader's condition inside the prose, which is what Elementum's band and state machinery does by selection instead of by "if".
- **Hedges and intensifiers together.** "may present themselves" beside "amazing time", "total confidence", "exactly what you want". The hedge protects the claim; the adjectives sell it. Elementum bans both the reflex hedge and the hollow affirmation (REA_04 §8.9).
- **The "not X, but Y" frame.** "It's not a moment to be passive, but to embrace your magnetic, independent nature". A construction Elementum's voice canon lists as a mannered device.
- **Still no picture.** Ten excerpts, zero objects, zero places, zero times of day. Across every surface the owner captured, The Pattern illustrates nothing; it names.
