## The card

- Construct: the opener formula "{Element} is your {Function}, and as a catalyst / as a friction, it is …" followed by the family's two drives, one clause per persona, each with its cost, in that persona's ruled domain (owner 2026-10-01: the concrete-life inventory is no longer required; the two personas of one placement are two either-ors, not two lists). The field carries one word of the pair's chemistry, the energy's or the core's, in the field; the self pair's Body definitions may carry the body's own material (sleep, food, recovery, stamina, muscle, breath, rest) (owner 2026-09-30, the list widened the same day to word stems and either element of the pair). Since 2026-10-14 (polish rule 3) that word is the energy's own plain verb or state, or the energy's name in the mechanism sentence ("There is more Earth in your mind than you use"): Water flows, pours, refills or settles; Wood grows, builds or reaches; Fire is warm, light or fuel; Earth holds, shelters, carries or is steady; Metal is hard, exact, holds the line or finishes. Never a prop: no blade, edge, ore, forge, cut, mountain, steel, sword, sheath, knife, whetstone, mine, sharp, branch, orchard, prune, root, stream, river, flood, flame, heat or burn anywhere in the definition (the gate blocks them, polish rule 2, and no longer counts them for E7). The definition is the family's two drives in one either-or, one clause per persona with its cost, in two or three sentences (the gate blocks a sentence with more than two commas, which retires the inventory); the tension sentence is required (blocking since 2026-10-14); the mechanism sentence is required in the definition (owner 2026-10-14, Q32); after the opener the reader is the subject of every sentence and of the last without exception; no turn word (but, yet, however, the difficulty is, and then); the close is at least eight words and the line the reader keeps. Family-level, no god split, and family-level by construction (owner 2026-09-30, re-cut 2026-10-01): the definition names one drive per persona of the family, each with its cost, anchored in that persona's ruled domain words from the cell facts (for the Action door the Horizon's drive toward the opening and the Steward's drive to keep; for Expression the Artisan's drive to feed and the Virtuoso's drive to shine), so the one definition serves both siblings; the domain nouns anchor the costs, they are not objects on display. The five Order friction definitions each carry the inner-judge sentence (the pressure ends up turned on yourself). **The five self pairs (金_金, 木_木, 火_火, 土_土, 水_水) open on the core's own volume instead of the pole word: "{Element} is your Body, and running thin / running over, it is …"** (the station's five self cells; REA_02 §5h, the core reads its own supply).

- Style: understandable without knowing either persona's name; the templated opener is a lawful match across cells; everyday nouns; the repetition law with the same cell's turn, advice and carry.
- Checks: ≤55w, opener formula present, one word of the pair's chemistry, the energy's or the core's material, in the field; the self pair's Body definitions may carry the body's own material (the harness blocks, matching word stems for either element of the pair; the element's own name in the opener does not count, the opener names it as a label), zero dashes, rep-block, one drive per persona of the family, each with its cost (the read decides; the harness note on a definition that hits only one persona's domain words stays); the tension sentence present (blocking since 2026-10-14, D9 re-cut); the v0.7.1 gate (no arena word, no turn word, no sentence over two commas with the opener stripped, the last sentence at least eight words with the reader as its subject: blocking; a third-person subject elsewhere a note; a causal joint present, a note when none).
