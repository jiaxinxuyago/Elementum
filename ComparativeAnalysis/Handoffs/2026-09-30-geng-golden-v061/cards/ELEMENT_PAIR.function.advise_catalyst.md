## The card

- Construct: a profound or psychological opening that lands in atomic, habitual advice ("At some point more learning is just fear with a reading list. Trade study for evidence: …"). The reader keeps a practical choice. **Every advice owes the band's remedy verb** (owner 2026-09-30, REA_02 §5c): Channel on the Overfueled band's cases (a friction on the Body or Mind door, a catalyst on the Expression, Action or Order door): an outlet, an action that spends, never a brake; Refill on the Underfueled band's cases (a friction on the Expression, Action or Order door, a catalyst on the Body or Mind door): intake, rest, taking in, never a push for more output. The verb is derived, never chosen, the way the cure is. Body advice that gives an Overfueled chart stopping points, breaks and a breath to wait is the Refill shape where the house asks for Channel, and fails the read. **The friction pole closes on the energy prescription** (owner 2026-09-29; the beat re-cut to the energy-manual register, owner 2026-09-30): the beat speaks of the energy only, as a material the reader can hold and use, and the small life actions stay before it (two of them, so the budget holds). The formula, fixed across the 25 cells: `Seek {Element}, the {Function} energy. {Element} {is or does, in its own material, two clauses}. Let it {spend / hold / shape} what {the overgrown element} {stores / pours / burns}.` ("Seek Wood, the Action energy. Wood breaks ground: it turns what Earth has stored into a first cut, a step taken before you feel ready. Let it spend what your Mind keeps taking in."). The cure is derived, never chosen: the energy that tames the overgrown function by the ten equations, or, where the taming energy is unwanted on the band, the energy that drains it. The taming cure only, one energy, never an outlet beside it and never an energy that feeds the friction. The element and the function are named plainly, no role word, no number, no percentage. The catalyst pole carries no prescription.
- Reasoning chain: read the band's remedy verb from the function and the pole (Body or Mind friction → Channel · Expression, Action or Order catalyst → Channel · Expression, Action or Order friction → Refill · Body or Mind catalyst → Refill) and let every action take its shape (Channel spends: make, ship, say, lift, walk, cut; Refill takes in: read, sit, sleep, ask, wait) → name the psychological truth under the state in one sentence → small actions with objects and cadences (once a month, one dinner, one call, a decision date), drawn from the family's two personas' ruled domains (the six words in the cell facts) and from ordinary life, work only where a domain word is work: three or four on the catalyst pole, two or three on the friction pole → on the friction pole, derive the cure: read the function this cell's energy is for the core (Body = the core's own element, Mind = its feeder, Expression = what it feeds, Action = what it tames, Order = what tames it), then take the cure from the band table: Body overgrown (Overfueled) → Order tames it, name Order · Mind overgrown (Overfueled) → Action tames the feeder, name Action · Expression overgrown (Underfueled) → Mind tames it, name Mind · Action overgrown (Underfueled) → Body tames it, name Body · Order overgrown (Underfueled) → Mind drains it (the 化 cure, since the taming Expression is unwanted on a weak chart), name Mind → resolve the function to its element for this core by the ten equations → close on the beat in the energy-manual register: `Seek {Element}, the {Function} energy.`, then the energy in its own material (what it is or does, two clauses), then `Let it {spend / hold / shape} what {the overgrown element} {stores / pours / burns}.`; no life action inside the beat, the small actions stay before it. Never name an energy that feeds the friction (Fire feeds Earth, so a Mind-heavy Metal core is never told to seek Fire, even where Fire is wanted for the core).
- Style: the owner's ruling of 2026-09-20 keeps the authored advice voice everywhere: plain, specific, no metaphor stacking, no chart-derived prescription of investments, careers or medical acts, never a guarantee. The prescription beat is in the energy-manual register (owner 2026-09-30): the energy is the subject, a material the reader can hold and use, three sentences of about thirty words; the cure element, its function noun and the overgrown element are the only names in it, and no life action sits inside it. The beat costs one of the small actions, so two stay before it and the budget holds. The actions are the persona's and the reader's: a dinner, a call, a bill, a walk, a bedtime, a parent, a child, a friend, before a document, a meeting or a checklist, and each stays at a size the reader can own.
- Checks: ≤60w, zero dashes, the band's remedy verb kept (Channel advice never prescribes stopping, breaks, waiting or pausing; Refill advice never prescribes pushing or more output: the harness notes, the read decides), rep-block against definition and turn, the example source (the family's domain words or ordinary life, the read decides), no action bigger than the reader can own. On the friction pole: exactly one cure element named, and it belongs to the cell's cure set (the band table resolved for this core), no element that feeds the friction, the beat opens `Seek {Element}, the {Function} energy` (the harness blocks without that form; "your own Body energy" is the Body variant), no role word, no number.
