## Where it stopped

The five-energy function pages are final and live. The Day Master page was not touched in this work. It still runs on the September P4 corpus: the yourNature band variant plus the gift and shadow trios from the door-tagged pools. That page is the next workstream. See `01_TASK_BRIEF.md`.

## What the owner expects in how you work

- Rulings come in pairs and choices. Each sample is shown before and after, with real text and a recommendation. The owner answers with short codes ("1A 2A 3A", "X5-02 B"). Never land a pattern in bulk before the owner has approved samples of it.
- Comparison pages are claude.ai artifacts. The owner reads on a phone, so keep them phone-friendly, with real app text.
- When asked for a loop, run it to the stated stop condition and notify the owner when results are ready.
- Rejected samples are informative. M3 was rejected because the fix fell into "X, so Y". M5 was rejected because the Sage seeks shelter, rather than giving it. Read RULINGS.md for the exact words.
