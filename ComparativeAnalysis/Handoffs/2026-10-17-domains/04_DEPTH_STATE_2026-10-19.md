## 2. Rulings in force

The full record is `03_RULINGS.md` in this folder. The ones every section must keep:
- **G1:** the domain-only prediction law covers Life Chapters and Stars too. It is in `Reading/Database/Prompts/backlog/2026-10-17_domains-domain-first.md`:
  - definite past patterns, tied to their gate's ages;
  - future trajectories with ages and decades;
  - digits and calendar years only in engine-printed labels;
  - no may, might, likely or perhaps;
  - hard news only with what carries the reader, and never illness, death, divorce, bankruptcy or sums;
  - no advice;
  - the reader is the subject of the last sentence.
- **G2:** readings are gender-neutral. The classical star texts are gendered; that never carries over.
- **Gates and ages:**
  - Year Gate to 15 (Early Chapter);
  - Month Gate 16–34 (Rising);
  - Day Gate 35–47 (Home);
  - Hour Gate 48 on (Late).
  - A stem seat is "at" the gate, in plain view; a branch seat is "inside".
- **Tiers:**
  - free: each section's hook;

  - Advisor: the Blueprint, the years, year stars, and the domain angles By decade and By year.

  - chart B (1990-09-17 05:00 Beijing, male; 乙 Underfueled; age 36);

- **Review pages:** phone-friendly claude.ai artifacts with real app text, before and after, a recommendation, and multiple-choice questions with the first option recommended. The owner answers in codes ("Q1 A, Q2 A").
- **Rulings:** the owner rules row by row, never in bulk. Wait for rulings before writing a corpus.
- **Writing:** the sealed-writer pattern. A handoff folder holds BRIEF.md, worklists and gate.mjs; agents write `out-N.json`; the main session lands it.
- **Then the pipeline:**
  1. station JSON (`Reading/Database/templates/by_axis/json/`);
  2. transcribe to `src/content`;
  3. `export-reading-templates --check`;
  4. `voice-audit`;
  5. `qa-selection-fixtures`;
  6. `npm run build`;
  7. push;
  8. verify dev.elementum.life and elementum.life.
- **Records:** append rulings to a `03_RULINGS.md` in your own handoff folder, and keep `Operations/Project_Management/PM_02_Pending_Tasks.md` current.
