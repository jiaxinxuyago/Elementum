How each new piece borrows from the page

  • The eyebrows. YOUR DOMAINS, YOUR LIFE CHAPTERS, YOUR STARS and YOUR ENERGY BLUEPRINT use the same spaced capitals as YOUR ENERGY MANUAL and YOUR ENERGY MAP.
  • The tiles. Each section is one tile shaped like the Energy Manual's: an icon, one bold-word claim, then a chevron. The Manual unfolds in place; these open their page.
  • Domains. Eight domain icons, the same set THE EDGE already uses. The dots under each name are the wheel's own colours: one dot per seat that rules the domain (Wealth: three Wood and one Metal). The two heaviest domains get a darker ring.
  • Life Chapters. Four gates as four stretches of life, each holding its seats as small versions of the wheel's watercolour plates. A seal-red marker shows "you · 31". The chapter you are in is in seal red; the coming ones are faded.
  • Stars. Chips in the words-note pill shape: gold for stars that help, outline for stars that test, dashed for the Nobleman who is arriving.
  • Going deeper. A seal-red divider, then the Blueprint tile. It names your structure, with a dark "Advisor" pill in the same style as the OVERFUELED pill, and lists its four parts.
  • The words note stays last, unchanged (R3 asks whether it gains the new words).

3 · How the tiers show on this page

Everything above is the free view, and the catalogue looks the same on every tier. The only tier mark is the Advisor pill on the Blueprint tile, which disappears for Advisor readers. The locks live one tap deeper, inside each section's page: Domains and Life Chapters open free and deepen with Seeker, Stars show badges free and readings with Seeker, and the Blueprint is Advisor only.
