5 · A domain reading: angle 1, by position

The reading is cut by seat. The lead seat opens with how you are, then every seat that rules the domain gets its own block, in gate order. The gate gives each block its time, so the climb (it knows me, how did it know, will that happen) survives inside the positions: the Year seat reads as your past, the Hour seats as your future. Decades move to angle 2.

The Domains · Wealth

Your money comes through the people close to you.
By positionBy decade · Advisor

How you are

You find money through other people, in a tip from a friend or a side venture with someone close. Because your openings arrive through people you trust, you say yes before you read the terms, and you earn in bursts more than in a steady line. The money you make fastest is the money you hold on to least.

You lose money most easily where you share it with friends and family, and you also find your safety net among those same people.

6 · The Life Chapters page: the four pillars

Each pillar is one chapter of life. A chapter card holds:

  • its two seats and their chapter reading;
  • the domains that chapter rules, as chips that open that domain's reading;
  • the decades that fall inside it.
Status follows your age: lived, now or coming. The chapter readings are drafts in the current voice, replacing the old life_chapter texts (shown in grey under the first card for comparison).
