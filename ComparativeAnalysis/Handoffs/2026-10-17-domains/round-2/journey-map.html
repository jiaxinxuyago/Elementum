3 · The four ways into a domain page

Every route ends on the same chart-wide page for that domain. This is the full fix for the old EDGE landing (D2): no route lands on a seat that does not hold the word.

A · From Your Reading (the main door)

Your Reading→ scroll, or tap "See where your life is ruled" →Beat 4 YOUR DOMAINS→ tap the Wealth tile →Wealth page

The reader meets the gates and the life line here first, before any domain page.

B · From an energy page

Your Reading→ tap the Wood dot →dot card→Wood page→ THE DOMAINS card, tap Wealth →Wealth page

The card lists only the domains this energy's seats rule ("3 Wood seats · of 4"). The page shows all four Wealth seats, from any energy.

C · From THE EDGE (a catalyst energy's function page)

Wood page→ THE FUNCTION →function detail→ THE EDGE, tap the Family door →Family page

D · From the Day Master page

Your Reading→ hero arrow →Day Master page→ a diagnosis row →that energy's page→ route B →a domain page

4 · Screen by screen

Reading tab · home · "YOUR READING"

Your Reading

  1. Beat 1 · You are. Seal, "You are" + archetype, manifesto, "{stem} · CAST FROM YOUR DAY OF BIRTH".
  2. Beat 2 · YOUR ENERGY MANUAL. One row, "{Core} is your {function} · it runs {Condition}". It unfolds to SEEK THESE and EASE THESE rows.
  3. Beat 3 · YOUR ENERGY MAP. The dominance wheel (unchanged), with the SEEK and EASE rails.
  4. Beat 4 · YOUR DOMAINS new
    • The claim ("Wealth and Family carry the most weight in your life.") and a sub line.
    • The teaching beat: four gates, two seats each, ten characters.
    • The life line, with "you · 31".
    • With no birth hour, the closed Hour Gate line.
    • Eight tiles, heaviest first. A domain with no seat shows a dashed tile.
    All free.
  5. The words on this page. Core, {Condition}, Catalyst, Friction.
  • Hero arrow → Day Master page · share icon or wheel centre → identity card share
  • A manual row or a wheel dot → dot card → that energy's page
  • Bridge "See where your life is ruled" → scrolls to beat 4 new · a domain tile → that domain's page new
  • Pills and word chips → a definition float → "Deeper in the Codex" → Codex (Guidance tab)

Reading tab · one tap in · back row "YOUR ENERGIES · {Energy}"

An energy page (one per energy)

  1. The hero card. Art, the 生/克 equation with the role, the dominance bar and the diagnosis.
  2. THE FUNCTION. "{Energy} is your {function}.", the verdict and the keyword chips.
  3. THE DOMAINS changed
    • Today: this energy's seats, each with chips and a teaser.
    • Now: "What Wood rules in your life", a count line, and one tile per domain its seats rule ("3 Wood seats · of 4" plus the headline).
  • Hero card → the mechanism · THE FUNCTION → the function detail
  • A domain tile → that domain's page new (it used to open the seat list, now retired)

Back → Your Reading.

Reading tab · two taps in

The mechanism

  1. THE MECHANISM: title, epigraph, base text, state turn. No onward taps.

Back → the energy page.

Reading tab · two taps in

The function detail

  1. THE FUNCTION. The definition.
  2. THE CHARACTER. The amount strip, the pole pill, the dial, three metric bars and keyword chips. A chip opens its passage.
  3. THE EDGE (catalyst page) or THE REMEDY (friction page). THE EDGE has three domain doors changed.
  • An EDGE domain door → that domain's page new. The domain sheet and its price line are retired.
  • THE REMEDY box → the remedy energy's page

Back → the energy page.

Reading tab · new screen · back row "YOUR DOMAINS · WEALTH" (from beat 4) or "{Energy} · WEALTH" (from an energy page)

A domain page (×8: Wealth, Family, Career, Social, Mind, Growth, Health, Love)

  1. The head card. "The Domains · Wealth", the headline ("Your money comes through the people close to you."), the count line ("4 seats rule Wealth … Year Gate, Day Gate and Hour Gate"), and the life line with this domain's seats lit. Free.
  2. How you are ("With money"). The lead seat's recognition, plus the classical pair line when one fires. Free. This is the "it knows me" step.
  3. What has already happened Seeker
    • Gate beats in time order, each with its age span ("The Early Chapter · birth to 15").
    • The last decade that ruled it ("An Alchemist decade · 2013 to 2022").
    • The gap line when your current gate holds no seat here.
    This is "how did it know that?"
  4. What is coming Seeker
    • The coming seats with ages and years ("The Home Chapter · from 35 · 2030").
    • The coming decades, and the strongest or heaviest window ("2043 to 2052").
    • Notes.
    This is "is that really going to happen?"
  5. The seats that rule Wealth.
    • One card per seat: term and 汉字, age span and face, domains.
    • The defline (free) and the full reading (Seeker).
    • "About the Steward".
    This is the positioning dive.
  • Free reader: "Open with Seeker" → the upgrade sheet (where steps 2 and 3 would be)
  • No birth hour: "Your Hour Gate is closed … Add your birth hour" → Discover your birth hour

Back → where the reader came from: Your Reading (beat 4) or the energy page.

Reading tab · from the hero arrow · back row "YOUR ENERGIES · DIAGNOSIS" (being restructured in a parallel session)

  5. "What's a Day Master …" and "Deeper in the Codex" (no action yet).

Back → Your Reading.

7 · Things I noticed while mapping (not changed)

  • After a purchase, the welcome ceremony sends the reader to an old screen (app-energymap, the legacy energy catalogue), not to Your Reading. That looks stale.
  • On the Day Master page, "What's a Day Master …" and "Deeper in the Codex" have no action yet.
  • On the Pillar chart, "What's a pillar? → Codex" has no action.
  • Five detail routes (ten gods, forces, chapters, patterns, seasonal) can only be reached by a direct link. No button leads to them.
  • Discover your birth hour always goes back to Profile, wherever it was opened from. From a domain page the reader lands on the Pillar chart once done, not back on the domain.

Where things stand: the domain screens run on dev.elementum.life (dev tools on) and stay off elementum.life until the corpus audit lands.
