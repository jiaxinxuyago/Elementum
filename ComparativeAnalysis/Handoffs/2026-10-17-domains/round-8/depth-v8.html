Where the classics disagree (owner I1: follow every text, note the differences). The research files hold every quote and every variant: research/shensha-rules.json and research/qiongtong-tiaohou.json. The ones that change a reading:
Nobleman and Scholar’s Star base. 三命通会 and 五行精纪 key them to the year stem. 子平 practice and most apps use the day stem. The day stem is the default here (W7).
The Nobleman for 庚. The majority verse gives 丑未, attested on Han bamboo slips. A minority verse (神峰通考, a 渊海子平 gloss) gives 寅午. The majority is the default.
The Ram’s Edge for yin stems. 子平 gives it to the yang stems only, and that is the default. An older rule gives the yin stems one too. A modern rule found in apps has no classical source.
Heavenly Joy. The modern rule (opposite the Red Phoenix) is used. 三命通会 also has a season-based Heavenly Joy that gives different results.
A star on its own base. The classics say a star is “seen” in the other pillars. The calculator never lets a base light its own seat (a 午 year is not its own Commander Star). Apps differ (W13).
The season table. 36 of the 120 cells differ between editions, 18 of them in substance. Most of the substantive ones are the summary table that apps copy, which reorders the text’s stems or folds its conditional stems in. The default follows the text (W14). The golden cell 三月庚金 differs only in a transcription slip.
Two texts were only partly reachable. 星平会海 (cited second-hand) and 渊海子平 (no stand-alone 神煞 chapter online). No print edition was checked.

How they are written. The same sealed-writer pattern as the domains corpus: a brief, worklists, a gate (2026-10-18-depth-template/gate.mjs) that every unit passes before it lands, then the voice audit and a page audit on composed pages. The gate already holds the master prompt's rules and the prediction law (G1). It also checks chart invariance: a unit names no character but its own. It also blocks any five-word run shared with the same seat's domain units, so a reader who opens both pages never reads the same sentence twice.
