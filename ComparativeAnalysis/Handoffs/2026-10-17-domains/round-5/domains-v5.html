Your Energy Blueprint

A Steward structure · 正财格

1 · Your structure

Your chart is built around money kept and value earned. The Wood hidden in your strongest seat shows on two of your stems, which makes the Steward the organising force of your life more than the Alchemist who holds that seat. You do best when the work in front of you has a clear return. draft

Formed, under strain. The Twin and the Rival stand close to your Stewards, so equals keep claiming a share. Fire would protect the structure, the pressure your chart lacks, and the decades you are in now and next bring it.

2 · What your chart runs on and lacks

Strongest: Wood and Earth carry two thirds of your chart, what you earn and what you think.

Missing: no Magistrate, General, Sage or Virtuoso sits in your seats. Nobody inside your chart tells you what to do or praises you for doing it, so you borrow structure and approval from outside. The Magistrate decade you are in now is the first to give them to you.

Your season (调候): born in late spring with heavy Earth around your Metal, your season asks for strong Wood (甲) to break the Earth up and Fire (丁) to temper you. Your chart carries the softer Wood and almost no Fire, so you sharpen fastest under a deadline someone else sets. draft

Useful energies (as on Your Energy Manual): Seek Wood, Water, Fire. Ease Earth, Metal.

3 · Seen and unseen

In plain view: the Steward twice and the Twin. People meet a careful provider who carries their own weight.

Inside: the Artisan, the Alchemist, the Horizon and the Rival. The private you wants comfort, strange ideas, open roads and a contest, and only the people you live with meet that side. draft

4 · Pairings and clashes

Peers beside the purse · 比劫夺财 · Hour Gate. You lose money most easily where you share it, and you find your safety net among the same people.

The maker feeds the purse · 食伤生财 · Year Gate. What you make turns into money more easily for you than for most.

Thinking starves making · 枭神夺食. You think so long about a thing that you miss the moment to make it.

Day binds with Year · 寅亥合. Your home life keeps pulling you back toward the family you came from. draft

Month binds with Hour · 辰酉合. What you build at work binds into what you keep in later life. draft

No clashes, punishments or harms: your chart argues less with itself than most.

The structure rule (E1, 子平真诠): the month branch 辰 hides 戊 (main), 乙 (middle) and 癸 (residual). 戊 does not show on any stem; 乙 shows on the Year and Hour stems. So the structure is taken from 乙: a Steward structure (正财格). The rest of the reading still says "the Alchemist holds your strongest seat". The Blueprint explains why both are true.
