The reason to make the domain page chart-wide, and not per energy: Wealth on the golden chart is held by four seats on two energy pages (three Wood seats, one Metal seat). Read per energy, the reader never sees that one seat sits in childhood and three sit ahead of them. Read across the whole chart, in gate order, the domain becomes a life story with a past and a future. That timeline is what makes the reading predictive.

2 · Meeting the seats: the teaching beat

THE DOMAINS is where the app first uses the ten characters by seat, so this is the moment to teach it (REA_06 L-row 2.6, position). The ladder's rule is "claims lead, teaching sits under the claim". So the hub opens on a claim about this reader, then three plain sentences, then the picture that does most of the teaching: the life line. Four gates as four stretches of life, the seats in them, and a mark at the reader's age. Left of the mark has happened. Right of it is still coming. The reader sees why the reading can talk about their past and their future before a single paragraph says so.

3 · The climb: one domain page, three steps

Every domain page climbs the same three steps, in this order. Each step has its own source in the chart, so each claim can be traced.

StepWhat the reader feelsWhat it saysWhere it comes from
1 · How you are"It knows me."The behaviour in this domain: the drive, its tension, its cost, present tense.The lead seat in this domain (the seat ranking that is already locked: month branch first). If a classical pair fires on this domain, its line follows.
2 · What has already happened"How did it know that?"Definite things that have already happened, each tied to an age span.The seats whose gate the reader has already lived (gate eras: Year to 15, Month 16 to 34, Day 35 to 47, Hour 48 on), plus past decades whose character rules the domain. A Day Gate seat also gives the pattern so far. A gate with no seat here gives one line on what that absence meant.
3 · What is coming"Is that really going to happen to me?"The trajectory: what each coming seat brings, from what age and year, and the strongest window.The seats whose gate is still ahead, labelled with the age and calendar year it opens. Then the coming decades (the ten-year luck pillars) whose character rules the domain, read as helping or heavy for this chart. When a decade's character is the same as a seat's in this domain, the two line up, and that is named as the strongest window.
This step needs a new law. The master prompt today bans biography ("never assert a past event, an age at which something happened") and allows exactly one tendency-framed predictive beat. BIZ_04 calls a predicted outcome "too harsh". Your goal for this section asks for the opposite. I propose a law for the domain reading only: Q8 to Q11.

Under the three steps sit the seats that rule this domain, one card each, with the seat's gate, age span and its full reading (the positioning dive). The god layer WHO RUNS IT and the old god-noun domain readings leave this page (Q13).
