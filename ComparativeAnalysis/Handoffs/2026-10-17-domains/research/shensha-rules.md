# 神煞 calculation rules: summary

Companion to `shensha-rules.json`, which holds the full tables, variants, quotations and source ids. Research date: 2026-10-18.

## What was read
- **First-hand classical text:**
  - 《三命通会》, 卷二/三/六/七
  - 《五行精纪》 (宋, the earliest compendium)
  - 《神峰通考》
  - 《渊海子平》 (Wikisource edition)
  - 《紫微斗数全书》卷二
  - 《协纪辨方书》卷四/五/七
- **Caveats:**
  - **《星平会海》:** this text was **not** available. Only its chapter list is online. Its rhymes are cited second-hand.
  - **《渊海子平》:** the Wikisource edition has no stand-alone 神煞 table chapter. Its 阳刃, 日贵, 魁罡 and women's chapters were used.

## Valence and gender
**Auspicious:** 天乙, 文昌, 天德, 月德, 禄, 将星, 红鸾, 天喜, 金舆.

**Mixed:**
- 驿马: 吉神 when intact, but 常人马多奔波.
- 华盖: 吉 but solitary.
- 羊刃: 古法 negative; 子平 reads it as power when 七杀 controls it.
- 空亡: it voids bad stars as well as good ones.
- 魁罡.

**Inauspicious:**
- 桃花: negative in the classics, mixed in modern writing.
- 孤辰, 寡宿, 劫煞, 亡神.

**Gendered readings:**
- 男忌孤辰, 女忌寡宿.
- 桃花 '妇人尤忌之'.
- 驿马 and 咸池 are bad in women's charts.
- In women's charts one 天乙 is good and many are bad.
- 金舆: '妇人不富即贵' / '男子多妻妾'.
- 二德 are positive for women (one dissent: 李九萬).
- 空亡: 寡鹄孤鸾.
- 魁罡 in a woman's chart 伤夫克子.
- 羊刃 and the separate 阴刃 count against wives and husbands.

## Every school difference found
1. **Day base or year base.** The classics (三命通会, 五行精纪) key the stem stars (天乙, 禄, 羊刃, 金舆, 文昌贵) to the **year** stem ('甲人'). 子平 and modern practice key them to the **day** stem. For the branch stars (驿马, 桃花, 华盖, 将星, 劫煞, 亡神), the classics use the birth year. Modern practice uses both year and day, and 神峰通考 already allows '或又以日上起' for 咸池. 红鸾, 天喜, 孤辰 and 寡宿 stay year-only in every source.
2. **天乙: the 庚辛 dispute.**
   - The 甲戊**兼**牛羊…庚辛逢马虎 version moves 庚 to 寅午. It appears in 神峰通考, in 渊海子平's 日贵 gloss, in 太白阴经 and in 曾门/金匮.
   - The 甲戊庚 version is on the Western-Han 肩水金关汉简 and a Six-Dynasties 式盘. It is also in 五行精纪, 三命通会 and 协纪辨方书, and it is the majority practice.
3. **天乙: which branch is the day 贵人 and which the night one.**
   - 三历会同 gives 甲戊庚 丑 by day and 未 by night.
   - 协纪辨方书 gives 阳贵 甲未 … 庚丑 and 阴贵 the reverse. 曹震圭 reverses it again.
   - The day/night line is also disputed: 卯酉, sunrise/sunset, 冬至/夏至, or 星出星没.
   - The branch pairs per stem never change, so a BaZi table is unaffected.
   - 日贵: 三命通会 gives day births 癸卯/丁亥, while 神峰通考 gives them 癸卯/癸巳.
4. **文昌.** The modern table (甲巳乙午…) is a different star from 五行精纪/三命通会's **文昌贵** (甲巳 乙亥 丙戌 丁辰 戊申 己午 庚寅 辛未 壬卯 癸丑, year stem) and from **文星贵** (甲午 乙巳 … 庚戌 辛亥). It is also often confused with **学堂** (the 长生 position). The usual explanation, 食神临官, fits only 8 of the 10 stems. The modern rhyme's source is unverified: it is credited to 星平会海, 三命通会 or 果老星宗, and it is not in the Wikisource texts of 三命通会, 五行精纪, 神峰通考 or 渊海子平.
5. **天德/月德.**
   - Majority practice matches any of the four stems; a minority matches the day stem only.
   - 神峰通考 gives 子月 as 子, against 巽 = 巳 in every other source.
   - 天德合 is listed for only 8 months.
   - 紫微斗数 has a year-based 天德 and 月德 that must not be mixed in.
6. **羊刃.** There are three positions:
   - 子平: yang stems only. This is the default.
   - 古法: 禄前一辰 for all ten stems (乙辰 丁未 己未 辛戌 癸丑), keyed to the year stem.
   - Modern: the 帝旺 of a yin stem (乙寅 丁巳 己巳 辛申 癸亥). No classical basis was found for this one.

   **阴刃** (禄后一辰, said to harm the husband) is a separate star.
7. **桃花.**
   - 墙内/墙外 has three incompatible definitions: (a) by pillar, year/month = 墙内 and day/hour = 墙外, the most common; (b) by base, year-derived = 外 and day-derived = 内; (c) a 子午卯酉 day with a 子午卯酉 hour = 墙外.
   - 遍野桃花 means 子午卯酉 all present.
   - 红艳煞, '临官遇劫桃花煞' and 年命桃花 are different stars that share the name.
8. **天喜.**
   - The modern rule (from 紫微斗数): the branch opposite 红鸾, by year branch.
   - The classical BaZi rule (三命通会, 五行精纪): 天喜神 by birth season, 春戌 夏丑 秋辰 冬未, which gives different results.
   - Date-selection (选择) books have a third, monthly version (成日).
9. **红鸾.** This is a 紫微斗数 import (神峰通考 carries it; 三命通会 and 五行精纪 do not list it). 五行精纪's 趙氏 '歲星' gloss matches its positions for 11 of the 12 branches, which may make it an earlier form.
10. **空亡.**
    - The day-pillar 旬 is primary and the year pillar secondary. 互换空亡 (year and day voiding each other) is the heaviest case.
    - Split rules: half-旬 (甲子 to 戊辰 void only 戌), and yang/yin (yang pillars void the yang branch).
    - 截路空亡, 真空亡 and 四大空亡 are different stars.
11. **魁罡.**
    - Four days (庚辰 庚戌 壬辰 戊戌), with 甲 explicitly excluded.
    - 壬戌 shows up as a fifth only through one verse variant in 渊海子平.
    - The classical pattern needs the day pillar; marking 魁罡 in any pillar is a modern habit.
12. **金舆.** 禄金舆 (禄 + 2) is standard. 五行精纪 also has 命金舆 (year branch + 2) and 马金舆 (驿马 + 2).
13. **孤辰/寡宿.** They are built by the directional group (方), not the 三合. Exemption '连属不言孤寡'.
14. **Skeptic school.** 神峰通考 (张楠) rejects several readings: 日贵格, 日德格 and 魁罡格; 金舆 as harmful to men; 桃花煞 and 八败 against women. Its view: read the stars only as secondary to the 用神.

## Implementation notes
- **Base pillar.** Classical phrasing such as '寅午戌生人见午' looks for the star in the *other* pillars. Whether the base pillar can carry its own star (a 午 year as its own 将星, a 辰 year as its own 华盖) varies by app. Decide this explicitly.
- **Where to match.**
  - 天德 and 月德 are matched against stems.
  - Everything else is matched against branches.
  - 魁罡 is a day-pillar identity check.
  - 空亡: the void branches are (b − s + 10) mod 12 and (b − s + 11) mod 12, where s and b are the base pillar's stem and branch indices.
