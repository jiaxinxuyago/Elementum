## The card

- Construct: `{lead, domains: [3], metric}`, one per ELEMENT_GOD cell on the catalyst pole. The head is not written: the app derives it as the remedy's head with the arrow turned up (the character's verb as the kicker, the energy's name at the title size, its function beneath; owner 2026-10-16, E1 of four). THE EDGE is the ceremonial entry from the gift to the Domains tier (owner 2026-10-16): it says in one sentence what the gift buys the reader, then opens three doors, one per ruling domain, each a large button with the domain's glyph and its name, nothing beneath (owner 2026-10-16: "Remove the text under each domain, unnecessary"). A tap opens the ceremonial sheet (the seat's teaser line, the seat's domain reading blurred behind the Seeker pill, the unlock with the price); unlocked, it lands in THE DOMAINS at that word. The energy page's THE DOMAINS tile stays the first entry. It sits in THE REMEDY's box at the remedy's size.
  - The **lead** is two sentences of thirty to forty-five words (owner 2026-10-16: Formula A, The Pattern's voice, "shorten it to 1-3 sentences"). The first names the energy and its three ruling domains in the table's order, as the reader's own: `<Energy> runs through your <d1>, your <d2> and your <d3>.` (the domain words lowercase; Social reads "your social life", since "your social" is not a noun). The second opens `It is` and gives each domain its clause in the same order, three clauses joined by commas and a final "and": what the gift is in that part of the reader's life, in The Pattern's register (second person, the inner experience before the outer fact, warm, present tense, no instruction). "Wood runs through your wealth, your family and your career. It is the thing quietly growing that lets you rest, the house you keep steady for everyone in it, and the long work you see through when everyone else has moved on." Each clause carries one picture at a size the reader owns; no imperative, no object-and-cadence list, no job list, no virtue word, no hedge (may, might, perhaps), no level or state of the chart, no relative the reader may not have, no four-word run shared with the cell's chips or ledger rows. The opening frame is fixed so the fifty read as one voice; the clauses are where the cell lives.
  - The **domains** are the god's three ruling domains in the canonical taxonomy (Wealth, Health, Career, Love, Family, Social, Mind, Growth), fixed per god (`backlog/2026-10-15_god-ruling-domains.md`), in the table's order. They are not written; the harness fills them from the table and the fixtures hold every catalyst edge to it. The app renders each as a door.
  - The **metric** is the key of the bar the gift draws on most, one of the function's three (Body: independence, competitiveness, solo_team · Mind: intuition, stability, instinct_research · Expression: warmth, creativity, reserved_expressive · Action: ambition, discipline, focused_multitasking · Order: leadership, responsibility, flexible_structured). The app renders its chart level as five dots in the head; the words never name a level.

- Checks: the shape (one lead, the three domains equal to the table's row in order, one metric); the lead two sentences of thirty to forty-five words, the first on the fixed frame with the three domain words in order, the second opening on It is with three clauses; the vocabulary zone on every word; no report-card register, no virtue list ("reliable, gifted, natural"), no capacity opener; no prescription of an investment or a medical act and no field of work as an instruction; no number, band or state of a chart; no arena imagery; no four-word run shared with the cell's chips, ledger rows, pair definition or advice; no "will never" guarantee; no phrase repeated across the core's cells; the sibling test by eye.
