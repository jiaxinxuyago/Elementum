Every chapter is an energy

“I want keep the energy as the subject for each life chapter. Instead of using Bazi characters and others, unravel the advise by saying what energy the life chapter carries, and then dive into each domain’s, energy manual’s advise after that.”

This round replaces round 1, which read each chapter through its characters. A chapter now opens on the energy its gate carries, using the same reader words as Your Almanac: the element, its function, whether your chart grows or releases it, and the lift. The domains and the Energy Manual’s advice follow from that energy. Every proposed line passes a new gate, and the sample texts use the real elements of each chart.

How a chapter reads

  1. The energy. What the chapter carries, its function, and whether your chart grows it or releases it. The lift meter shows the result.
  2. Underneath. The ground energy and how it meets the top one, in the Almanac’s five ground lines.
  3. Where it lands. The eight-domain board, then a line for the gate’s own domain and the two weightiest, each saying what the chapter favours.
  4. Working with it (the chapter you are in now). The Energy Manual’s part: one line on how the chapter’s energy works for you, then the energy to seek and the decade that brings it.
  5. Decades. Each decade is an energy too: one line each, and the current decade in full with its ground and what it favours.
  6. Years (Advisor). The year’s energy and where it lands, the same lines Your Almanac uses.
