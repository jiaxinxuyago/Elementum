- **The keys are your keys:**
  - `ENERGY <fn> · year.<state>`: 14 states, the Almanac's format ("{el} brings <Function> for a whole year, …"). The `need`, `grow` and `heavy` states for Mind, Expression, Action and Order; `core_under` and `core_over` for Body. Your `ENERGY order · year.need` is kept word for word.
  - `ENERGY <fn> · year.<state>.b`: 14 more. Years come in pairs of one element, so Life Chapters reads the `.b` line for the second year of a pair. A single Year page can ignore them.
  - `TEMPLATED tpl_almanac · land.year.<bind|clash|punish|harm|frame|repeat>.<year|month|day|hour>`: all 24 year landings.
  - `{el}` is filled with the year's element.
