# Your Almanac · one action rule per energy (draft for PM_04 ALM-AR1)

The 5 November priority list, row #10 (`BIZ_07` §7), asks for "one action rule per energy", so that the Almanac tells the reader how to use the period's energy (pillar 3, PL2 A) and doesn't narrate what will happen. This draft covers the five functions × grow or release, plus one even line. Nothing here is live. Placement and wording wait on ALM-AR1.

| Energy | Grow (↑) | Release (↓) |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Use your Body on the load only you can carry. | Use your Body to hand one load to someone else. |
| Mind | Use your Mind on one thing worth learning properly. | Use your Mind to decide once, then stop reviewing. |
| Expression | Use your Expression to finish one thing and show it. | Use your Expression on one thing said well, and hold the rest. |
| Action | Use your Action on the task that pays you back. | Use your Action to finish one task before you take another. |
| Order | Use your Order to set one deadline and keep it. | Use your Order on the rules that matter, and let the rest go. |
| Even (any) | Use your {Energy} at your usual pace. | |

Proposed placement (recommended): one line under the energy card on the Day, Month and Year pages, labelled "Use it", above the ground line. The reminder body would use the same line in place of "Read your day." once the reminders are on. The layout belongs to the design director.

Once ruled, the lines go into the station as `TEMPLATED tpl_almanac · rule.<fn>.<grow|release|even>`, through a sealed writer and the gate.
