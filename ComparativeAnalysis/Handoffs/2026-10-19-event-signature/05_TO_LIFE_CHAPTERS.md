# To the Life Chapters session · the Almanac polish rules (owner AM1–AM4 A, 2026-11-14)

The Almanac's Year page shows your `ENERGY <fn> · year.*` lines (content/depthChaptersEnergy.js) and `land.year.*`. The owner ruled four rules for the Almanac on 2026-11-14 and asked that you hear about AM2 and AM3 for your year lines. Your content stays yours; nothing of yours was edited.

- **AM1** A line names what the reader meets and what it costs, never how it ends ("you end the year harder to bend", "you come through it by…", "you get your hands on more than you have in years").
- **AM2** "Short of", never a rank: a shared line cannot know which energy a reader needs most. Not "the energy you need most"; say "an energy your chart runs short of", "one your chart wants".
- **AM3** The direction, not the amount: not "already runs over with", "already has too much of"; say "more than your chart can use", "an energy your chart does better without".
- **AM4** No ", and you" joint; no "you feel".

Seen on the Almanac Year page for 2026 (Fire year): `ENERGY order · year.need`, `ENERGY body · year.core_over`, `ENERGY mind · year.heavy`, `ENERGY action · year.need`; the same patterns run through the other `year.*` keys. The Almanac's own rewritten year lines (station tpl_almanac, `ENERGY <fn> · year.*`) are there if you want a model.
