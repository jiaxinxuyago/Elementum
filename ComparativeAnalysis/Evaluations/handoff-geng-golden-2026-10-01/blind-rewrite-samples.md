### 金_劫财 · The Rival · cost to others (Keeps score)

You measure yourself against the people closest to you, and the closer they are, the more precisely you measure. Closeness gives you a yardstick, so the people you love become the people you compete with. Because a fair fight needs someone keeping count, you keep it, quietly, in shared stakes and small favours. Each tally you keep adds a distance the other person feels before they can name it. Over time they learn to bring you their losses and keep their wins to themselves.

### 土_偏印 · The Alchemist · cost to others (Overprepared)

You are more comfortable getting ready than being judged on what you did. Preparing feels like progress and carries no risk of being wrong, so you stay in it longer than the work needs. The longer you stay, the more finished the thing has to be before you let it out, which means it never quite is. The people waiting on you asked for something you could already give, and each delay you can explain teaches them to stop asking. What they end up receiving is your standard, not your help.

### 水_食神 · The Artisan · cost to you (Nourishing)

Taking care of people is where you feel most like yourself. Being useful is the nearest thing you have to feeling safe, so giving comes before anything you might want. Because the giving is where the safety is, letting someone give back feels like giving the safety up, and you decline it without noticing. Each time you decline, the people around you learn that you need nothing. You end up empty among people who were taught, by you, not to notice.
