## Lens (a) · perspective

What the study says The Pattern does (§1.5 lens a). Every sentence has the reader as its subject in the second person, and the app stands beside them as the one who already knows. No third party ever looks at the reader: the cost to others is the reader's own act ("lose yourself trying to please loved ones", P2; "you will need to be honest about them with each other", T29). Close, level, impersonal; no camera, no chorus.

What the v0.7 candidates do. The ledger moved: 15 of 15 passages open on the reader as a flat claim, where the original opened 10 of 15 on a scene or a witness ("It is Thursday night", "A friend was in town", "People collaborate with you in armor", "They arrive wound tight", "Fifty a month since college", "The call everyone dreaded"). But 7 of 32 descriptive fields still hand the last sentence to other people as its subject, the same count as the original (7), and all five cost_others passages do it:

- "The people beside you get the half already leaving." (木_偏财 Far-sighted, Q25, which the owner flagged: do not end on a comparison to others)
- "The friend pays the tally without ever seeing it." (金_劫财 Keeps score, Q34)
- "People keep what you told them and a little distance from the teller." (the nature desc, Q03) and "people learn where to stand" (the presence line, Q05)

What stayed stagnant and why. The cost_others angle was re-cut on the card to "lands on what that act takes from the people in the persona's domain, stated as a fact of the reader's drive", but the writer rendered "lands on" as a last-sentence reaction shot, which is the retired outside door's landing ("lands in its last sentence on what others see or pay", the 2026-09-29 Q52 re-cut, still quoted in the fn_reading card's iteration log and in the skeleton spec's notes as the row's history). The P4 cards still carry the old exemplars with the reaction close: the self_card card's exemplar presence line "the people near you dress for it", and the yourNature_desc baseline in the pack, "People keep the clarity you hand them and stay careful around the edge it came from", so the writer matched them. The study's own fine-tuning sentence ("what it takes from the people in the persona's domain") invites a sentence about the people; the polished pass reads it as a sentence about the reader's act and its price.

The rewrite rule applied. The last sentence of every descriptive field has the reader, the reader's act or the reader's price as its subject; other people appear only as the object of the reader's act ("the people waiting pay first", "the friend pays in trust, without ever knowing a count was kept", "you are left with the truth and a little less company each time"). No sentence reports what others see, say, decide or learn.

## Lens (b) · analytical angle

What the study says The Pattern does (§1.5 lens b). A profile card analyses the origin, the drive, the tension between two drives and the cost as a need, in that order; the angle is always an analysis of the reader's interior; the passage never analyses an event, an object or a witness. The spec's sentence 2 (§1.4) has Elementum state the mechanism "from the chart's supply", the energy's volume in ordinary words.

What the v0.7 candidates do. The chain is there in the ledger, so the angle moved from "what happened" to "what you keep doing and why". But the mechanism sentence, the one sentence Elementum owns and The Pattern lacks, analyses the chart in the Blade's workshop: 8 of 15 ledger mechanism sentences and 4 of 5 definitions carry an arena noun of the core (edge, blade) or of the energy (branch, heat, flow) instead of the energy's volume.

- "Earth piles into your mind faster than the edge spends it, so the next book feels like progress." (土_偏印 One more source)
- "More Metal sits in your chart than one life spends, so surplus edge hunts for another edge." (金_劫财 Keeps score)
- "Little Water reaches your chart, so what gets out arrives sharp, shaped on the edge first." (水_伤官 Quotable); and in the definitions, "ground that keeps taking and gives no edge back", "heat the edge answers to"

What stayed stagnant and why. The card told the writer to do it. `fields/ELEMENT_GOD/ELEMENT_GOD.fn_reading.md`, the construct, step (2): "the mechanism, why the drive runs, stated flat from the chart's supply (the energy's volume in ordinary words: the shelter closing over the blade, Earth feeding the mind faster than it is spent)". The parenthetical gives two examples and the first is the arena. The card's three DRAFT exemplars reinforce it ("Earth feeds your mind faster than you spend it", "Earth piles up in your mind until the plan is safer than any attempt could be"), and the master prompt's Angle Map says "An element-by-persona cell speaks the persona's cost in the element's arena (The General's unpermissioned pressure spoken in fire imagery)" and check 5, "Imagery inside the cell's arena only", which reads as a demand for imagery rather than a fence around it. On the definitions, E7 (the chemistry word) was satisfied with the Blade's prop ("edge" in three of five definitions) rather than the energy's verb, because the register's E7 row lists "edge, blade, ore, forge" first among Metal's stems and the skeleton's spec repeats "an arena noun, its verb" with the noun first. The skeleton's spec notes for every definition field still say "a concrete-life inventory of what the function does when it runs right / overgrows" (`handoff.mjs`, and the task line in `lib.mjs` line 230), although the card was re-cut on 2026-10-01 to the family's two drives: the writer was handed the old instruction in the file it filled.

The rewrite rule applied. The mechanism sentence names the energy and its volume in ordinary words and nothing else from the material ("There is more Earth in your mind than you use", "Water is scarce in you", "You carry little Fire"); the chemistry word a definition owes is the energy's own plain verb or state (grows, pours, heat, hold, hard) and never a prop of the core; the definition names the family's two drives as one either-or, not an inventory.

## Lens (c) · language

What the study says The Pattern does (§1.5 lens c). A small abstract set (security, values, inner self, connection, conflict, pull, energy, need, please, lose yourself), plain syntax, sentences of 7 to 30 words near one length, not one object noun, the astrology kept in the title. The plainness test is "matter-of-fact" (T36) and "everybody can understand it" (T37).

What the v0.7 candidates do. Arena nouns in 16 of 32 descriptive fields (the wider set), staged objects in the P4 descs even where the card no longer asks for a picture, 26 of 104 sentences under 10 words (the tags), and 10 words the gate flags outside the zone (safest, plentiful, farther, scout, trap, neglect, hum, demotion, unrepaid).

- "The true sentence, the fixed shelf, the meal nobody asked for, the call made first, and the person is fed before they knew they were hungry." (gifts[4], an inventory of objects where the card asks for a drive and its cost)
- "Another book, another quiet evening turning it over, and the thing you were sharpening for is still uncut." (shadows[3])
- "The wish to feed has you cooking and telling the story" (the Water definition, which the owner flagged: no cooking as an Expression example)

What stayed stagnant and why. Three prompt lines. The master prompt's syntax law still says "Sentence lengths must span at least a threefold range inside a paragraph: a long winding sentence, then a short one" and "Fragments are rationed to one per door passage", which the writer honoured with a short tag at the end of most passages (the study itself keeps burstiness as Elementum's anti-machine law, item 11, and the keep list carries it, so the writer was right to obey it; the rule has to be scoped so that the short sentence is never the close). The same law says "Concrete nouns over abstractions in the mechanism base and on the Day Master page", and the pool descs are on the Day Master page, so the P4 descs became inventories of concrete nouns; the STEM.gifts chain step (2) still asks the writer to "say the same mechanism in the stem's material, as something recognisable in a week of the reader's life", and "the stem's material" is the arena by definition. The zone misses come from the register's A13 being enforced on the function page only, so the P4 descs and the advice carry no word-level check in the writer's head.

The rewrite rule applied. The vocabulary zone of §1.4 at B1 to B2 (security, need, control, comfort, closeness, pressure, please, lose yourself, hold back, give in, push, pull, trust, want, know, understand, feel); sentences of 10 to 24 words, declarative, with one sentence shorter than that only where it carries the cost; no inventory of objects in any desc; an object only inside a claim and only from the persona's domain (the Artisan's table survives once, "last at your own table"); the pool descs held to the same zone as the function page.

## Lens (d) · voice

What the study says The Pattern does (§1.5 lens d). Confident on the verdict, tentative on the cause, gentle and matter-of-fact throughout, never witty, never performing: no sentence exists for the writer's sake; the passage states the reader's drive, tension and cost and stops.

What the v0.7 candidates do. The passages perform at the close. 7 of 32 end on a tag of six words or fewer, and several more end on a turn of phrase that is the writer's, not the reader's:

- "Mostly it is the second." and "The years do not." (the card's own DRAFT exemplars, which the writer took as the target close)
- "Finding it would end the hunger." (土_偏印 One more source); "Wise, and still not started." (the Earth definition); "The edge decides which." (the Wood definition)
- "Right is not the same as welcome." (the nature desc); "and you have to be a person anyway" (gifts[6]); "Permission is what this costs you." (火_七杀 Takes charge)

What stayed stagnant and why. The engraving identity leads the master prompt: "The voice is 'the engraving that reads you': an artifact older than its reader that somehow knows them. It is never conversational. Not the cool older sister, not the therapist, not the guide." The amendment that follows ("It reads back by naming the reader's drive, its tension and its cost in plain words") is one sentence inside a paragraph whose every other sentence asks for the made thing, and the paragraph closes on "Mythic frame, mechanism precision, cost courage, in formal dress". A writer reading that paragraph first writes an engraving, and an engraving ends on a line you could carve. The fn_reading card says "no aphorism close by default" and then gives three exemplars that close on an aphorism ("Mostly it is the second", "The years do not", "They go without"). The syntax law's "Endings vary: some land on the meaning, some end on the image" licenses the image close on a descriptive field. And "Read aloud: it does not perform" (check 10) has no test attached, where the study supplies one: the therapist winding down the session (T36).

The rewrite rule applied. End on the cost in a full declarative sentence; no tag line, no image close, no line that exists to be quoted; no wit at the reader's expense; the plainness test before the close: could the therapist winding down say this sentence without embarrassment. Confidence stays on both the claim and the cause (the cause is the chart's, so it is stated flat), and the engraving's dress is the ban list, not the carved ending.

## Structure

What the study says The Pattern does (§1.3 and §1.4). One construct everywhere, so the reader learns the shape and trusts it: a flat claim, the mechanism, the either-or in the plainest words on the page, the cost as the reader's own difficulty, need or price; the summary card repeats its pages; the label above the passage carries the astrology.

What the v0.7 candidates do. The ledger follows the chain: 15 of 15 open on a claim and 15 of 15 carry an either-or by eye, though the gate sees it in only 9 of 15, because the second clause drops the reader in six of them ("or because waiting feels like a demotion"). Outside the ledger the construct did not land: the gate's D9 note fires on 26 of 44 descriptive units, among them all 10 verdict units, three of the five definitions, and six of the seven P4 descriptive units. The verdicts kept the shipping shape (a truth plus one consequence on "so" or "and"), the Earth definition kept the inventory ("Study, comfort, preparation, shelter:"), and the P4 descs kept the two-sentence portrait.

What stayed stagnant and why. Three causes. The verdict card (`ELEMENT_PAIR.cta_verdict.md`) was not touched by v0.7: it still asks for "a behavioural truth plus ONE tendency-framed consequence beat" and its exemplar has no tension, so the verdicts could not change. The skeleton's spec notes for the definitions still say "a concrete-life inventory" (above). And D9 is a read flag, never a gate, so a writer who met the ban list and the word range shipped a clean pass with 26 missing tension sentences; the round's README recorded the notes as "the joiner form is a floor the read decides on", which is correct as a gate policy and wrong as a writer's instruction.

The rewrite rule applied. Every descriptive field carries the joined either-or (both clauses the reader's own acts, joined by or, but, yet, while, or whether ... or not), including the one-sentence verdicts and the presence line; the owner's picks are kept on their chip and their first sentence and carried into the chain; where a pick is a staged scene (Q16 Brooding, Q24 Compounding, Q42 Nourishing), the drive under the scene is kept and the scene is cut.

## Nebula · the moves to stay away from

Plain prose without a cost or a mechanism drifts to the market floor, and the polish risks it more than the arena ever did, so the N1 moves are named here as the fence (owner transcription, nebula). The sun-sign address ("Today, Taurus"): never a label inside the prose, never "as a Blade". Abstract virtue nouns strung together (comfort, stability, determination, satisfaction, passions, joy, values): the plain psychological noun is allowed one at a time inside a tension, never as a list and never as the verdict. Generic pictures ("cooking a delicious meal, enjoying nature, or spending time with loved ones"): a picture that could be anyone's is worse than no picture; an object survives only inside a claim and only from the persona's domain. Hedges beside intensifiers ("You may feel a heightened sense of determination ... making it a great day"): the cause is stated flat from the chart and the verdict carries no amazing, ideal, great, exactly what you want. The closing string of imperatives ("Remember to balance ... be mindful ... Stay true to yourself"): imperatives live in the advice fields only, two or three, with objects. What keeps a plain passage on The Pattern's side of the line and off Nebula's is the cost: every one of the 54 polished fields ends on what the drive costs the reader, which N1 never does.

## The polish rules, numbered, for v0.7.1

1. The mechanism sentence of a descriptive field names the energy and its volume in ordinary words and nothing else from the material: "There is more Earth in your mind than you use", "Water is scarce in you", "You carry little Fire". No arena noun, no arena verb, no prop of the core in it.
2. No arena imagery in any descriptive field (the ledger passages, the definitions, the pool descs, the nature desc after its reception sentence, the presence line, the verdicts): no blade, edge, ore, forge, cut, mountain, steel, sword, sheath, knife, whetstone, mine, and none of the energy's props (branch, orchard, stream, flame) as metaphor. The mechanism base, the sign paragraph, the manifesto, the face, the turns and the carry lines keep their material by the ruling.
3. The chemistry word a definition owes (E7) is the energy's own plain verb or state (grows, flows, pours, holds, heat, hard, rest) and is never satisfied by a prop; the register's E7 list is read with the verbs first.
4. The definition names the family's two drives as one either-or, one clause per persona, each clause carrying its cost; the inventory is retired from the skeleton's spec notes and the task line, not only from the card.
5. Every descriptive field, the one-sentence verdicts included, carries one tension sentence: two clauses that are both the reader's own acts, in the second person, joined by or, but, yet, while, or whether ... or not. The D9 note stays a read flag in the gate and becomes a requirement in the writer's instruction and the skeleton's spec.
6. The either-or is written in the plainest words on the page, from the §1.4 zone at B1 to B2: security, need, control, comfort, closeness, pressure, please, lose yourself, hold back, give in, push, pull, trust, want, know, understand, feel. No Latinate word, no word the zone check would flag, in the tension sentence.
7. The cost is the last sentence, stated as the reader's own difficulty, need or price, in a full declarative sentence of at least eight words. No tag line, no image close, no aphorism, no fragment at the close.
8. The last sentence's subject is the reader, the reader's act or the reader's price. On the cost-to-others angle the cost is what the reader's act takes from the people in the persona's domain, still as a fact of the reader's drive; other people never appear as the subject of a sentence that reports what they see, say, decide or learn.
9. Sentences run 10 to 24 words, declarative; the burstiness law's short sentence is never the close and never a fragment; one sentence under ten words per passage at most, and only where it carries the cost.
10. No inventory of objects in a desc or a definition; an object survives only inside a claim and only from the persona's ruled domain, at a size the reader can own; nothing from the kitchen on the Expression page unless the persona rules it.
11. The owner's pick is the starting point of every field: the chip and the first sentence are kept and carried into the chain; a picked scene keeps its drive and loses its stamp, its objects and its narration.
12. The plainness test runs before the close on every descriptive field: the sentence could be said, without embarrassment, by the therapist winding down a session (T36). A sentence that fails it is cut, not rephrased.
13. The reader zone (A13) is enforced on the pool descs, the nature desc, the presence line and the advice as it is on the function page; the ten flagged words of v0.7 (safest, plentiful, farther, scout, trap, neglect, hum, demotion, unrepaid) are the negative list.
14. The advice fields keep their own shape: one plain truth sentence, two or three small actions in the band's remedy verb and the cure's register (on an Order cure, someone above you, a deadline, a judge; never a friend), the ruled beat on the friction pole, no aphorism close on the catalyst pole, the smallest action last.
15. The card exemplars are re-cut to the rules above before the next round: the fn_reading DRAFT exemplars lose their tag closes, the self_card and yourNature_desc exemplars lose their reaction closes, and the cta_verdict exemplar gains its tension.
16. The master prompt's "Who is speaking" leads with the construct sentence (the reader's drive, tension and cost in plain words) and the engraving follows as the dress; "Mythic frame, mechanism precision, cost courage, in formal dress" moves to the mechanism base and P4 paragraph where it applies.
