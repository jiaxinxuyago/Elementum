## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **The same lived-beat opener on every seat ("More than once" / "At least once").** 9 of 10 Love seats' lived beats use it (or "at least one"), and 6 Health units do. Examples: bijian_day_branch `Love.lived` "More than once a good relationship of yours turned into a contest"; qisha_day_branch `Love.lived` "At least once a relationship has worn you down". The template allows it once per page, but across the domain the "how did it know" move becomes a formula readers learn to spot.
2. **"Since your late forties / late teens" as the step-2 opener.** It appears 23 times on the Health page, and twice on one page on D005, D087, D100 and D147. Examples: qisha_month_branch `Health.lived` "Since your late teens your body has kept up…"; shishen_hour_branch `Health.lived` "Since your late forties your sleep has deepened…". On D005 the gap line uses it as well. The brief bans a repeated opener within a page.
3. **The comfort-into-excess formula in Artisan and Sage units.** It appears 11 times ("slides into" ×7, "turned into excess" ×4) across 7 units. Examples: `GOD shishen · decade_lines.Health.heavy.lived` "Comfort turned into excess in those years"; shishen_day_branch `Health.ahead` "Comfort slides into excess in the busiest of these years". Both are on D181. Different characters end up sharing one sentence.
4. **Hour units name the people after the reader without the ruled range.** 10 units: qisha_hour_branch `reading` ("the younger people around you"), shishen_hour_branch `reading`, zhengcai_hour_branch `reading`, zhengyin_hour_branch `reading`, bijian_hour_stem `Growth.ahead` ("those younger than you"), jiecai_hour_stem `Growth.ahead`, piancai_hour_branch `Wealth.ahead`, pianyin_hour_stem `Mind.ahead`, zhengyin_hour_branch `Mind.ahead`, zhengyin_hour_stem `Mind.ahead`. The units that do carry the range repeat it word for word 14 times ("children or younger relatives or someone you train"). Both habits need fixing: once per page, at the first naming.
5. **Day-Gate seat readings all close on "From your mid-thirties …".** 9 of 10 Love seat readings do. Examples: bijian_day_branch `reading` "From your mid-thirties your private life settles around one level bond"; zhengguan_day_branch `reading` "From your mid-thirties a lasting partnership steadies the rest of your life". On a Health page with two Day readings (D044, D087) the close repeats on one screen, and for readers past 47 it is also stale (M2).
6. **No age awareness in composition.** This covers M1, M2 and M3 together: 16 pages lose a step, 9 pages give the wrong tense, and 10 pages show decades at absurd or illness-adjacent ages. Examples: D054 "Your strongest window · 2049 to 2058" at 90; D185 "COMING The Home Chapter · now to 47 … From your mid-thirties". This is the main reason the Love page scores below the template despite good unit text.

## 4 · The three best lines and the three weakest

**Best**
1. "You are slow to notice how much they carry, and slower to thank them." (zhengyin_day_branch `Love.recognition`). A plain, owned cost, and the reader is the subject. The comparative escalation is what makes it screenshot-worthy.
2. "At least once you cooked and cared for someone who gave little back, and you stayed longer than the relationship deserved." (shishen_day_branch `Love.lived`). A definite past pattern without a dated event. "How did it know" without cruelty.
