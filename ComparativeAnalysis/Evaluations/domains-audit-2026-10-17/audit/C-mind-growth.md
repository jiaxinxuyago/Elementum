# Audit C · Mind and Growth · 2026-10-17

**A caveat on the sample.** All 52 readers are aged 47 to 68. The app's audience is 18 to 35, so this sample never shows the climb a young reader gets (Month lived plus Day "pattern so far" plus Day and Hour ahead). The same engine rules will produce different failures for a 25-year-old, and this sample cannot show them.

## 2. Findings

### MAJOR (a proposed rule, with counts and examples)

**M1 · C1 · Step 3 is missing on 10 of 52 pages.**
- Pages: D024, D072, D085, D149 (Mind); D004, D019, D049, D143, D155, D163 (Growth).
- Station key: none (absent). The last printed beat is e.g. `[GOD shangguan · decade_lines.Mind.heavy.lived]` on D024 and `[POSITION qisha_hour_branch · domain_climb.Growth.lived]` on D004.
- What is wrong: the page ends on the past. The "is that really going to happen to me?" step the owner wants does not exist. No "Your later gates hold no …" note prints to explain the silence either.
- Rule:
  1. Every page prints a COMING step.
  2. When no ruling decade falls in the next two and no Day or Hour ahead beat applies, the engine prints:
     - the ahead beat of each reached Hour seat (a new `domain_climb.X.ahead` written for the rest of the Late Chapter, which readers over 48 now never get);
     - failing that, the first remaining ruling decade;
     - failing that, the "Your later gates hold no X seat …" note with a decade line.
  3. The gate fails any page with zero COMING units.

**M2 · C1/C4 · The decade lines are age-blind.** Twenty-five COMING decade units on 24 pages open at age 75 or later, and 8 of them at 85 or later. Four lived decades open in early childhood but are written for adults.
- Station keys: `[GOD bijian · decade_lines.Growth.heavy.ahead]` D027 (Your heaviest window, 2046 to 2055, ages 88 to 97); `[GOD zhengyin · decade_lines.Mind.heavy.ahead]` D127 (2053 to 2062, from 90); `[GOD shangguan · decade_lines.Mind.heavy.ahead]` D160 (from 89); `[GOD bijian · decade_lines.Growth.heavy.lived]` D123 (1964 to 1973, ages 1 to 10).
- Quoted: "Comparison with peers drives you hard in these years" (from 88); "Your thoughts run hot in these years, and every rule you meet invites a quarrel" (from 89); "Stubbornness slowed you in those years, because you refused help that would have saved time" (ages 1 to 10).
- What is wrong: a line written for a working adult is printed into the reader's nineties or infancy, which breaks credibility and turns "what is coming" into an unintended reminder of mortality.
- Rule:
  1. Step 3 considers only decades that start before age 80.
  2. The strongest or heaviest window is chosen only among decades that start before 80.
  3. A decade lived that starts before 12 uses a childhood variant: `decade_lines.X.pole.lived_child`, written in the register of the Year Gate lived beats ("As a child…").
  4. Counts here: 13 Mind and 12 Growth units open at 75 or later (D009 ×2, D032, D035, D052, D060, D067, D092, D119, D122, D127, D160, D194; D010, D027, D040, D061, D086, D104, D117, D123, D125, D152, D169, D175). The lived decades opening before 10 are D123 (1), D104 (5), D010 (6) and D060 (9).

## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The decades take no account of the reader's age.** Twenty-five COMING decade units open at 75 or later, and 4 lived decade units open before age 10. The decade text is the same at 30 and at 90.
   - D027 "Comparison with peers drives you hard in these years" (from 88).
   - D123 "you refused help that would have saved time" (ages 1 to 10).
2. **Step 3 is missing.** Ten of 52 pages have no COMING step. Over 48, Hour seats lose their ahead beat and nothing replaces it.
   - D004 (two Hour seats, page ends on lived).
   - D163 (ends on the gap line).
3. **Templated lines are pasted in without checking their neighbours.** Gap lines contradict an overlapping decade on 8 pages, and pair lines contradict the lead on 5. Both units are written context-free and appended.
   - D160 gap against the Sage decade.
   - D067 "learn best alone" against "learn best inside a frame".
4. **Openers and frames repeat across a page.**
   - "Since your late forties" opens two units on 9 pages.
   - "in these/those years" appears two or more times on 16 pages, because every decade line carries it.
   - "Because you…" opens a sentence two to four times on 16 pages, because it is the mechanism slot of every recognition and reading.
   - Examples: D077 ("in these years" ×2, "Since your late forties" ×2), D125 ("Because you" ×4).
5. **Seat readings echo their own defline.** In 17 of 31 readings, the first sentence restates the defline's phrase ("working years", "later years / later life", "unusual form", "real care"). The golden pianyin_month_branch reading does it too, so the habit was learned from the template.
   - zhengyin_month_branch: "your working years are carried by…" / "Your working years rest on…".
   - bijian_hour_branch: "your later years rest on a few people…" / "Your later life runs on a small group of people…".

## 4. The three best lines and the three weakest

**Best**
1. "You are remembered for the problem you solved, and you are left out of the meeting after it." (`POSITION pianyin_month_stem · reading`) A plain cost, an owned cause, and the screenshot line, with no image.
2. "You give your partner your best ideas first, and your harshest opinions too." (`POSITION shangguan_day_branch · domain_climb.Mind.recognition`) One sentence holds the gift and its cost in the same act, for the same person.
3. "Your progress is fastest the moment someone you respect works beside you, and you put that moment off." (`POSITION bijian_month_branch · domain_climb.Growth.recognition`) A private conflict, named in the reader's own terms, with the missed chance stated outright.

**Weakest**
1. "Comparison with peers drives you hard in these years, and the standard you hold yourself to leaves little ease." (`GOD bijian · decade_lines.Growth.heavy.ahead`, as D027's heaviest window from 88) A sound line in an absurd slot: the page's peak prediction lands in the reader's nineties.
2. "Your thoughts run hot in these years, and every rule you meet invites a quarrel. Work that uses the energy keeps you clear…" (`GOD shangguan · decade_lines.Mind.heavy.ahead`) Imagery, a pronoun with no referent, and an implied instruction, repeated on 5 pages.
3. "Your generosity runs years past your written plans, and you give the people after you more stories than records." (`POSITION piancai_hour_stem · reading`) The reader cannot say what it claims; it is a closing image where a kept sentence should be.
