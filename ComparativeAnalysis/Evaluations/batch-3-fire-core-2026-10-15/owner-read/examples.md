# Batch 2 examples · the Wood core · fable-v075-fire

## 火_比肩 · friction · row 1 · chip "Goes it alone" (was "Too proud")

**drive**

- original (47w): Your flame refuses to be seen flickering. The struggle stays private, the help gets declined at the door, the apology exits in a dialect nobody recognizes as one. Being impressive is mandatory, being witnessed mid-failure is not survivable, and the policy costs you exactly what it protects.
- fable-v075-fire (40w): You go it alone by default, before anyone refuses to help. Waiting on help feels like being slowed, so you do it yourself rather than explain it to anyone. Keep in mind that you are faster alone and not lighter.

**cost to you**

- original (47w): The business wobbled for five months before anyone knew, because the version of you at dinners kept performing success on schedule. When the closure finally announced itself, friends were hurt in an unexpected direction: you had let them toast a ghost. The pride had spent their trust.
- fable-v075-fire (45w): Going it alone means the whole load sits on one back, yours. You put in extra hours rather than accept the hand, because being helped feels like being less. You pay for every hand not taken out of your own rest, and your body remembers.

**cost to others**

- original (46w): People edit their offers of help into compliments before approaching you, a translation service everyone runs and nobody acknowledges. Your worst seasons are visible only in hindsight, dated by gaps in the photo record. The support you never requested was available the whole time, warming nobody.
- fable-v075-fire (43w): Whenever a job splits two ways, you carry both ends alone. Holding it yourself matters more than keeping a peer in the loop, so you leave them nothing to carry. Remember that you keep from them half the job and half of you.

## 土_食神 · catalyst · row 1 · chip "Gives first" (was "Hearty")

**drive**

- original (44w): Your warmth arrives in generous portions, seconds included. The laugh is real and audible from the driveway, the welcome fills doorways, the food comes in servings that assume everyone is recovering from something. People leave your presence heavier and lighter at the same time.
- fable-v075-fire (45w): Giving comes to you before the question does. Being useful is how you feel you belong, so you give first rather than wait to be asked. You lose nothing by waiting for the question, since a gift asked for is the one that gets kept.

**cost to you**

- original (44w): You hugged the departing intern on their last day and they cried, then laughed, then took the container of food you had somehow prepared. It was a Tuesday. Nobody else had planned anything. Your goodbyes just come catered, and people remember them for years.
- fable-v075-fire (43w): Before the asking, you have usually already given. Because the gift comes ahead of the need, you never learn whether you were wanted or only useful. You pay in a closeness you never get to test until you let the asking come first.

**cost to others**

- original (42w): Invitations to yours get accepted at a suspiciously high rate, and everyone brings appetites they deny elsewhere. Around your table, people eat like their grandmother is watching and talk like nobody is. The recipe everyone asks for is not really the food.
- fable-v075-fire (40w): Giving first is your way of saying you noticed. Each time you give instead of waiting to be needed, you take from the people closest to you the small pride of asking. Leave them the asking along with the giving.

## 水_七杀 · catalyst · row 1 · chip "Clear-headed in crisis" (was "Cool-headed")

**drive**

- original (43w): Your temperature drops as situations heat up, which is backwards from most people and priceless in a crisis. The angrier the meeting, the clearer your sentences get. Decisions made in your coolness hold up later, when everyone rereads what the heat almost signed.
- fable-v075-fire (43w): You get clearer the harder things press, because only pressure stops your guessing. Inside a crisis you want the next move more than the right feeling. If you bring that clear head into a calm week, you keep it for the next crisis.

**cost to you**

- original (43w): Both founders were shouting by the time you spoke, and your first sentence was a number. Your second was a date. The shouting had nowhere to attach itself to numbers, and the meeting ended with a plan bearing your fingerprints and their signatures.
- fable-v075-fire (45w): Clear-headed in crisis is the self you trust. Since calm gives you nothing to push on, you chase the next crisis rather than rest in a quiet one. You pay for every skipped quiet week in sleep, and rest counts as much as a crisis.

**cost to others**

- original (43w): Opponents find your calm the most alarming thing in the fight. While they heat up, you cool down, and every degree they rise hands you another advantage. Beating you requires making you blink first. The people who have tried compare notes afterward, uselessly.
- fable-v075-fire (44w): In a crisis you take the clear head and every decision. Deciding fast feels safer than watching anyone decide slowly, so you carry the whole crisis rather than share it. Hand one piece over, because a crisis is learned by holding part of it.

## 火_金 · the pair cell

**definition, friction**

- original (35w): Metal is your Action, and as a friction, it is reworking what should be finished: renegotiating settled things, polishing shipped work, effort spent softening what was already solid. Done is a decision you keep reopening.
- fable-v075-fire (48w): Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Metal keeps it exact, and nothing you claim counts until it is finished. As a friction, you take on past your strength, and you start a new venture rather than keep the money and love already yours.

**definition, catalyst**

- original (30w): Metal is your Action, and as a catalyst, it is your heat applied to hard, valuable problems: negotiations, difficult skills, work that resists until it yields. Easy tasks waste you.
- fable-v075-fire (50w): Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Metal makes it exact, so what you take on ends finished or not at all. As a catalyst, you have more drive than work for it, and you chase the next opening more than you finish anything to keep.

**the remedy (friction advice)**

- original (38w): Finished things are load-bearing, so stop testing them. Close three open loops this week and do not reopen them. Give every task a final pass, then a lock. Aim the reworking energy at one genuinely unsolved problem instead.
- fable-v075-fire (59w): Seek Fire, the Body energy. Fire is warmth kept in: fuel, rest, the light that keeps itself lit before it works on anything. Let it hold what Metal spends. You say yes to more than you can finish, and tired is where you make your worst deals. Sleep on any new venture before you answer. Eat lunch sitting down.
