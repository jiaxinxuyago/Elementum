## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Word echo across a page, driven by fixed shared text (MINOR, not MAJOR).** Count: 31 of 35 pages carry a content word ×3 or more (only P111, P116, P107 and P167 are clean or carry joints only). Most of the echo comes from a shared field (the pair head, the remedy or the edge) meeting a keyword passage, not from one passage alone. Examples: P093 "answer" ×5 (fixed head, "Hard on yourself" close, "Hates being wrong" ×2, remedy); P120 "hold" ×5 (definition, "Keeps a confidence" ×2, "Learns it properly", edge). Each is a one-word swap (MT3-10 to MT3-19). It is not MAJOR because no rule change is needed, and C is the only criterion it costs.
2. **The shared remedy and tail repeat the pair head's own verb (MINOR, 2 cells, 6 pages).** 木_金's remedy "You answer to more rules" repeats the head's "rules you answer to" (P091 to P093). 火_金's head says "claim" twice, the Horizon tail adds a third, and the remedy's "your claims" a fourth (P097 ×4; P098 and P099 ×3). Writers of the diagnosis line should not reuse the head's key verb. MT3-10 fixes the first. The second is left, because the remedy is at the 60-word cap and has no clean one-word swap.
3. **Round-2 fixes that landed in part (MINOR, 2 fields, 3 pages).** P098 and P099 "Avoids change" took the opener of MT2-07 but kept the close and the partner reading (MT3-03). P103 "Takes it easy" took the new middle and close of MT2-09 but kept the contradicting opener (MT3-04). Both should be checked against the full approved text when they are fixed.
4. **Persona tails that restate a keyword's main act (settled, not filed).** Count: at least 4 on these pages (P101, P107, P113, and P091 "easing off feels unsafe" beside "Never off duty"). This goes against the letter of the M2 ruling ("The tail shares no main act with its own persona's keywords"). The owner's A3 ruling keeps the originals, so this is recorded only.

No Balanced definition contradicts its keywords (R2-1 landed on all five). No chart quantity is given as a cause. Every remedy's cure is classically right.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "You stopped finding errors long ago and now check for comfort." (P115, P117 · `K2 金_偏印 · friction.ledger[0].drive`). The cost and the hidden need fit in eleven plain words, and the reader can test it against their own week. This is better than The Pattern.
2. "You leave them a bruise the truth alone would not have left." (P105 · `K2 金_伤官 · friction.ledger[0].cost_others`). It separates the truth from the delivery, so the cost to others is named exactly and with no charge. It is a screenshot line.
3. "You lose a peer for each argument you win." (P111 · `PAIR 金_金 · function.definition_friction`). A whole friction is put as one even trade, at B1. Also strong: "Your own pleasures wait in an account that grows while the years go by" (P101).

**Weakest**
1. "Whenever you take it easy, you take people with you." (P103 · MT3-04). It tells a reader whose drive is "Hard to please" that they take it easy, and the next sentence contradicts it.
2. "You avoid change for yourself, and they go without any too." (P098, P099 · MT3-03). It closes on its own opener, leaves "any" to be decoded, and sits after "the person you love inside the agreed life", which reads as a partner.
3. "You raise the stakes out of wanting someone worth beating more than someone easy to keep." (P114 · MT3-08). The idea is good, but the sentence needs two reads, and it is the fifth "friend" on the page.
