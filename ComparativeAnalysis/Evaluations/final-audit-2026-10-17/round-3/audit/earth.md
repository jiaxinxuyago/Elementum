## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **A shared text contradicts a keyword on its own page (not MAJOR; 3 fields, 5 pages).** Examples: the 木_土 remedy prescribes "Begin one small project" to a reader who "would rather open a new thing than close an old one" (P061 to P063, E3-01). The 金_土 2+2 definition makes the reader slow to trust, beside a keyword about trusting a hunch (P084, E3-04). P066's picker pairs "leaves you short" with "never finds you short" (E3-02). Each needs one field changed or one clash pair added. A cheap guard: when a remedy act or a shared tail is rewritten, re-read it against all six keywords of both personas on that pole, as M2 already requires.

2. **The same content word inside one field (not MAJOR; 7 fields, 10 pages).** Examples: "claim" ×3 (P061 definition), "demand" ×3 (the 水_土 remedy, P085 to P087), "standard" ×3 (P089), "answer" ×4 (P080), "alone" ×2 (the 木_土 remedy; "Never asks" on P073 and P075), "already yours" ×2 (P087). The per-page compound check flags most of these, but nothing gates on them. A per-field check (any content word ×3, or any repeated three-word phrase ×2) would catch them before shipping.

3. **Idioms that misfire (not MAJOR; 6 pages).** "You run empty on" (P069), "lose at the same size" (P077), "protect them as your own" (P080), "a prize means more with your people inside it" (P078), "you wait on an offer waiting on you" (P086), "Your patience is what you teach with first" (P083). The first three are TWEAKs above. The last three parse on a second read, so I list them as weak passages, not findings.

4. **R2-4 leftovers (not MAJOR; 1 field on Earth).** "Your partner lives through every swing beside you" (P078, E3-03). Outside my pages, water.md has the same kind of slip in a Rival cost_others field ("a partner carries losses they never signed for"). The cross-energy auditor should sweep singular "partner".

5. **Observation only, no finding.** "feel / feels like" carries the motive on many friction pages (P068 and P081 ×5; P061, P063, P069, P075, P079 ×3 to ×4). It lives in the comparative middle sentence the owner settled. Two notes for the consolidator:

They never meet on one page.
   - **Spelling.** "judgement" (Earth ×4) sits beside "judgment" (Fire, Wood), and "practice" as a verb (P065) beside "practise" (Fire).

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**
1. "The rule you found pointless was somebody's floor." (P071, `[K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]`). It names the cost to others with no charge, in nine plain words, and the reader sees their own act from the other side.
2. "You protect a record with very few wins on it." (P074, `[K2 土_劫财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`). The Rival's pride turns against itself in one image of the reader's own making. It is sharper than The Pattern's "you may need extra time".
3. "Being reached feels like being spent, so you turn hard to reach before anything is asked." (P079, `[K2 土_偏印 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`). Owned cause, therapy-literate, and screenshot-ready. It is exactly the "how does it know" register.

(Close runners-up: "Whatever someone learns from you, they learn without shame." P084; "A worried table goes quiet soon after you sit down at it." P083; "You refuse the rest that no one else can take for you." P085.)

**Weakest three passages**
1. P066, "Shares the win" beside "Saves first": "it leaves you short … Your own cushion never gets the chance to grow" and then "A bad month never finds you short." The page contradicts itself about money, which is the domain it is about.
2. P063, "Spread too thin": "You are spread across more things than any one of them can use." It does not parse, and its close repeats the remedy's "say no" a few lines above it.
3. P080, "Same old answers": "you give the old one rather than the one asked for. You send them off with your answer in place of theirs." It is a tangle of "answer" and "one", and the reader cannot tell what was asked.
