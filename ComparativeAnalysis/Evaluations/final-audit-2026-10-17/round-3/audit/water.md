## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**
1. "You already hold most of the permission you sit waiting for." (`[K2 水_正官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive]`, P129.) A permission close in the BIZ_04 sense: it names the flaw and hands back the way out in eleven words. It is better than The Pattern.
2. "Mocking is your first language, and plain praise comes out like a foreign one." (`[K2 水_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive]`, P141.) It is exact, a little funny, and the line a therapy-literate reader would post.
3. "Keep in mind that you go cold wanting someone to come and find you." (`[K2 水_七杀 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.drive]`, P127, P129.) The reminder phrase carries the want, as the ruling says it should, and it turns a cold act into a tender one.

*Runners-up:* "Work that needed you this month goes to someone with an easier smile." (P131); "Time with you comes with a scoreboard running." (P150); "Those who love you know your habits better than your heart." (P145, P147); "You run empty long before you run quiet." (P141).

**Weakest three passages**
1. "Going first is where your boldness lives. Little Water backs you, and you jump in before reading the plan, while early mistakes come out of your savings. Your bank balance carries the lessons everyone after you gets for free." (`[K2 水_劫财 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`, P149.) The amount of Water stands in for a reason, and the middle sentence runs three clauses long (W-011).
2. "In balance, the current moves at your pace. What flows out and what comes back stay level." (`[PAIR 土_水 · function.definition_balanced]`, P173.) Two keyword passages below it on the same page deny it (W-012).
3. "You take every rule to heart faster than you take anything in." (`[PAIR 火_水 · function.advise_friction]`, P127 to P129.) It is the diagnosis every Fire-core friction reader meets on this energy, and it does not survive a first read (W-014).
