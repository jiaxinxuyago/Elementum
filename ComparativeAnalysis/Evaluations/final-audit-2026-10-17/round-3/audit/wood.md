## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **"Feel" carries the comparative middle (not MAJOR).** 19 of 35 pages use "feel/feels/felt" three or more times. Eight pages use it four or five times: P007, P008 and P009 ×5; P012, P014, P015, P020 and P027 ×4. Examples: P007 "feels unsafe … feels safer … feels more like safety … feels like loss … feels ready"; P015 "Every slip feels like … Being needed feels safer … an exception feels like … Standing firm feels safer". Most of these sit in the "X feels safer than Y" middle the owner settled, so I file only the two remedy uses that can change freely (W3-009). Suggestion for any future writing, not a fix: a definition or remedy should avoid "feel" when the ledger above it already uses it.
2. **The same joint opens two middles on one page (not MAJOR; settled frame).** This happens on 8 of 35 pages: "Since … Since" on P014, P017 and P018; "Because … Because" on P019, P022, P024, P027 and P154. Round 2 counted the same pages. W3-004, W3-005 and W3-007 remove three of them as a side effect. I filed no finding only for the joint.
3. **Every close on a single-persona friction page is a loss (not MAJOR).** This is true on 5 of the 10 single-persona friction pages: P002, P008, P019, P020 and P026. Examples: P002 "You fund next year's bet…" / "never take the rest you earned" / "you spend their say in it too"; P020 "a place that returns none of it" / "the easy days that all your counting was meant to buy" / "while you wait to trust". BIZ_04 counts a page of nothing but losses as a harshness risk. Here THE REMEDY follows straight after and gives the open door, each close is a specific act and never a verdict, and P002 and P026 are the best pages on the energy. So this is not a defect, and I filed no finding. If the owner wants the BIZ_04 three-kind mix enforced, the cheapest lever is one want or permission close per single-persona friction page.
4. **An assumed household or beloved (not MAJOR).** 3 fields remain on 3 pages: "partner" (P003), "the one you love" (P020) and "Your family never wonders…" (P024). Each is fixed locally (W3-003, W3-006, W3-007). "The one closest to you" (P023) is R2-4's own wording and I left it.
5. **Word echoes the compound check misses (not MAJOR).** The check counts words used three or more times. It misses a word used twice in one passage or sentence, and a phrase repeated between a definition's head and its tail: "piece" (P001, P003), "seldom" (P011), "you keep … you keep" (P011 edge), "what you let out" (P028) and "the rules you answer to" (P153). All of them are fixed as TWEAKs or W3-008. A gate that flags any word of four letters or more used twice inside one field would catch them.
6. **One persona drifts within a page (not MAJOR).** The Rival "would rather share the risk than own the call" (P005) beside "Goes first". The Sage "would rather shelter someone than be sheltered" (P012) beside a definition of unmet shelter. Both are single fields (W3-001, W3-002). D2 holds everywhere else on Wood: the ten personas read as the same people across both poles and the Balanced pages.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**
1. "Friends get a sharper plan and a slower yes." `[K2 木_偏印 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]` (P010, P152). The gift and its price fit in nine plain words, and a reader could check it against last week.
2. "With a show always running, you never find out how much you are liked as you are." `[K2 木_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]` (P026). It names the fear under showing off without passing judgment. This is The Pattern's "lose yourself trying to please", but sharper.
3. "You give up the easy days that all your counting was meant to buy." `[K2 木_正财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]` (P020). The cost turns the reader's own reason back on itself: what the saving was for is what the saving takes away.

**Weakest three passages**
1. "Being the safe place is how you feel most yourself, so you would rather shelter someone than be sheltered. Several friends got through their worst year under your roof." `[K2 木_正印 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[1].angles.drive]` (P012). It denies the Sage's ruled want, contradicts the definition above it and makes up a past.
2. "Because the easy choice calms you now and the right one pays later, you take calm rather than right. Count the good chances you let go by to stay comfortable, since few of them come back." `[K2 木_食神 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[2].angles.cost_self]` (P027). It is the one friction passage that ends on an order, and its middle uses adjectives as nouns.
3. "Because being generous keeps you freer than being owed, you give rather than build anything shared." `[K2 木_偏财 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]` (P022, P154). The reason runs backwards (a giver is the one owed), so the cost never lands.
