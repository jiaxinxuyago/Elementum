# Cross-energy audit · round 3 · the function pages · 2026-10-17

Auditor: the cross-page seat (SOP §5 step 3), round 3. Scope: all 175 pages in `round-3/pages/` (150 single-face and 2+2 pages, 25 Balanced pages). I followed the reading list in ../AUDITOR_BRIEF.md and ROUND3_BRIEF.md, which overrides the first brief where they differ. I also read ../RULINGS.md and the round-2 cross report, so I could check what the fixes did. I did not open the other auditors' round-3 files.

**Method.** I parsed the 175 pages from `round-3/pages.json` into 657 distinct station texts and read them across energies, not page by page:
- **Each persona** on all 5 elements and both poles: the definition tail, every ledger passage shown and the edge.
- **Each pair cell's** catalyst, friction, blend and balanced definitions side by side, with its remedy.
- **Every 2+2 page**, checked for keywords that contradict each other.
- **Every Balanced page**, checked against its own keywords.
- **Per reader:** for each core, the five pages one chart shows in three configurations. Underfueled is Body and Mind as catalyst, the rest as friction. Overfueled is the reverse. Balanced is all five balanced. That gives 243 persona sets for each core in each unbalanced configuration.
- **What changed:** a diff of the round-2 station texts against round 3. 182 texts changed, 24 are new, and 35 left the pages, mostly the catalyst advice that the app no longer shows.

I judged only what the app shows. Catalyst and Balanced pages end on THE EDGE, and friction pages end on THE REMEDY.

**Not re-filed (settled):** restatement (A3); the comparative middle sentence; the Sage's pull toward shelter; the bars and the Instinct dial; the Virtuoso's "Mind" domain; shared stakes and "raise the stakes"; the three friction Horizon+Steward pages that show 2+1 (P057, P099, P135), whose decision is pending; "A catalyst gift close may end on other people".
