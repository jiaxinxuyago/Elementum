## 4 · The best and the worst

**The three best lines**
1. "The rule you found pointless was somebody's floor." (P071, `K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others`). It names the cost to others in nine plain words, with no charge, and turns the camera round on the reader's own act. That is better than The Pattern.
2. "Stopping at the half that helps is honest too." (P069, `K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive`). This is a permission close as BIZ_04 means it. It hands back the reader's own value (honesty) instead of scolding the habit, and nobody would read it as a judgement.
3. "You trade being reached for a loneliness you only feel later." (P079 and P081, `K2 土_偏印 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self`). A flat cost, therapy-literate, and ready to screenshot.

(Close runners-up: "You wanted a place among them, and ranking felt like the quick way in." P074; "A worried table goes quiet soon after you sit down at it." P083; "People who lose to you still shake your hand." P090.)

**The three weakest passages**
1. P087, "Follows every rule": "You find fair by the book easier than kind off it." It is the only sentence on Earth that a B1 reader has to unpack, and "off it" points toward "off the books" (E5-01).
2. P063, the definition beside the remedy: "what you take on grows heavier than you can bear" … "Your load has grown past what you can bear." It is one idea in the same words, about forty lines apart on one page (E5-02).
3. P082 and P164, "Solo thinker": "Your best thinking happens alone … Thinking best out of reach, you let the solo hours run on." The middle sentence starts by saying the opener again (E5-09).
