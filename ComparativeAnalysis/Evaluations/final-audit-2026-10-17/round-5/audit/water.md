## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **A fix applied in a different wording leaves an echo with a shared field. Not MAJOR.** This happened in 4 fields on 6 pages: WA5-10 (P133 to P135), WA5-12 (P140), WA5-15 (P146) and WA5-16 (P147). Each round-4 fix cured its target, but the landed wording reused a word that the page's shared remedy already carries ("treat", "leave … before", "spill", "pile up"). The cause is in the process, not the rules: a fix to a tail or a remedy should be checked against the other fixed texts it shares a page with. Every case is a one-word swap.
2. **Small word echoes that the round-4 sweep did not reach. Not MAJOR.** There are 4 fields on 5 pages: "without asking" (P145, P147), "lands" (P142), "in the open / openly" (P147) and "You would rather" ×2 (P135). These are the last clear cases. What is left is within tolerance: "feel" ×3 to ×4, mostly inside the settled "X feels Y" middle; "know" ×4 on the Mind page P126; "flow" ×2 in the P174 definition, where no clean swap exists; and "safer than" ×2 on P123, the round-4 fix as proposed.
3. **The want-or-permission closes from the F3-01 rule are close to a formula. Not filed (owner rule).** On Water, "You are allowed to …" closes 4 passages (P122, P140, P145, P147), and a "what you want" close ends 4 (P121, P123 "All you really want"; P128 "What you want most"; P133, P135 "What you want underneath"). Each reader meets only one Water page, so no reader sees the same opener twice in this energy. Whether readers meet it across energies is a cross-audit question, as in round 4.
4. **Two chips on one 2+2 page sharing a first word. Not filed.** The W4-02 clash fix makes P147 show "Never asks" (Twin) beside "Never enough" (Rival). "Never" also opens two General chips, including the owner-approved rename "Never satisfied" (X4-05). It is a function word rather than a persona's keyword word, and the pair reads as a deliberate echo. One related case is filed: on P126 the Sage's passage uses the Alchemist's chip word "deep" (WA5-18).
5. **The persona fit of the remedies. Clean.** I re-checked each friction remedy against all six keywords it serves. The new 土_水 act ("Put aside one sum for a treat, then spend it") works both ways: the Horizon keeps before she spends, and the Steward spends what she kept. The other four acts are unchanged from round 4 and still hold. No remedy act repeats by meaning within a core.
