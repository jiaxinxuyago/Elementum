## P001 · Wood core · Wood page · friction · The Twin

**THE FUNCTION** Wood is your Body.
Your Body is the strength the whole of you stands on. Wood makes it a reach that keeps going once started. Running over, you hold your way against a peer's advice, or treat shared ground as yours alone. You keep your way and lose the people beside you.

**THE CHARACTER** Friction · Release it.
Bars: Independence · Competitiveness · Solo · Team

**KEYWORD · Asks no one** (The Twin, drive)
Asking comes last, well after the point where it would have worked. You hold more of yourself than one load needs, and you carry all of it instead of owing anyone a piece. You are allowed to owe someone a piece of the load.

**KEYWORD · Always right** (The Twin, cost_self)
Being right is a comfort you never run out of. Admitting a mistake feels like losing part of yourself, and you defend a wrong call as much as a right one. You come out of each argument right and without the lesson it held.

**KEYWORD · Keeps distance** (The Twin, cost_others)
You hold people at a distance that feels polite from your side. Being left alone matters more than being reached for, and that gives your friends no way in. You cost them, with that distance, a friend they could have reached.

**THE REMEDY**
Seek Metal, the Order energy. Metal is the line that holds, a rule you did not write and a date you cannot move. Let it shape what Wood keeps growing. You carry more push than any one thing asks for. Give one plan to someone above you and take their deadline as final. Finish the small thing first.
