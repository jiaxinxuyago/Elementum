## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Balanced pages diagnose a shortfall under a balance definition (MAJOR, W2-001).** This happens on 4 of the 5 Wood Balanced pages and on at least 15 of the 25 across the corpus. Examples: P151 "what goes in keeps pace with the reach" over "Holding yourself up takes more than you are taking in"; P152 "you take in about as much as you give out" over "You run on your existing stores … until it runs out". It is the only pattern on Wood that needs a new text type or rule.
2. **Chart arithmetic as the cause (not MAJOR).** It appears in 6 Wood fields, shown 10 times on 9 pages (P013, P015, P019, P020, P021 twice, P022, P023, P024, P154). Across the corpus there are 7 fields; the seventh is on Water ("You have more Water than time to settle it"). Examples: "With more Wood than strength under it, you chase the fresh start over the finish" (P019); "You carry more Wood than stamina to match, so you trust proof over feeling" (P021), which is also a non sequitur. The rule is already in force, so this is a breach, not a new rule. W2-002 to W2-007 close it, and a gate pattern for "(more|less) [Element] than" and "than [Element] to" would stop it coming back.
3. **Assumed relations and a claimed past (not MAJOR).** This affects 6 fields on 7 pages: "Your partner" (P023), "The person beside you has never once doubted you" (P024), "a child or student" (P028, P155), "the person above you" (P014), "Promotions you deserved have gone to louder people" (P017), and "Several friends got through their worst year under your roof" (P012). Each is fixed locally (W2-008 to W2-013). The catalyst gift close seems to invite these lines: when a writer reaches for a concrete payoff, they make up an event.
4. **The blend picker still leaves opposites (not MAJOR).** 3 of the 10 Wood 2+2 pages are affected (P006, P024, P027), and P006 is 3+1. Three new antonym pairs and a rule to keep 2+2 close it (W2-016).
5. **A shared text that fits only one persona (not MAJOR).** There are 2 fields on 7 pages. The 水_木 advice's "bursts" belongs to the Virtuoso but sits on the Artisan pages (P028, P155). The 金_木 remedy calls the Body "rest" on all three Metal-core friction pages. Both are fixed in place (W2-017, W2-018).
6. **Page-level echo of openers and of one word family (not MAJOR; the comparative middle itself is settled).** On 8 pages, two middles open with the same joint: "Since … Since" on P014, P017 and P018; "Because … Because" on P015, P019, P022, P024 and P154. On 3 pages (P022, P024, P154), two passages open with "Whenever". "Safe/safer/safety" appears 4 times each on P007 and P008. Findings are filed only where an exact phrase or opener repeats (W2-020, W2-021, W2-023, W2-024, W2-026 to W2-028, W2-030, W2-036, W2-039). One note on the mechanical compound check: it counts stems such as "com", "someth", "mak" and "lett", and it misses word families. "safe/safer/safety" ×4 on P007 and P008 is not flagged.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**
1. "You fund next year's bet with the money this year needed." `[K2 木_劫财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.drive]` (P002). The cost is an act in the reader's own money, unhedged and true or false of their year. It is better than The Pattern.
2. "Never saying no is how you stay loved." `[K2 木_食神 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]` (P025, P027). The owned cause in seven words. The Pattern's P2 takes a paragraph to say as much.
3. "You win the moment by talking back and spend the week on the repairs." `[K2 木_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.drive]` (P026). The gain and the price sit in one breath, and nothing is charged against the reader. Runners-up: "Trusting takes you years, losing it an afternoon." (P021); "Wanting to be fair, you give them a page in place of yourself." (P015); "Never bending keeps you honest and keeps you tired." (P014).
