**What round 1's fixes did.** The Steward no longer sits under a chaser's definition (P056). All 40 unique catalyst passages now close on the gift, a flat price or the want; none ends on an instruction (M1 holds). **What is left.** Two new structural defects that need rules: the Balanced pages run the catalyst advice, and the 2+2 picker produced a blend with no Steward in it. The rest is local. Shared pair text still fits one persona in four fields. Five fields assume a partner or a joint purse. Three advice acts repeat inside one core. There are also a handful of grammar slips.
