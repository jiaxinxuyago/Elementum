# Cross-energy audit · round 2 · the function pages · 2026-10-17

Auditor: the cross-page seat (SOP §5 step 3), round 2. Scope: all 175 pages in `round-2/pages/` (150 single-face and 2+2 pages, plus the 25 new Balanced-chart pages P151–P175). Reading list followed: ../AUDITOR_BRIEF.md, ROUND2_BRIEF.md (it overrides the first brief where they differ), SOP, 07 benchmarks, BIZ_04 and primer §1–§3. The other auditors' round-2 files were not opened.

Method. The 175 pages were parsed from `round-2/pages.json` into 668 distinct station texts. I read each persona across its 5 elements × 2 poles (definition tail, every ledger passage shown, edge). I read each pair cell's catalyst, friction, blend and balanced definitions side by side, together with its advice and remedy. I read every 2+2 page for keywords set against each other, and every Balanced page against its own keywords and advice. **Per reader:** for each core I put together the five pages one chart shows, in three configurations: Underfueled (Body and Mind catalyst; Expression, Action and Order friction), Overfueled (the reverse) and Balanced. I tried every persona choice (3^5 = 243 sets per configuration) and counted what repeats. I diffed the round-1 station texts (`../pages/`) against round 2 to find what the fixes changed: 339 texts changed and 17 are new.

Settled items were not re-filed: restatement (A3), the comparative middle sentence, the Sage's inclination toward shelter, the bars, the Mind/Instinct dial and the Virtuoso's "Mind" domain.

## 3 · What the round-2 changes did, rule by rule

**M1 (catalyst closes).** Holds. None of the 200 catalyst passages closes on an imperative or an "If/When you …" fix. The forced-fix sentences that did not parse in round 1 (X-17 to X-21) are gone.

**M2 (definitions).** The structure holds on all 175 pages: pair head (sentences 1–2) + persona tail; one shared text on the 50 blend pages, none naming the split; one balanced text on 25 pages, none saying "as a catalyst". At the pair level D3 now passes in 25 of 25 cells. The Body and Action cells that blurred in round 1 (火_火, 水_水, 金_金, 木_土, 火_金, 金_木) separate thin from over in their heads and blend texts. Two gaps remain:
- The Horizon tails reintroduce the blur on the same Action cells (X2-06).
- The clause "the tail shares no main act with its own persona's keywords" is often not met. Every Twin friction tail restates "Never asks / carries it alone" ("every load stays yours to carry", P037; "weight you carry by yourself", P073).
