## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Balanced pages carry the catalyst's shortage advice (MAJOR, E2-01).** 3 of 5 Earth Balanced pages contradict their own definition outright, and a fourth does so mildly. Across the corpus, 25 of 25 Balanced pages read `advise_catalyst` and at least 12 open on a shortage. Examples: P161, "In balance … grow at the same pace", then "The ground under your plans is thinner than they need"; P165, "the frame holds without closing in", then "Put yourself under one."

2. **The 2+2 picker breaks in both directions (MAJOR, E2-02).** 4 of 10 Earth blend pages are affected, and 6 blend pages corpus-wide are not 2+2 at all. Examples: P063 has three Horizon keywords and one Steward; on P066, safety is "somewhere next to go" and also "the known thing".

3. **Assumed dependants, partners and bosses (not MAJOR; 6 fields, 7 pages).** "grow up" in your care (P070, P162), "savings meant for two" (P074), "a raise" (P086), "keep the one you have rather than look around" (P065), "your family does what you ask" (P085), "the people at home" (P066). Each is a one-field fix under a rule that already exists (E2-03 to E2-08).

4. **Joints and pronouns that misfire (not MAJOR; 7 pages).** "Whenever" or "Where" used as "because" (P069, P070, P072, P162); a pronoun with no antecedent (P064 and P161 "them"; P083 "teach it"); an opener that dangles (P078). Examples: "Whenever you would rather show a result than explain a plan"; "you hand them plenty one year".

5. **Shared fields collide with the passages at the word level (not MAJOR; 15 pages).** The remedy, edge or pair head repeats a content word that a keyword passage already uses: "body" (P061 to P063), "grow" (P067 to P069), "long before" (P064, P066, P161), "alone" (P082, P084, P164), "hold" (P079, P088, P165), "world" (P080), "stay" (P065). All are fixed by TWEAKs E2-24 to E2-31. A per-page word check that includes the shared fields would catch these before shipping.

6. **"feel / feels" carries the motive (observation only, no finding).** "feel" appears 4 or more times on 7 pages (P063 ×6; P068 and P081 ×5; P061, P069, P075, P079 ×4), and "feels like" 2 or 3 times on 6 pages. This lives inside the comparative middle sentence the owner settled, so I file nothing. The permission close appearing twice on one page (P069, P081) is the one place it shows; E2-10 fixes P081.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**The three best lines**
1. "The rule you found pointless was somebody's floor." (P071 `[K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]`). The reader sees her own gift from the other side in seven plain words. It names the price without a charge, which is better than anything in The Pattern's excerpts.
2. "You talk away trust that comes back slower than it left." (P068 `[K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`). The act, the cost and the asymmetry come in one sentence a reader would screenshot.
3. "You protect a record with very few wins on it." (P074 `[K2 土_劫财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`). It names the hidden logic of avoidance flat, and the reader recognises it as true of her week.

(Close behind: "Those you love come home with stories you were not part of." P081; "You trade being reached for a loneliness you only feel later." P079; "You refuse the rest that no one else can take for you." P085.)

**The three weakest passages**
1. "At home, enough is the mood you set. Whenever enough comes early for you, you settle for the people in your care rather than push for them. Those in your care grow up without the itch of always wanting more." (P070, P162, E2-03). It assumes children, "settle for the people" reads as contempt, and the second sentence does not parse.
2. "Your patience is what you teach with first. You teach it a second time instead of watching someone struggle, and take the struggle that made it theirs." (P083, E2-09). The pronouns point at the wrong nouns, and round 1 already flagged it.
3. "Where a raise is there for the asking, you wait for permission. … you wait on an offer waiting on you. You lose the raise you never ask for, year after year." (P086, E2-05). It assumes a boss, and the middle clause has to be untangled.
