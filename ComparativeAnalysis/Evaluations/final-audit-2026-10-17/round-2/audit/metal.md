## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Balanced pages speak as catalyst pages (MAJOR, MT2-01).** Count: 5 of 5 Metal Balanced pages, and all 25 in the corpus, show `advise_catalyst`, with its shortage diagnosis and its "grow" steps, under "Balanced · Keep the mix." Examples: P167 "Your drive is short" under "In balance, you claim what you can carry"; P170 "You let in less than the world has to offer" under "What passes it settles in, and you trust it enough to use it."
2. **M6 passed its gate but not the reader (MAJOR, MT2-02).** Count: 9 of 10 Metal advice and remedy texts repeat an act that the same reader meets on another energy page, in every one of the five cores. The gate's family list missed share, tell, class, cut, choose-one, attend and weekly-slot acts. Examples: strong Water gets "Share one rough idea with a group", "Tell one person about a plan you have kept quiet" and "Tell three people about the venture you have in mind"; weak Metal gets "Cut one commitment" and "Drop one project … turn down the others".
3. **The blend picker breaks 2+2 when keywords clash (MAJOR, MT2-03).** Count: 1 Metal page (P099) and 6 of 50 blend pages in the corpus, two of them with only three keywords. Examples: P099 (three Horizon, one Steward, drive angle twice); P057 (three Horizon keywords, no Steward).
4. **Assumed relationships remain (MINOR, four fields on five pages).** A partner (P098 "for two … your partner", P099 "a partner … your partner"), a child (P106, P168 "You teach a child"), and people under the reader (P093 "anyone under you"). These are local rewrites, not MAJOR (MT2-06, -07, -08, -12).
5. **Balanced definitions that the persona's own keywords contradict (MINOR, 3 of 5 Balanced pages).** The pair-level balanced text promises an act that one persona's catalyst keywords deny. Examples: P169 "still let a peer share the weight" above "you build alone rather than with help"; P167 "see it through" above "your patience runs out somewhere in the middle". These are local here (MT2-18 to -20). If other auditors find the same on their Balanced pages, it needs a rule: a balanced tail claims no act that a catalyst keyword of either persona contradicts.
6. **The element as the cause on the golden chart (MINOR, 2 fields, P110 and P111).** "With Metal running over, you live on comparison" and "Metal piles up in you, so your bets run on nerve". ## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "You stopped finding errors long ago and now check for comfort." (P115, P117 · `K2 金_偏印 · friction.ledger[0].drive`). It names the cost and the hidden need in eleven plain words, and a reader recognises it from their own week. This is better than The Pattern.
2. "A softened truth feels like a lie with manners to you" (P107 · `K2 金_伤官 · catalyst.ledger[0].drive`). The cause is given as the reader's own logic, and the phrase is quotable without leaving B1.
3. "Whenever a friend falls short of the measure, you keep the measure rather than the friend." (P112, P114, P169 · `K2 金_比肩 · catalyst.ledger[1].cost_self`). An either-or in the reader's own act, with no image needed. This is The Pattern's construct done better.

**Weakest**
1. "Underneath it, you want to be known as someone who truly listens." (P106, P108, P168 · MT2-11). The want has nothing to do with the chip or the line before it, and it appears on three pages.
2. "You avoid change so well that your partner goes without any too." (P098 · MT2-07). It sneers, it assumes a partner, and it repeats the opener.
3. "Metal piles up in you, so your bets run on nerve to spare and caution borrowed." (P111 · MT2-15). The cause is chart arithmetic, and the second half does not parse. A good close is wasted after it.
