## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **A content word ×3 or more on one page (not MAJOR).** The compound check flags it on 31 of 35 pages. Most cases are joins: the 2+2 picker sets two personas' passages side by side, or a shared pair head meets a tail. Examples are "keep" ×5 on P042 and "comfort" ×3 on P033. Where one field has an obvious swap I filed it (F4-02, F4-06 to F4-11). The rest sit inside settled middle sentences or inside the chip's own subject, such as "want" on P041 and "feel" on P045. This is the main reason C scores stay at 2 or 3. It is a polish item, not a rule gap.
2. **Settled comparative joints (not filed).** "So you", "and you", "rather than" or "more than" appears ×3 or more on 15 pages. Examples are "so you" ×5 on P042 and "and you" ×4 on P033, P036 and P060. Settled point 2 applies.
3. **The F3-01 closes landed and work (not MAJOR).** All 15 friction pages now carry a want or permission close. The new closes are strong ("Your talent is yours to enjoy with nobody watching", P032; "You are allowed to be generous in private", P038). One side effect: when a permission close names an act, it can give the remedy's act away early. I found 1 cell on 2 pages (P037 and P039, F4-05). A later writer gate could check that a permission close does not share an act with that core's remedy. One cell does not make a MAJOR.
4. **Pending head sentences (not filed, pending with the owner).** These cover 2 pair cells on 8 pages:
   - 木_火 "whatever you do shows" over "What little shows" (P034, and the shared head on P035, P036 and P156).
   - 水_火 "reaches people in time" (P058 to P060 and P160).

   They cost P034 a point on A1 and four Water pages a point on A2. They are the largest remaining defect on these pages once the owner rules.
5. **Leftover setting assumptions (not MAJOR; 1 filed).** Four fields remain:
   - "Keeping the home as it is" (P044, filed as F4-04);
   - "In meetings" (P050, kept as landed);
   - "a colleague" (P053);
   - "Under your roof" (P048).

   Only P044's changes the sense of a sentence. The others are mild settings that do not assume a partner, child or boss.
6. **Near-twin chips across personas (noted for the cross auditor, not filed).** "Gives freely" (Artisan, P034) and "Gives easily" (Horizon, P058). "Never off" (Rival, P038) and "Never off duty" (General, P049). "Holds things in" (Alchemist, P043) and "Holds back" (Steward, P056). Whether M5 means one chip word or one chip verb per persona is for the owner and the cross auditor. No single reader sees both chips of a pair on the Fire page.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**

1. "You pay for it in the body, and the body keeps a longer memory than you do." [K2 火_食神 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[2].angles.cost_self], P033. It states the cost flat, with no charge. For a therapy-literate reader it echoes "the body keeps the score" without quoting it. It is more specific and more quotable than anything in The Pattern's P2.
2. "You were buying a place at the table that was already yours." [K2 火_劫财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive], P039. It hands back the want under the flaw in eleven plain words, with no verdict at all.
3. "What you want, under it all, is to be liked with the lights off." [K2 火_伤官 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[1].angles.cost_self], P035. It names the performer's want by its opposite, and the reader would screenshot it.

- "Your talent is yours to enjoy with nobody watching." (P032, new this round)

**Weakest three passages**

1. P042, [PAIR 火_火 · function.definition_catalyst]: "Fire makes it warm, so you recover by doing. Running thin, the reserve goes first, because you keep moving instead of stopping to refill." The definition contradicts itself on how the reader recovers (F4-03).

Feeling comforted matters more to you than feeling well, and you take comfort now over health later." The opener does not say what it means, and "comfort" comes twice in one sentence. Its excellent close is wasted on a reader who has already stumbled (F4-02).
