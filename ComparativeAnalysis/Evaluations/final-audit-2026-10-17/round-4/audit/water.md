## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **F3-01 closes are becoming a formula. Not MAJOR (owner rule; passed to the cross audit).** On Water, "You are allowed to …" closes 4 passages (P122, P140, P145, P147), and "All you … want / are after …" closes 4 (P121, P123, P133, P135). Across all 175 pages, "You are allowed to" appears 20 times in 14 distinct sentences. A reader with two or three friction energies may meet the same opener on each one, which is the "formula" risk BIZ_04 itself names. Most of these closes are good. Two replacements are weaker than the cost closes they replaced: P133 (filed, W4-04) and P140 ("You are allowed to care openly about what is dear to you"; a style loss, not filed). No rule change is proposed. A cross-energy check of how often one reader meets the same permission opener would settle it.
2. **A shared remedy act that fits only one persona. Not MAJOR.** One field on 3 pages: 土_水 "Finish one thing you started before you open anything new" (W4-03). It is the same kind of problem round 3 fixed on 水_水. I checked the other four Water remedies against both personas: 木_水 "Make one choice this week" covers both the Alchemist and the Sage. 火_水 "Begin one thing … only yours" covers both the General and the Magistrate. 金_水 gives one act per persona under a shared diagnosis. 水_水's new diagnosis covers both the Twin and the Rival. By reader set (energyRoles.js: concentrated = friction on self and resource; open = output, wealth and officer), I found no act repeated by meaning. One borderline pair is left alone: 土_水 "Keep one evening a week at home" and 土_金 "Enjoy one evening of music" share the form "evening" but are different acts (rest at home, and listening without judging).
3. **Shared texts that pile up an ordinary word on a page. Not MAJOR.** About 12 pages are affected. The fixed texts (pair head, remedy, edge) reuse a word the ledger also uses. Examples: "day" ×3 (P122, P139, P141), "lands" ×2 (P136, P173), "come(s) out" ×2 in one definition (P143, P144), and "strength" ×3 (P147, P175). The TWEAKs clear the clear cases. What is left ("feel" ×3 to ×4, mostly inside the settled "X feels Y" middle) is within tolerance.
4. **2+2 pairs that nearly clash. Not MAJOR.** One is filed: "Never asks" × "Spends shared money" on P147 (W4-02). Two were checked and not filed because they can be reconciled on one reader: P126 "Goes deep" × "Soaks it up" (deliberate depth and ambient soaking are both ways of learning) and P138 "Moves early" × "Shows up daily" (exits from ventures and steadiness with people are different domains).
5. **One pole sentence that contradicts itself. Not MAJOR.** The 水_水 friction marker "Running over … no outlet / no way out" appears in 3 tails (W4-01). A one-word swap fixes it.
6. **Shared chip words across personas ("drift": Sage "Lets it drift", Artisan "Drifts along"). Not filed.** The two never meet on one chart, and the owner ruled that no Sage keyword is rebuilt. Left to the cross audit under M5.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**The three best lines**
1. "You already hold most of the permission you sit waiting for." (`[K2 水_正官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive]`, P129.) A permission close that names the flaw and hands back the way out in eleven words. It is better than The Pattern.

**The three weakest passages**
1. "All you are after is the freedom of the open road." (`[K2 水_偏财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.drive]`, P133, P135.) A stock phrase where the most insightful Horizon close used to be (W4-04).
