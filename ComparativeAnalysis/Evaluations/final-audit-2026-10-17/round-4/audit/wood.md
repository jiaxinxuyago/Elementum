## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **"Before" is Wood's signature word, and it piles up (not MAJOR).** Six pages use it four to six times: P022, P024 and P154 ×6; P010 and P152 ×5; P012 ×4. "Before being asked" and "before anyone asks" occur on 9 pages. Examples: P022 has "before anything is obvious … long before your circle … before asking … before being asked … before the sign … before anyone asks"; P010 has "before an answer … before anyone has named it … before you join in … before your help … before anyone says it". Starting early is the right idea for Wood, but the compound check does not count function words, so nothing caught this. W4-T13 and W4-T14 remove the two echoes inside edges. The rest are spread across fields that are fine one at a time.
2. **Permission closes settle on one form (not MAJOR).** "You are allowed to …" closes a passage on 6 of 15 friction pages (P001, P002, P003, P008, P013, P015). The other permissions are "You are free to …" (P014) and "… is yours to decide" (P019). No page carries two, so no page is wrong. A reader who opens all five energy pages may meet the form on several of them, which is a question for the cross auditor. W4-M6 uses a want close rather than add a seventh.
3. **A word repeated inside one passage, missed by the page count (not MAJOR).** The compound check flags words used three or more times per page, so a word used twice inside one passage or sentence slips through. Seven fields have this: "reach" (P001), "split" (P002), "carry" (P005), "try" (P009), the "…, and you stay" shape (P020), "pace" (P153) and "before … before" in one edge sentence. All of them are fixed as TWEAKs. A gate on any word of four letters or more used twice inside one field would catch them.
4. **"Feel" in the settled middle (not MAJOR; settled).** 19 of 35 pages use "feel" three or more times, and nearly all of those uses sit in the "X feels safer than Y" middle the owner kept. Examples: P014 "order feels … a written rule feels surer … a new plan feels like … a clear order feels safer"; P015 "Every slip feels like … Being needed feels safer … an exception feels like … Standing firm feels safer". I changed only the one use outside a middle (W4-T11).
5. **Gift closes on the cost-to-others angle (not MAJOR; allowed).** Four catalyst cost-to-others passages close on a gift to others: P006 "Your lead gives slower friends a path already cut", P016 "whole teams reach finish lines…", P028 "Those you teach learn without fear…" and P029 "quieter people find the nerve…". The owner allows this. Only P029's gift contradicts its own middle (W4-M5).
6. **Claims about the reader's past (not MAJOR).** One field on 3 pages remains: "because you stood in worse" (W4-M4). P018's "because everyone saw you work for it" is close to it, but it follows from the passage's own act, so I left it.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best three lines**
1. "Wanting to be fair, you give them a page in place of yourself." `[K2 木_正官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.cost_others]` (P015). The cost to others is exact and has no charge in it, and the need behind it is named in three words.
2. "You take the small treats out of their days, one price at a time." `[K2 木_正财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_others]` (P021). The habit is concrete and the cost is plain. Any reader who counts aloud will know it.
3. "You win the moment by talking back and spend the week on the repairs." `[K2 木_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.drive]` (P026). The gain and the price fit in one sentence, the reader can check it against last week, and it is quotable.

**Weakest three passages**
1. "Sitting quietly among friends is enough." `[K2 木_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]` (P026). It is a greeting-card permission, and it replaced the energy's sharpest cost line (W4-M6).
2. "Nothing outside you sets your limits." `[PAIR 木_木 · function.advise_friction]` (P001 to P003). The diagnosis reads as an affirmation, which is exactly what this audience distrusts (W4-M1).
3. "You walk away with their ease and leave them guessing what would please you." `[K2 木_七杀 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]` (P013). The idiom turns the cost into a theft and adds a hint of holding back on purpose (W4-M3).
