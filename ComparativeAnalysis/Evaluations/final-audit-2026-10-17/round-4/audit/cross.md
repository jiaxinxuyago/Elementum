# Cross-energy audit · round 4 · the function pages · 2026-10-17

Auditor: the cross-page seat (SOP §5 step 3), round 4. Scope: all 175 pages in `round-4/pages/`: 150 single-face and 2+2 pages, plus 25 Balanced pages. I followed ../AUDITOR_BRIEF.md and ROUND4_BRIEF.md, which overrides it where they differ. I also read SOP.md, RULINGS.md, the benchmarks (07), BIZ_04, the primer §1 to §3 and the round-3 cross report, so I could check what the round-3 fixes did. I did not open the other auditors' round-4 files.

**Method.** I parsed `round-4/pages.json` into its station texts and read them across energies, not page by page:
- **Each persona** on all 5 elements and both poles: the definition tail, every ledger passage shown and the edge.
- **Each pair cell's** definitions side by side: catalyst, friction, 2+2 and Balanced, with the remedy.
- **Every 2+2 page**, checked for keywords that contradict each other or the shared definition.
- **Every Balanced page**, checked against its own keywords. I also read each core's five Balanced definitions together, as one reader meets them.
- **Per reader:** for each core, the five pages one chart shows, in all three configurations. Underfueled has Body and Mind as catalyst and the other three as friction. Overfueled is the reverse. Balanced has all five balanced. That gives 243 persona sets per core in each unbalanced configuration, and one set per core for Balanced.
- **What changed:** a diff of the round-3 station texts against round 4. 127 texts changed, 7 are new on the pages and 4 left them. The picker changed its keywords on 3 pages (P033, P063, P066).

I judged only what the app shows. Catalyst and Balanced pages end on THE EDGE, and friction pages end on THE REMEDY.

**Not re-filed (settled or pending):**

- The comparative middle sentence.
