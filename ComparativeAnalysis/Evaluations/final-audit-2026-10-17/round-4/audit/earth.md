## 4 · The best and the worst

**The three best lines**
1. "The rule you found pointless was somebody's floor." (P071, `[K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.catalyst.ledger[2].angles.cost_others]`). It names the cost to others with no charge in nine plain words, and the reader sees their own act from the other side. It is better than The Pattern, which never turns the camera round like this.
2. "To you, being hard to convince feels like being hard to fool, and you make people defend a plan instead of taking it." (P069, `[K2 土_伤官 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[0].angles.cost_others]`). An owned cause the reader will recognise at once, in the either-or shape The Pattern uses at its best.
3. "You trade being reached for a loneliness you only feel later." (P079 and P081, `[K2 土_偏印 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.cost_self]`). A flat cost, therapy-literate, and ready to screenshot after its opener, "Being reached feels like being spent".

(Close runners-up: "You protect a record with very few wins on it." P074; "Year after year, the treats you promised yourself stay on the list." P065; "Whatever someone learns from you, they learn without shame." P084.)

**The three weakest passages**
1. P066, the definition and "Shares the win": "Your cushion grows slowly and never quite feels like enough." … "Your own cushion never gets the chance to grow." The page contradicts itself about money, the subject it is about. "Loyal" then adds a second contradiction of motive.
2. P074, "Spends to win": "You would rather cover the whole bill than split it, even with money you pooled with friends." After three rounds, the reader still cannot tell whose money pays and why paying is winning.
3. P063, as a whole page: "hold" five times, "feels like losing" twice, two closes opening "You leave", and "You hold onto things you cannot bring yourself to drop." It is the one page on Earth that reads as written from a short word list.
