# Cross-energy audit · the function pages · 2026-10-17

Auditor: the sixth (cross-page) seat in SOP §5 step 3. Scope: all 150 pages in `pages/` (wood, fire, earth, metal, water), read by persona, by pair cell and by chart, not page by page. Criteria: D2 persona truth, D3 pole coherence, cross-corpus sameness (what one reader meets again across the five energy pages of their own chart), the definitions and remedies/advice as a family, and the metric bars and verdicts against the text. Reading list followed: SOP, 07 benchmarks, BIZ_04, primer §1–§3 (§5–§6 of the primer were read for the function and persona names only). The other five auditors' files were not opened.

Method. The 150 pages were parsed into their 550 distinct station texts (50 definitions, 50 remedies/advice, 50 edges, 400 ledger passages). Each persona was read whole across its 5 elements × 2 poles (40 passages, 5 edges); each pair cell's two definitions and two advice fields were read side by side; every blend page was read for contradictions between the two faces. "Per chart" counts simulate what one reader sees: for each core, every combination of one page per element (6^5 = 7,776 five-page sets), reported as min / mean / max.

## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **The catalyst ledger prescribes, and prescribes the opposite of the verdict (MAJOR, X-01).** 169 of 200 catalyst closes are imperatives or "if/when you …, you …" fixes, against 3 of 200 friction closes; about 134 tell the reader to rein in the persona's own act under "Grow it." Examples: "Throw a round once, since what they lose with you is the fun." (P042); "Let someone else take charge sometimes, since always leading means never being carried." (P094). The forced fix also produces most of the corpus's unparseable sentences (X-17 to X-21).

2. **The reader's five pages run on the same frames (MAJOR, X-05).** The edge frame appears on 50 of 50 edges, the remedy frame on 25 of 25, a comparative middle sentence in 297 of 400 passages, the gerund-chip opener in 113 of 400, and 17 to 24 comparative joints per chart on average. Examples: "Wood runs through your family, your growth and your social life. It is the calm of …, the pace …, and the friend …" (P004); "Seek Water, the Mind energy. … Let it hold what Fire burns." (木_火, 水_火, 金_火, word for word).

3. **Sleep, sitting down and lost rest stand in for every remedy and every cost (MAJOR, X-06).** Sleep appears in 22 of 50 advice/remedy texts (5 of 5 cells for the Wood and Water cores), sitting in 14 of 50, and about 28 ledger closes price the cost in lost rest. Examples: "Sleep on every yes before you give it." (木_土); "You pay for getting there first in sleep you never get back." (P085).

4. **The two poles of a pair cell blur in a quarter of the cells (MAJOR, X-02).** 6 cells fail and 4 are partial, all of them Body or Action, where thin and over produce the same acts. Examples: 水_水 ("standing alone and matching a rival" / "keep your own company … and race people"); 火_金 ("chase the next opening" / "start a new venture").

5. **Blend pages contradict themselves (MAJOR, X-03).** All 10 Horizon+Steward blends and 2 others (12 of 50 blend pages). Examples: P021 "what you chase hardest" + "you stay in a love the way you stay anywhere"; P102 "starting fast" + "Finishing things settles you more than starting them".

6. **Personas borrow each other's chips and motives (MINOR, except the Sage, which is MAJOR under X-04).** 9 chips are shared across personas, with 2 collisions inside one chart. Most borrowing runs one way: Artisan → Steward, General → Twin, Sage → Alchemist. Examples: "Finishes things" (Artisan P106 / Steward P101); "Plays it safe" twice in a Fire-core chart (P128, P098).
