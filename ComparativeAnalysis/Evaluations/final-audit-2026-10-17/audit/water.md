## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Catalyst closes are advice. (MAJOR, W-001.)** 38 of 40 unique catalyst passages, and 0 of 40 friction passages. Examples: "Push sometimes, because being patient is also the hand you kept back" (水_正印 cat [2], P125); "When you read the plan first every third time, you hold on to more of your money" (水_劫财 cat [1], P149). This is the main reason catalyst B (3.00) trails friction B (4.27).

2. **The pair definition and the advice or remedy contradict a face. (MAJOR, W-002.)** 10 pages, 4 of 5 pair cells (木_水 cat, 土_水 fr, 土_水 cat, 金_水 cat). Examples: 金_水 cat "You keep your kindness and your wit waiting past their moment" against "Giving comes to you before anyone has asked" (P142); 土_水 cat "your Action is slow to begin" against "Your strongest moment is the start" (P136). Only 水_水 is clean on both poles.

3. **The comparative joint is saturated. (MAJOR as a rule, W-003.)** "Rather" ≥2 on 23 pages, ≥4 on 5; "ahead of" ×3 to ×4 on 4 pages. Examples: P129 ("rather" ×5); P149 ("ahead of" ×4).

4. **One cause frame, one opener frame. (Not MAJOR; watch it.)** Sentence 2 of most passages is "[A] feels/comes/suits [comparative] than [B], so/and you [act]". "Feels" is the verb in about 20 of 80 unique passages, and the same shape with "comes easier", "matters more", "suits you better" or "bothers you more" covers most of the rest. Sentence 1 is often "[chip gerund] is how / is where / keeps you…": "Keeping it in keeps it from being judged", "Picking the moment keeps you from wasting yourself", "Being hard to reach is where you rest", "Going first is where your boldness lives". Each instance is good. Read across a page, the frame shows: P133 and P135 carry "feels" ×4, including the remedy's "stopping feels like losing". The fix belongs in the W-003 rule: vary the cause verb within a cell.

5. **Water volume stands in for the owned cause. (Not MAJOR.)** In about 12 of 80 unique passages the "why" sentence is a quantity of Water, not the reader's own need: "More Water moves through you than you keep, so you pay for everyone's good time" (水_劫财 fr [1], P146); "You hold more Water than you show" (水_比肩 fr [0], P145). The ledger may speak of the energy (primer §3). But when the volume replaces the need, the cause reads as fate, not as owned (BIZ_04, "owned cause"). The pattern is concentrated in the 水_水 cell (5 of its 6 friction passages). Proposal: at most one volume sentence per page, and always paired with the need.

6. **Cramming at the cap garbles catalyst closes in 火_水. (Not MAJOR; local to two cells.)** The five garbled passages (W-010 to W-012, W-014, W-015) all run 45 words and end on a reassurance clause bolted on after a comma: "…and one named strength costs no trust"; "…and one running ahead takes nothing from you". The same cells' friction passages run 36 to 45 words and read cleanly. The W-001 re-cut removes the need for the bolted-on clause.
