## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Catalyst passages end on advice.** This is **MAJOR** (W-001). Of the 40 catalyst closes, 36 end on advice: 20 imperatives, 9 conditional fixes and 7 fix tails. Examples: P023, all three closes ("Stay patient …", "Spend one …", "Say it once …"); P006, "Wait one beat, because …" followed by "you can let one end". The friction pole has 0 of 40, which is why friction pages score B 3.93 and catalyst pages 3.20.
2. **One cause frame across the energy.** This is **MAJOR** (W-002). In 60 of 80 passages the second sentence gives the cause as a comparison: "rather than" ×21, "ahead of" ×16, "instead of" ×15, "feels like" ×13, "feels safer" ×8. Examples: P014 has the frame in three of three passages; P016 has "rather than / rather than / instead of" in its three choice clauses. As a result, 25 of 30 pages score C ≤ 3.
3. **Rest and sleep are the default cost.** This is not Major. Nine passages name rest, sleep or tiredness as the price: 木_劫财 F and C cost_self, 木_正印 C cost_self, 木_七杀 F cost_self, 木_偏财 F and C cost_self, 木_食神 F cost_self, 木_伤官 F cost_others, and 木_七杀 C cost_others. Three pair fields prescribe sleep or a nap. Examples: "You trade your sleep for that open job and never get the hours back" (P013); "You lose sleep to each yes" (P025). A reader with Wood across several pages meets the same price again and again; the polish round should vary it (time, chances, closeness, credit).
4. **The diagnosis in the pair field restates the definition.** This is not Major. It happens in 5 of 10 remedy and advice fields, and in one of them the diagnosis fits only one of the two gods. Examples: 金_木 remedy "You start more than your body has strength to finish" under "You wear yourself out before your plans are finished"; 火_木 advice "You give out faster than you take in" under "the intake falls behind the output". Fixes: W-024 and W-027 to W-029.
5. **Abstract quantity sentences.** This is not Major. Ten passages and two remedies explain the mechanism as a quantity: "You hold more of yourself than one load needs", "More drive sits in you than one plan can use", "With more Wood than strength under it", "You have more push than Wood to put it into", "Carrying less Wood than drive to spend". These are the hardest sentences on their pages and the least like The Pattern, which never shows the machinery. Keep at most one per page, and only where the definition does not already say it.
6. **The "spend their X along with your own" close for cost to others.** This is not Major. It closes 5 passages: "You risk their savings along with your own", "You spend your partner's years along with your own", "You spend their rest along with yours", "You spend their quiet months as well as your own", "you spend their say in it too". Each one alone is good, and the first two are among the strongest lines. Together they make a formula, and a reader with two Wood pages will see it twice.

Also seen, below pattern level: on three cells the catalyst cost passage repeats the friction one (木_比肩 "Does it alone" / "Asks no one"; 木_劫财 "Loves a race" / "Never stops racing", both paying in rest; 木_正官 "Holds the line" / "By the book", "the rule in place of a second chance" / "the rule rather than a hearing"). D3 asks the poles to differ in direction. This is flagged for the cross-energy auditor.
