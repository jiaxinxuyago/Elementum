## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **The pole says one thing and the keywords another (MAJOR, MT-01).** Pair definitions and advices describe thin Metal through one god's behaviour, and the other god's catalyst chips say the opposite. Count: 11 of 15 catalyst pages, plus 2 friction pages (火_金 with The Steward). Examples: P118, "As a catalyst, the test gets skipped" above "You test everything before you let it in"; P101, "you chase the next opening more than you finish anything to keep" above "Finishing things settles you more than starting them".
2. **One centre-sentence shape (MAJOR, MT-02).** The centre sentence is "[cause], and/so you A rather than / ahead of / instead of B". Count: 64 of 80 unique passages; "rather than" ×2 or more on 20 of 30 pages; "ahead of" used 13 times as a disguised "rather than". Examples: P112, six "rather than"; P111, "Your bets are sized to the thrill ahead of the margin".
3. **The catalyst close is an instruction, and usually a brake (MAJOR, MT-03).** Count: 23 of 40 catalyst passages close on an imperative plus "since/because"; about 17 of them tell the reader to do less under "Grow it."; 4 pages repeat THE ADVICE. Examples: P094, "Let someone else take charge sometimes, since always leading means never being carried" (under an advice telling the reader to take charge); P112, "Let one favour into the making" next to "Let one peer carry a job you could do yourself".
4. **The remedy's diagnosis repeats the page (MINOR).** In 3 of 5 friction remedies (木_金, 金_金, 水_金; 6 pages), the remedy's fourth sentence restates the definition close or a drive passage. Examples: P091, "The hardest judge you answer to lives in your head" after "the one judge you never satisfy" and "no judge harder than yourself"; P115, "You keep testing what already passed" after "the test never ends" and "You are never done checking".
5. **The chip as the subject of the opener (MINOR).** 33 of 80 unique passages open by turning the chip into a gerund subject with a frame verb ("Being X is…", "X-ing comes…", "X is how you…" ×6, "X is the only way you know" ×2). Two or three passages on one page then open alike. Examples: P093, "Being hard on yourself is a rule…" and "Hating being wrong is the part of you…"; P096, "Taking charge comes to you…" and "Doing it properly is the only way you know."
6. **The element as the cause on the golden chart (MINOR).** In the 比劫 friction mechanisms, Metal itself is the reason: "There is Metal to spare in you", "With Metal running over", "Metal piles up in you". These are 3 passages on all 3 golden pages (P109 to P111), where D10 wants the reader's own need. Examples: MT-06 and MT-08. The fourth golden passage, 金_劫财 drive "Keeps score" ("With Metal running over, you live on comparison more than company"), is acceptable because the need (comparison) follows in the same clause.
