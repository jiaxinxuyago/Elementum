The BaZi frame holds on all 30 pages. Fire is Expression for Wood, Body for Fire, Mind for Earth, Order for Metal and Action for Water. Most A1 defects are therefore not in the theory. They come from pair-level text written for one of the two gods and laid over the other (finding F-01).

## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Pair-level text fitted to one god (MAJOR, F-01).** 8 pair fields on 4 of 5 cells. They put a contradiction directly under or above the ledger on 9 of 30 pages. Examples: P056, a saver's ledger under "you chase a new opening rather than keep what you have"; P058, "Starts fast" between "the start comes late" and "You feel safer planning a thing than starting it".
2. **Catalyst passages close on advice (MAJOR, F-02).** 39 of 40 unique catalyst passages end on an imperative (29) or an "If/When you…, you…" reward (10), and 24 of those use "…, because/since…". Examples: "Ask first, since you leave them a bet twice the size they chose."; "Allow the book one exception, because you give up chances that cannot wait." With the advice box, a catalyst page gives 8 to 10 instructions.
3. **One sentence-2 frame per passage (MAJOR, F-03).** 72 of 80 passages use the comparative "X more than Y, so you A rather than/ahead of B". "Ahead of" appears 23 times, often meaning "instead of". Examples: "you give ahead of being asked"; "you ask ahead of trying".
4. **The chip word drums (not MAJOR).** In 17 of 80 passages the chip's own word appears 3 to 5 times inside about 40 words (chip words are exempt from C, but the passage still drags). Examples: "By the book" (book ×4, P053); "Afraid to slip" blend (slip ×4, P051); "Looks after people" blend (look after ×3, P048); "Self-starting" (start ×4, P040). Fix in the polish round: at most twice per passage.
5. **The cost-to-others close leans on "take/keep … from them" (not MAJOR).** 10 of 26 cost-to-others passages use it. Examples: "you take from them the chance to stand beside you through it" (P043); "take from them the ease of being wrong" (P051). It never repeats on one page, but across the five energy pages a reader will hear the formula. Vary it in the polish pass with flat acts ("You load them with more than they wanted").
6. **Two chips of one god pull in opposite directions on the same page (not MAJOR).** This happens on 4 pages: the Artisan both "never rests" and "drifts" (P031, P033); the Sage both "protects too much" and "leans on others" (P044, P045). Fixed by F-16 and F-23; the cross-energy auditor should check whether the same split shows on other elements.

**Best three lines**

1. "You were buying a place at the table that was already yours." [K2 火_劫财 · fn_reading.friction.ledger[1].angles.drive], P039. It names the want under the flaw with no charge at all, and it is the most screenshot-ready close on the 30 pages.
