## 1 · The door rule and "no places"

**What the card says** (your wording): "no clock times, no places, no narration, no detail that does not sharpen the conclusion."

**What the gate actually blocks:** a clock time ("at eleven at night", "9:04", "o'clock"), "it is Thursday night", a season as the anchor ("in winter"), and a street name ("on Maple Street"). It does not block a kitchen, a desk, a table, a house, or a street without a name. So the card is stricter than the gate.

**Lines with a place that pass the gate today, and read as good pictures:**

- "People park their trust with you long-term, the way families keep valuables at one grandmother's house." (土_正印, outside)
- "There is a shelf of your containers in half the kitchens on the street, waiting to be returned, in no hurry." (土_食神, outside)
- "They arrive wound tight, sit down at your kitchen table, and something loosens over the second hour." (the original Water scene you saw at Q42)

**Lines with a place stamp that fail, and should:**

- "You keep three ventures alive across two cities" (the Q25 boast; the two cities add nothing the trait needs)
- "On paper it is three businesses in two cities." (its scene door)

**Proposed fix:** the card reads "no place stamps" instead of "no places": a named city, street, shop or venue used as the anchor of an act is out; an ordinary place that is itself the picture (a kitchen, a grandmother's house) stays. This matches what the gate does and what you called "not a big deal".

## 2 · The opener and repeated wording

**The turn under the new law:** "With too much Earth, the shelter closes over the blade: so much preparation that the edge never leaves the mine. Comfort begins to bury what it formed. Dig out and cut something real."

**Where "too much" would repeat on one page** (DRAFT of what a writer could produce under v0.6 with no window rule): the turn opens "With too much Earth," and the advice's truth sentence two fields later opens "Too much preparation is fear with a reading list." Two "too much" in the reader's path within a screen.

**What the current laws catch and miss.** The four-gram law catches a four-word run shared by two fields. It does not catch a two-word formula phrase repeated. Nothing today checks a window of three sentences.

**A caution before making it a blanket rule.** The corpus repeats phrases on purpose, and those lines are among its best:

- "Friends plan around you the way builders plan around bedrock" (戊 gift)
- "Months of fine, fine, fine, and then one Tuesday the ground moves" (土_伤官 trait)
- "Things get started, and things get finished, in that order more often than not." (甲 balanced presence)

A rule that bans any repeated phrase within three sentences kills these. The repeat you object to is a formula word, not a figure.

**Proposed rule (v0.6.1, app-wide):** a formula phrase (too much, too little, running over, running short, running thin, Seek, the energy that) appears at most once per field and never twice within three consecutive sentences across adjacent fields of one page. Deliberate anaphora of content words stays lawful. Harness: a note on the page skeleton listing formula phrases that occur twice within a three-sentence window; blocking if you rule it so.

## 5 · Chips: a guided selection

**Your objection is right, twice.** "Steady under pressure" and "Crisis performer" are both things an admirer would say. "No dressed-up answers" is a scolding wearing a gift's clothes. The card's examples contradict its own criterion.

**The criterion, sharpened.** A virtue is an evaluative word about the person (steady, calm, gifted, at ease): it could go on a CV. A symptom is an act the reader catches themselves at, said without praise or blame: it could be said with a wince or a grin. Gifts are symptoms that pay; shadows are symptoms that cost. A negation ("No filter", "No dressed-up answers") reads as a verdict, not a symptom, and belongs to the shadow side if anywhere.

**Candidate sets for the three golden gifts, for your pick** (the current chip first):

| Door | Current | Candidates |
|---|---|---|
| Order (七杀) | Crisis performer | Calmest when it breaks · Decides while others freeze · First to move when it breaks · Steady under pressure (virtue, for comparison) |
| Expression (食神/伤官) | No dressed-up answers | Says the plain thing · Answers straight · Words come easy · Speaks with ease (virtue, for comparison) |
| Action (正财/偏财) | Plays the long game | Cuts so it can grow · Plants and prunes · Builds to last · Lets nothing sprawl |

**Station chips that read as virtues today** (the harness note would flag these): Unshakeable, Unfazed, Inspired, Decisive, Self-made, Deep-rooted. And chips that read as verdicts: Toxic, Bossy, Fickle, Stuck. A guided pass over the 231 chips is a Stage 3 batch of its own; the examples in the card are what to fix now.

**Proposed card examples, replacing the ones you objected to:** "Calmest when it breaks" passes, "Steady under pressure" fails (a virtue); "Says the plain thing" passes, "Speaks with ease" fails (a virtue) and "No dressed-up answers" fails (a verdict); "Chases every idea" passes, "Too many fires" fails (a bare image).

## 7 · The chemistry word: where it applies and what it filters

**Which fields.** Three only: the two definitions (`definition_catalyst`, `definition_friction`: "Water is your Expression, and as a catalyst, it is …") and the mechanism base (the sourced image of the pair). Never the doors, chips, advice, verdict or Day Master fields. The definition is the one field whose subject is the energy itself, which is why the rule lives there: "Water is your Expression, and as a catalyst, it is output that flows" keeps water in the sentence; "it is the part of your effort that reaches another person" is a true sentence about a person with no water in it.

**What the rule filters today, and whether it should.** 25 of 50 shipping definitions fail. Read five of them:

- 土_水 catalyst: "Water is your Action, and as a catalyst, it is moving value passing through your steady hands: money, opportunities, people in motion who need somewhere reliable to land." (moving, in motion, land: water and earth in verbs the list lacks)
- 木_水 catalyst: "Water is your Mind, and as a catalyst, it is how you refill: quiet intake, reading, long conversations, time alone that lets impressions settle into understanding." (refill, settle)
- 火_木 friction: "Wood is your Mind, and as a friction, it is more fuel than one flame can use: courses, books, mentors, plans, all of it feeding a fire that burns hotter, not longer." (wood as fuel, seen from the Fire core)
- 火_水 friction: "Water is your Order, and as a friction, it is caution soaking through everything: every plan reviewed until the moment passes, every spark second-guessed." (soaking)
- 水_木 friction: "Wood is your Expression, and as a friction, it is pouring into everything that stands still: every person developed, every idea watered, your depth spread thin." (watered, pouring)

Every one of these carries the chemistry. The list misses them because it has "soak" and not "soaking", "flow" and not "refill", and only the energy's element, never the core's. Measured: with word stems and either element of the pair, 3 of 50 fail, all three the self pair's Body definitions ("sleep, food, recovery, the physical base"), which speak of the body as the material and should be exempt or given body words.

**Proposed fix:** the list becomes stems (soak-, pour-, water-, refill, settle, moving, fuel, feed-, spark, hold-, ground-, grow-, build-, cut-, polish-, temper-, rigor), a definition may carry either element of the pair's chemistry, and the self pair's Body definitions accept the body's own material words. Same rule, right list.

## 8 · The gate, in plain words, on one page

The gate is the automatic checker. Before anyone reads a generated field, it reads it and reports every rule the field breaks, naming the rule and the ruling. A field with a blocking finding goes back to the writer; a field with only notes goes to the read with the notes attached. It is the reason the owner's read is bounded: only fields that pass every hard rule reach the deck.
