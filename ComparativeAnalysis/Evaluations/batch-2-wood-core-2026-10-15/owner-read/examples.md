# Batch 2 examples · the Wood core · fable-v075

## 木_比肩 · friction · row 1 · chip "Asks no one" (was "Standoffish")

**drive**

- original (46w): Your default distance reads as a fence with no gate signage. You join late, leave early, and hold conversations at arm’s length even when your arm wanted otherwise. People conclude you prefer it this way. Some days they are wrong, and the fence cannot say so.
- fable-v075 (43w): Asking comes last, well after the point where it would have worked. You hold more of yourself than one load needs, and you carry all of it instead of owing anyone a piece. Keep in mind that not asking spares everyone except you.

**cost to you**

- original (42w): At the reunion you spent forty minutes near the drinks table in a pleasant holding pattern, waiting to be approached, while three people across the lawn did the same thing, waiting for you. Everyone drove home slightly lonely. The photos look fine.
- fable-v075 (45w): Your hands are all you trust with hard jobs. Asking feels like admitting a shortfall, and you take the long way alone ahead of the short way with help. You pay for asking no one in rest you skip, and the bill is only yours.

**cost to others**

- original (45w): New people get warned that you take a while, delivered as advice, functioning as a moat. By the time anyone crosses it, they find someone warmer than advertised and mildly puzzled about the moat’s reputation. You built it in increments. It reads as architecture now.
- fable-v075 (42w): Whenever an offer comes, you have already finished alone. Owing nothing counts for more than being close, and that keeps your nearest people outside every struggle. By asking no one you leave them no way of being needed, a loss worth noticing.

## 火_食神 · catalyst · row 1 · chip "Gives freely" (was "Radiant")

**drive**

- original (47w): Your enjoyment broadcasts. The good meal, the small win, the Tuesday sunset: whatever you savor gets amplified through you and lands on everyone nearby, brightening the whole table by contact. Joy in your hands is not private property. It is a utility, and you run the grid.
- fable-v075 (40w): Giving is the easiest thing you do. You would rather be useful than be thanked, and you give ahead of being asked. Keep giving this freely, and notice its one price, the flat hour when nothing is needed from you.

**cost to you**

- original (46w): You liked the street musician, so the whole group stopped, and because the group stopped, a crowd formed, and because you clapped like that, others did, and the musician had her best hour of the year. She thanked you specifically. You had simply enjoyed her loudly.
- fable-v075 (41w): Something to give away is where you are most at ease. Giving comes before wanting, and you would sooner give one more thing than name what you need. Take a turn too, because your enjoyment is the gift you keep skipping.

**cost to others**

- original (47w): Event planners and grandmothers agree on you: seat that one centrally. Your delight is the yeast of gatherings, invisible in the recipe and responsible for the rise. Parties you miss get described afterward with a slight shrug: it was nice. Nice is the word for your absence.
- fable-v075 (42w): Whenever the people around you need something, you have given it before they ask. Providing feels safer than receiving, so you hand out more than you let in. Let them give back sometimes, because you have been taking that chance from them.

## 水_偏印 · friction · row 1 · chip "Thinks too much" (was "Submerged")

**drive**

- original (44w): You live below your own surface. The real opinions, the real feelings, the real plans sit in deep water while a calm, pleasant version of you handles the daylight. People know the surface fluently. The rest of you has not taken visitors in years.
- fable-v075 (43w): You think a decision over long after it stopped needing thought. Water comes into you faster than you spend it, and one more angle feels like care rather than delay. You know by now that you only learn from the choices you make.

**cost to you**

- original (44w): At your own birthday dinner, surrounded by people who love you, you had the familiar sensation of attending remotely. The laughing went fine. The thank-you speech went fine. On the drive home the quiet person in the deep finally exhaled, alone again, at last.
- fable-v075 (45w): Thinking too much is your way of not being wrong. A wrong call scares you more than a late one, so you think a choice over past its day. Keep in mind that you lose chances this way without a sound, and sleep with them.

**cost to others**

- original (40w): The people closest to you describe a wall of perfect friendliness with something unreadable behind the glass. They stopped diving for you after enough attempts came back empty-handed. Now everyone treads water together politely, and calls it knowing each other.
- fable-v075 (44w): Whenever someone needs an answer, you think it over once more. Getting it right matters more to you than getting it to them, and you hand over the wait. It is worth remembering that you give more by replying now than by replying right.

## 木_土 · the pair cell

**definition, friction**

- original (38w): Earth is your Action, and as a friction, it is holding past your means: too many commitments owned, maintained, and defended at once, each taxing energy you no longer have spare. Everything you keep charges rent on you.
- fable-v075 (47w): Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Earth gives it weight, so whatever you claim needs keeping. As a friction, providing outgrows your strength, and you take on another venture while you guard every saving. Over time you carry more than you finish.

**definition, catalyst**

- original (31w): Earth is your Action, and as a catalyst, it is where your reach becomes something held: savings, property, positions, commitments that stay put. You do best when ambition lands somewhere concrete.
- fable-v075 (49w): Your Action is what you go and build, claim and provide. Earth makes it steady, so what you claim lasts. As a catalyst, the wanting outruns the keeping, and you chase the next venture instead of holding what you have. You end up owning far less than you built.

**the remedy (friction advice)**

- original (43w): Lightness is also a form of wealth. Sell, delegate, or close one standing obligation this month. Before acquiring anything, name its yearly cost in hours. Keep a short list of what you would defend in a hard year, and loosen everything off it.
- fable-v075 (58w): Seek Wood, your own Body energy. Wood is the trunk under everything you reach for: sleep, food, rest, the strength that stands before it carries. Let it hold what Earth piles up. You agree to more than you have strength for. Eat a real meal before any decision about money. Sleep on every yes before you give it.
