# Elementum language use recommendations

Date: 2026-09-18. Status: recommended editorial specification, grounded in the current reading system. Examples are comparison candidates, not approved production copy.

## Editorial direction

Keep the human voice. “Never good enough” is clear, emotionally recognizable language for an inner experience. “Finds more faults” changes the meaning and sounds more like an observer grading the reader. The user's preference is a useful calibration of this product's voice, though it does not establish a universal reader preference.

A good edit preserves the experience, image and source mechanism while correcting the particular claim that fails. Comprehensibility does not require emotional neutrality. Precision does not require a clinical voice. Direct second-person language remains part of the portrait.

## Language fit by surface

| Surface | Preserve | Check before approving |
|---|---|---|
| The Sign / stem overview | Third-person elemental myth and each stem's own arena | No second-person psychology imported into the myth; imagery distinguishes the stem |
| Your Nature | Opens with You; image-to-person bridge; direct band portrait; emotional landing | 50–75 words; one coherent selected band; no invented personal decline or guaranteed perfect results |
| P4 trait chip | Recognizable personality phrase, concise and easy to say | Keep emotional idioms; label and description agree; gift/shadow valence must read clearly with the chip alone under phrase law v5 |
| P4 trait description | Material capability and a recognizable cost or gift | Recorded echo_of mechanism remains intact; do not turn capability into a guaranteed life outcome |
| Carry | Short elemental image with the same practical direction as the source | Valence × volume match; yin carry overrides and the September 17 mechanism_yin extension where specified; wide is not automatically widest |
| Function definition | Plain family-level explanation from ELEMENT_PAIR | ≤55 words under the later field ruling; understandable without knowing either god's name |
| Function ledger | ELEMENT_GOD character; trait/scene/outside entry variety; full sentences | 35–55 words per door; selected keyword agrees with all three variants; example demonstrates the claim |
| Function advice | Family-level psychological insight landing in an ordinary action | ≤60 words; action follows the stated problem; no chart-derived investment prescription |
| Persona name and meaning | Elevated “The [Noun]” name with a plain explanation | Keep locked names such as The Magistrate; define them instead of renaming them for syllable count |
| Domains / positions | Their own later, more personal registers and conditional context | Do not export every function-page restriction here or treat a domain vignette as established biography |

Do not apply one universal word budget or one register to all rows. The concept ladder determines when names and explanations appear. The current function-page vocabulary zone is roughly B1–B2 conversational English, with permitted edge words such as Fickle, Brooding and Contrarian when the surrounding text makes them clear. Function-page chips have a 1–3-word budget. A phrase such as Plays it safe is allowed because it is already familiar.

The documents' audience claims are design assumptions for this review, not new demographic research conducted here.

## Four questions for every proposed edit

1. **Meaning:** Is it the same feature, cost or feeling? Do not replace an inner feeling with an outward behavior merely because the latter is easier to measure.
2. **Reasoning:** Can the claim be traced through the correct source axis and selected state? A beautiful sentence can still belong to the wrong function, god or band.
3. **Reading:** Would a person understand it on first reading and say something like it about themselves? Read the whole paragraph aloud.
4. **Scope:** Is this a recognizable tendency or example, or is it pretending to know an actual event, another person's private thoughts, or a guaranteed outcome?

The original text is a valid winner. Do not require an edit for every flagged row.

## Directness, scenes and certainty

Keep “you” and ordinary scenes. Objects and a clock-time can make an example vivid. A scene beginning “The meeting starts in five minutes…” does not necessarily assert a verifiable event in the reader's life. It establishes a representative situation.

The concern increases when the passage claims the reader actually predicted a neighbor's breakup three months before it happened, completed a course at a stated age, or is secretly judged in a particular way by friends. Remove the unwarranted history or certainty while keeping the scene's function.

Use “can,” “when” and “if” where needed for meaning, not as a mandatory prefix. Rotating every paragraph into “When you…” would undo the three-door design. An outside door can show observable effects without claiming privileged access to people's thoughts.

Distinguish emotional absolutes from outcome absolutes. “Never good enough” names a felt standard. “Never be broke” promises solvency. They do not need the same treatment.

Avoid guarantees about flawless work, wealth, fertility, affection or vindicated intuition. Advice should leave the reader a practical choice. The reasoning behind a symbolic portrait can be disclosed without claiming that a psychology citation scientifically validates its chart mapping.

## Reasoning and authoring specification

For each revised unit, record its field path, source axis, stem/element/god, selected state, source claim, interpretive bridge, original copy, candidate, reason and review status. Keep the existing echo_of link where applicable. Do not silently widen a parent mechanism to justify a preferred child phrase.

Distinguish:
- Classical relationship or condition.
- Psychological translation used to describe an experience.
- Elemental image or illustrative scene.
- Personalized prediction or advice.

A citation to a psychological construct supports using that construct carefully; it does not by itself establish that the chart selects it accurately. Likewise, a classical verse does not automatically justify every downstream behavioral detail.

Author in station JSON under REA_05, regenerate the documented twins, and perform deliberate runtime transcription. Do not edit generated Markdown as the source of truth. Keep the reading pack as a review snapshot with its own version.

## Acceptance protocol

1. Review original and candidate together, with the actual title/chip and its expansion.
2. Confirm the latest field contract, surface reachability and source/state trace.
3. For P4, compare the neighboring stem and the other trait from the same door. For a function ledger, compare both god siblings and all three doors.
4. Assemble the real reading with pair definition, selected weighted rows and advice. Check opening rotation, repeated claims, adjacent contradictions and too many crafted endings.
5. Run scoped mechanical checks for word budgets, banned terms and punctuation. A lint pass does not prove emotional quality.
6. Read aloud for natural verbs, varied sentence length, at most one fragment per door and an ordinary sentence amid the crafted lines.
7. Compare with intended readers: ask them to paraphrase the meaning, select the more natural version, explain what feels personal, and identify anything that feels imposed or untrue. Test intermediate-English comprehension directly rather than inferring it from syllables.
8. Record the result as retain original, adopt candidate, revise again or unresolved. Do not call a rewrite more resonant until this comparison supports it.

The first-pass 101 flags should be triaged through this protocol. Neither the first replacements nor these examples are a batch-rewrite mandate.
