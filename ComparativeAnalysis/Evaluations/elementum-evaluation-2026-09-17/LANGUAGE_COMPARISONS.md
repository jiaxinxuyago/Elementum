# Elementum language comparisons: preserve the voice, repair the claim

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: editorial comparison candidates, not production changes.
Companion documents: [FULL_EVALUATION.md](./FULL_EVALUATION.md), [140_TRAIT_LEDGER.md](./140_TRAIT_LEDGER.md), [UNIT_NOTES.md](./UNIT_NOTES.md).

## What this addendum changes

The user's feedback is that “Never good enough” feels more natural, comprehensible and personal than my proposed “Finds more faults”. I agree that my replacement weakened the phrase. It changed an inner feeling into an outward checking behavior and sounded more like an assessment.

This addendum supersedes the earlier recommendation to rename that label. Keep “Never good enough” as a candidate. The existing evaluation and ledger remain the historical first pass; their proposed replacements are not all mandatory improvements. In particular, the count of 101 flagged traits is a count of first-pass flags, not proof that 101 rewritten versions would read better.

The distinction is between:
- **Natural emotional language:** a recognizable way a person describes an experience.
- **Unsupported claims:** invented history, guaranteed outcomes, private knowledge of others, or a mechanism that differs from its named source.
- **Assembly contradictions:** text selected for the wrong band, door or volume.

A phrase can succeed at the first and still need work on the second or third. “Never” is not automatically a flaw: “Never good enough” can name a feeling, while “never be broke” promises an outcome.

The examples below preserve labels, metaphor and rhythm where they work. Some are deliberately left unchanged. None has been shown by reader testing to be more resonant or more accurate for a particular chart. The aim is to make the tradeoffs concrete enough to judge.

## Side-by-side reading examples

Original passages are quoted from the supplied review pack, not silently replaced with current-station wording. Source links identify the exact original lines. Candidate text is a proposal for comparison, not approved copy.

### 1. Never good enough

甲 · Order shadow · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L68)

**Original · Never good enough**

> Every rule met, every critique taken, until the standards stop shaping you and start shrinking you. The harshest voice you hear is your own, and it never says done.

**Recommended candidate · Never good enough**

> Every rule met, every critique taken, and done still doesn't feel good enough. The standard moves just as you reach it.

**Treatment:** Keep the original label. Optional light edit to the description.

**Why:** The label names a familiar inner experience in ordinary language. It need not be read as the app judging the person's worth. My earlier replacement, Finds more faults, changed that experience into fault-finding and sounded less natural. The proposed description makes the label's internal meaning explicit without losing its sting.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original description is also a viable candidate. This is a preference to compare with readers, not an objectively necessary correction. The chart-to-inner-criticism interpretation still needs qualification at the source level.

### 2. Takes it personally

丁 · Order shadow · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L257)

**Original · Takes it personally**

> A criticism of the work lands as a criticism of your soul, every time. The checking costs more than the mistake would, and the hardest judge of the flame is you.

**Recommended candidate · Takes it personally**

> A criticism of the work lands somewhere deeper. You go back to fix the mistake and find yourself questioning more than the work.

**Treatment:** Keep the label. Reduce the absolute claim.

**Why:** Takes it personally is already a recognizable personality phrase. The revision removes every time and the claim that the checking always costs more than the error, while preserving the feeling of criticism becoming personal.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original soul image has more intensity. It can remain if that intensity fits the desired voice. Inner criticism remains an authored interpretation, not a consequence established by a chart percentage.

### 3. Keeps reworking it

丁 · Action shadow · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L256)

**Original · Keeps reworking it**

> Signed, shipped, agreed, and you are back at it with a smaller brush. Done is a decision you keep reopening.

**Recommended candidate · Keeps reworking it**

> Signed, shipped, agreed, and you are back at it with a smaller brush. Done is a decision you keep reopening.

**Treatment:** Keep unchanged as a comparison control.

**Why:** The phrase is human, the behavior is visible, and the smaller-brush image adds character without hiding the mechanism. The last sentence connects the feeling to a specific habit. Turning it into process language would weaken it.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The opening is a triad, which the existing house rule discourages. I recommend allowing this deliberate, compact sequence instead of mechanically deleting it. This is an explicit proposed exception, not a claim that the original obeys every existing ban.

### 4. Can't switch off

丙 · Body shadow · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L188)

**Original · Can't switch off**

> More heat than your days can spend. Sleep will not come, intensity leaks into every conversation, and rest feels like a punishment.

**Recommended candidate · Can't switch off**

> More heat than the day gives you somewhere to put. When the work stops, you find another thing to start. Even rest feels like something you should be doing better.

**Treatment:** Keep the label and heat image. Replace the asserted sleep symptom.

**Why:** The label already captures a recognizable tension. The revision shows difficulty disengaging without telling the reader that they cannot sleep. Rest becoming another task preserves the emotional cost.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** This still describes an interpretive personality pattern. It is not a diagnosis or a guarantee about how the reader behaves. It belongs only in an eligible shadow state.

### 5. Mentors everyone

癸 · Expression shadow · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L633)

**Original · Mentors everyone**

> Every stalled person and every half idea gets your rain. You leave greener ground behind you everywhere, and none of it is yours.

**Recommended candidate · Gives too much**

> A friend's half-formed idea gets your full attention. You help it grow, then look back at the work you left waiting.

**Treatment:** Offer a clearer shadow label while retaining the care-and-growth mechanism.

**Why:** Mentors everyone is easy to understand but can read as praise when the chip stands alone. Gives too much makes the cost visible immediately. The description keeps the generous impulse and shows what it displaces.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** Gives too much is less distinctive and applies broadly. If labels always appear with descriptions, keeping Mentors everyone is defensible. Compare both in the actual UI before replacing it.

### 6. Misses nothing

丁 · Mind wide gift · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L242)

**Original · Misses nothing**

> With this much fuel behind the flame, the small tell, the changed tone, the detail everyone walked past all catch your eye. Three months later it turned out to be the whole story.

**Recommended candidate · Draws on experience**

> What you have taken in gives the flame something to work with. A lesson comes back when a difficult task gives it a use.

**Treatment:** Change the claim if the existing source relationship is retained.

**Why:** The source, 火_木.carry.wide, concerns available fuel, with the function definition explaining accumulated learning. It does not establish infallible detection or a prediction confirmed three months later. This revision returns to learning becoming useful.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** Misses nothing is the more striking label. If perceptiveness is an intentional part of Ding's identity, author and justify that mechanism in the source first, then derive a perceptiveness chip from it. A softer label such as Notices the details alone would not fix the present source mismatch.

### 7. Rekindles people

丁 · Expression gift · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L243)

**Original · Rekindles people**

> Your belief in someone works at close range and lasts. A friend arrives half given up, and somewhere over tea at your table, the pilot light catches again.

**Recommended candidate · Keeps warmth alive**

> You can turn a good evening into a gathering people return to. What began as warmth gets a place in their lives.

**Treatment:** Change the mechanism only if the named source remains unchanged.

**Why:** The original is evocative and emotionally accessible. Its issue is not poor language: 火_土.function.definition_catalyst describes enthusiasm becoming durable output, including gatherings becoming traditions. Emotional rescue is a different claim. The candidate keeps warmth and human connection while making something repeatable.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original may be the better standalone passage. Keeping it would require a coherent source for encouragement rekindling another person's effort. Do not flatten good writing merely to conceal a source-design problem.

### 8. Quality guaranteed

辛 · Order gift · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L497)

**Original · Quality guaranteed**

> Pressure organizes you instead of scattering you. Nothing leaves your hands that you do not believe in, and years later your work is the one still holding up under inspection.

**Recommended candidate · Better under pressure**

> A clear standard gives your attention a place to land. You can work with a demanding review, turning each correction into a sharper next draft.

**Treatment:** Keep the pressure-to-focus idea. Remove the outcome guarantee.

**Why:** A response to pressure can be described without guaranteeing lasting superiority or inventing a future inspection. The candidate remains a recognizable trait phrase rather than an evaluation label.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** Better under pressure still needs the description's clear-standard context. It must not imply that unlimited pressure improves performance. The unresolved Xin/Fire source qualification remains separate from this wording edit.

### 9. The Jewel's strong-core portrait

辛 · Your nature · Overfueled · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L466)

**Original**

> You are the gem still under the wheel, and the hand that holds it, and the eye that isn't satisfied. The standard grades everything all day, itself included. What you release is flawless because releasing is the hard part. The polish never really stops. It just changes what it's working on when the desk finally clears.

**Recommended candidate**

> You are the gem still under the wheel, with your own hand holding it there. The standard follows the work long after the task is done. Even when you let something go, you can still see the part you would change. The polish follows you home.

**Treatment:** Preserve the portrait and its tension. Remove flawless output as a fact.

**Why:** The original image of being both gem and polisher is worth keeping. The correction is about the claimed result: difficulty releasing work does not make that work flawless. The revision retains the relentless inner standard and a strong closing image.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original has a more elaborate rhythm. The candidate is shorter, but shortness is not automatically better. Compare their emotional force, not just reading level.

### 10. The Vine's weak-core portrait

乙 · Your nature · Underfueled · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/01_day_master_templates.md#L96)

**Original**

> You keep the vine's gift of finding windows, even in seasons when you knock on fewer doors. The winding takes more out of you than it used to, so you pick your walls with care now. Nothing about that is retreat. A climber who chooses the wall is simply done wasting green on stone that won't hold it.

**Recommended candidate**

> You keep the vine's gift of finding windows, even in seasons when you knock on fewer doors. Finding another way takes effort, so you choose which walls deserve the climb. Some openings can wait. The vine needs somewhere worth reaching.

**Treatment:** Keep the Vine image. Remove an assumed history of decline.

**Why:** The current chart state does not establish that the reader once had more energy. The candidate removes than it used to, now and the implied past of wasted effort, while preserving selective persistence.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original ending is more defiant and may resonate more. A still lighter option is to change only the second sentence, then separately review the ending's implied biography.

### 11. The Steward's wealth reading

金_正财 · Wealth · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/03_ten_god_element_templates.md#L1438)

**Original**

> Your wealth is machined: earned exactly, kept polished, compounded without drama. You will never be flashy and never be broke. Invest in quality that holds its edge, including your own skills.

**Recommended candidate**

> You like knowing what is there and what it took to build. Keeping something in good order can feel as satisfying as acquiring it. The care shows in what you choose to maintain.

**Treatment:** Remove the financial guarantee and chart-based investment direction.

**Why:** Never be broke is a prediction of material outcome, not expressive shorthand for a personality tension. This is a substantive claim correction, unlike keeping Never good enough as the name of an internal feeling.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The candidate is quieter than the original. Emotional force can be developed further, but a promise of financial security cannot be restored simply because it reads well.

### 12. Serene: a scene

水_正印 · Serene · scene · [Original source](../elementum-review-pack/03_ten_god_element_templates.md#L1752)

**Original · Serene**

> The kitchen caught fire, briefly, at the family gathering, and your voice never left its usual register: pan lid, baking soda, window, done. The story afterward was all about the flames. You remember it as four small tasks that happened to be slightly warm.

**Recommended candidate · Serene**

> When plans fall apart around a table, you can give the room one small thing to do next. The problem stays the same size. It becomes easier to begin.

**Treatment:** Keep the label. Use an ordinary conditional scene instead of invented emergency history.

**Why:** The original has strong narrative control, but supplies a past event and compresses an emergency procedure into a personality illustration. The candidate retains calm expressed through a next step, without implying guaranteed emergency competence.

**What it gives up / what remains open:** The original is more cinematic. A product can use vivid fictional scenes, but it should frame them as illustrations consistently rather than presenting them as the reader's past.

## How to choose between the versions

Read the original and candidate as finished reading passages before reading the editorial notes. Judge them in the actual layout: a label alone and the same label with its description can communicate different things.

Use four separate questions:
1. Would a person naturally use this phrase about themselves?
2. Is the meaning understood on the first read?
3. Does the passage feel emotionally specific without telling the reader an invented fact?
4. Does it express the mechanism and state actually selected by the assembly?

Keep a strong original when the candidate is merely more literal. Revise the smallest part that carries the unsupported claim. Change a label when its ordinary meaning creates a real misunderstanding, not just because a longer explanation is possible.

Do not use reader recognition alone as evidence that the chart assignment is valid. Testing language preference, comprehension and emotional response is different from testing whether charts distinguish personality patterns.

## Revised editing priorities

1. Preserve natural self-description, especially concise familiar phrases.
2. Preserve the best image and the emotional tension.
3. Remove outcome guarantees and invented biography with the smallest effective edit.
4. Fix source or selection problems at their source instead of hiding them with neutral prose.
5. Keep distinctiveness in view: a safer but generic replacement can be a worse editorial result.
6. Treat optional stylistic changes as options. Reserve mandatory corrections for actual contradictions or unsupported consequential claims.

This is a comparison addendum rather than a finalized house style specification. It records a revised editorial direction informed by the user's preference: accuracy and coherence should strengthen the human reading experience, not turn it into a behavior report.
