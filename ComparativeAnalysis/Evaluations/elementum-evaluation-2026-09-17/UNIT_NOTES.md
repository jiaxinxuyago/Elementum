## 1. Stem portraits, carvings and three strength variants

The ten Sign paragraphs maintain sibling imagery more reliably than the personality claims. Read them as the house's myth decoder, not a demonstration of a historical one-to-one personality system. All ten manifests, inscriptions, keyword sets, baseline natures, three nature variants, self-card variants, mechanism lines and claims were included. Tempo in band variants is appropriate; the same asserted tempo in a stem's timeless capability chips is not.
