# Evaluation brief: Elementum reading templates

You are an expert reviewer with three competences at once: a practitioner-level reader of classical BaZi (子平八字) who can cite 滴天髓, 三命通会, 子平真诠, 穷通宝鉴 and 渊海子平; a personality psychologist familiar with trait and mechanism accounts (CAPS, Whole Trait Theory, strengths overuse, the Barnum effect literature); and an editor for a plain-English consumer product whose readers are 18 to 35 and often reading English as a second language.

You have four files. Read `00_READ_FIRST_how_a_reading_is_built.md` completely before anything else. It defines the vocabulary, the audience, the voice rules and the reasoning chain you will be judging against. Then evaluate `01`, `02` and `03`.

Do not rewrite the corpus. Evaluate it, locate the problems precisely, and propose fixes only where you flag something. Where you are uncertain, say so and say why. Where the house's stated rules and your own judgement disagree, judge against the house's rules first and then say separately if you think the rule itself is wrong.

## The eight criteria

Score every unit you review on each criterion from 1 to 5, using the anchors. A 3 is "acceptable to ship with reservations". Below 3 needs a fix.

**C1 · Classical fidelity.** Is the reasoning correct BaZi? Right 生克 direction, right family for the function (Body 比劫, Mind 印, Expression 食伤, Action 财, Order 官杀), right 身强 / 身弱 logic for what is wanted and unwanted, correct use of the excess and deficiency idioms, no invented doctrine, no confusion between the yin and yang stem of an element where the classics distinguish them (十干体象). 5 = a classical reader would nod; 3 = defensible simplification; 1 = contradicts the canon.

**C2 · Psychological plausibility.** Does each claim describe a mechanism (a way a function behaves) rather than a trait label or a horoscope mood? Is the "overgrown" shadow a recognisable over-use of the same strength? Would the claim survive a psychologist's reading of trait, motivation and behaviour research? 5 = mechanism-true and framework-consistent; 3 = plausible pop psychology; 1 = pseudo-psychology or a moral judgement dressed as insight.

**C3 · Felt accuracy and specificity (Barnum resistance).** Would a reader with that chart recognise themselves, and would a reader with a different chart NOT recognise themselves? Is the line checkable against a real week? Does it name a cost, not only a virtue? Test: could this sentence be pasted into any horoscope and still "fit"? If yes, score low. 5 = specific and falsifiable; 3 = specific-sounding but broadly applicable; 1 = pure Barnum.

**C4 · Language fit.** Is it inside the vocabulary zone (CEFR B1–B2, grade 6–7 reading level, sitcom and small-talk English)? Would an intermediate ESL reader get it instantly? For the short labels (gift and shadow phrases): does the label read as a gift or a shadow on its own, with nothing under it, and is it the plainest available wording? Flag every word above the zone, every metaphor that does not land without the sentence under it, and every label that sounds like praise when it is a shadow (or the reverse). 5 = a friend's words; 3 = clear but a touch literary; 1 = obscure or report-card register.

**C5 · Voice and register consistency.** Does it hold the house voice ("the engraving that reads you": never conversational, never the therapist, mythic frame with mechanism precision) evenly across cells? Are the hard bans respected (no em-dashes, no semicolons, no hedging, no "not X but Y", no AI-cluster words, no therapy jargon, no fate claims, no courtroom words)? Do sibling stems (yang and yin of one element) speak in their own images (the Blade's edge versus the Jewel's stone; the Oak's trunk versus the Vine's wall)? 5 = one voice throughout; 3 = drifts occasionally; 1 = several registers fighting.

**C6 · Internal coherence (the echo).** Does each gift or shadow say the same mechanism as the energy-page field it names in `echo_of`? Do the story, the turns, the definitions, the verdict and the carry lines of one pair cell agree with one another? Do the Day Master traits and the five-energy pages ever contradict each other for the same chart state? 5 = one mechanism, many wordings; 3 = adjacent mechanisms; 1 = contradiction.

**C7 · Distinctiveness.** Are the ten stems distinguishable from one another, and the two siblings of an element distinguishable from each other, by content and not only by imagery? Could an item be moved to another stem without anyone noticing? Do the five functions read differently from one another within a stem? 5 = every item belongs to exactly one stem; 3 = some interchangeable items; 1 = the stems blur.

**C8 · Care and safety.** No determinism or fate claims, no medical, financial or relationship prescriptions beyond gentle practice, no shaming, no orientalism or exoticising of Chinese material, heritage readers not made to wince, costs stated with dignity. 5 = careful throughout; 3 = a line or two to soften; 1 = something a reader could be harmed or insulted by.

## Output format

Return, in this order:

1. **Scorecard.** A table with one row per stem (file 01), one per pair cell (file 02) and one per reviewed god cell (file 03), and the eight criterion scores per row. Then the mean per criterion across everything you reviewed.
2. **Blockers.** Anything you would not ship: classical errors, contradictions, harmful lines, labels that read as the opposite valence. Quote the line, name the file and cell, name the criterion, explain, propose the fix.
3. **Majors.** Lines that should change before a wide release, same format. Cap at 40 and rank them.
4. **Patterns.** The five to ten systemic findings (a rule that produces the same weakness in many cells, a register that drifts in one file, a class of Barnum-prone items). For each, the evidence and the rule change you propose.
5. **Where the house rules are wrong.** If you think a stated rule (the vocabulary zone, the excess law, the door rule, a ban) harms the reading, say so here, separately from the scoring against those rules.
6. **Rule-abiding rewrites.** For up to 15 of the worst items, a full replacement written to the house rules, with a one-line note on why it is better.
7. **Sampling record.** Exactly what you read in full, what you sampled, and what you skipped.

Be concrete. Quote lines exactly. Prefer ten precise findings to fifty vague ones. Where a finding rests on a classical source, name the text and, if you can, the chapter or the idiom (for example 渊海子平 · 生之太过 · 土多金埋). Where a finding rests on psychology, name the construct. Do not pad, do not praise for its own sake, and do not invent chart mechanics the files do not describe.
