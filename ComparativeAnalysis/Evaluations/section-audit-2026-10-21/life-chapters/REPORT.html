MAJOR · nothing done, your ruling first

M1

Accuracy · the luck-pillar start (calculator)

In 3,000 random births, 186 (6%) start their decades about ten years off; 121 of them are backward-direction births between 小寒 and 立春 (about 6 January to 3 February), where calculator.js counts back to the previous 大雪 instead of 小寒. Example: born 1951-01-19 22:00, female: the app starts the first decade at 14, the classical count (三日为一岁, 渊海子平 / 三命通会) gives 4.5. Those readers see the wrong decade on Life Chapters, Domains and the Almanac. A further 15% are a year off (the birth hour is ignored, the term dates are day-level, the F1 drift). Auditor G1 met one on page L013 independently. The calculator is shared: a fix moves existing readers' decades in every section.

M2

Readability · ", and you" closes almost every unit

The reader-last law is met with the same joint in nearly every unit: 16 to 34 times a page (mean about 25). It is the main reason C averages 2.4. All four reports filed it.

M3

Accuracy · the "underneath" lines

Five ground lines carry every page, with one fixed reader clause each ("you put both to work" ×33 on one group's pages). They ignore direction: under an energy the chart releases, "you feel it hold all the way through" and "at full strength" read as gifts (35 units on 21 pages).

M4

Accuracy · what carries the reader is a fixed energy

Heavy and release lines name a fixed helper ("a fixed routine and someone who holds you to your word" is Order; "study and a mentor" is Mind). On many charts that helper is itself heavy, so the page contradicts itself: the heavy Expression year does it on all 8 of its pages. Mind release is carried by Order images (deadlines, stakes) in 8 of 35 units; Expression release is cured with more output in 13 of 35. Body release is carried by more peers.

M5

Accuracy · people and roles the age cannot have

Year-Gate landings give readers in their sixties living parents and "a parent or grandparent"; Month-Gate lines give readers over 55 a workplace and a contract; Day-Gate landings say "your partner" to readers under 30; late decades give readers in their sixties and seventies bosses, deadlines and exams; four units name brothers and sisters (gendered, and the chart cannot know them).

M6

Repetition · the decade repeats its chapter

When the current decade has the same energy and direction as the current chapter (10 pages), its favours pairs repeat the chapter's domain lines (L053 repeats all three) and the manual and decade line share a carry phrase.

M7

Accuracy · Love lines compare two kinds of partner

Six Love lines set one kind of partner against another ("a partner you can disagree with over one you obey"); a reader with a partner reads it as advice to pick someone else. On five pages the Love line favours more of an energy the chart already has too much of.

M8

Accuracy · two energies "you need most" on one page

On three pages two different energies are each called "the energy you need most" (the "need" state is any welcomed energy at 10% or less, and a chart can have two).

M9

Accuracy · the child decade line on teenage decades

The .early (child) decade lines reach decades that run mostly through ages 13 to 24 ("grown-ups who were quick to correct you" at 15 to 24).
