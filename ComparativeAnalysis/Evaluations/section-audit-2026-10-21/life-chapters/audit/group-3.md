## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Same energy, said twice (MAJOR, G3-001).** On 7 of 24 pages the current decade has the current chapter's function and direction. The domain lines, favours pairs, manual and decade line then repeat each other's domains and carries: L053 repeats all three domains, and L060 three. Examples: L053 "favours moving over lying low … a brisk walk" with "A brisk walk over an extra nap". L056 manual "gives your strength the shape it has lacked" with decade "It gives your strength a shape".
2. **Landings ignore the reader's age (MAJOR, G3-002).** 27 Year-Gate lines on 13 pages give readers aged 59–66 parents and older relatives, including "a parent or grandparent" for a 67-year-old. 7 placements of "grandparent" go to readers aged 44–67. 22 Month-Gate lines give readers aged 55–72 workplaces and contracts. Examples: L053 (2027) "a parent or grandparent"; L051 (2031) "You tie yourself to a workplace … by contract".
3. **The ", and you" close (MAJOR, G3-003).** Every page, 16–31 times (mean about 25). The ruling that the reader closes each unit has hardened into one joint. Examples: L073 ×31; every ground line ("…, and you feel the two pull apart").
4. **Heavy units with nothing to carry the reader (not MAJOR; 5 unit rewrites, G3-005 to G3-009).** There are 15 placements on 12 pages, against ruled Q10. Examples: `mind · decade.release.lived.a` "you came out of them knowing more than you could yet use" (6 pages); `action · decade.release.now` "You end it with a clear count of what your life costs to run" (4 pages). A gate check would help: a release or heavy unit must name an agent that carries the reader.
5. **Sequence words that are false on the page (not MAJOR; TWEAKs G3-030 to G3-032).** "Again" and "more" assume the decade or chapter before had the same energy: 7 placements on 7 pages. Examples: L060 "your own energy again" after a Wood Mind Early Chapter; L065 "This decade brings Fire again" after two Wood decades.
6. **One carry noun per function (not MAJOR; G3-014, G3-020 to G3-022).** Expression release always leans on "a craft" or "a stage", and Mind grow and Order release lean on "a mentor". This keeps D2 true but breaks C on the page. Examples: L059 "a craft" ×3; L057 "mentor" ×4.

D2 and D3 hold well across the group. Each function reads as the same force in chapter, decade and year: Order presses or bears down, Mind shelters or smothers, Action reaches or spreads thin, Expression gives an outlet or loosens the tongue, Body steadies or turns into stubbornness. The grow and release texts differ only in whether the chart wants the force. The exceptions are G3-006 and G3-012.

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "Passing the work into younger hands spends that strength well, and you are finding that a lighter grip holds more." (L063, `body · chapter.hour.release.now`) It gives the cost, the carry and a closure the reader keeps, all in the Late Chapter's own terms.
2. "Since your mid-thirties home has offered so much comfort and counsel that decisions wait, and a partner often thinks for you." (L066, `mind · chapter.day.release.now`) This is the Pattern's move: a private habit named in plain words, with no blame.
3. "Since your late forties you have been looked after and advised from every side, and what you built runs without much from you." (L052, L053, L062, `mind · chapter.hour.release.now`) It is specific, the reader recognises it, and it is exactly the excess of Mind.

**Weakest**
1. "A quiet coolness settles between you and a parent or grandparent this year, and you warm it again with a visit or a call." (L053, L054 at 67, `land.year.harm.year`) It is impossible for the reader, and it gives the template away.
2. "These years brought Fire, more thinking than your chart could use, and you learned to wait and weigh things before you moved." (L063, L072, `mind · decade.release.early.a`) A release that reads as a virtue, with no carry.
3. "From your late forties there is more within reach than you have time for, and the people after you bring much of it. You keep your hand on what matters most." (L066, L068, `action · chapter.hour.grow.coming`) A gift written as overload, with a vague close.
