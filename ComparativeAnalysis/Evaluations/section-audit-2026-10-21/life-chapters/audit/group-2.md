## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Carriers ignore the chart's strength. MAJOR (G2-X1).** About 40 units on 23 pages. A heavy or release line names a fixed carrier function, and the composed page then contradicts it. Examples: L031, where the 2026 cure "a fixed routine and someone who holds you to your word" sits beside 2030 "the pressure and duty your chart already carries too much of"; L037, where the manual says "study or support" beside "more Mind than your chart can use".
2. **Ground lines ignore direction. MAJOR (G2-X2).** 35 units on 21 pages. Examples: L039, where "more Order than your chart can bear" is followed by "Under it runs more Water, and you feel it at full strength"; L042, where "Under it runs Earth, which feeds the Metal, and you feel it hold all the way through" sits under a released Order.
3. **Landings and lines ignore age. MAJOR (G2-X3).** About 19 units on 9 pages. Examples: L045, age 71, "a parent or grandparent"; L042, age 26, "a break in plans you made together" with the people after you.
4. **One energy says the same thing in every section. Not MAJOR, a D1 and A3 drift.** About 14 pages. When the chapter, the manual, the domains and the current decade share an energy, they repeat its cure. Examples: L041, where three Mind-release chapters give about twelve sentences of "act before you are ready"; L028, where "talent … audience … at work and among friends" appears three times. Fix through G2-M5, M6, M7, M14 and M16, and a writing note that a domain line names the domain's own act, not the function's general cure.
5. **"Again" and "more" slips. TWEAK-level, systemic.** 8 pages. The `.a` / `.b` wording is swapped in the Expression release decades, and a hard-coded "again" appears in the lived Body Rising Chapter. Examples: L042 "This decade brings Fire again" on the first Fire decade; L050 "your own energy again" after an Earth Action Early Chapter.
6. **Heavy units with no carrier (Q10 A). MINOR.** 6 pages: L029, L037, L040, L043, L045, L046. Examples: "You end it with a clear count of what your life costs to run"; "you learned to wait and weigh things before you moved".

## 4 · The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "Passing the work into younger hands spends that strength well, and you are finding that a lighter grip holds more." (`body · chapter.hour.release.now`, L044, L046) It names the cost, the cause and the open door in one sentence, and it is quotable.
2. "Since your late teens your voice has outrun your position, and the people in charge have not always enjoyed it." (`expression · chapter.month.release.now`, L029, L032) The reader recognises the private conflict in their own words, at Pattern level.
3. "Since your late teens you have gathered qualifications and advice faster than chances to use them." (`mind · chapter.month.release.now`, L038, L040, L041) It states a surplus as a fact about the reader's week, with no hedge.

**Weakest**
1. "Words come out sharper than you mean, and you keep your footing through a fixed routine and someone who holds you to your word." (`expression · year.heavy`) The carrier is the chart's own burden on all 8 pages where it appears.
2. "A quiet coolness settles between you and a parent or grandparent this year" (`land.year.harm.year`, age 69 to 71 on L043, L044, L045, L049). It claims what the chart cannot know, and older readers will notice at once.
3. "From birth to fifteen brothers, sisters and children your age gave you someone to keep up with." (`body · chapter.year.grow.lived`) It misparses on first read as "fifteen brothers".
