## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **The ", and you" close everywhere. MAJOR (G1-027).** It appears on 25 of 25 pages, 23 to 34 times per page. Examples: "In Career, this chapter favours a clear brief over a free hand, and you do your finest work inside limits someone else has set." and "Under it runs Fire, which the Water holds down, and you put both to work." It is the main reason C scores 2 on every page.
2. **Underneath lines carry the relation but no ground. MAJOR (G1-028).** There are 99 units on 25 pages, built from five reader clauses. Examples: "and you put both to work" (×33) and "and you feel it hold all the way through" (×30). Coming chapters read in the present tense (24 units).
3. **Comma-less age openers (garden paths). MAJOR as a rule (G1-001), fixed by TWEAKs.** There are 11 units on 22 pages. Examples: "From birth to fifteen brothers, sisters…" and "Since your mid-thirties appetite and opinion…".
4. **Late-life decades written for working age. MAJOR (G1-026).** There are 13 placements on 11 pages. Examples: "deadlines that stack up and people above you" at sixty-six (L004), and "more to earn and more to spend" at seventy-two (L001).
5. **Love lines that pick between partners. MAJOR (G1-025).** Six units reach 17 pages. Examples: "a partner you can disagree with over one you obey" and "a partner with a strong will of their own over one who agrees".
6. **Heavy Almanac landings with no carry, and two "need most" energies on one page.** These are MINOR and MAJOR respectively (G1-029 to G1-031, G1-024). The landings appear on 20 pages, for example "by your own choice or by theirs". The double "need most" appears on 3 pages, for example "Fire brings Expression … the energy you need most" and then "Earth brings Action … the energy you need most" two years later (L008).

## 4 · The best and the worst

Best:
- "A patient teacher helped you through, and you learned that slow was not the same as wrong." (ENERGY order · decade.release.early.b, L017). It names a child's private fear in the reader's own words, and the cost and the carry share one sentence. It is better than The Pattern here.
- "From your mid-thirties two strong wills share one household, and a partner who feels like an equal also feels like a contest." (ENERGY body · chapter.day.release.coming). It is the T25 push-and-pull move with no hedge, and readers will screenshot it.
- "Since your late teens you have gathered qualifications and advice faster than chances to use them. … you are learning to move before you feel ready." (ENERGY mind · chapter.month.release.now, L014). A plain cost, an owned cause, an open door.

Weakest:
- "Under it runs Earth, which the Wood holds down, and you put both to work." (tpl_almanac · ground.presses, ×33). It is empty, and the reader could not say whether it is true of their week.
- "This chapter rewards reaching. It pays you back for every offer you take up and put to use straight away." (ENERGY action · chapter_manual.grow). The chapter is the subject, and it reads like a slogan, not a manual.
- "You come under scrutiny at work this year, a review or a rule applied to you more strictly than to others." (tpl_almanac · land.year.punish.month). Hard news that leaves the reader with nothing, which is the BIZ_04 "sentenced" failure.
