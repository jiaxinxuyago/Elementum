## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The release carrier is drawn from the wrong function (MAJOR, X-001, X-002).** 21 release units carry an excess with either the same force or the force that feeds it: Mind by Order (8 units, e.g. `mind · year.heavy` "a firm deadline", `mind · decade.release.lived.b` "A job with real stakes"), and Expression by more output or by rules from above (13 units, e.g. `expression · chapter_manual.release` "A craft that takes patience", `expression · chapter_domain.Career.release` "limits someone else has set"). Body leans the same way in Career and Wealth (X-005, X-006). Action and Order are clean: Action is carried by your own strength or peers, and Order by Mind or friends, in every unit. The gate should check each release unit's carrier against a table per function: Body→Expression/Order/Action; Mind→Action; Expression→Mind/Action; Action→Body; Order→Mind/Body/Expression.
2. **People the chart cannot know (MAJOR, X-004, X-007).** 10 units presume a partner, a living parent or grandparent, or siblings. The landings alone reach 107 page instances: the day landings on under-30 pages (L024–L038) and the year landings on 60+ pages (L043–L056). Examples: `land.year.harm.day` "name it to your partner"; `body · chapter.year.grow.lived` "brothers, sisters and children your age".
3. **The favours pairs restate the domain lines (MAJOR, X-003).** 18 instances on 10 of 74 pages, whenever the current decade shares the chapter's function and direction (L053 repeats three of three). Examples: `mind · chapter_domain.Mind.release` with `mind · favours.decade.Mind.release`; `body · chapter_domain.Family.release` ("giving ground") with its pair ("Giving way on small things").
4. **Heavy units with no carrier (MINOR, X-011, X-012).** 7 energy units and 5 hard landings, about 160 page instances, break Q10 A. Examples: `action · decade.release.now` "You end it with a clear count of what your life costs to run."; `land.year.clash.month` "by your own choice or by theirs." A gate check for a carrier in every release, heavy, core_over, clash and punish unit would stop the drift.
5. **Repeated closes and images (MINOR, X-013 to X-017).** The superlative close ("than ever", "than any before", "than at any time") in 16 units across all five functions; "talent" in 12 of 70 Expression units, which reaches 43 pages and is five times on L028 and L034; "deadline" in 13 units; sleep or early nights in 11 Health units; "harder to break/shake/bend" in 3 Order units; the handover cure in 7 of 10 Late release lines; Order's "with ease/suits you" in 4 grow closes; "you felt" in 4 of 5 ground lines, under one fixed frame up to four times a page. None of these is wrong alone. Together they make a page read templated.
6. **The last clause leaves the reader (TWEAK-level, no finding filed per unit).** 17 units end on a clause whose subject is not the reader ("and it stayed handled", "and they noticed", "and the role sits easily on you"). 9 of them are Action or Order grow lines. Several of these are among the best lines (below), so this reader recommends the gate read "the reader is the subject of the last sentence" as its first clause, not its last.

**D2 overall:** the five functions do read as five forces. Body is standing among equals, Mind is learning and shelter, Expression is voice and appetite, Action is reach and practical life, and Order is structure and pressure. Each holds the same force across chapter, decade and year, with the exceptions in patterns 1 and 5. **D3 overall:** Mind, Action and Order poles agree on the force and differ in direction. Expression and Body blur where the release cure is the force itself.

## 4. The best and the worst

**Best**
1. `ENERGY action · decade.grow.lived.a`: "You learned to handle money, and it stayed handled." A past pattern, plain words, a quotable turn, and it is Action and nothing else.
2. `ENERGY body · chapter.hour.release.now`: "…and you are finding that a lighter grip holds more." It states the cost and the way through in one image, true to too much of one's own strength.
3. `ENERGY order · decade.release.early.b`: "A patient teacher helped you through, and you learned that slow was not the same as wrong." It fits a child, the carrier is Mind (correct), and it names a private belief in the reader's own words.
