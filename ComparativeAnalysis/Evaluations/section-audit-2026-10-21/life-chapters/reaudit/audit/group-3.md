## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **Non-contrastive ", but you" in domain lines (MAJOR, G3-001).** 26 of 68 domain units, on 21 of 31 pages. The M2 fix on the ", and you" closes swapped the joint without changing the logic. Examples: "In Career, this chapter favours finishing what you started over taking on the next offer, but each thing you finish is worth two you begin." · "In Health, this chapter favours resting before you are tired over pushing through, but you gather strength from long sleeps and slow mornings."
2. **The reader is the object, not the subject, of the last sentence (MAJOR, G3-002).** About 62 units, and 6 to 10 per page. The shared year and landing lines lean on "costs you" and "asks you", and the underneath lines on "the X on you". Examples: "the quiet costs you ease in the place you rest" · "the load on you is topped up as fast as you put it down".
3. **"Finish one thing" stands in for four functions and both poles (MAJOR, G3-003).** 16 units in Expression, Action, Body and Mind, including Expression grow. Examples: "One piece of work finished over two half started" (Action) · "one piece finished over three begun" (Expression).
4. **The current chapter's carry said three to five times on one page (MAJOR, G3-004).** 7 pages, all Expression or Mind release. Examples: L089, the pause or hour alone ×5; L078, "decision" ×3.
5. **Underneath lines repeat their frame and sometimes their sentence (not MAJOR; G3-018, G3-019, G3-028 to G3-031).** Every page opens three or four lines on "Under it", and most close on "the pressure/weight/load/strain on you". In 6 pages the same sentence appears twice. Examples: L062 drains.release.lived ×2; L089 matches.release.lived ×2.
6. **Child lines on decades that start at eleven (not MAJOR, G3-006).** 11 pages. Examples: "the same toys and the same turn" (ages 11–20, L063); "pockets full of finds" (ages 11–20, L087).

## 4. The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "Passing the work into younger hands spends that strength well, and you are finding that a lighter grip holds more." (`body · chapter.hour.release.now`, L070) The cost and the carry sit in one plain act, and the close is a line a reader keeps.
2. "Once a friend asked what you were waiting for, you started to use what you knew." (`mind · chapter.month.release.lived`, L063 L085) This names the private stall of Mind release in the reader's own words, with no image.
3. "You understood old choices better and forgave a few of them." (`mind · decade.grow.late.lived.a`, L999) It is Pattern-grade: an interior claim, flat and true to Mind grow late in life.

**Weakest**
1. "In Career, this chapter favours finishing what you started over taking on the next offer, but each thing you finish is worth two you begin." (`action · chapter_domain.Career.release`) It carries a false turn and a ranking, and it duplicates Expression's Career line.
2. "You pay for it by sharing credit with equals, and it is a price worth paying." (`body · decade.grow.now`) A gift is sold as a cost, with a stock phrase, and the reader is not the subject.
3. "Under it runs Wood, and the Metal is busy holding it down, but you carry less of its weight meanwhile." (`ground.presses.release`) It carries a false turn, a pronoun with two possible referents and a dangling "meanwhile".
