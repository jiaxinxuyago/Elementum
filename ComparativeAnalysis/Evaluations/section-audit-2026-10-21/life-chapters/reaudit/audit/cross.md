## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The close joint moved instead of thinning (MAJOR, XR-01).** 91 of 384 prose units end ", and you" (49) or ", but you" (42). In the domain lines it is 52 of 80. Per page the mean is 3.6 ", and you" plus 4.4 ", but you" closes. Most of the "but" joints have no contrast. Examples: `body · chapter_domain.Wealth.release` "but you keep more when no one else can draw on it", and `order · chapter_domain.Mind.grow` "but you think best with a plain goal". The year lines have their own version: 18 of 38 second sentences hang on ", while / , as / , though / , but".
2. **Grow lines speak the language of need (MAJOR, XR-02).** 12 non-Body grow units, plus 10 Body grow units, say "short of", "hungry", "gone without" or "lacked". On 18 pages two or three non-Body energies are each the one the chart lacks. Examples: L008 (Order "short of", Action "hungry", Expression "short of", while the chip says seek Fire, Earth), and L001 (Body "short of" in the child decades, Mind "gone without" in the current decade, Body "runs short of" in 2028).
3. **Release reads as amount (MAJOR, XR-03).** "than your chart can use" appears ×10, and ×14 with its variants, including three shared heavy year lines where AM3 governs. Examples: `order · year.heavy` "more duty than your chart can use", and `mind · chapter.hour.release.now` "your chart holds more than it can use". Its replacement, "works against your chart", is already ×10.
4. **New tic: carriers stated as possessions (MAJOR, XR-04).** 10 of 10 heavy year units end on "stay open to you", "are yours to call on", "are always within reach", "is yours to take" and the like. Examples: `body · year.core_over.b`, `order · year.heavy.b`.
5. **Hard landings stop on the cost (MAJOR, XR-05).** 13 of 18 clash, harm and punish landings have no carrier. 178 of 318 placements sit under a non-heavy year. "costs you" appears ×7 in the set and "patience" ×5. Examples: `land.year.punish.month`, `land.year.harm.year`.
6. **One carrier kit (MINOR, XR-16).** "walk" ×18, "someone close" ×11, "time alone" ×7, 25 "With X, you Y" closes, and "You learned" ×24 in the lived lines. The neutral acts M4 asked for are correct, but they have made the release closes of five functions sound alike. Examples: `body · chapter.month.release.lived`, `order · chapter.month.release.lived`.

## 4. The best and the worst

**Best**
1. `mind · chapter.hour.grow.now`: "You give good advice now because you finally take your own." This is the Pattern construct in one line: a flat claim about the reader, the mechanism, and a cost turned into growth. It is screenshot-ready.
2. `expression · decade.release.late.lived.a`: "You kept your welcome by letting the people after you finish their sentences." It names a release cost and its way through as one specific act an older reader will recognise, and it neither preaches nor wraps it.
3. `action · decade.release.early.b`: "You found ease with a cousin or a close friend, though you grew capable sooner than a child needs to." It is a cost stated plainly, as the reader's own, and it is true to excess Action in a child.

**Worst**
1. `order · year.heavy`: "Your time is claimed from above and from the side, while a quiet hour alone and someone close to talk to stay on your side." It has two senses of "side", an amount framing, the stock possession tail, and the reader is not the subject.
2. `order · chapter.month.grow.now` on L008: "and your chart is short of it". The chip on the same page names other elements to seek, so the page contradicts itself about what the chart lacks.
3. `ground.matches.release`: "a pull at both levels, but you find no softer layer underneath." It is abstract, a reader cannot check it against their week, and its joint has no logic. It is the weakest of 24 placements of the ground set.
