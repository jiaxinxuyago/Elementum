## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The false "but" in domain lines (MAJOR, G2-001).** 57 of 154 domain lines in this group close on ", but you", across 13 units. In about 18 of the 23 distinct units, nothing is contrasted. Examples: "favours making something over beating someone, but you do your best work when the effort goes into the thing itself"; "favours rest… but you gather strength from long sleeps and slow mornings". It reads as the M2 rewrite trading ", and you" for a wrong joint. ", and you" closes also remain at about three per page (88 in 30 pages, six each on L035 and L044), so M2 is only partly met.
2. **Need language baked into fixed units (MAJOR, G2-002, M8).** Ten pages call two different welcomed energies the one the chart lacks. Examples: L035 (Fire "runs short of" with Earth "runs short of") and L058 (Body "always run short of" with Mind "runs short of"). The same wording causes the "gone without" and "long been short of" contradictions with earlier periods (G2-004 to G2-006, on 5 pages).
3. **The reader is not the subject of landing lines (MAJOR, G2-003).** 21 of 31 landing units open on the event or on other people, and seven share the "costs you" frame. Examples: "A small resentment grows at home…" (14 pages) and "Your work or the part you play in the world changes sharply…" (9 pages). These units are shared with the Almanac, so this needs an owner ruling.
4. **Carry framed as an offer in heavy years (not MAJOR, G2-012).** Four year units close on "stay open to you", "are yours to call on", "are always within reach" or "is yours to take", on 22 of 30 pages. Examples: year.core_over and year.heavy.b (order). The fix is a rewrite of four units.
5. **Restatement across sections of one page (not MAJOR, G2-014 to G2-019).** A chapter's cure reappears in its domains, manual or the next decade. Examples: L031 says "talk less" six times; L046 says "decide for yourself" three times. Seven pages show it. It comes from keyed units that each carry their own cure for the same energy.
6. **Same unit twice on one page (not MAJOR, G2-013).** The ground lines and landings have no `.b` variant. This happens on 5 pages: L032, L033, L038, L042, L060.

## 4. The best and the weakest

Best:
- "With friends who knew you before any of it, you keep a part of yourself no role can claim." (`ENERGY order · chapter.month.release.now`, L036 L043) The cost is plain, the cause is owned and the door is open, in words a reader would post.
- "You give good advice now because you finally take your own." (`ENERGY mind · chapter.hour.grow.now`, L049 L060) This closes the gift on a quiet turn of self-recognition, as The Pattern does, without any flattery.
- "A practical project shared with a partner got you out of your head, and you left knowing that thinking needs a task." (`ENERGY mind · chapter.day.release.lived`, L055) A lived release, carried by a neutral act, ending on a line the reader keeps.

Weakest:
- "You stay busy by choice, which is a different thing from staying busy." (`ENERGY action · chapter.hour.grow.now`, L051) The sentence cancels itself on its own close.
- "In Career, this chapter favours one commitment over several, but you do your best with fewer people to answer to." (`ENERGY order · chapter_domain.Career.release`, 6 pages) The "but" promises a contrast that never comes, the commonest defect in the group.
- "Your time is claimed from above and from the side, while a quiet hour alone and someone close to talk to stay on your side." (`ENERGY order · year.heavy`, 10 pages) "Side" appears twice, and the reader is handed an offer instead of an act.
