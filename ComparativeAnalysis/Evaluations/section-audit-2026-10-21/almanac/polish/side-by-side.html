Year pillar changes for births in the hours around 立春. The month pillar changes for births within a day of a 节 and for 1 to 7 January. Most of the luck-pillar shift is the start age moving by a few months, because exact term dates change the day count.

What the re-audit still flags

These come from the new audit and need your ruling before anything changes. The reports are in almanac/reaudit/audit/.

  • AP1 · The "asking you" tail. 30 of the 42 energy lines checked end on a participle, often "asking you to…". The landing then says "asks" again. Proposed: the energy line ends on a full verb, and the landing is the only line that says "ask".
  • AP2 · One cost formula. 9 of 17 day landings open their second sentence the same way ("Defending it takes…", "Clearing it costs you…"). Proposed: vary the shape across the set.
  • AP3 · "Already hold". The grow lines claim the reader already has the energy ("a discipline you already keep"). A shared line cannot know that. Proposed: describe what the energy adds, not what the reader has.
  • AP4 · Soft lines still settle well. The month grow lines and the bind landings still promise good outcomes ("a calmer house", "the weeks look after you"). Proposed: hold them to AM1 the way the clash and harm lines already are.
  • AP5 · Back-to-back months repeat. Paired stems give two months in a row the same element and function, so the month energy line repeats word for word. Proposed: a second variant keyed by yin or yang (17 new lines).
  • Smaller items. The headline "Earth crowds you" uses an amount word (AM3). The pillar-return and turned-day lines run over their word budget. Two Day pages show no favours rows (I will check the resolver). On Year pages, an exact pillar repeat could count as a punish (YR-2, an engine rule).
  • Life Chapters. The Year page's energy and landing lines are theirs, and they still say "the energy you need most" and "already has too much of". I sent them a note with AM1 to AM4 (Handoffs/2026-10-19-event-signature/05_TO_LIFE_CHAPTERS.md).
