## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Soft months settle themselves (AM1), MAJOR F01.** 9 units, 18 showings on 14 pages. Examples: "Study deepens and rest comes easier, as the weeks look after you" (M024); "Each evening brings you back to a calmer house" (M083). Grow lines also praise skill the chart cannot know: "the duty you already carry well", "work you already do well".
2. **Paired months repeat the lead line, MAJOR F25.** 10 consecutive-month pairs in this sample show an identical energy line, for example M024/M026 and M083/M085. This is structural: it follows from the stem pairs.
3. **Hard bonds are the section's strength (not a defect).** All 12 clash, punish and harm landing units (20 showings) name a plain cost with no settlement, as AM1 asks. Examples: "Your old place moves under you, and the new one is not yet clear" (clash.year); "Words do little to close it, and it asks for your time instead" (harm.hour). They are the model for the F01 rebuild.
4. **The page echoes its own headline word.** 7 pages repeat a content word three or more times across headline, energy, landing and favours. Examples: "plan" ×3 (M058, M072), "reach" ×4 (M087), "work" ×4 (M008), "patience" in headline and landing (M082). Fixes: F14, F21, F22, F23.
5. **The week tic.** 22 of the 42 distinct energy and landing units name "the week(s)" ("through the weeks", "week by week", "week after week", "by the third week"), and the compound check flags "week" ×3 on 4 pages (M075, M082, M085, M107). No single unit is wrong. The fix is to vary the time frame when the units are next rebuilt (the F01 rewrites already avoid it).
6. **AM3 wording stuck onto the wrong noun.** 2 units, 5 showings: "more strain than your chart can use" (order.heavy, M018 M021) and the headline "Earth crowds you" (body.release, M042 M046 M063). Fixes: F12, F19.
