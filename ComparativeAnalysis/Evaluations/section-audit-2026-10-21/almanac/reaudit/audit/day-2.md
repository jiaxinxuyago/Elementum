## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **The "asking" tail and the doubled "ask"** (R2-01, MAJOR): 16 energy units end on a participle, 12 of them on "asking", and 6 pages carry "ask" twice. Examples: D081 "asking you to follow it through" with "it asks for your word"; D097 "asking you to let it out" with "asks you to decide".
2. **"reach" as the house's filler verb** (R2-02 to R2-04, R2-22): three ground units, the action headline "widens your reach" and action need.b repeat it, so 4 pages show "reach" ×3 or more. Examples: D093 ×4, D054 ×3.
3. **Hard repeats with no cost** (R2-11 to R2-13): all three repeat landings on the Day, Year and Hour Gates name a task instead of a cost, which breaks AM1. Repeat.month (D109, "your newer work waits behind them") shows the right shape. Examples: D097, D115.
4. **Soft bonds and stars settled in the reader's favour** (R2-09, R2-10, R2-15, R2-16, R2-17): 5 units. Examples: "Shared chores go faster", "trouble passes you lightly".
5. **Headline halves that do not match their landing** (R2-07, R2-08, R2-13): 3 headline units across 4 pages. Examples: "home comes to a decision" over a frame landing; "a deal at work settles" over a colleague and a stalled task.
6. **Word repeats inside one energy line** (R2-19 to R2-23): 5 units. Examples: "level … level", "weighs … weight".
