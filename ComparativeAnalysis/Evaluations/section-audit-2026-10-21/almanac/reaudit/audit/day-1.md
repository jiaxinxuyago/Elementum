## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Grow lines assert presence ("already").** 7 of 7 grow energy units, on 13 pages, e.g. "an energy you already hold and still welcome" (D005) and "adding to a discipline you already keep" (D050). This is a MAJOR, R1-M1. It is the mirror of AM3: the line should give the direction, never the volume.
2. **Participial tail in energy lines, with "ask" as the stock verb.** 14 of 18 energy units, e.g. "boxing you in by noon" (D006) and "asking you to follow it through" (D039). "Ask" appears in 11 of the 35 non-favours units seen, and repeats on 4 pages. MAJOR, R1-M2.
3. **One frame for landing costs (gerund, "it", cost verb).** 9 of 17 landing and star units, e.g. "Clearing it costs you a plain conversation" (D006) and "Answering it again eats into hours" (D011). MAJOR, R1-M3.
4. **Outcome endings (AM1).** 8 units say how the period ends rather than what it costs. Examples: "leaving you spent by dusk" (D004), "lands late or lighter than promised" (D012), "clearer than before" (D019). Others: "comes to a head" (echo.month, D006), "the sting stays with you through the day", "boxing you in by noon", "leaving you tired… by the day's close", "Your pace shifts before you can say why". Not MAJOR: each is local, R1-m2 to R1-m4, R1-m7, R1-m11 to R1-m13. echo.month is left for the polish round.
5. **Headline, landing and reminder say one thing three times.** Content words repeat across a page on 17 pages, e.g. D034 ("a new month begins", "A new month opens today", "The month changes today") and D011 ("returns" ×3). The headline half and the push both echo the landing's noun. A rule capping the landing's key noun at one appearance outside the landing would clear most of it.
6. **Unscored resolver note.** D033 (壬辰 on a 甲 chart) and D048 (庚申 on a 己 chart) show no favours rows. Pages with the same god and state show them (D014 壬子 shows pianyin rows; D035 庚戌 shows shangguan rows). This is worth a check in the resolver or the page builder. I could not verify it from the pages alone.
