## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Outcome promises close the friction (M1).** 11 landing units on 19 of 34 pages, plus 6 grow energy units. Examples: "You let it pass, and the warmth between you stays" (D001); "You adjust faster than the people around you" (D014, D032). This is the main reason B sits at 3: The Pattern names the conflict and stops, while these lines solve it for the reader. MAJOR.
2. **One joint and one verb carry the page (M2).** "and you" ×2 or more on 31 of 34 pages, and "you feel" in four of five ground units. Examples: D019 "and you" ×4; D006 "feel" ×3. MAJOR.
3. **Units written for one bond kind but served for several.** 2 echo units on 5 pages and 3 headline halves on 9 pages. Examples: echo.month says "clash" on a punish month (D006); echo.year says "strain … strikes" on a repeat (D044); head.land.clash.year says "old"; head.land.bind.year says "pulls at". MINOR (m1, m2, t1, t3, t4).
4. **Roles the gate does not name.** 5 units on 8 pages. Examples: "an older colleague" (Tianyi, D048); "the familiar lecture" (D023); "training others" (D014, D029); "Bending a house rule" (D010, D020); "Someone checks your work" (D050). MINOR.
5. **The Month Gate stacks "work".** "work" ×4–5 on 5 pages (D011, D015, D035, D050, plus D025 with "push/back" ×3). The causes are head.energy.action.release ("piles work"), PUSH clash.month restating the headline, and order.grow.b. Examples: D050 "Wood sharpens you, and work pushes back … Someone checks your work … A plan you settled at work … Work pushes back today". MINOR (t2, m7, m16).
6. **Favours rows that argue with the page.** 3 units on 3 pages. Examples: "Quiet company over a full house" on a family-gathers day (D016); "A careful look over a quick yes" as the grow row for The Horizon (D047). MINOR.
