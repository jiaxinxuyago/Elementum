## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **Release read as surplus** (9 pages, F01). `heavy` is sign-based, but the lines claim volume. Examples: M036 "the energy your chart already has too much of" (no Fire in the chart); M018 "a chart already full of it" (Metal only hidden).
2. **Every hard bond ends well** (16 pages, 11 of 12 hard-bond units, F02). Examples: M018 "come out of it on firmer footing"; M114 "trust returns by the month's end". The landing becomes reassurance, and B stays at 3.
3. **The joint ", and you"** (30 of 36 pages at ×2 or more, two at ×3). The ground, energy and landing units each close on it. Examples: M042 "and you meet each small push…", "and you feel it at full strength", "and you rebuild it…"; M102 three times. F04 removes one per page. The energy and landing rewrites in F01 and F02 vary the rest.
4. **Gate words stacked** (work ×3–5 on 7 pages, month ×3–4 on 7, home ×3 on 2). The headline land-half, the energy line, the ground line and the landing all name the gate. Examples: M008 "work" ×5; M083 "home" ×3 with "closer" twice. F04, F09, F10 and F22 cut most of it. A further rule: the headline land-half and the landing share no content word beyond the gate noun.
5. **Favours rows from bonds the page never names** (10 pages). Examples: M002 Family "An early word with relatives over silent hurt" (酉戌 harm, unshown); M106 Love "Breaking a home routine over the same evenings" (酉酉 repeat, unshown). The bonds are real, but the reader gets advice for an event the page never mentions. F19 shows the cost. Suggest a favours_land row shows only for the lead bond, or carries a three-word gate tag.
6. **Superlative state lines** (5 pages, F06 and F07). Examples: M086 "the energy you need most"; M090 "the structure your chart is shortest of", on the same chart one month apart.
