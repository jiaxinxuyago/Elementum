# Your Almanac · audit · Year pages and the unreached units (Y003–Y118, U999)

Auditor: sealed, read-only. Pages: `groups/year-and-unreached.md` (14 Year pages for 2026 丙午, one raw page U999 of 44 units).

**Who owns what on these pages (checked against the code, not assumed).** `almanacResolve.js` reads every Year energy line and every Year landing through `chapterUnit()`, so Life Chapters' `ENERGY_CHAPTER_UNITS` (`content/depthChaptersEnergy.js`) win wherever they exist. Life Chapters has all 14 non-even year energy keys and all 24 `land.year.*` keys. So **every "THE YEAR CARRIES" line and every year "WHERE IT LANDS" line on Y003–Y118 is a Life Chapters unit** even though it carries an Almanac key (12 of 13 differ from the Almanac's own text; `order · year.need` is word for word the same in both). On the Year pages the Almanac owns the **headline halves**, **`ground.matches`**, **`land.quiet`** and the **engine clause**. Findings on the Life Chapters lines are in §4. No "THE YEAR IN …" Domains lines appear on these pages, because they are unwritten or hidden. U999 is all Almanac. One exception is `ENERGY mind · year.grow`: Life Chapters also has that key, so the Almanac's text is never shown and the unit is dead (§4).

## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **", and you" in every page's spine (MAJOR on the Life Chapters side; t1 fixes the Almanac's part).** It appears 2–3 times on 14 of 14 Year pages. Every year energy line (13 of 13 Life Chapters units) ends "X, and you Y", `ground.matches` adds one, and 4 of 7 year landings add a third. Y003: "…and you keep your footing…" / "…and you feel it…" / "…and you see it…". Y112: "…and you get your hands on…" / "…and you feel it…" / "…and you notice…". U999 shows the same frame in 10 of 12 Almanac even lines.
2. **Horizon-shared units that speak one horizon (4 pages).** `land.quiet` says "in the day" on Y037, Y107 and Y109. `head.land.harm.month` says "a remark" on a year (Y003). Rule: units shared across Day, Month and Year must name no period, or must be split by horizon.
3. **Headline echoes the lift chip (2 pages).** Y037 has "Fire weighs on you" under "lift Weighs on you". Y111 has "Fire leans on you" under "lift Leans against you". Release halves should name the function's cost, as the good ones do ("draws on your reserves", "piles work on you").
4. **Even-state lines describe nothing happening (12 units, not MAJOR because they are rare).** Examples: "Rules stay in the background today, and you hardly notice them." · "Your obligations neither grow nor shrink, and you carry them as you always have." These are true of everyone and cannot be checked against a week (the Post-Barnum law). "neither … nor" appears in 5 of them. When they are rebuilt, give each even line one plain, checkable behaviour (for example, what the reader does with the spare margin).
5. **"year" density (6 pages ×3).** "for a whole year" (energy) + "this year" (landing) + "The year's" (engine): Y087, Y103, Y104, Y112, Y117 and Y037's family. Most of it sits in Life Chapters lines. The engine clause cannot change, so the energy opener could drop "whole" or the landing its "this year".
6. **Engine gate attribution for frames (M1).** 2 of 14 Year pages here. It hits any chart whose natal pillars hold the passing branch beside a frame partner, on every horizon.
