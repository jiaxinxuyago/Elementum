## 3 · Systemic patterns

1. **States worded as volume, not valence** (X01, X02). 17 heavy or over lines and 12 need lines, plus Life Chapters' year lines. About 25% of heavy states are thin, and 37% of charts with a need state have more than one. Examples: "the energy your chart already has too much of" (`mind · day.heavy.a`) and "the energy you need most" (`order · day.need.b`).
2. **The ", and you" second sentence, doubled by the ground line** (X03). 84 prose units, 71 of 76 energy lines and 4 of 5 ground lines use it, so it shows on every page. Examples: "…, and you feel spent by dusk" over "…, and you feel it soften as it goes" (D004). "you feel" appears in 20 units, and D006 has "feel" three times.
3. **The same words twice on one screen: head, body and push** (X12–X15, X19, X20, X23, X24, X32, X33, X47–X51). 15 units. Examples: `head.star.yuede` "effort turns trouble into gain" over "Effort turns trouble into gain today"; `head.land.bind.day` "home draws you closer" over "Home draws you in … closer than usual".
4. **One bond read two ways across its own family** (X04–X11, X18, X28). 10 units, where the headline, body, favours pair or echo says something different from the bond kind or from each other. Examples: harm.year, where the head has the relative misreading you, the body has you letting it pass, and the favours pair and month line have you speaking early; echo.month, which says "clash" under a harm landing.
5. **Month favours pairs re-saying the day pair** (X57, X58, X63–X81). 5 exact and about 17 near repeats, all between one god's day and month pair of the same domain and pole. Examples: `A good teacher` (zhengyin Mind helping, day and month); `Listening more / the last word` (shangguan Social heavy).
6. **Plans and time stamps as stock images.** "plan" appears in 29 units, and every clash day line is "a plan changes" (4 of 4). "comes loose" appears 3 times. Time-of-day closes appear 14 times ("by noon", "by dusk", "by evening", "before evening", "by the afternoon", "by the third week", "by the last week", "by the month's end" ×3, "tonight" ×2, "this evening"). Examples: `land.day.clash.day` "A plan at home changes at short notice"; `land.day.clash.year` "changes a plan without warning". This is not filed unit by unit. Suggested rule for the next pass: at most two clash lines per horizon may turn on a plan.
