## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The gate writes the beat, and the character only colours it (MAJOR: X-001 to X-004).** At the Day and Hour Gates, characters that share a gate and a domain converge on one beat. Count: 10 clusters, about 60 units. The blurs are: Love at the Day Gate (Twin, Rival, General, Virtuoso, then Steward, Magistrate, Sage); Family at the Day Gate (Artisan, Magistrate, Steward, Sage); Health at the Day Gate (Artisan, Sage, General); Family at the Hour Gate (General, Magistrate, Steward, then Twin, Rival, Virtuoso); and Wealth (Horizon and Rival). 5. **One stock register flattens the quiet characters (MINOR).** "Steady" appears in 103 units (Sage 24, Steward 23, Magistrate 13), "quiet" and "quietly" in 75, and "calm" in 41.
