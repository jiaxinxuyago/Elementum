## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The new composer dropped two round-1 rules: MAJOR (DD-001, DD-003).**
   - The first rule suppressed the gap row under a ruling decade. Without it, 15 of 25 gap rows overlap a ruling decade. Examples: D007 (the gap says "less reading and study", the Sage decade and year fill your evenings with books) and D136 ("doing more than study" against "you read and studied more than you acted").
   - The second rule kept decades inside the window their lines are written for. Without it, 12 decade rows on 12 pages read infancy in an adult voice. Examples: D202 (ages 1 to 10, "a mentor outside the usual") and D136 (ages 3 to 12, "the most important work you have done").
   - Both fixes are in `domainsResolve.js` and are not mirrored in `depthResolve.resolveDomainView`. A qa assertion per rule would stop them coming back.
2. **An unreached Hour seat leads young readers' pages: MAJOR (DD-002).** This happens on 9 pages; on 7 the Hour seat's text speaks of later life. Examples: D035 at 34, "You grow in later life by watching bold people", and D137 at 23, "You see further in later life, and faster."
3. **The pair lines ignore the classical conditions: MAJOR (DD-004).**
   - 枭神夺食 shows on 6 pages but is classically lifted on 5 of them. Examples: D100 (three wealth stars, Underfueled) and D014 (Underfueled, where the Alchemist's own decades read as helping).
   - 杀印相生 fires on 8 of 13 Overfueled charts and 伤官配印 on 2 of 5.
4. **The same idea twice, the lead and its own lived beat: not MAJOR, but worth a writing rule.**
   - About 18 pages repeat a seat's recognition cost in that seat's lived beat, or in the pair line. Examples: D083 (the Sage's "stay too long" three times) and D059 (childhood books three times). The same happens on D002, D012, D043, D048, D085, D095, D113, D136, D146, D150, D154, D158, D171 and D179.
   - Proposed rule for the next writing round: the lived beat must add a past fact the recognition does not hold, and the pair line must be a counterweight to the lead it follows.
5. **Watchers in the closes: not MAJOR.** 8 units close or turn on other people noticing, underrating or admiring the reader, against The Pattern's construct of no third party looking at the reader.
   - Examples: "and others notice before you do" (`zhengyin_month_stem.Mind.recognition`) and "Younger people noticed before you mentioned it" (`bijian_hour_stem.Growth.lived`).
   - The same move appears in "you are admired for it more than anyone says", "the people around you noticed it before you did", "the thing people remark on" and "the people around you often underrate where you are".
6. **Frames repeat across a page: not MAJOR.**
   - "You grow" opens 28 sentences across the Growth units (five on D012 and on D068), and "Your thinking" opens 8 across the Mind units (four on D007 and on D142).
   - Other recurring frames: "come out of the decade" or "come out of it" in 5 units, "left you more capable/skilled" in 4 units (three times on D048), and "restless" closes or turns 6 units. The Alchemist Mind units say "few people" or "nobody around you" shared or followed in 4 units ("people around you" three times on D178).
   - Also for the owner: 13 Hour-gate units name "younger people", "someone younger" or "the daring young", and only one says "the people after you" (Q10). Example: "You pass those ideas to younger people in plain words" (`pianyin_hour_branch.Growth.ahead`). The ruling's range covers them in spirit; whether the wording must be literal is the owner's call.
