## 3. Systemic patterns

1. **The lead seat says itself twice (MAJOR, DB-001).** 13 of 28 Wealth pages. How you are and the same seat's lived beat share the act and often the nouns. Examples: piancai_day_branch on D026 ("you say yes before you read the terms" / "by saying yes before you had read the terms"); zhengcai_month_branch on D069, where a second Steward seat in the same gate makes it a third telling ("kept to your own plan while friends chased quick gains").
2. **Text that does not fit the reader's age (MAJOR, DB-002 and DB-003).** 8 Wealth pages (plus 2 partly) show a lived decade from childhood in adult terms, and the resolver's fallback serves Hour-seat recognitions that describe later life to readers under 48 (1 page, 7 raw units). Examples: D114, "you saved steadily from regular work" for ages six to fifteen; D135, "a deal offered by someone younger" for a reader of 24. The Love lived beats and the age-neutral Steward Hour recognitions (D041, D122) show the fix can be done.
3. **One joint closes two units in three (MAJOR, DB-005).** 108 of 156 units close on ", and you …". Examples: every one of D008's six units closes on it; D180 has sixteen on one page. The cause is the gate's reader-last test, which this joint passes most easily.
4. **Banned words that got past the gate (not MAJOR; TWEAKs DB-021 to DB-027, DB-039, DB-040 and MINOR DB-010).** 7 units carry "luck" or "lucky", 2 "should" and 1 "often", across 22 of the 49 pages. Examples: D103's headline "Your luck with money improves the further you go"; the Steward heavy line's "more often than it should" on 6 pages. The cause: the Domains seat and decade units were gated by the 2026-10-17 Domains gates (`domains-template`, `domains-batch`, `domains-r2`), whose ban lists have no "luck". The word joined the gates from 2026-10-18 on. No gate bans "should", or a bare "often" or "sometimes", and `tools/voice-audit.mjs` has no "luck" either. Add the four words to the Domains gate and the voice audit and re-run them over the station.
5. **Neighbouring characters read as one force (not MAJOR; MINOR DB-009 to DB-016).** 8 unit sets on 13 pages: the Rival as the Horizon in Wealth (D105 shows both on one page), the Rival as the General and the Twin in Love (both headlines "push back"), the Steward made of the Artisan, Magistrate and Sage lines in Love, the General as the Magistrate and the Alchemist, the Sage as the Steward and the Magistrate (D999). Examples: "Your money comes in bursts, and leaves with friends" (Rival) against "Your money arrives in windfalls and leaves through friends" (Horizon); "You fall for people who push back" (Rival) against "You love people strong enough to push back" (General). Each is a local rewrite. A gate check on a shared four-word run between two characters' units of the same domain and part would catch the next one.
6. **Counsel by condition, and "Your X" as the last subject (not MAJOR; MINOR DB-017 to DB-019).** About 7 units end on "only where you have put the details in writing", "only where you set the number", "when your share and theirs are kept apart" or "when the limit is fixed before anyone asks", which turns a description into advice. Five units end with "Your plans …", "Your savings …", "Your reputation …", "Your family …" or "Your talent …" as the subject, which the gate lets through on its `^(Your|What you)` test. Examples: D114 has two "in writing" closes on one page; D999 `pianyin_month_stem · Career` closes "Your reputation travels further than your title, and it travels slowly."

Also MAJOR and not repeated above: DB-004 (食伤生财 fires without the strength condition, on 6 of 6 pages, all Underfueled) and DB-006 (every Love ahead beat promises a shared home at 35, on 10 of 10 pages).

## 4. The best and the worst

**Best**
1. "More than once a relationship cooled because your honesty arrived before your warmth, and you remember which words did it." (`shangguan_day_branch · Love.lived`, D152). The Virtuoso's cost is named as the reader's own act, with no verdict and no hedge. It is the line a reader screenshots.
2. "More than once you have stayed a little longer for the life around someone than for the person." (`piancai_day_branch · Love.lived`, D027). The Horizon in love in eighteen plain words. It is a private admission that most readers have never put into words.
3. "You have more put by than you feel you have, and you relax a little each time you check." (`zhengcai · decade_lines.Wealth.heavy.ahead`). This is 富屋贫人 (财多身弱) in plain English: a heavy decade's cost and what carries the reader through, in one sentence (its first sentence needs DB-027).

**Weakest**
1. "Careful earning paid off in those years, and you saved steadily from regular work." shown for ages six to fifteen (D114; DB-002). It is false for every reader it reaches this way, and it reads as machine-made.
2. "Your best money moments so far came through someone else, a tip or a job offer or a deal brought to you by a friend. You lost money the same way, by saying yes before you had read the terms." directly under a recognition that says the same thing (D026; DB-001). Step 2 repeats step 1.
3. "Your income has arrived unevenly from your late teens, a good year followed by a thin one. More than once you lent or spent a windfall on friends within weeks …" one block below the Horizon's "Good years and thin ones have followed each other since your late teens … More than once you have spent a windfall on friends" (D105; DB-009). Two characters, one sentence, one page.
