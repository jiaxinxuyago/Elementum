## The characters

The ten gods are called characters, always with "the": Twin 比肩 · Rival 劫财 · Artisan 食神 · Virtuoso 伤官 · Horizon 偏财 · Steward 正财 · General 七杀 · Magistrate 正官 · Alchemist 偏印 · Sage 正印.

What each stands for:
- **Twin:** self-reliance, equals.
- **Rival:** competition, sharing what you have.
- **Artisan:** ease, appetite, making things.
- **Virtuoso:** a sharp voice, talent that rebels.
- **Horizon:** range, side doors, windfalls.
- **Steward:** careful keeping, earned value.
- **General:** pressure that forges, hard tests.
- **Magistrate:** rules, duty, a role.
- **Alchemist:** strange knowledge, private study.
- **Sage:** shelter, learning, being looked after.

## The four gates

| Gate | Chapter | Ages | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year | Early | birth to 15 | the first home, family of origin |
| Month | Rising | 16 to 34 | work, study, parents and siblings |
| Day | Home | 35 to 47 | the self, a partner, the home |

A character "at" a gate sits on its stem, in plain view. A character "inside" a gate sits in its branch, out of sight.

## What a Domains page is

One domain (Love, Family, Career, Wealth, Social, Mind, Growth, Health) for one real chart, read through the characters seated in the chart that rule it. The parts, in order:
- **TITLE.** How many seats rule the domain and in which chapters, plus a headline (3 to 12 words).
- **HOW YOU ARE.** The lead seat's recognition (30 to 65 words): how the reader is in this domain now.
- **PATTERN.** Present only when a classical pairing targets the domain.
- **SEAT.** One block per seat (22 to 55 words):
  - `lived` for a gate already reached, in the past tense;
  - `ahead` for one still to come.
- **GAP.** A gap row (14 to 32 words) when the reader's current gate holds no seat in this domain.
- **NOTES.**
- **BY DECADE** (Advisor). The ten-year luck periods whose character rules the domain (18 to 45 words each). Each is either helping or heavy for this chart, and lived, now or ahead.
- **BY YEAR** (Advisor). The years of the current decade whose character rules the domain (14 to 30 words each). Rulings Y1 and Y2: one line per character and domain, the same line whether the year helps or weighs.
- **SEAT CARD (context).** The full seat reading. Score the page on the domain parts; file a finding on a seat card only if it contradicts the domain text beside it.

## What a Blueprint page is

The Advisor tier's deep decoding of one chart, in four parts:
1. **STRUCTURE (格局, 子平真诠).**
   - The structure reading (22 to 50 words).
   - WHY: the revealed-stem rule, composed by the engine from a slot template.
   - STATE: formed, under strain or broken, with the strain note.
   - WHAT PROTECTS IT: the remedy and the decades that bring it, from a slot template.
2. **RUNS ON AND LACKS.**
   - STRONGEST: the dominant elements, from a slot template.
   - MISSING: one line per absent character.
   - SEASON: the 穷通宝鉴 (调候) reading for the Day Master born in that month.
   - SEASON NEED: how the chart holds each character the season asks for (shown, rooted or hidden).
3. **SEEN AND UNSEEN.**
   - IN PLAIN VIEW: a "You come across as …" line for each character on show.
   - INSIDE: what the reader wants in private, for each character inside a branch.
   - CLOSE.
4. **PAIRINGS AND CLASHES.**
   - The classical god pairings present (TG_PATTERN).
   - The branch bonds between gates, read as their consequence: bind 六合, clash 六冲, punish 刑, harm 六害, frame 三合 and half frames, self-punish 自刑.
   - A line when there is no hard bond.

The Chinese and the labels print separately; the text carries no Chinese. Texts are keyed by character, structure, season cell or bond, never by chart, so one unit serves every chart that has its key. A unit must not lean on anything else in the chart (chart invariance). The engine fills slot templates with chart facts ({element}, {count}, {god}, {when} and others); judge the filled sentence as the reader sees it.

## The laws in force

- **The domain-only law, the past (Q9):**
  - definite past patterns tied to an age span;
  - never a single dated event, a place or a named person.
- **People (Q10):** "a partner" at the Day Gate; "the people after you" at the Hour Gate; gender-neutral everywhere (no he, she, husband, wife).
- **Hard news (Q11):**
  - allowed only when tied to a decade or a gate, and with what carries the reader through;
  - never illness, death, divorce, bankruptcy, accidents or sums of money;
  - no pain words;
  - at most one body part in a line.
- **No advice (Q15).** No imperative, no "should", no counsel line. Describe what happens and what the reader does.
- **Voice:**
  - second person, present tense (past tense for lived beats), with the reader as the subject of the last sentence;
  - no hedging: never may, might, perhaps, probably, likely, tends to, sometimes, often;
  - no digits in the text: ages and years live in the labels.
- **Punctuation:** periods and commas only, at most two commas a sentence. No colon, dash or semicolon. No italic. B1–B2 British English.
- **Banned words:**
  - delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, intricate, robust, seamless, foster, underscore, showcase, leverage, vibrant, nestled, profound, realm, unlock, elevate, resonate, navigate, landscape, journey;
  - trauma, trigger, boundaries, attachment style, regulate, nervous system;
  - destiny, fate, karma, cosmic, universe, palace, pillar, ten gods, BaZi, day master, luck.
- **Locked relation words:** "feeds you · shapes you · you feed it · you shape it · more of you". Never "tames".

## Accuracy (A1) carries the most weight

Check that the BaZi logic holds, and cite the classical source for each accuracy finding (子平真诠, 滴天髓, 穷通宝鉴, 三命通会, 渊海子平):
- **Each character reads as itself.** A Steward line must not read as the Horizon, a General line must not read as the Magistrate, an Alchemist line must not read as the Sage.
- **Each domain reads as itself.** Wealth is money, Family is relatives and home, Career is work, Social is friends and circles, Mind is thinking and learning, Growth is becoming more yourself, Health is the body, Love is a partner and romance.
- **A gate's beat fits its ages and life area.** An Early Chapter lived beat fits a child.
- **Tense.** A lived line is in the past; a coming line is in the future.
- **Helping and heavy.** A helping decade must not read as a burden; a heavy decade names its cost and what carries the reader through.
- **The structure reading fits its 格.** For example, 正官格 is rank and rules, 七杀格 pressure, 食神格 ease and making, 伤官格 a rebellious talent, 正财格 steady earning, 偏财格 range, 正印格 shelter and learning, 偏印格 strange knowledge, 建禄格 self-made, 月刃格 and 月劫格 the Rival.
- **The STATE note.** It fits its breaker and the classical rule:
  - 比劫 on a wealth structure;
  - 伤官见官;
  - 枭神夺食;
  - 官杀混杂;
  - 财坏印;
  - a self structure with nothing to use its strength on.
- **The SEASON reading matches 穷通宝鉴** for that Day Master in that month: the elements asked for, in order.
- **A bond line fits its gates and its kind:**
  - bind: a tie that holds both;
  - clash: a change from tension;
  - punish: repeated friction;
  - harm: a quiet irritation;
  - frame: an alliance.
- **No contradictions on a page.** The structure, the state, the pairings, the seen lines and the domain beats must not contradict each other.
- **No claim the chart cannot know.**

## Adapted D criteria

- **D1, section roles.** Each part does its own job:
  - How you are: now;
  - the seat beats: past and future by gate;
  - the decades and years: their own periods;
  - Blueprint 1: what the chart is built around;
  - Blueprint 2: its balance;
  - Blueprint 3: public against private;
  - Blueprint 4: its tensions.

  No part pre-empts or repeats another on the same page.
- **D2, character truth.** The same character reads as the same force wherever it appears: in a seat, a decade, a year, a seen line, a missing line or a season need. The ten read as ten different forces.
- **D3, pole coherence.** The helping and heavy versions of one character's decade agree on what the force is and differ only in whether the chart wants it. The lived and ahead beats of one seat agree on what the seat is.
