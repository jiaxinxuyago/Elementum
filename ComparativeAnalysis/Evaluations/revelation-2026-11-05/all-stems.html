In context: the Vine

Chart B (1990-09-17, 05:00), a Wood core, assembled from the drafts below. Five of its ten cards: the Naming, who the Vine is, its heaviest energy (Metal, 74%), its need (Water, 0%) and its type.

The Naming

Your Day Master

The Vine

Yin Wood, the green that climbs and finds a way.

You get where you're going, one way or another.

Your Day Master is the element of the day you were born, read as your core self. It is not your year animal.

Who the Vine is

Wood is the element of growth. It reaches, builds and keeps going.

As a Vine, you are that patient reach in a person. You might notice:

  • You change the route without changing where you're headed.
  • You bend where others break, and arrive intact.
  • You find the one opening in a wall and grow through it.

People read you as easygoing. Inside, you are working three routes at once.

You find a way around what stops other people, and they call it luck.

Metal · your Order · 74% · Release it

Metal is the blade: the pruning edge that shapes a tree.

Order is the rules, deadlines and people you answer to.

In you. You have a great deal. Pressure from outside sits on you most days, and you hold yourself to every standard.

You might notice you check a rule twice before you bend it.

Try this: Let one rule go this week and see what holds.

Water · your Mind · 0% · Grow it (your need)

Water is rain and river: it soaks the roots that feed a tree.

Mind is how you take things in: learning, thinking, rest, the people you lean on.

In you. You have none of it in your chart. You learn by doing, and rest feels like lost time.

You might notice you start a task before you have read the instructions.

Try this: Give yourself one quiet hour a day to read or rest.

Your type

The Vine · Order mode

Order mode: you hold yourself to rules and to the standards others set.

Your chart leans on Order. The Water you need is rest and support: time to take things in.

Start here: Give yourself one quiet hour a day to read or rest.

Oceans and Rains carry the Water you need. Who in your life is one?
