Flagged issue · CG1

Labels without ground

"They don't understand what Blade, Earth or Mind energy means. They need more description … how their mind works like earth, and what earth represents, so that to relate to themselves."

Why it happens. One card stacks the Blade, Earth, Mind and "feeds" and defines each with the next ("Your Mind is where the world soaks in and becomes yours"). The definitions are poetic, not something you can check against your week. The 33% is never turned into "you gather more than most". Our grounded writing already exists ("Earth is the bank and the bed, the ground that keeps water to one course"; "Being vague feels worse to you than being wrong"), but it sits deep in the energy pages and the Day Master page.

What the research found. 16Personalities never shows a code without its plain word, then gives a nickname, a one-line tagline and about 750 words about you, with contrast and named struggles. The Pattern uses no system words at all, just human trait titles, and reviewers call it "scarily accurate". FateTell explains each Day Master with one image from nature, then disclaims that it describes personality. Nobody links an image to a trait to a behaviour you would notice. That chain is open ground for us.

How to rewrite the revelation

  1. Recognition before definition. The first sentence after your name is a true thing about you: "You'd rather say the true thing than the easy one."
  2. Every label climbs a ladder. What it is in nature, what it runs in a life, what your amount looks like in you, a moment you'd notice, and one thing to try.
  3. One idea per card, meaning before numbers. The percentage and the bar confirm a sentence you already understood.
  4. Teach the model once. "Everyone holds all five elements. In you, each one runs a part of your life", with all five functions in plain words, before any single energy.
  5. Name the strength and its cost, then validate. Bring the locked line "You say what others soften, then quietly pay for being the one who did" forward, with a contrast: "People see someone sharp and certain. Inside, you are checking every word for whether it is true."
  6. Say "Day Master" once, with what it is and that it is not your year animal.
  7. End on use and on people. Explain the type in a sentence, make the need an action, and ask "Who in your life is one?" as the share hook.
  8. Five named ideas on the first pass: Day Master, element, the five energies as functions, your type, your circle. Overfueled, Friction and Catalyst, and the relation verbs move behind a tap.
