- **(b) 3/5.** The plate and the stage are memorable, but "The Blade" has no meaning attached until Day Master card 2, which is behind a tap after the revelation.
- **(c) 3/5.** The five energies and their percentages are shown on the Naming and on every energy card. The reader sees "what I have" but not what any of it is.
- **(d) 1/5.** "The Blade · Mind mode" is unexplained. "Mind mode" reads as a setting, not a self.

### Comprehension · 5 / 25

- **(a) 1/7.** The revelation alone introduces:
  - the Blade and Yang Metal;

  - "Earth is your Mind energy" (an element mapped to an abstract noun);

  None of the three rungs touches the reader's own week.

### Resonance · 7 / 25

- **(a) 2/8.** The first 30 seconds are a name, a painting and five percentages. The first sentence about the reader ("You are the Blade, the Metal that has already been through fire.") is a metaphor about the sign, not a recognisable truth about the person.

- **The Day Master page's middle cards:** "Your nature" (the inscription, the portrait, "What people notice") is our best resonance writing. It just arrives too late.

## 4 · The flagged issue · CG1 · Labels without ground

**What the owner found:** "NONE of these revelation pages echoes with me … my friends don't understand what Blade, Earth or Mind energy means. They need more description … how their mind works like earth, and what earth represents, so that they can relate to themselves."

**What the research says:**
- **16Personalities never shows a code without its plain word.** Every letter has a word and a definition, and every type has a nickname plus a one-line tagline, before 750 words of "you" prose.
- **The Pattern uses no system terms at all.** Its trait cards have human titles ("Sensitive & Over Giving").
- **FateTell explains each Day Master with one image from nature**, then disclaims that the image says nothing about personality, so it stops at the image.
- **Nobody links an image to a trait and then to a behaviour.** That three-step chain is open ground, and it is exactly what the owner asked for.

**The root cause, in our content:**
1. **Labels stack:** a Day Master (the Blade), an element (Earth), a function (Mind) and a relation (feeds) on one card, each defined by the next.
2. **The definitions are poetic, not observable.** "Where the world soaks in" can't be checked against a Tuesday.
3. **No line converts the amount into behaviour.** 33 % is a number, not "you gather more than most".
4. **The grounded writing exists but is buried.**
   - `pairs.js` `advise_friction`: "Earth is the bank and the bed, the ground that keeps water to one course … Keep one evening a week at home with nothing planned".
   - The `dm_claims`.
   - The Day Master page's portrait.

   None of it reaches the first pass.

## 5 · How to rewrite the revelation (proposal)

### The principles (each from the research or the owner)

1. **Recognition before definition (16P, The Pattern).** The first sentence after the name is a checkable truth about the reader, not about the sign.
2. **The explanation ladder (CG1, our open ground).** Every label climbs five rungs:
   - **nature:** "Earth is the ground: it holds and it absorbs";
   - **function:** "Mind is how you take things in: learning, rest, the people you lean on";
   - **in you, from the amount:** "You have a lot of it. You gather and prepare more than most";
   - **you might notice:** one concrete moment;
   - **try this:** one small action.
3. **One new idea per card; meaning before numbers.** The percentage and the bar confirm a sentence the reader has already understood.
4. **Teach the model once (16P's Roles).** One card says "Everyone holds all five elements. In you, each runs a part of your life", with all five functions in plain words, before any single energy.
5. **Name the strength and its cost together, then validate (16P, The Pattern).** Promote the locked inscription and claims to the revelation.
6. **Say "Day Master" once, with what it is and what it is not:** "the element of the day you were born, read as your core self; not your year animal" (`BIZ_07` §7 #5).
7. **End on use and relationship (Pillar 3, research §cross-product).** The type card says what the mode means in a sentence, names the need as a concrete action, and points to "Who completes your chart" (the circle) as the share hook.
8. **Spend the vocabulary budget on five ideas:** the Day Master, the element, the five energies (as functions), the type, and the circle. Overfueled, Friction and Catalyst, and the five relation verbs move behind a tap or after the first reading. Grow and Release survive only as the label on a "Try this".
