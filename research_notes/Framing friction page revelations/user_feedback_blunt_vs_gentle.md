# How users of astrology and personality apps react to blunt versus gentle negative content

Scope note: these notes collect user and critic reactions, 2019-2026. Most user quotes come from press pieces, App Store pages, or Chinese media write-ups. They are anecdotes, not a representative sample. Direct fetches of Reddit, Google Play, The Atlantic, Repeller, Junkee and Scout Magazine failed (blocked, 403, 404 or DNS), so Reddit-specific material is thin. See the Gaps sections.

## 1. Co-Star: the "mean", "brutal", "passive-aggressive" push notifications

### Takeaway
Co-Star's bluntness was the meme engine. Users screenshot "being dragged" and share it, and many describe the sting as wanted ("hurt so good", "sometimes you just need that"). A visible minority turn notifications off or call them harmful. The company itself admitted in 2019 that some lines "go a bit too far" and rewrote them. The founder frames the tone as friend-talk and satire ("we will troll you a little bit"). The blunt lines that land are short, imperative and behavioural ("Correct all the time-wasting at your job"). The ones that draw backlash are ominous, contextless or fatalistic ("Your eternal rest nears. Goodbye.").

### Inferences
- Bluntness clearly drove virality: screenshots, "dragged" tweets, and memes the CEO said staff were "laughing at". It also drove a small, vocal opt-out ("too damn intense") and a harm critique. The press record shows no evidence of mass churn caused by tone. No retention data was found.
- Accepted bluntness: specific, behavioural, aimed at an action today, read as a friend's tease, and dismissible. Rejected bluntness: fatalistic (death, "not worth it"), contextless, with no explanation or way forward, or tone-deaf to world events.
- Co-Star moved away from vague copy toward bluntness because users said vague was worse. This is early evidence that blandness also costs something.

## 2. The Pattern: "scarily accurate", "called me out", "made me cry"

### Takeaway
The Pattern's negative content is accepted as "accurate" because it is written in plain psychological language with no zodiac jargon. It pairs strengths with habits and describes inner experience ("difficult to feel a sense of security") rather than verdicts. Founder Lisa Donovan says she tested readings on real people. The main complaint is not that it is insulting. It is that repeated reminders of the same unhealthy patterns, with no advice, feel "negative". Repetition and paywall complaints outnumber tone complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Bustle (Carolyn Steber, 15 May 2024), "I Tried The Pattern App & It's As 'Scarily Accurate' As They Say": "the deeper I read the more seen I felt", "everything was just so true", "this was also scarily true". She said it felt like it was "reading me to filth". Negative lines she accepted: she might "hesitate to commit"; she is "easily distracted" when not at her best; "At times, it can be difficult to feel a sense of security". Downsides she named were product issues, not tone: "a bit boring and clinical" and a paywall. — [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)
- Bustle founder profile: Donovan tested her readings "on everyone from friends to Uber drivers"; growth to about 18M profiles came almost entirely by word of mouth; praised as "scarily accurate" by Issa Rae, Channing Tatum and "SZA's therapist". A user said they didn't believe in astrology but couldn't stop checking. (Search snippet.) — [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/rule-breakers/the-pattern-lisa-donovan-made-the-best-astrology-app)
- Hattie Crisell's 2023 profile: the app avoids astrology's language and symbols. "You won't find references to Aquarius or Capricorn." (Search snippet.) — [Hattie Crisell](https://www.hattiecrisell.com/new-blog/2023/7/7/meet-pattern-astrology-founder-lisa-donovan)
- Newsweek (2019) covered Channing Tatum publicly "freaking out" over its accuracy, which reportedly drove an outage. — [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099)
- Sponsored TikTok framing uses "called out" as a selling point: "Ever been called out by #thepattern? It's one of the most accurate astro apps I've used!" (a #patternpartner ad). — [TikTok @yourmomshoroscope](https://www.tiktok.com/@yourmomshoroscope/video/7096546421469547822?lang=en)
- Holisticism calls it "spookily accurate" and "downright freaky". It recommends Sanctuary if The Pattern feels "a little too real". (Search snippet.) — [Holisticism](https://www.holisticism.com/journal-library/these-5-astrology-apps-give-spookily-accurate-horoscope-readings)
- US App Store (4.0 stars, about 16K ratings at fetch time) — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727?see-all=reviews):
  - "Uncanny" (30 Apr 2021): "This app is surprisingly accurate. Not just once in a while, constantly."
  - "Good but could be better" (8 Jan 2022): "explains parts of my character in ways that are uncannily precise", but "it mainly focuses on telling you about them, instead of offering a ton of advice to help" and "I just get a recycling of the same messages."
  - "PLEASE fix this problem! But great app!" (15 Oct 2020): "it could all be written in less of a negative tone"; "it does start to feel negative to be reminded of the same unhealthy patterns".
  - "OMG this app is even better than it was before!" (10 Mar 2024): "I laughed, I cried than laughter while crying"; "how deeply accurate and well written the information was".

  - Complaints are dominated by repetition, paywall and AI changes: "every week you get the same prompts basically" (Feb 2023); "Feeling wary about new AI chat feature… Use with caution" (Aug 2025).

### Inferences
- Plain, jargon-free, interior description ("you may hesitate to commit") reads as being seen. The flaw is framed as a pattern with a cause, not a judgement. Pairing it with strengths prevents the "insult" reading.
- The failure mode is unresolved negativity: naming the same flaw repeatedly with no path forward. Users ask for advice, not softer words.

## 3. CHANI and Sanctuary: gentle, therapeutic or supportive tone

### Takeaway
CHANI's supportive, mindfulness-and-justice tone is praised as emotionally honest ("acknowledges… emotional complexity without bypassing it"). Criticism targets preachiness or politics and repetition, more than vagueness. Sanctuary is positioned in press as the softer alternative to The Pattern. Its complaints are about pricing and live-chat billing, not tone. I found little direct evidence of "fluffy/Barnum" complaints about either app.

### Cited Findings
- A CHANI review (competitor astrology blog, 2026) says the content acknowledges "systemic stress, collective difficulty, and emotional complexity" without bypassing it. Another praises "the finest human-written astrological content" and its mix of astrology and mindfulness. (Search snippets; possibly biased sources.) — [Aurae Astrology](https://www.auraeastrology.com/blog/chani-app-review-2026-an-astrologers-honest-opinion); [Nostradamu](https://nostradamu.com/compare/chani-review)

(Search snippets.) — [App Store CHANI reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chani-your-astrology-guide/id1532791252?see-all=reviews); [Horoscopevibes](https://horoscopevibes.com/blog/chani-accurate-honest-2026-review)

## 4. 16Personalities/MBTI and Enneagram: weaknesses, sins, unhealthy levels

### Takeaway
Personality-type communities treat naming flaws as the hook: "This callout hurt me" TikToks, "unhealthy [type]" catalogues with labels like "The Manipulator". Truity markets the Enneagram's "internal look at your worst fear" as its appeal. Critics, notably Chinese commentary on MBTI, say mainstream results are flattering ("never offending or criticizing") and that users then use type labels to excuse flaws. Barnum research says mild flaws inside a flattering profile raise perceived accuracy.

### Cited Findings
- Psychology Junkie's "The Unhealthy Versions of Every Enneagram Type" gives blunt nicknames such as "The Manipulator" (unhealthy 2) and "The Egomaniac" (unhealthy 3). (Search snippet.) — [Psychology Junkie](https://www.psychologyjunkie.com/the-unhealthy-versions-of-every-enneagram-type/)
- Riso-Hudson levels run from "liberated" down to "pathologically unhealthy". - Religious-studies professor Fran Grace: in chaotic times people crave tools "that help us change the one thing we can control — ourselves". University of Michigan psychologist Robin Edelstein sees value mainly as a conversation starter. (Search snippets.) — [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/enneagram-having-moment-thank-millennials-100039047.html); [U-Michigan](https://prod.lsa.umich.edu/psych/news-events/all-news/faculty-news/are-you--the-achiever--or--the-enthusiast---why-so-many-people-i.html)
- The Paper (澎湃, 7 Apr 2022) on MBTI: results "总体上传递着取悦、赞美和'能够理解'的意思，永远不会令人感到冒犯和被批判" ("convey flattery, praise and 'being understood', never making people feel offended or criticised"). A user: "虽然结果有差别，但每次看解析，都觉得挺准" ("results differ, but every time I read the analysis it feels quite accurate"). — [澎湃新闻](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_17499318)
- Self-labelling or excuse example (same source), a user: "我持续性焦虑、敏感多疑、内耗严重，是因为我是INFP。这种人格的特征就是这样，所以我很正常。" ("I have persistent anxiety, am suspicious and suffer heavy inner friction because I'm an INFP. That's just how this type is, so I'm normal.") The author warns: "'工具'变成了'目的'，人被测试结果反向控制" ("the tool becomes the goal; people are controlled by their results"). — [澎湃新闻](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_17499318)
- Barnum mechanism: profiles mix flattering traits with mild flaws, and people "interpret the minor criticisms as proof of the description's honesty". Weaknesses framed as humble-brags inflate acceptance. Forer's 1949 profile ("While you have some personality weaknesses, you are generally able to compensate for them") was rated 4.30/5 for accuracy. — [Atticus Li](https://atticusli.com/replication-crisis/forer-barnum-effect/); [Wikipedia: Barnum effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnum_effect)

### Inferences
- In type communities, plainly named flaws are a source of identification and humour ("called out"), because the flaw belongs to the type, not to the individual. The type absorbs some of the blame.
- The harm mode here is less hurt and more self-labelling and excuse-making: "I'm normal because I'm INFP".

- No direct r/mbti or r/Enneagram threads or 16Personalities user reviews retrieved. No evidence found on whether users prefer type descriptions that name flaws plainly over flattering ones; the claim rests on meme culture and the Barnum literature.

## 5. Evidence of harm and content-design responses

### Takeaway
Experimental evidence shows negative horoscopes measurably worsen interpretation, cognitive performance and creativity compared with positive ones. The mood effect appears only among believers. Anecdotal harm reports cluster around ominous, contextless lines. The only documented content-design response found is Co-Star rewriting lines that "go a bit too far". No content warnings or opt-in "harsh mode" were documented among these apps, though Chinese users built their own opt-in harsh mode through prompts (section 6).

### Cited Findings
- Clobert, Van Cappellen, Bourdon & Cohen (2016), *Personality and Individual Differences* 101:348-355. Across three experiments (N about 195, 189 and 193), positive versus negative forecasts led to more positive interpretation of ambiguous events, better cognitive performance and more creativity. Positive horoscopes reduced negative emotions only among astrology believers. Effects were stronger for people with low internal locus of control. — [ASU/Elsevier](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/good-day-for-leos-horoscopes-influence-on-perception-cognitive-pe)
- Feedback research: low-self-esteem individuals accept negative feedback more than high-self-esteem ones. Lower self-esteem relates to low mood after non-applicable negative feedback (search snippet summarising a review). — [Brown 2010 PDF](https://faculty.washington.edu/jdb/articles/Brown%20(2010,%20Once%20more%20with%20feeling).pdf)
- Co-Star harm anecdotes: "too damn intense" (opt-out); "death omens and negativity"; "alarming without context… anxiety-inducing". — [Yahoo/Cosmopolitan](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/meet-banu-guler-ceo-co-150000063.html); [Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/school/ucsc/why-co-star-deserves-no-stars-horoscope-horror-story/); [birthchart.app](https://birthchart.app/blog/co-star-app-review-2026-honest/)
- Company response: Co-Star "changed a lot of the content" after seeing some lines "go a bit too far" (2019). — [Jezebel](https://www.jezebel.com/why-are-co-stars-daily-notifications-so-rude-1833747335)
- The Pattern user asks for "less of a negative tone" and advice rather than repeated reminders. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727?see-all=reviews)
- A DeepSeek user asked for a vicious reading and was left emo late at night. This is paraphrased in the article, not quoted. — [澎湃新闻 2025-03-06](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30306283)

### Inferences
- The experiments suggest the most vulnerable readers are believers and people with low internal locus of control (fatalistic readers), which is likely much of an astrology or BaZi audience. Negative fate framing, which implies "you can't change this", is riskier than negative trait or pattern framing with agency.

### Gaps
- No documented content warnings or "gentle/blunt mode" settings in Co-Star, The Pattern, CHANI or Sanctuary were found. No clinical or case data on astrology-app-induced anxiety beyond opinion pieces.

## 6. Chinese-language feedback (小红书 / 知乎 / 澎湃: 八字, AI算命, MBTI)

### Takeaway
In early 2025 Chinese users actively requested 毒舌 (venomous-tongued) BaZi readings from DeepSeek. Harsh outputs went viral and comment sections filled with requests for the prompt. Users called them "嘴太毒" (too vicious) while still finding them "挺准" (quite accurate). Harshness, when opted into and comic, was entertainment. Reception split evenly between "准得可怕" (terrifyingly accurate) and "胡说八道" (nonsense), and critics attribute the accuracy feeling to the Barnum effect.

### Cited Findings
- Prompt users shared: "要说话犀利、一针见血，别拐弯抹角，用最直白、狠辣的语言回复我" ("speak sharply, hit the nail on the head, no beating around the bush, reply in the bluntest, most ruthless language"). — [数英 Digitaling, 2025-02-12](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1316496.html)
- Sample viral outputs: "年轻时犟成驴，中年穷成狗，晚年睡天桥骂老天不开眼" ("stubborn as a donkey in youth, poor as a dog in middle age, sleeping under a bridge cursing heaven in old age"). Another: "改命偏方：争取猝死在工位上，至少能讹一笔丧葬费" ("fate-changing remedy: try to drop dead at your desk, at least you'll get funeral money"). — [数英](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1316496.html)
- Reception: "这些毒舌的回复在社交平台上频频被网友点赞，评论区都是求调教指令的" ("these venomous replies get liked constantly; comment sections are full of people asking for the prompt"). People anticipate the "暴击" (critical hit). — [数英](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1316496.html)
- User 涂冰: "就是嘴太毒了，说我的八字是老天赏土吃" ("it's just too venomous, it said my chart means heaven feeds me dirt"). Yet also: "我觉得算得挺准的，甚至算出了我和前男友分手的时间" ("I think it's pretty accurate, it even got when I broke up with my ex"). — [数英](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1316496.html); [新浪科技](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-02-11/doc-inekccea2631873.shtml)
- Scale: the 小红书 "DeepSeek算命" topic had nearly 20M views and more than 110k discussions (search snippet). — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250226A04FPG00)
- On 小红书, comment sections split: half find AI "准得可怕" (terrifyingly accurate), half "胡说八道" (nonsense). — [澎湃新闻 2025-03-06](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30306283)
- 少数派 (sspai) argues the "太准" feeling comes from unverifiable vague predictions, i.e. the Barnum effect. It documents chart-calculation errors and answers changing on refresh. — [少数派](https://sspai.com/post/97667)
- MBTI results are seen as never offending, and labels are used to normalise flaws (see section 4). — [澎湃新闻 2022](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_17499318)

### Inferences
- The 毒舌 trend is strong evidence that a sizeable segment wants bluntness. It was opt-in, comic and hyperbolic, so the harshness worked as entertainment, not judgement. Even then, one user found it "too venomous" and another felt emo, so the boundary is personal.
- "说中" (being hit by an accurate reading) and "扎心" appeal coexist with skepticism. Accuracy is the currency, and harshness amplifies the felt accuracy when the content is specific (e.g. the breakup timing).

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve specific 知乎 or 豆瓣 threads on "算命太准/太扎心", nor reviews of dedicated 八字 apps (e.g. 问真八字, 测测). The word "扎心" did not appear verbatim in the fetched articles.
