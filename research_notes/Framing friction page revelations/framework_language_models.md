# How Established Frameworks Phrase a Person's Flaws: Language Models for a "Friction" Section

Scope note: verbatim excerpts are kept short because the sources are copyrighted. Direct fetches of 16personalities.com returned HTTP 403, so the 16P quotes below come from search-engine text of the 16P pages and have not been checked against a live page. Hogan, Gallup, Enneagram Institute, Bustle and Gizmodo were fetched directly. The research ran to about 20 tool calls, so some areas are thin; those are listed under Gaps.

## 1. 16Personalities: how "Weaknesses" are written (INTJ, ENFP)

### Takeaway
16P gives each weakness a short adjective heading ("Arrogant", "Overly Critical", "Unfocused"). The prose under the heading then softens it. Each flaw is traced back to a strength, written in the third person ("INTJs", "these personalities"), hedged with "can", and closed with a cost or a concession. It reads as candid in the heading and gentle in the body.

### Cited Findings
- INTJ weakness headings include "Arrogant", "Dismissive of Emotions" and "Overly Critical". [16Personalities INTJ S&W](https://www.16personalities.com/intj-strengths-and-weaknesses) (search text)
- INTJ "Arrogant", verbatim from search text: "INTJs might be knowledgeable, but they're not infallible. Their self-assurance can blind them to useful input from other people. These personalities can also come across as needlessly harsh or single-minded in trying to prove others wrong." Devices: a concession followed by "but", the strength (self-assurance) named as the cause, "can", and "come across as" (a perception, not a verdict). [16Personalities INTJ](https://www.16personalities.com/intj-strengths-and-weaknesses)
- INTJ "Dismissive of Emotions": "Unfortunately, ignoring emotion is its own type of bias – one that can cloud this personality type's judgment." This turns the type's own value (rationality) back on itself. [16Personalities INTJ](https://www.16personalities.com/intj-strengths-and-weaknesses)
- INTJ "Overly Critical": "When the people in their lives fail to match their level of restraint, INTJs can appear scathingly critical. But this criticism can be unfair – based on arbitrary standards rather than a full understanding of human nature." Devices: a "When…" trigger, "appear", then a plain judgement ("can be unfair"). [16Personalities INTJ](https://www.16personalities.com/intj-strengths-and-weaknesses)
- ENFP weakness headings include "Unfocused", "Disorganized", "Overly Accommodating" and "Overly Optimistic". [16Personalities ENFP S&W](https://www.16personalities.com/enfp-strengths-and-weaknesses) (search text)
- ENFP "Unfocused" opens with praise before the flaw: "The thrill of a new project – especially one that involves collaborating with other people – can bring out the best in these personalities. But ENFPs are known for having ever-evolving interests." [16Personalities ENFP](https://www.16personalities.com/enfp-strengths-and-weaknesses)
- The search engine's summaries of other ENFP entries (paraphrase, not verbatim): optimism "can tip into naive choices, such as trusting people who haven't earned it". Without boundaries they "can take on too much". Avoiding routine chores "can become a significant source of stress". [16Personalities ENFP](https://www.16personalities.com/enfp-strengths-and-weaknesses)
- For comparison, Crystal Knows writes the same flaw with a reason attached: "Because INTJs tend to be direct and honest when communicating their thoughts, they can be perceived as arrogant, at times." [Crystal Knows INTJ weaknesses](https://crystalknows.com/personality-type/intj/weaknesses)

### Inferences
- The 16P formula is: [blunt adjective heading] → [credit the strength or concede] → "But / Unfortunately" → [trait] "can" [verb] → [cost, often to others or to the person's own goals]. The heading does the candid work and the body takes the sting out.
- 16P writes in the third person ("INTJs", "these personalities"). This keeps the reader at a distance. Second person ("you") would hit harder.
- Perception verbs ("come across as", "appear", "be perceived as") place the flaw in how others see the person, not in what the person is. This softens the line, but it also names the cost to others, which keeps it honest.

### Gaps
- Live verbatim text for every ENFP entry could not be checked (403). Premium report wording (e.g. "Strategies" sections) was not retrieved.

## 2. Enneagram (Enneagram Institute / Riso-Hudson levels; Truity)

### Takeaway
The Riso-Hudson Levels of Development are the strongest device found for making a flaw a state rather than an identity. Unhealthy behaviour sits further down a nine-step range ("Average" 4–6, "Unhealthy" 7–9) that people move along. The level descriptions are blunt, though, and go as far as "completely ruthless".

### Cited Findings
- Type 8 Basic Fear: "Of being harmed or controlled by others". Flaws are rooted in a fear, not in badness. [Enneagram Institute Type 8](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/type-8)
- The brief Type 8 description hedges: Eights "can also be ego-centric and domineering." [Enneagram Institute Type 8](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/type-8)
- The Type 8 level descriptions get steadily blunter. Level 5: "Begin to dominate their environment, including others." Level 6: "Become highly combative and intimidating to get their way." Level 7: "Defying any attempt to control them, become completely ruthless." Level 8: "Develop delusional ideas about their power, invincibility, and ability to prevail." Level 9: they "may brutally destroy everything that has not conformed to their will." Each level opens with a verb of change ("Begin to", "Become", "Develop"). [Enneagram Institute Type 8](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/type-8)
- The Levels page frames this as a state: "healthy traits can deteriorate into average traits and possibly into unhealthy ones". Moving between Levels happens "when we change states within our personality". "We are shifting within the spectrum of motivations, traits, and defenses". The Levels are "a measure of our capacity to be present". At lower Levels there is "less and less real freedom and less real consciousness", while at higher ones "we are freer and less driven by compulsive, unconscious drives". People shift toward being "clearer, more free, grounded" or toward being "anxious, resistant, reactive". The Levels are "a continuous guide to self-observation". [Enneagram Institute: Levels of Development](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/?p=21)
- The Levels were found by Don Riso in 1977 and developed with Russ Hudson in the 1990s. Their book *The Wisdom of the Enneagram* gives each type a "Wake-Up Call" and "Red Flag". [Enneagram Institute search result](https://www.enneagraminstitute.com/?p=21); [book listing](https://www.exlibris.ch/de/buecher-buch/english-books/don-riso/wisdom-of-the-enneagram/id/9780553378207/)

### Inferences
- The Enneagram's candour comes from the scale. It can be very harsh at Levels 7–9 because the frame says "this is where the pattern goes if left unchecked", not "this is you".
- "Wake-Up Call" and "Red Flag" are early-warning devices: a recognisable small behaviour that signals the slide starting. A friction section could use the same idea, naming the first sign rather than the worst case.
- Process verbs ("Begin to", "Become") describe a slide in motion, not a fixed trait.

### Gaps
- The verbatim wording of the Wake-Up Call and Red Flag lines and the "passions"/"fixations" vocabulary (e.g. Eight's passion "Lust") were not retrieved. Truity's Enneagram pages were not fetched.

## 3. Gallup CliftonStrengths "balcony/basement"; Hogan derailers; DiSC "under stress"

### Takeaway
All three corporate tools build flaws on the same base: a strength that is overused, misused or put under pressure. Gallup is the gentlest ("basement", "misapplied"). Hogan is the bluntest: its derailer descriptors are flat adjectives ("devious", "passive-aggressive", "indifferent to the feelings of others"). DiSC conditions every flaw on stress ("under stress… may become").

### Cited Findings
- Gallup: balconies are "the positive characteristics of a theme". Basements are "the potential vulnerabilities of a theme, when misapplied or managed in an unhealthy or disengaged manner". "Any strength used in an unhealthy manner can sometimes be a weakness". "A strength itself can sometimes be a stumbling block on the path to accomplishment". Its example basement for Adaptability is "the inability to stay the course due to being easily distracted by shifting circumstances". [Gallup: Balconies and Basements](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/251207/balconies-basements-strength-ever-problem.aspx)
- Gallup's remedy is to use another strength: "To avoid the basement of one theme, lean into another theme!" [Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/251207/balconies-basements-strength-ever-problem.aspx)
- Achiever basement words in a university handout attributed to Gallup: "unbalanced, brown-noser, overcommitted, can't say no". These are blunt, colloquial labels, not Gallup's own page text. [UConn handout PDF](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/289/2024/03/991_Balcony-Basement-Strengths-Handout-1-1-1-1-2-1.pdf); [Spalding handout](https://services.spalding.edu/hr/files/2025/08/Balconies-and-Basements-2.pdf)
- Hogan HDS: "qualities that emerge in times of increased strain". "Left unchecked, these can disrupt relationships, damage reputations". "When the pressure's on, the line between strength and weakness isn't always clear". "drive becomes ruthless ambition, and attention to detail becomes micromanaging". [Hogan Development Survey](https://www.hoganassessments.com/?p=41)
- Hogan's high-end scale descriptors (verbatim):
  - Excitable: "display dramatic emotional peaks and valleys regarding people and projects"
  - Skeptical: "are negative or cynical and expect to be betrayed"
  - Reserved: "are indifferent to the feelings of others"
  - Leisurely: "are passive-aggressive and agenda driven"
  - Bold: "seem assertive, self-promoting, and overly self-confident"
  - Mischievous: "are impulsive, limit testing, and at times, devious"
  - Colorful: "are attention-seeking, dramatic, and socially prominent"
  - Diligent: "may be inclined to micromanage"
  - Dutiful: "are excessively eager to please superiors"

  Each scale also has a low-end risk; for example, Reserved low is "are too concerned about the feelings of others". [Hogan HDS](https://www.hoganassessments.com/?p=41)
- Hogan's framing of derailers as "personality strengths that can get people into trouble during times of stress, burnout, complacency" came up in search results from Hogan's site. [Hogan](https://www.hoganassessments.com/?p=19751) (search text)
- DiSC D-style in a reproduced profile: "Under stress: May become impatient, demanding, and fearful of appearing weak or being taken advantage of." [Mahidol doc reproducing DiSC](https://www.rama.mahidol.ac.th/rama-km/web-api/api/Download/GetDataDownloadByKey/3356)
- DiSC's own blog: D-styles "may become impatient and harsh under pressure." [DiSC Profile blog](https://discprofile.com/blog/workplace-culture/disc-styles-and-stress)
- A DiSC crisis guide from Michigan State HR: "Typically, under stress, we may be 'more of' our usual style. Given enough unaddressed stress we may eventually get to the point that our strengths are overused and begin to work against us." [MSU HR: DiSC in Crisis](https://hr.msu.edu/ua/professional-development/DiSC%20in%20Crisis.html)

### Inferences
- Hogan's two-sided scales are a useful model: both too much and too little of a trait carry a risk. This avoids a "your strength has a dark side, sorry" tone, because there is no safe end.
- Hogan's verbatim descriptors are the most candid language found in any mainstream framework ("devious", "indifferent to the feelings of others"). This is probably acceptable because the report sits in a coaching context with a stress condition attached.
- The Gallup "lean into another theme" remedy fits a friction section well: the fix for one element's excess comes from another part of the person's own chart.

### Gaps
- Full Hogan sample report narrative (e.g. "You may…" coaching lines) and official Everything DiSC report wording were not retrieved. The DiSC quotes are secondary.

## 4. The Pattern and Co-Star: verbatim negative lines and habits

### Takeaway
Co-Star's push notifications are second person and sometimes deliberately rude ("You talk about other people because you don't have your own life"), and that rudeness is part of how the app spreads. The Pattern uses soft second-person conditionals ("At times, it can be difficult to…"). Readers still describe it as "reading me to filth" and "scarily accurate", which suggests specific gentle phrasing can feel more exposing than blunt phrasing.

### Cited Findings
- Co-Star example notifications: "You talk about other people because you don't have your own life" (described as "really rude"), "Embrace the possibility of abundant joy", "Avoiding your ex's social media is always a good idea". The writer says the insights "can feel like a free therapy session." The Gizmodo headline calls Co-Star "brutally" honest. [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/android-users-can-finally-get-in-on-co-star-s-brutally-1841067701)
- Rolling Stone described Co-Star's range as moving from brooding melancholia to vaguely Confucian advice to the practical (e.g. fold your laundry). [Rolling Stone AU](https://au.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/the-unexpected-joy-of-co-star-42305) (search summary)
- Examples from German coverage: "We're all gonna die someday, so take a chance today" and "Take yourself out to dinner tonight". [OMR](https://omr.com/de/co-star-app-viral/) (search summary)
- Daily Dot covered Co-Star notifications as a meme genre. [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/co-star-astrology-app-push-notifications-memes) (not fetched)
- The Pattern lines quoted in a Bustle review:
  - "At times, it can be difficult to feel a sense of security."
  - "Whether you're comfortable with it or not, you're different."
  - When not at her best, she can be "easily distracted" and might "hesitate to commit".
  - "If you're able to communicate, you can avoid hurt feelings."

  The reviewer said it was "reading me to filth", that everything felt "just so true", and on the commitment line: "yes, this was also scarily true." [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)
- The Pattern's reputation for being "scarily accurate" is so common it is "almost the Pattern's unofficial slogan". It avoids sun-sign language and splits results into instincts, growth and relationships. [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price); [Men's Health AU on Channing Tatum](https://menshealth.com.au/channing-tatum-astrology-app-pattern/)

### Inferences
- The Pattern's device: second person, a time-limited hedge ("At times"), an inner-experience verb ("feel", "hesitate"), and a conditional remedy ("If you're able to…"). It names a private feeling, not a public behaviour, which is why it feels intimate and not insulting.
- Co-Star's device: a short, unhedged, second-person line, often imperative or accusatory, sent at a random time. It is candid to the point of insult, and it works as entertainment and something to screenshot, not as self-knowledge.
- "Whether you're comfortable with it or not, you're different" admits the reader may resist the claim before making it. This pre-emptive acknowledgement is a useful candour device.

### Gaps
- The exact "You may find that…" construction in The Pattern could not be confirmed from a primary screenshot. No sourced data was found on how Co-Star's rude lines affect retention or trust.

## 5. Classical BaZi and Chinese astrology (English and Chinese)

### Takeaway
Classical Chinese fate texts are blunt and outcome-focused, with one key twist. 子平真诠 treats 七杀, 伤官 and 劫/刃 as "inauspicious" gods that are handled by control or transformation (制/化), not denied. So the classical text says the bad thing plainly and immediately names what tames it. Popular Chinese web writing is blunter still (character-insult lists). Modern English practitioners soften heavily ("may", "hints of", "assuming no remedies").

### Cited Findings
- 子平真诠《论用神》 (as summarised in the translated series) groups 七杀, 伤官, 阳刃 and 枭神 as gods that are "不善而逆用", meaning not good and handled against the grain. 七杀 likes 食伤 to control it (制伏) and dislikes 财/印 feeding it. 伤官 likes 印 to restrain it or 财 to transform it. [YKZ: ZPZQ 论用神 translation](https://ykzhong.substack.com/p/zpzq-1-discussing-application-god)
- Popular Chinese web writing on 伤官 (verbatim): "骄傲自大、心眼小言语伤人，做事儿不顾及别人的感受，喜好背后讲人是非，容易得罪人，缺少贵人帮助，事业容易失败" (arrogant, petty, wounding speech, inconsiderate, gossips, offends people, lacks patrons, prone to career failure). 七杀 is "重利益、手段强硬、与人不和" (profit-focused, heavy-handed, at odds with others). 劫财 is "好占有、缺乏爱心" (possessive, lacking in love). [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/395655830_553480) (search summary of the article; treat as popular, not classical)
- 伤官见官: when 岁运 strengthen 伤官 against 正官, "命主则会有胆犯官", which can bring 官非、口舌诉讼 (legal trouble, disputes). Writing on women's charts reads 伤官见官 as 叛逆、不服管教 (rebellious, won't be governed), affecting marriage. This last reading is a gendered, judgemental pattern to avoid. [Sohu](https://m.sohu.com/n/478572350/); [Master Sean Chan (ZH)](https://www.masterseanchan.com/zh-hans/blog/%E5%85%AB%E5%AD%97%E4%B8%AD%E7%9A%84%E7%A0%B4%E6%A0%BC%E7%A0%B4%E6%A0%BC/) (search summary)
- Modern English practitioner (Master Sean Chan) on Hurting Officer:
  - "certain character flaws which may lead to complications at work"
  - outcome: "losing authority and the favour of people above you"
  - 伤官见官: "a broken chart structure and a very negative one assuming it has no remedies"
  - one blunt aside about three 伤官: "Honestly, it's rather excessive."
  - quotes the saying 伤官伤尽最为奇 (a fully exhausted Hurting Officer is most remarkable), i.e. the flaw, fully resolved, becomes distinction

  [Master Sean Chan: Hurting Officer case study](https://www.masterseanchan.com/blog/hurting-officer-bazi-case-study-direct-officer-missing)
- A Joey Yap-branded profile chart renames the ten gods as archetypes: "The Warrior" (Seven Killings), "The Performer" (Hurting Officer), "The Leader" (Rob Wealth). This is a positive rebrand of names that are ominous in Chinese. [Scribd: Joey Yap profile chart](https://www.scribd.com/document/442187803/Introduction-Note) (low-quality source; single example)
- The 六神篇 text treats 伤官 as context-dependent: helpful (泄身, 生财) when the Day Master is strong, harmful (忌神) when weak. [Qidian 六神篇](https://m.qidian.com/chapter/1001576475/304685126/) (authorship unconfirmed)

### Inferences
- The classical BaZi device closest to "strength-overuse" is 制/化 (control/transform) together with 身强/身弱 (strong/weak Day Master). A god is a flaw only in a given balance, and every flaw comes with its named antidote element. This is structurally candid and fits Elementum's element language directly, e.g. "Hurting Officer unrestrained → Resource restrains it".
- The tone split: classical text (terse verdict plus remedy) is candid. Popular Chinese web content (stacked insult adjectives, gendered marriage warnings) is harsh and fatalistic. Western-market practitioners (hedged "may", archetype rebranding) are sugarcoated.
- The classical saying 伤官见官，为祸百端 (Hurting Officer meeting Officer brings a hundred calamities) is widely cited in popular articles. Its exact textual origin was not verified here.

### Gaps
- Verbatim original text of 子平真诠《论七杀》《论伤官》 and 滴天髓 lines was not retrieved. ctext.org or an authoritative annotated edition (徐乐吾注) is needed.
- No sourced data on the tone of modern Chinese apps (测测, 问真八字). This could not be checked within the call budget.
- No Jerry King or Imperial Harvest examples were retrieved.

## 6. Candid horoscope and tarot writers (Brezsny, Chani Nicholas, Lanyadoo, Susan Miller)

### Takeaway
Little sourced verbatim material was found. What was found shows two positions. Brezsny and Chani Nicholas frame hard news inside a self-acceptance or "pronoia" philosophy that is optimistic by design. Lanyadoo's brand is "no-bullshit" and centres on accountability.

### Cited Findings
- Brezsny named the column "Free Will Astrology to dispel the notion that the stars alone determine our fates". "Pronoia", a term coined by John Perry Barlow, is "the opposite of paranoia". [The Sun Magazine: Secrets of Pronoia](https://www.thesunmagazine.org/articles/25454-secrets-of-pronoia)
- Brezsny's philosophy treats life as a labyrinth with a reward at its centre, not a minefield (paraphrase of his interview). [Village Voice: Astro Turf](https://www.villagevoice.com/astro-turf/); [Monterey County Weekly interview](https://www.montereycountynow.com/news/cover/rob-brezsny-on-god-media-and-why-we-re-better-off-than-you-think/article_a724c4fa-ce9a-5c71-ba83-330241bc67d7.html) (search summaries)
- Jessica Lanyadoo is described as a "no-bullshit mystical advice-giver" whose approach centres on the relationship to the self and on accountability. [NYLON](https://www.nylon.com/astrologer-jessica-lanyadoo-book-interview); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lanyadoo)
- Chani Nicholas's book is titled *You Were Born For This: Astrology For Radical Self-Acceptance*. She stresses that planets can guide you but can't do the work for you. [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2019/12/31/astrologer-chani-nicholas-self-acceptance); [Domestic Workers podcast](https://www.domesticworkers.org/?p=345)

### Inferences
- In this genre, candour usually comes as agency ("free will", "accountability", "the planets can't do the work for you"), not as a description of a flaw. Hard news is reframed as a task.

### Gaps
- No verbatim hard-news horoscope lines from Brezsny, Nicholas, Lanyadoo or Susan Miller were retrieved. Their columns are copyrighted and were not fetched. This needs direct sampling of current columns.

## 7. Cross-framework devices, candour rating, and reader reception

### Takeaway
Eight recurring devices appear across the frameworks. Candour depends less on which device is used than on two things: whether the flaw is named plainly somewhere (heading, descriptor, verdict) and whether its cost to others is stated. The reception research shows unfavourable statements are believed less than favourable ones, so candid lines need specificity and a framing that makes them feel like feedback to be accepted.

### Cited Findings (reception)
- Dickson & Kelly's (1985) review: acceptance of Barnum profiles "depends on the relevance and favorability of the profile and to some extent on the type of assessment utilized". [Dickson & Kelly review PDF](https://cortecs.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/the-barnum-effect-in-personality-assessment-a-review-of-the-literature.pdf)
- A 1997 study (Davies, n = 71 students) found that "favourable Barnum personality statements were rated more accurate than unfavourable statements", and statements were rated more accurate when framed as feedback than as test items. [Search-surfaced abstract; likely Vilnius University journal](https://www.journals.vu.lt/psichologija/article/view/4893) (author/URL attribution not fully verified)
- A 1989 PSPB study on astrology and the Barnum effect found favourable descriptions were generally seen as more accurate. The exception: astrology believers accepted a negative astrological description as much as a positive one. [PSPB 1989](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0146167289154010) (search summary; probably Glick, Gottesman & Jolton, "The fault is not in the stars", not verified here)
- User reception in practice: The Pattern's soft-but-specific negatives produced "reading me to filth" and "scarily true" ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)). Co-Star's rude lines produced shareable "brutally" honest coverage ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/android-users-can-finally-get-in-on-co-star-s-brutally-1841067701)).

### Device inventory (each grounded in the sections above)

**1. Strength-overuse / strength-misused**
- Where: Gallup (basement "when misapplied"), Hogan ("drive becomes ruthless ambition"), 16P (self-assurance → arrogance), DiSC ("strengths are overused").
- Candour: candid if the cost is named; sugarcoated if the line stays at "too much of a good thing".

**2. Under-stress / under-pressure conditional**
- Where: DiSC ("Under stress: May become…"), Hogan ("emerge in times of increased strain"), The Pattern ("when not at her best").
- Candour: medium. It is honest about when the flaw appears, but it can excuse the flaw.

**3. Levels / states / direction of travel**
- Where: Enneagram Levels ("Begin to…", "Become…", "deteriorate into").
- Candour: allows very harsh wording (Level 7: "completely ruthless") without labelling the reader's identity.

**4. Behaviour or perception, not identity**
- Where: 16P ("come across as", "appear"), Crystal ("be perceived as"), Hogan ("seem").
- Candour: softening, but it keeps the cost to others visible.

**5. Cost to others named**
- Where: 16P ("needlessly harsh"), Hogan ("disrupt relationships, damage reputations"), Master Sean Chan ("losing… the favour of people above you"), popular BaZi ("容易得罪人").
- Candour: the most candour-carrying device.

**6. Blunt label heading, soft body**
- Where: 16P headings ("Arrogant"), Hogan descriptors, Gallup handout ("can't say no").
- Candour: candid at a glance.

**7. Named remedy from within the system**
- Where: Gallup ("lean into another theme"), BaZi 制/化 (印 restrains 伤官; 食伤 controls 七杀), The Pattern ("If you're able to communicate…").
- Candour: candid, because the flaw is real enough to need a fix.

**8. Humour or rudeness / imperative one-liners**
- Where: Co-Star ("You talk about other people because…"; "drink water").
- Candour: candid but uncaring. It works for virality, not insight.

**Other devices**
- Pre-emptive acknowledgement of resistance: The Pattern ("Whether you're comfortable with it or not…").
- Two-sided risk (too little is also a flaw): Hogan high/low scales; BaZi 身强/身弱 context.
- Positive archetype renaming: Joey Yap ("The Warrior" for 七杀). This is sugarcoated.
- Questions to the reader: Co-Star's in-app flow asks which life areas are going well or badly ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/android-users-can-finally-get-in-on-co-star-s-brutally-1841067701)). No verbatim reflective-question examples were sourced from the other frameworks.

### Inferences
- Candid-feeling recipe, supported across sources: a plain noun or adjective for the flaw + its root in a strength or element balance + a trigger ("when…/under…") + a concrete cost to someone else + a fix drawn from the person's own system. Hogan, the Enneagram Levels and classical BaZi 制化 come closest. 16P headings plus Gallup remedies are the gentler versions.
- Sugarcoated-feeling markers: only "may/can" with no cost, positive archetype renaming, "too much of a good thing" with no consequence, and third-person distance.
- The reception findings suggest that because unfavourable lines are believed less, specificity (The Pattern's private-feeling detail) and the system's own logic (BaZi believers accepted negative descriptions as readily as positive ones) carry the credibility. Bluntness alone does not.

### Gaps
- No controlled study was found comparing these specific devices (e.g. "under stress" vs. "strength-overuse") for perceived accuracy or user satisfaction.
- The Snyder, Shenkel & Lowery (1977) review was only cited secondhand and was not verified.
