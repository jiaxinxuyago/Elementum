The energy tile — format & examples

Each energy in your chart is a tile on the reading shelf. Closed, it’s a thin spine with its mark and presence; tapped open, it becomes the card below — a hook over a painted ground, with the calibration role and dominance laid in. Five tiles, always all five present.

Calibration signs — the energy-tile iconography

Every open energy tile carries up to two calibration glyphs in its top-right corner (the role each energy plays in the chart) and a 100% dominance bar above the hook. Five roles, color-coded; a single energy may hold two — e.g. Metal is both your Core and a source of Resistance. These are the primitives reused across the catalogue, energy cards, and chips.

Core Core
Your Day Master's element — the anchor the whole reading is built around. Solid filled disc.
Used on: the dominant energy tile · day-master border on P4/P5.
Catalyst Catalyst ↑
An energy that feeds and strengthens your edge. Upward arrow, sage-green. A major catalyst gains an emphasis ring.
Used on: supportive energy tiles · "feeds your edge" hooks.
Resistance Resistance ↓
An energy that drains or costs you — the friction to manage. Downward arrow, cinnabar-red.
Used on: tiles that "cost you" · paired with Core on an over-dominant element.
Missing Missing —
An energy absent from the chart (0%) — must be borrowed or cultivated. Dashed hollow ring, muted.
Used on: the ghost tile (Fire here) · grayscale thumbnail + dashed dominance sliver.
Ally Ally
An energy that steadies and balances you — supportive without being a primary driver. Linked rings, slate-blue. (Defined for completeness; not in this demo chart.)
Used on: secondary supporting tiles when the chart calls for it.
The 100% dominance bar. All five energies' shares in one track; the tile's own segment shows full-strength with a highlight ring, the others muted, a 0% energy as a dashed sliver — so each tile reads its weight relative to the whole chart, not in isolation.

The wheel — geometry explorations · ink-wash register

All variants share the painted language: brush enso ring over quiet paper, wash-medallion nodes with Set E3 marks, ghost dashed, ↑/↓ badges, node ⌀ ∝ presence. P3 commits to presence seating + whisper generation chords; the alternates answer the spoke question and the order question.

In P3
W·0 · committed

Whisper chords

All five generation relations as inner chords at 10% ink — present, never loud. Only Earth feeds Metal carries pigment: the wheel's structure underlines the prescription ribbon.

W·A · alternate

Quiet ring

No spokes, no chords — brush ring + nodes only, one clockwise hint. The calmest reading surface; the cycle waits inside the cards' expanders.

W·B · alternate

Weighted spokes

A hairline from every node to the seal, stroke weight ∝ presence; the ghost's thread is dashed. Says "every element relates to YOU" at the cost of a busier center.

W·C · alternate

Marked axis

Spokes only where the prescription points: catalyst ↑ in green, friction ↓ dashed in rust. The quietest wheel that still encodes the ribbon structurally.

W·D · alternate

Cycle seating

Nodes seated in generation order (Metal → Water → Wood → Fire → Earth) so every chord runs neighbor-to-neighbor — the cycle reads true around the ring. Trade-off: presence hierarchy no longer reads top-down.
